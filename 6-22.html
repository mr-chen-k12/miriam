Student Test Sheet

Question 1

Five gallons equals how many quarts? (Hint: 1 gallon = 4 quarts)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Know the rule: 1 gallon = 4 quarts.

2. Multiply the number of gallons by 4:

5 gallons × 4 quarts = 20 quarts

Question 2

Liam gave the cashier a $20 bill to buy a new toy model car. He received $4.50 back in change. How much did the toy car cost?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the change from the money Liam gave the cashier:

$20.00 − $4.50 = $15.50

Question 3

Amy is 4 years older than Ben. Ben is two times as old as Cody. If Amy is 12 years old, how old is Cody?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Find Ben's age first: Amy is 12 and she is 4 years older than Ben. So, Ben is 12 − 4 = 8 years old.

2. Find Cody's age: Ben is 2 times as old as Cody. So, divide Ben's age by 2:

8 ÷ 2 = 4 years old (Cody is 4 years old).

Question 4

Write a fraction to show each of the following descriptions:

(a) 1 out of 4 equal slices of a pizza eaten.

(b) 3 out of 5 pieces of candy shared.

(c) 5 out of 8 colored pencils used.

Teacher Answer Key

Answers:

(a) 1/4

(b) 3/5

(c) 5/8

Question 5

One-third of the 27 students in Mr. Smith's class voted to play kickball at recess. How many students voted to play kickball?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-third of a group, divide the total number of students by 3:

27 ÷ 3 = 9 students

Question 6

Looking back at Question 5, how many students did not vote to play kickball?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the students who voted for kickball from the total class size:

27 − 9 = 18 students

Question 9

Estimate the sum of 57 and 34 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then, find the exact sum.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Estimate: Round 57 up to 60. Round 34 down to 30. Add them up: 60 + 30 = 90.

2. Exact Sum: Add the original numbers together: 57 + 34 = 91.

Question 10

Maya tracked the number of books she read over the last few months: 4, 3, 5, 3, 6.

(a) Which number of books shows up the most often (the mode)?

(b) How many books did she read in all?

Teacher Answer Key

Answers:

(a) Mode: 3 (It appears twice, which is more than any other number).

(b) Total: 4 + 3 + 5 + 3 + 6 = 21 books.

Question 13

Calculate: $30.00 − ($12.50 + $4.50)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Always solve the numbers inside the parentheses first: $12.50 + $4.50 = $17.00.

2. Subtract that amount from $30.00:

$30.00 − $17.00 = $13.00
