1. Two quarters, three dimes, four nickels, and five pennies is how much money?

Answer: $1.05

Explanation: Calculate the value of each coin type and sum them up:

  • 2 quarters = \( 2 \times \$0.25 = \$0.50 \)
  • 3 dimes = \( 3 \times \$0.10 = \$0.30 \)
  • 4 nickels = \( 4 \times \$0.05 = \$0.20 \)
  • 5 pennies = \( 5 \times \$0.01 = \$0.05 \)

Total = \( \$0.50 + \$0.30 + \$0.20 + \$0.05 = \$1.05 \)

2. Marcus separated 40 library books as equally as possible into 6 stacks.
(a) How many stacks had exactly 7 books?
(b) How many stacks had 6 books?

Answer: (a) 4 stacks, (b) 2 stacks

Explanation: Divide the total number of books by the number of stacks: \( 40 \div 6 = 6 \) with a remainder of \( 4 \).

This means if we put 6 books in each of the 6 stacks, we have used 36 books. There are 4 leftover books. To distribute as equally as possible, we place 1 extra book on 4 of the stacks. Therefore, 4 stacks get 7 books, and the remaining 2 stacks keep 6 books.

3. Sarah paid $5.00 for a notebook and received $1.25 back in change. If the tax was $0.15, how much did the notebook cost without tax?

Answer: $3.60

Explanation: First, find the total cost of the notebook including tax by subtracting the change from the amount paid:

\( \$5.00 - \$1.25 = \$3.75 \) (total cost with tax)

Next, subtract the tax amount to find the base cost:

\( \$3.75 - \$0.15 = \$3.60 \)

4. Emily wrote each of her 15 vocabulary words four times. In all, how many words did she write?

Answer: 60 words

Explanation: Multiply the number of words by the number of times each was written:

\( 15 \text{ words} \times 4 \text{ times} = 60 \text{ words in total} \).

5. Round 4321 to the nearest thousand. Round 6789 to the nearest thousand. Find the sum of the two rounded numbers.

Answer: 11000

Explanation:

  • \( 4321 \) rounded to the nearest thousand is \( 4000 \).
  • \( 6789 \) rounded to the nearest thousand is \( 7000 \).

Sum: \( 4000 + 7000 = 11000 \).

6. What is the tally for 14?

Answer: Two groups of five and four single vertical lines.

Explanation: In tally marks, each group of five is represented by four vertical lines and one diagonal line crossing them out. For 14, you draw two such groups (making 10) and four extra vertical lines.

8. One fourth of the 32 pencils are broken. How many pencils are broken? Draw a picture to illustrate the problem.

Answer: 8 pencils

Explanation: To find one fourth of a number, divide it by 4. \( 32 \div 4 = 8 \).

We can draw 4 boxes to represent the fourths, each containing 8 pencils, and shade one box to show the broken ones.

9. Segment AB is 20 mm long. Segment BC is 35 mm long. Segment AD is 85 mm long. How many millimeters long is segment CD?

Answer: 30 mm

Explanation: The total length of segment AD is the sum of its parts: \( AD = AB + BC + CD \).

We know: \( 85 = 20 + 35 + CD \)

\( 85 = 55 + CD \)

\( CD = 85 - 55 = 30 \text{ mm} \).

10. Which digit in 8.943 is in the hundredths place?

Answer: 4

Explanation: In the decimal 8.943:

  • The 9 is in the tenths place.
  • The 4 is in the hundredths place.
  • The 3 is in the thousandths place.
11. If a quart of juice weighs about 2 pounds, then about how many pounds does a gallon of juice weigh?

Answer: 8 pounds

Explanation: There are 4 quarts in 1 gallon. If each quart weighs 2 pounds, a gallon weighs \( 4 \times 2 = 8 \text{ pounds} \).

13. \( 3^2 + \sqrt{9} + N = 20 \)

Answer: N = 8

Explanation: Evaluate the exponents and roots first:

\( 3^2 = 9 \)

\( \sqrt{9} = 3 \)

Substitute these values back into the equation:

\( 9 + 3 + N = 20 \)

\( 12 + N = 20 \)

\( N = 8 \)

20. \( 2535 \div 5 \)

Answer: 507

Explanation: Perform long division:

  • \( 25 \div 5 = 5 \) (remainder 0)
  • Bring down 3. \( 3 \div 5 = 0 \) (remainder 3)
  • Bring down 5, making 35. \( 35 \div 5 = 7 \) (remainder 0)

Result: 507

21. \( 4326 \div 6 \)

Answer: 721

Explanation: Perform long division:

  • \( 43 \div 6 = 7 \) (remainder 1)
  • Bring down 2, making 12. \( 12 \div 6 = 2 \) (remainder 0)
  • Bring down 6, \( 6 \div 6 = 1 \) (remainder 0)

Result: 721

22. \( \$45.60 \div 8 \)

Answer: $5.70

Explanation: Divide keeping the decimal in place:

  • \( 45 \div 8 = 5 \) (remainder 5)
  • Bring down 6 past the decimal, making 56. \( 56 \div 8 = 7 \) (remainder 0)
  • Bring down 0, \( 0 \div 8 = 0 \)

Result: \$5.70

23. Draw and shade rectangles to show that \( 1 \frac{1}{2} \) equals \( \frac{3}{2} \).

Answer & Explanation:

Draw two whole rectangles and divide each into 2 equal sections (representing halves). Shade both sections in the first rectangle (representing 1 whole, or \( \frac{2}{2} \)). Shade 1 section in the second rectangle (representing \( \frac{1}{2} \)).

Counting the shaded parts, you have exactly 3 shaded sections, which demonstrates that \( 1 \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2} \).

25. Shade 50% of the square you drew in problem 24. How many square centimeters did you shade?

26. Write forty-five thousandths as a fraction and as a decimal number.

Answer: Fraction: \( \frac{45}{1000} \), Decimal: 0.045

Explanation: The term "thousandths" means the denominator of the fraction is 1000. So we have \( \frac{45}{1000} \).

To write this as a decimal, the number 45 must end in the thousandths place (the third digit after the decimal point). Therefore, we place a zero in the tenths place: 0.045.

27. Which of these polygons has NO lines of symmetry?
(A) Rectangle
(B) Equilateral Triangle
(C) Scalene Triangle
(D) Square

Answer: C

Explanation: Let's check each shape:

  • A: Rectangle. Has 2 lines of symmetry.
  • B: Equilateral Triangle. Has 3 lines of symmetry.
  • C: Scalene Triangle. All sides and angles are different, so it cannot be folded to match perfectly. It has 0 lines of symmetry.
  • D: Square. Has 4 lines of symmetry.
28. Which of these polygons is NOT a quadrilateral?
(A) Pentagon
(B) Rhombus
(C) Trapezoid
(D) Rectangle

Answer: A

Explanation: A quadrilateral is a polygon with exactly 4 sides. Rhombuses, trapezoids, and rectangles all have 4 sides. A pentagon has 5 sides, making it the only option that is not a quadrilateral.

29. In a quarter of an hour, the minute hand of a clock turns how many degrees?

Answer: \( 90^\circ \)

Explanation: A full clock circle represents one hour and spans \( 360^\circ \). A quarter of an hour covers exactly one fourth of the circle, which is \( 360^\circ \div 4 = 90^\circ \).

30. Compare: \( 3.5 \bigcirc 3.50 \)

Answer: \( 3.5 = 3.50 \)

Explanation: Trailing zeros after a decimal point do not change the value of the number. Both numbers have a 3 in the ones place and a 5 in the tenths place. Thus, they are equal.
