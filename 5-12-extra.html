Math Practice Quiz

Topic 1: Making Change

2. John bought lunch for $15.20. Tax was $1.10. He paid with a $20.00 bill. How much change does he receive?
3. Sarah bought a plant for $22.45. Tax was $1.55. She paid with a $50.00 bill. How much change should she get back?
