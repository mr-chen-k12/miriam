2. David separated 45 science books as equally as possible into 6 stacks.
(a) How many stacks had exactly 7 books?
(b) How many stacks had 8 books?

Answer: (a) 3 stacks, (b) 3 stacks

Explanation: Divide the total number of books by the number of stacks: \( 45 \div 6 = 7 \) with a remainder of \( 3 \).

This means if we put 7 books in each of the 6 stacks, we have used 42 books. There are 3 leftover books. To distribute them equally, we place 1 extra book on 3 of the stacks. Therefore, 3 stacks get 8 books, and the remaining 3 stacks keep 7 books.

3. Mark paid $5.00 for a notebook and received $2.15 back in change. If the tax was $0.25, how much did the notebook cost without tax?

Answer: $2.60

Explanation: First, find the total cost of the notebook including tax by subtracting the change from the amount paid:

\( \$5.00 - \$2.15 = \$2.85 \) (total cost with tax)

Next, subtract the tax amount to find the base cost:

\( \$2.85 - \$0.25 = \$2.60 \)

11. If a quart of juice weighs about 2 pounds, then about how many pounds does a gallon of juice weigh?

Answer: 8 pounds

Explanation: There are 4 quarts in 1 gallon. If each quart weighs 2 pounds, a gallon weighs \( 4 \times 2 = 8 \text{ pounds} \).

13. \( 4^2 + \sqrt{16} + N = 25 \)

Answer: N = 5

Explanation: Evaluate the exponents and roots first:

\( 4^2 = 16 \)

\( \sqrt{16} = 4 \)

Substitute these values back into the equation:

\( 16 + 4 + N = 25 \)

\( 20 + N = 25 \)

\( N = 5 \)

23. Draw and shade rectangles to show that \( 1 \frac{1}{3} \) equals \( \frac{4}{3} \).

Answer & Explanation:

Draw two whole rectangles and divide each into 3 equal sections (representing thirds). Shade all 3 sections in the first rectangle (representing 1 whole, or \( \frac{3}{3} \)). Shade 1 section in the second rectangle (representing \( \frac{1}{3} \)).

Counting the shaded parts, you have exactly 4 shaded sections, which demonstrates that \( 1 \frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{3} \).

26. Write thirty-seven thousandths as a fraction and as a decimal number.

Answer: Fraction: \( \frac{37}{1000} \), Decimal: 0.037

Explanation: The term "thousandths" means the denominator of the fraction is 1000. So we have \( \frac{37}{1000} \).

To write this as a decimal, the number 37 must end in the thousandths place (the third digit after the decimal point). Therefore, we place a zero in the tenths place: 0.037.

28. Which of these polygons is NOT a quadrilateral?
(A) Rectangle
(B) Hexagon
(C) Trapezoid
(D) Square

Answer: B

Explanation: A quadrilateral is a polygon with exactly 4 sides. Rectangles, trapezoids, and squares all have 4 sides. A hexagon has 6 sides, making it the only option that is not a quadrilateral.
