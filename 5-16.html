Context for Problems 1-3: "Baskets Made by Joe"
1. How many baskets did Joe make in the first game?
Answer: 4 baskets

Explanation: Locate "Game 1" on the horizontal axis (x-axis) of the graph. Trace up to the data point and look across to the vertical axis (y-axis) to read the number. The point aligns with 4.
2. Each basket is worth 2 points. How many points did Joe score in Game 5?
Answer: 14 points

Explanation: First, find how many baskets Joe made in Game 5 by looking at the graph (7 baskets). Since each basket is worth 2 points, multiply the number of baskets by 2:
\( 7 \times 2 = 14 \).
3. How many more points did Joe score in Game 5 than in Game 3?
Answer: 2 points

Explanation:
Game 5 Points: 7 baskets \(\times 2 = 14\) points.
Game 3 Points: 6 baskets \(\times 2 = 12\) points.
Subtract the two values: \( 14 - 12 = 2 \).
4. The 3-pound melon cost $1.44. What was the cost per pound?
Answer: $0.48 (or 48¢)

Explanation: Divide the total cost by the total weight:
\( \$1.44 \div 3 \).
\( 144 \div 3 = 48 \), so \( \$1.44 \div 3 = \$0.48 \).
5. Jim spun all the way around in the air and dunked the basketball. Jim turned about how many degrees?
Answer: 360 degrees

Explanation: Spinning "all the way around" implies completing a full circle. A full circle measures 360 degrees.
6. Sammy bought a pair of shoes priced at $47.99. The sales tax was $2.88. Sammy gave the clerk $60.00. How much money should he get back?
Answer: $9.13

Explanation:
Step 1: Find the total cost. \( \$47.99 + \$2.88 = \$50.87 \).
Step 2: Find the change. \( \$60.00 - \$50.87 = \$9.13 \).
(Note: The student originally calculated the sum incorrectly as $51.87, which led to a wrong final answer of $8.13).
7. If the perimeter of a square is 1 foot, how many inches long is each side?
Answer: 3 inches

Explanation:
Step 1: Convert feet to inches. 1 foot = 12 inches.
Step 2: A square has 4 sides of equal length. Divide the perimeter by 4: \( 12 \div 4 = 3 \) inches.
8. A dollar bill weighs about 1 gram. Use this information to estimate the number of dollar bills it would take to weigh 1 kilogram.
Answer: 1,000

Explanation: There are 1,000 grams in 1 kilogram. If each dollar bill weighs 1 gram, it would take \( 1000 \div 1 = 1000 \) dollar bills.

18. \( 328 \times 4 \)
Answer: 1312

Explanation:
\( 8 \times 4 = 32 \) (Write 2, carry 3)
\( 2 \times 4 = 8 \). Add carry: \( 8 + 3 = 11 \) (Write 1, carry 1)
\( 3 \times 4 = 12 \). Add carry: \( 12 + 1 = 13 \)
Result is 1312.

Answer: 1376

Explanation:
Multiply by the ones digit: \( 43 \times 2 = 86 \)
Multiply by the tens digit: \( 43 \times 30 = 1290 \)
Add the partial products: \( 86 + 1290 = 1376 \)

26. \( 60 \times 700 \)
Answer: 42,000

Explanation: Multiply the non-zero digits: \( 6 \times 7 = 42 \). Then count the total number of zeros in the factors (one from 60, two from 700 = three zeros) and append them: 42,000.
27. Draw and shade circles to show that \( 2 \frac{1}{2} \) equals \( \frac{5}{2} \).
Answer:
Explanation: To show \( 2 \frac{1}{2} \), you need 3 whole circles split into halves. You completely shade 2 whole circles (which is 4 halves) and shade half of the 3rd circle (1 half). Counting the shaded halves gives \( 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 \) halves, which proves it equals \( \frac{5}{2} \).
28. \( 4 + 3 + 27 + 35 + 8 + N = 112 \)
Answer: \( N = 35 \)

Explanation:
Step 1: Add the known numbers together.
\( 4 + 3 = 7 \)
\( 7 + 27 = 34 \)
\( 34 + 35 = 69 \)
\( 69 + 8 = 77 \)
Step 2: Set up the simplified equation: \( 77 + N = 112 \).
Step 3: Subtract 77 from 112 to find N: \( 112 - 77 = 35 \).
29. Segment BC is 1.7 cm long. How many centimeters long is segment AB?
Answer: 1.8 cm

Explanation: Looking at the ruler provided in the image, point A is at the 0 cm mark and point C is at the 3.5 cm mark. The total length of AC is 3.5 cm. We are given that BC is 1.7 cm.
Since \( AB + BC = AC \):
\( AB + 1.7 = 3.5 \)
\( AB = 3.5 - 1.7 = 1.8 \) cm. You can also verify this by observing point B rests exactly on the 1.8 mark on the ruler.
30. Write a decimal addition problem that is illustrated by the lengths of segments AB, BC, and AC in problem 29.
Answer: \( 1.8 + 1.7 = 3.5 \)

Explanation: The length of segment AB (1.8) added to the length of segment BC (1.7) equals the total length of segment AC (3.5).
