Saxon Math 4 Cumulative Test 9B
Solutions & Explanations

Problem 1
Six hundred fifty-five tickets were sold for the first performance of a play. Altogether nine hundred ninety-three tickets were sold for two performances. How many tickets were sold for the second performance?

We know the total tickets sold and the amount sold for the first performance. To find the remainder, we subtract.

Total tickets = 993
First performance = 655

$$ 993 - 655 = 338 $$
Answer: 338 tickets
Problem 2
Benjamin paid $5.00 for an item that cost $4.73. How much money should Benjamin get back?

To find the change, subtract the cost from the amount paid.

$$ \$5.00 - \$4.73 $$

Borrowing calculation:
1. $10 - 3 = 7$
2. $9 - 7 = 2$
3. $4 - 4 = 0$

Answer: $0.27
Problem 3
Round 424 to the nearest hundred.

Look at the tens digit (2). Since 2 is less than 5, we round down.

Answer: 400

Problem 5
Use words to write 12.4.

The decimal point is read as "and". The position to the right of the decimal is the "tenths" place.

Answer: Twelve and four tenths
Problem 6
It is night. What time was it 2 hours 30 minutes ago according to this clock? (Clock shows 10:10).

1. The clock shows 10:10. Since it is night, it is 10:10 PM.

2. Subtract 2 hours: \( 10:10 - 2 \text{ hours} = 8:10 \text{ PM} \).

3. Subtract 30 minutes: \( 8:10 - 30 \text{ minutes} \).

  • \( 8:10 - 10 \text{ mins} = 8:00 \)
  • \( 8:00 - 20 \text{ mins} = 7:40 \)
Answer: 7:40 PM (or just 7:40)
Problem 7
To the nearest quarter inch, how long is segment AB?

The ruler shows inches divided into quarters. Point B is past the 1 inch mark. It aligns with the third mark after the 1. The marks represent \( \frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{4} \).

Answer: \( 1\frac{3}{4} \) inches
Problem 8
To what mixed number is the arrow pointing?

The number line is between 6 and 7. There are 7 tick marks between the numbers, creating 8 spaces. This means the scale is in eighths.

The arrow is pointing to the 3rd mark after the 6.

Answer: \( 6\frac{3}{8} \)
Problem 9
There were three hundred thirty-five books in the first shipment. There were eight hundred twenty-eight books in the second shipment. How many fewer books were in the first shipment?

"How many fewer" means we need to find the difference between the two amounts.

$$ 828 - 335 $$

Calculation:
\( 8 - 5 = 3 \)
\( 2 - 3 \) (borrow from 8, making it 7 and 12) \( \rightarrow 12 - 3 = 9 \)
\( 7 - 3 = 4 \)

Answer: 493 books
Problem 10
Find the missing number: $$ \begin{aligned} n \\ - \underline{364} \\ 942 \end{aligned} $$

This is a subtraction problem where the top number is missing. We add the bottom number and the difference to find $n$.

$$ n = 942 + 364 $$ $$ 942 + 364 = 1306 $$
Answer: 1306
Problem 11
Find the missing number: $$ \begin{aligned} 778 \\ - \underline{\;\;s\;\;} \\ 547 \end{aligned} $$

To find the number being subtracted ($s$), we subtract the answer from the top number.

$$ s = 778 - 547 $$ $$ s = 231 $$
Answer: 231
Problem 12
Solve for n: \( 6n = 54 \)

This means 6 times what number equals 54?

$$ n = 54 \div 6 $$
Answer: 9
Problem 13
Calculate: \( \$6.09 - \$2.56 \)

Align the decimal points and subtract.

$$ \begin{aligned} 6.09 \\ - \underline{2.56} \\ 3.53 \end{aligned} $$
Answer: $3.53
Problem 14
Calculate: \( 60 \times 3 \)

Multiply 6 by 3 to get 18, then attach the zero.

$$ 6 \times 3 = 18 \rightarrow 180 $$
Answer: 180
Problem 15
Calculate: \( 52 \times 4 \)

Multiply step by step:

  1. \( 4 \times 2 = 8 \)
  2. \( 4 \times 50 = 200 \)
$$ 200 + 8 = 208 $$
Answer: 208
Problem 16
Calculate: \( 72 + (8 \times 6) \)

Follow the order of operations (parentheses first).

  1. \( 8 \times 6 = 48 \)
  2. \( 72 + 48 = 120 \)
Answer: 120
Problem 17
Calculate: \( 7 \times (3 + 5) \)

Do the addition inside the parentheses first.

  1. \( 3 + 5 = 8 \)
  2. \( 7 \times 8 = 56 \)
Answer: 56
Problem 18
Calculate: \( \$7.29 + 43\text{¢} + \$7 \)

Convert everything to dollars (decimal form) first.

  • \( 43\text{¢} = \$0.43 \)
  • \( \$7 = \$7.00 \)

Add: \( 7.29 + 0.43 + 7.00 \)

$$ 7.29 + 0.43 = 7.72 $$ $$ 7.72 + 7.00 = 14.72 $$
Answer: $14.72
Problem 19
Calculate: \( 0.75 - 0.50 \)

Subtract the decimals.

$$ \begin{aligned} 0.75 \\ - \underline{0.50} \\ 0.25 \end{aligned} $$
Answer: 0.25
Problem 20
Calculate: \( \sqrt{49} + \sqrt{16} \)

Find the square roots first.

  • \( \sqrt{49} = 7 \) (because \( 7 \times 7 = 49 \))
  • \( \sqrt{16} = 4 \) (because \( 4 \times 4 = 16 \))
$$ 7 + 4 = 11 $$
Answer: 11
