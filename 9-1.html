Question 1

Eight feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply the number of feet by 12:

8 feet × 12 inches = 96 inches

Question 2

Gabriel handed the cashier a $60 bill to buy a pair of sneakers that cost $34.85. How much change did he receive?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the cost from $60.00:

$60.00 − $34.85 = $25.15

Question 3

Liam is 6 years younger than Ava. Ava is three times as old as Mia. If Ava is 27 years old, how old are Liam and Mia?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Liam: 27 − 6 = 21 years old.

2. Mia: 27 ÷ 3 = 9 years old.

Liam is 21 and Mia is 9.

Question 4

Write a fraction to show each description:

(b) 6 out of 14 markers are red.

(c) 4 out of 7 sandwiches were eaten.

Teacher Answer Key

Answers:

(a) 5/11

(b) 6/14

(c) 4/7

Question 5

One-fourth of the 36 students in Mr. Lee's class brought lunch from home. How many students brought lunch from home?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-fourth of 36, divide by 4:

36 ÷ 4 = 9 students

Question 6

Looking back at Question 5, how many students did not bring lunch from home?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the students who brought lunch from the total:

36 − 9 = 27 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 94 and 47 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then find the exact difference.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Estimate: 94 → 90 and 47 → 50, so 90 − 50 = 40.

2. Exact Difference: 94 − 47 = 47.

A school concert started at 2:25 PM and ended at 5:05 PM. How long did the concert last?

Question 17

Is the sum of 542 + 317 even or odd? Explain how you know by looking at the ones digits.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

542 + 317 = 859, so the answer is odd.
