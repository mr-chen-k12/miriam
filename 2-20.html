Math Practice Worksheet 2

1. Sarah earns $8.25 an hour for babysitting. How much does she earn if she works for 5 hours? Write an equation to solve the problem.
Answer: \$41.25
Equation: \( \$8.25 \times 5 = x \)
Explanation: Multiply her hourly rate by the number of hours worked. \( 8.25 \times 5 = 41.25 \).
2. It takes 3 cups of flour to make 1 batch of cookies. How many cups of flour does it take to make 4 batches? Make a table to solve the problem.

3. The movie starts at 7:15 p.m. and is 2 hours and 10 minutes long. What time does it end?
Answer: 9:25 p.m.
Explanation: Add 2 hours to 7:15 p.m. to get 9:15 p.m. Then add the 10 minutes to get 9:25 p.m.
4. A recipe calls for 12 pints of milk. The store only sells milk in quarts and gallons. What is the most efficient combination of containers to buy exactly 12 pints?
Answer: 1 gallon and 2 quarts (or 1 gallon and 1 half-gallon if available, but strictly using quarts and gallons, 1 gallon + 2 quarts).
Explanation: There are 2 pints in 1 quart, and 4 quarts in 1 gallon (so 8 pints in 1 gallon). 12 pints equals 6 quarts. 6 quarts can be grouped into 1 gallon (4 quarts) with 2 quarts left over.
5. Write 15,039 in expanded form. Then use words to write the number.
Answer:
Expanded Form: \( 10000 + 5000 + 30 + 9 \)
Word Form: Fifteen thousand thirty-nine
Explanation: Break down the place values: 1 ten-thousand, 5 thousands, 0 hundreds, 3 tens, and 9 ones.
6. Find the third multiple of 8. Find the fifth multiple of 4. Subtract the smaller from the larger. What is the result?
Answer: 4
Explanation:
1. Third multiple of 8: \( 8 \times 3 = 24 \)
2. Fifth multiple of 4: \( 4 \times 5 = 20 \)
3. Subtract: \( 24 - 20 = 4 \)
7. If October 1st is a Wednesday, what is the date of the third Wednesday in October?
Answer: October 15th
Explanation: The first Wednesday is the 1st. Add 7 days to get the second Wednesday (the 8th). Add 7 more days to get the third Wednesday (the 15th).
8. If \( x - 7 = 15 \), then what does \( x + 3 \) equal?
Answer: 25
Explanation: First, solve for \( x \) by adding 7 to 15, giving \( x = 22 \). Then substitute \( x \) into the second expression: \( 22 + 3 = 25 \).
9. What is the perimeter and area of this rectangle? Measurements are in centimeters.
Answer:
Perimeter = 26 cm
Area = 40 sq cm
Explanation: The perimeter is the total distance around the outside: \( 8 + 8 + 5 + 5 = 26 \). The area is length times width: \( 8 \times 5 = 40 \).
10. Compare these fractions. Draw and shade two congruent rectangles to show the comparison: \( \frac{1}{3} \) and \( \frac{2}{6} \)
Answer: They are equal (\( \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{6} \)).
Explanation: \( \frac{2}{6} \) simplified is \( \frac{1}{3} \) (divide top and bottom by 2). Both represent the same proportion of a whole.
11. A number line spans from 4 to 5 with 4 equal intervals. To what mixed number is an arrow pointing if it rests on the first mark after the 4?
Answer: \( 4 \frac{1}{4} \)
Explanation: The space between 4 and 5 is divided into 4 sections, so each mark is \( \frac{1}{4} \). The first mark is \( 4 \frac{1}{4} \).
12. How many faces, edges, and vertices does a standard cube have?
Answer: 6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices.
Explanation: A cube has 6 flat square surfaces (faces), 12 straight lines where the faces meet (edges), and 8 corners where the edges meet (vertices).
13. \( 1.25 + 4.50 + 0.75 + 2.10 \)
Answer: 8.60
Explanation: It's easiest to group numbers that make whole numbers. \( 1.25 + 0.75 = 2.00 \). Then \( 4.50 + 2.10 = 6.60 \). Add them together: \( 2.00 + 6.60 = 8.60 \).
14. \( \$5.50 + \$2.99 + \$10.00 \)
Answer: \$18.49
Explanation: Line up the decimals. \( \$5.50 + \$10.00 = \$15.50 \). Then \( \$15.50 + \$2.99 = \$18.49 \).
15. \( 50.00 - (14.25 + 6.50) \)
Answer: 29.25
Explanation: Solve parentheses first: \( 14.25 + 6.50 = 20.75 \). Then subtract from 50: \( 50.00 - 20.75 = 29.25 \).
16. \( \$15.00 - (\$1.50 + \$3.25) \)
Answer: \$10.25
Explanation: Solve parentheses first: \( \$1.50 + \$3.25 = \$4.75 \). Then subtract from 15: \( \$15.00 - \$4.75 = \$10.25 \).
17. \( 52,104 - 28,346 \)
Answer: 23,758
Explanation: Set up vertically. Borrowing is required across multiple places (e.g., borrowing from the 1 hundred to subtract 6 from 4).
18. \( 842 \times 5 \)
Answer: 4,210
Explanation: \( 2 \times 5 = 10 \) (carry 1). \( 4 \times 5 = 20 \), plus 1 is 21 (carry 2). \( 8 \times 5 = 40 \), plus 2 is 42.
19. \( \$4.15 \times 6 \)
Answer: \$24.90
Explanation: \( 5 \times 6 = 30 \) (carry 3). \( 1 \times 6 = 6 \), plus 3 is 9. \( 4 \times 6 = 24 \). Place the decimal two spots in.
20. \( 520 + y = 700 \)
Answer: \( y = 180 \)
Explanation: Subtract 520 from 700 to find the missing addend. \( 700 - 520 = 180 \).
21. \( 47 \div 6 \)
Answer: 7 Remainder 5
Explanation: 6 goes into 47 seven times (\( 6 \times 7 = 42 \)). Subtract 42 from 47 to get a remainder of 5.
22. \( 63 \div 8 \)
Answer: 7 Remainder 7
Explanation: 8 goes into 63 seven times (\( 8 \times 7 = 56 \)). Subtract 56 from 63 to get a remainder of 7.
23. \( 85 \div 9 \)
Answer: 9 Remainder 4
Explanation: 9 goes into 85 nine times (\( 9 \times 9 = 81 \)). Subtract 81 from 85 to get a remainder of 4.
24. Find the product of \( 7 \times 1,000 \) using mental math.
Answer: 7,000
Explanation: Multiply the non-zero digits (\( 7 \times 1 = 7 \)) and attach the three zeros from the 1,000 to the end.
25. List all the factors of 24.
Answer: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24
Explanation: Factors are the numbers you multiply to get 24. The pairs are: \( 1 \times 24 \), \( 2 \times 12 \), \( 3 \times 8 \), and \( 4 \times 6 \).
26. Round 14,892 to the nearest thousand and the nearest hundred.
Answer:
Nearest thousand: 15,000
Nearest hundred: 14,900
Explanation:
- For thousands, look at the hundreds digit (8). Since it's 5 or more, round 4 up to 5.
- For hundreds, look at the tens digit (9). Since it's 5 or more, round 8 up to 9.
27. If \( 3m = 27 \), which of these equations is false?
A) \( m + 1 = 10 \)   B) \( 2m = 18 \)   C) \( m - 2 = 5 \)   D) \( m \times 0 = 9 \)
Answer: C and D
Explanation: First, solve \( 3m = 27 \) to find \( m = 9 \).
A) \( 9 + 1 = 10 \) (True)
B) \( 2(9) = 18 \) (True)
C) \( 9 - 2 = 7 \) (Not 5, so False)
D) \( 9 \times 0 = 0 \) (Not 9, so False).
Both C and D are false statements.
28. Three quarters (coins) are what fraction, decimal, and percent of a dollar?
Answer: Fraction: \( \frac{3}{4} \) (or \( \frac{75}{100} \)), Decimal: 0.75, Percent: 75%
Explanation: Three quarters equal 75 cents out of 100 cents. As a fraction, 3 out of 4 parts of a dollar is \( \frac{3}{4} \).
29. Which of these numbers is prime?
9, 15, 21, 23
Answer: 23
Explanation: A prime number only has 1 and itself as factors. 9 can be divided by 3; 15 can be divided by 3 and 5; 21 can be divided by 3 and 7. Only 23 cannot be divided evenly by anything other than 1 and 23.
30. A school has 412 students. If each student needs exactly 3 pencils for the year, approximately how many pencils should the school order in total?
Answer: Approximately 1,200 pencils (Exact: 1,236).
Explanation: Round 412 to 400 for a quick estimation. \( 400 \times 3 = 1200 \). The exact calculation is \( 412 \times 3 = 1236 \).
