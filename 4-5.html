1. Farmer Jones had 96 apples on his tree. Sarah picked one fourth of them. How many apples did Sarah pick?

Answer: $24$ apples

Explanation: To find one fourth of $96$, divide the total number by $4$.
$$96 \div 4 = 24$$

2. Forty-five percent of the students brought their lunch from home. What percent of the students did NOT bring their lunch from home?

Answer: $55\%$

Explanation: The total amount of students represents $100\%$. Subtract the percentage that brought lunch to find the percentage that did not.
$$100\% - 45\% = 55\%$$

3. Each box contains an average of eight pencils. How many pencils are in 120 boxes?

Answer: $960$ pencils

Explanation: Multiply the number of boxes by the average number of pencils per box.
$$120 \times 8 = 960$$

4. Estimate the sum of 5431 and 6892 by rounding each number to the nearest thousand before adding.

Answer: $12,000$

Explanation:
Step 1: Round $5431$ to the nearest thousand $\rightarrow 5000$
Step 2: Round $6892$ to the nearest thousand $\rightarrow 7000$
Step 3: Add the rounded numbers:
$$5000 + 7000 = 12000$$

5. What is the value of 6 pennies, 5 dimes, 4 quarters, and 2 nickels? Write the answer using a dollar sign and decimal point.

Answer: $\$1.66$

Explanation: Convert each coin type to its monetary value and add them together:
6 pennies = $\$0.06$
5 dimes = $\$0.50$
4 quarters = $\$1.00$
2 nickels = $\$0.10$
$$\$0.06 + \$0.50 + \$1.00 + \$0.10 = \$1.66$$

6. One third of the guests ate chocolate cake. There were 75 guests in all. How many guests ate chocolate cake?

Answer: $25$ guests

Explanation: Find one third of the total amount of guests by dividing $75$ by $3$.
$$75 \div 3 = 25$$

7. What number is indicated on the number line below?

Answer: $-2$

8. Marcus has an octagon and a pentagon. What is the total number of sides on the two polygons?

Answer: $13$ sides

Explanation: An octagon has $8$ sides. A pentagon has $5$ sides.
$$8 + 5 = 13$$

10. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?

Answer: $20$ cm

Explanation: The perimeter is the distance around the outside of the shape. Add all four sides together.
$$6 + 6 + 4 + 4 = 20 \text{ cm}$$
