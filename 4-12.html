Math Worksheet Solutions (Problems 1 - 30)

1. Samantha bought oranges at 8 cents per pound. She spent 104 cents. How many pounds of oranges did Samantha buy?
Answer: 13 pounds

Explanation: Divide the total amount spent by the cost per pound.
104 ÷ 8 = 13
2. Kevin stacked 312 wooden blocks to make a tower. His little brother knocked down 87 of them. How many blocks remained standing?
Answer: 225 blocks

Explanation: Subtract the number of blocks knocked down from the total number of blocks.
312 - 87 = 225
3. David read his book for one third of a 150-minute flight. How many minutes did he spend reading?
Answer: 50 minutes

Explanation: Divide the total minutes by 3 to find one third.
150 ÷ 3 = 50
4. One fifth of the cars in the parking lot are red. What fraction of the cars are not red? What percent of the cars are not red?
Answer: \( \frac{4}{5} \) and 80%

Explanation: The whole group is represented by 1. Subtract the fraction of red cars to find the fraction that are not red.
\( 1 - \frac{1}{5} = \frac{4}{5} \)
To convert \( \frac{4}{5} \) to a percentage, multiply by 100: \( \frac{4}{5} \times 100 = 80\% \)
5. If one side of a regular pentagon is 4 centimeters long, then what is its perimeter in (a) centimeters? (b) millimeters?
Answer: (a) 20 cm (b) 200 mm

Explanation: A regular pentagon has 5 equal sides.
(a) Perimeter in cm = 4 × 5 = 20 cm
(b) Since 1 cm = 10 mm, multiply by 10: 20 × 10 = 200 mm
6. Which of these arrows could be pointing to 4250? (Arrows A, B, C are spaced on a number line between 4000 and 5000)
Answer: A

Explanation: The halfway mark between 4000 and 5000 is 4500. 4250 is exactly halfway between 4000 and 4500. Arrow A, positioned at the 1/4 mark of the segment, perfectly corresponds to 4250.
7. There are 2 pints in a quart, and 2 quarts in a half-gallon. A pint is what fraction of a half-gallon?
Answer: \( \frac{1}{4} \)

Explanation:
1 half-gallon = 2 quarts
1 quart = 2 pints
Therefore, 1 half-gallon = 2 × 2 = 4 pints. This means 1 pint is \( \frac{1}{4} \) of a half-gallon.
8. A bag of flour weighs 5 kilograms. How many grams is that?
Answer: 5000 grams

Explanation: There are 1000 grams in 1 kilogram.
5 × 1000 = 5000
9. Estimate the product of 593 and 8.
Answer: 4800

Explanation: Round 593 up to the nearest hundred, which is 600. Multiply the rounded number by 8.
600 × 8 = 4800
10. It is late at night. What time was it 12 hours ago if the clock indicates 11:30?
Answer: 11:30 a.m.

Explanation: Because it is late at night, the current time is 11:30 p.m. Exactly 12 hours before any p.m. time is the exact same time in a.m.
11. Compare: \( \frac{2}{5} \bigcirc \frac{1}{3} \). Draw and shade two rectangles to show the comparison.
Answer: \( \frac{2}{5} > \frac{1}{3} \)

Explanation: To compare mathematically, find a common denominator (15).
\( \frac{2}{5} = \frac{6}{15} \)
\( \frac{1}{3} = \frac{5}{15} \)
Since 6 > 5, \( \frac{2}{5} > \frac{1}{3} \).

27. 6 + 9 + 4 + 3 + N + 8 + 2 = 45
Answer: N = 13

Explanation: Add all the known numbers together first.
6 + 9 + 4 + 3 + 8 + 2 = 32
Substitute back into equation: 32 + N = 45.
Subtract 32 from both sides: N = 45 - 32 = 13.
28. Find the perimeter and area of this rectangle: (Grid shows a rectangle 9 units wide and 6 units high).
Answer: Perimeter = 30 units, Area = 54 square units

Explanation:
Perimeter is the total length of the outside border: P = 2 × (length + width) = 2 × (9 + 6) = 2 × 15 = 30 units.
Area is length times width: A = 9 × 6 = 54 square units.
29. Name the transformation that would move a triangle facing North to be identically shaped but facing East.
Answer: Rotation

Explanation: A transformation that turns a figure around a fixed point, changing its direction (like from North to East) without altering its size or shape, is called a Rotation (specifically, a 90-degree clockwise rotation).
30. The first four multiples of 12 are 12, 24, 36, 48. What are the first four multiples of 120?
Answer: 120, 240, 360, 480

Explanation: Since 120 is 10 times 12, its multiples will be 10 times the multiples of 12.
12 × 10 = 120
24 × 10 = 240
36 × 10 = 360
48 × 10 = 480
