🌟 Word of the Day

shimmer
SHIM-er · verb

What it means: to shine with a soft, wavering light — like sunlight dancing on water.

Example: “The lake shimmered in the morning sun.”

✏️ Try it today: Use shimmer in a sentence of your own. What shimmers where you live — the sun on water, a shiny rock, a sequin on a jacket?
