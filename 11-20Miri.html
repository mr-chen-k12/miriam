1

Marybeth could jump 42 times each minute. At that rate, how many times could she jump in 8 minutes? Write an equation to solve the problem.

She jumps 42 times in each of 8 minutes, so we multiply.

$$42 \times 8 = n$$ $$n = 336$$

Answer: $42 \times 8 = 336$ — she could jump 336 times.

Write four multiplication/division facts using 8, 9, and 72.

$$8 \times 9 = 72 \qquad 9 \times 8 = 72$$ $$72 \div 8 = 9 \qquad 72 \div 9 = 8$$

Answer: the four facts above.

4

What is the sum of $\sqrt{36}$ and $\sqrt{64}$?

$\sqrt{36} = 6$ because $6 \times 6 = 36$, and $\sqrt{64} = 8$ because $8 \times 8 = 64$.

$$6 + 8 = 14$$

Answer: 14

5

Compare: $\dfrac{1}{3} \;\bigcirc\; 50\%$

$50\%$ is the same as $\dfrac{1}{2}$. One third is a smaller piece than one half.

Answer: $\dfrac{1}{3} < 50\%$

6

a. Round 5280 to the nearest thousand.
b. Round 5280 to the nearest hundred.

a. 5280 is between 5000 and 6000. The hundreds digit is 2 (less than 5), so round down.

b. 5280 is between 5200 and 5300. The tens digit is 8 (5 or more), so round up.

Answer: a. 5000   b. 5300

7

This array of 12 stars shows that 4 and 3 are factors of 12. Draw a different array of 12 stars that shows two other factors of 12.

Make 2 rows of 6 stars: $2 \times 6 = 12$.

Answer: a 2-by-6 array shows that 2 and 6 are factors of 12. (A 1-by-12 array, showing 1 and 12, also works.)

8

Find the fourth multiple of 6. Then find the third multiple of 8. Compare these two multiples.

Multiples of 6: 6, 12, 18, 24 → fourth is $4 \times 6 = 24$.

Multiples of 8: 8, 16, 24 → third is $3 \times 8 = 24$.

Answer: $24 = 24$ — the two multiples are equal.

Juan Ponce de León explored the coast of Florida in 1513. In 1800, the federal government of the United States moved to Washington, DC. Write a later − earlier = difference equation and solve it to find the number of years that elapsed from 1513 to 1800.

$$1800 - 1513 = d$$ $$d = 287$$

Check: $1513 + 287 = 1800$ ✓

Answer: 287 years

A square has one side that is 7 inches long.

a. All 4 sides are 7 in.: $\;4 \times 7 = 28$

28

a. Three pennies are what fraction of a dollar?
b. Write the value of three pennies as a decimal part of a dollar.
c. Three pennies are what percent of a dollar?

A dollar is 100 pennies, so 3 pennies is 3 out of 100.

Answer: a. $\dfrac{3}{100}$   b. \$0.03   c. $3\%$

29

Which of these numbers is a prime number?   A 6   B 7   C 8   D 9

A prime number has exactly two factors: 1 and itself.

$6 = 2 \times 3$, $\;8 = 2 \times 4$, $\;9 = 3 \times 3$ — not prime. $\;7$ has only factors 1 and 7.

Answer: B 7

30

What is the sum of these lengths? Write three answers using different units. $\;1\text{ yard} + 2\text{ feet} + 12\text{ inches}$

Change everything to feet: 1 yard = 3 feet, and 12 inches = 1 foot.

$$3\text{ ft} + 2\text{ ft} + 1\text{ ft} = 6\text{ ft}$$ $$6\text{ ft} = 2\text{ yd} \qquad 6 \times 12 = 72\text{ in}$$

Answer: 6 feet, 2 yards, 72 inches

Real-World

Each day Jamaal delivers 30 newspapers in 1 hr 30 min. At this rate, how many newspapers would he deliver each hour? Explain your answer.

1 hr 30 min is three 30-minute periods, so he delivers $30 \div 3 = 10$ newspapers every 30 minutes.

Time30 min1 hr1 hr 30 min
Papers102030

One hour is two 30-minute periods: $10 + 10 = 20$.

Answer: 20 newspapers each hour
