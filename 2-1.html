Problem 1
Four hundred fifteen tickets were sold for the morning show. Altogether seven hundred twenty-five tickets were sold for both shows combined. How many tickets were sold for the second show?

Subtract the morning show tickets from the total to find the difference.

$$ 725 - 415 $$

Calculation: $5-5=0$, $2-1=1$, $7-4=3$.

Answer: 310 tickets
Problem 2
Maria paid $10.00 for a book that cost $8.45. How much change should she get back?

Subtract the cost from the amount paid.

$$ \$10.00 - \$8.45 $$

Regrouping: $1000 - 845 = 155$.

Answer: $1.55

Problem 6
It is afternoon. What time was it 3 hours and 30 minutes ago according to this clock?

The clock shows 4:15 PM.

  1. Subtract 3 hours: $4:15 \rightarrow 1:15$ PM.
  2. Subtract 30 minutes: $1:15 - 15 \text{ mins} = 1:00$. Then back 15 more minutes = $12:45$.
Answer: 12:45 PM

Problem 20
Calculate: \( \sqrt{64} + \sqrt{9} \)

$\sqrt{64} = 8$ (since $8 \times 8 = 64$)
$\sqrt{9} = 3$ (since $3 \times 3 = 9$)

$$ 8 + 3 = 11 $$
Answer: 11
