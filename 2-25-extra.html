Similar to Question 13 (Multi-digit Subtraction)

13a. $$ \begin{array}{r} 53,104 \\ - \quad 28,476 \\ \hline \end{array} $$
Answer: 24,628

Explanation: Subtract column by column from right to left. You will need to regroup (borrow) multiple times.
$$ \begin{array}{r} \small{4}\small{12}\small{10}\small{9}\small{14} \\ \cancel{5}\cancel{3},\cancel{1}\cancel{0}\cancel{4} \\ - \quad 28,476 \\ \hline 24,628 \end{array} $$

13b. $$ \begin{array}{r} 81,052 \\ - \quad 34,918 \\ \hline \end{array} $$
Answer: 46,134

Explanation: Subtract from right to left, borrowing when the top digit is smaller than the bottom digit.
$$ \begin{array}{r} 81,052 \\ - \quad 34,918 \\ \hline 46,134 \end{array} $$

Similar to Question 14 (2-digit Multiplication)

14a. $$ \begin{array}{r} 68 \\ \times \quad 8 \\ \hline \end{array} $$
Answer: 544

Explanation: Multiply the ones column first: $8 \times 8 = 64$. Write down the 4 and carry the 6. Then multiply the tens column: $6 \times 8 = 48$. Add the carried 6: $48 + 6 = 54$.
$$ \begin{array}{r} \small{6} \\ 68 \\ \times \quad 8 \\ \hline 544 \end{array} $$

14b. $$ \begin{array}{r} 43 \\ \times \quad 9 \\ \hline \end{array} $$
Answer: 387

Explanation: Multiply the ones: $3 \times 9 = 27$ (write 7, carry 2). Multiply the tens: $4 \times 9 = 36$. Add the carried 2: $36 + 2 = 38$.
$$ \begin{array}{r} \small{2} \\ 43 \\ \times \quad 9 \\ \hline 387 \end{array} $$

14c. $$ \begin{array}{r} 95 \\ \times \quad 6 \\ \hline \end{array} $$
Answer: 570

Explanation: Multiply the ones: $5 \times 6 = 30$ (write 0, carry 3). Multiply the tens: $9 \times 6 = 54$. Add the carried 3: $54 + 3 = 57$.
$$ \begin{array}{r} \small{3} \\ 95 \\ \times \quad 6 \\ \hline 570 \end{array} $$

Similar to Question 17 (Division with Remainders)

17a. $53 \div 8$
Answer: 6 R 5 (or 6 $\frac{5}{8}$)

Explanation: Divide 53 by 8.
$8 \times 6 = 48$.
$53 - 48 = 5$, so there is a remainder of 5.
Written as a mixed number, it is $6 \frac{5}{8}$.
