Lesson 73: Geometric Transformations & Math Practice

Use this information to answer problems 1–3:
Thirty students are going on a field trip. Each car can hold five students. The field trip will cost each student $5.

1.
How many cars are needed for the field trip?

Answer: 6 cars

Explanation: Divide the total number of students by the number of students each car can hold:
\( 30 \div 5 = 6 \).

2.
Altogether, how much money will be needed?

Answer: $150

Explanation: Multiply the total number of students by the cost per student:
\( 30 \times \$5 = \$150 \).

3.
Don has saved $3.25. How much more does he need to go on the field trip?

Answer: $1.75

Explanation: Subtract the amount Don has already saved from the total cost of the field trip:
\( \$5.00 - \$3.25 = \$1.75 \).

4.
During the summer the swim team practiced \( 3\frac{1}{2} \) hours a day. If practice started at 6:30 a.m., at what time did it end if there were no breaks?

Answer: 10:00 a.m.

Explanation: \( 3\frac{1}{2} \) hours is equal to 3 hours and 30 minutes. Add 3 hours to 6:30 a.m. to get 9:30 a.m. Add the remaining 30 minutes to reach 10:00 a.m.

5.
Half of the 48 pencils were sharpened. How many were not sharpened? What percent of the pencils were not sharpened? Draw a picture to help you solve the problem.

Answer: 24 pencils, 50%

Explanation: Half of 48 is \( 48 \div 2 = 24 \). Because exactly half are sharpened, the other half (50%) are not.

6.
What number is \( \frac{1}{4} \) of 60? Draw a picture to help you solve the problem.

Answer: 15

Explanation: To find \( \frac{1}{4} \) of 60, divide 60 by 4:
\( 60 \div 4 = 15 \).

7.
One gallon of water will be poured into 1-quart bottles. How many 1-quart bottles will be filled?

Answer: 4 bottles

Explanation: There are 4 quarts in 1 gallon. Therefore, the water will perfectly fill 4 one-quart bottles.

8.
Each side of a regular polygon has the same length. A regular hexagon is shown below. How many millimeters is the perimeter of this hexagon? (Based on the image's centimeter ruler).

Answer: 60 mm

Explanation: The ruler in the image measures one side of the hexagon from the 1 cm mark to the 2 cm mark, which means the side length is 1 cm.
1 cm = 10 mm.
A regular hexagon has 6 equal sides.
Perimeter = \( 6 \times 10 \text{ mm} = 60 \text{ mm} \).

9.
A mile is five thousand, two hundred eighty feet. The Golden Gate Bridge is four thousand, two hundred feet long. The Golden Gate Bridge is how many feet less than 1 mile long?

Answer: 1,080 feet

Explanation: Subtract the length of the bridge from the length of a mile:
\( 5,280 - 4,200 = 1,080 \).

10.
Which of these numbers is not a multiple of 90?
A. 45    B. 180    C. 270    D. 360

Answer: A. 45

Explanation: A multiple of 90 is the product of 90 and an integer (e.g., \(90 \times 1 = 90\), \(90 \times 2 = 180\), etc.). 45 is a factor of 90, not a multiple.

11.
What number is halfway between 300 and 400?

Answer: 350

Explanation: The difference between 400 and 300 is 100. Half of 100 is 50. Add 50 to 300 to find the halfway point: 350. Alternatively, take the average: \( \frac{300 + 400}{2} = 350 \).

12.
\( 37.56 - 4.2 \)

Answer: 33.36

Explanation: Line up the decimals and subtract:
\( 37.56 - 4.20 = 33.36 \).

13.
\( 4.2 + 3.5 + 0.25 + 4.0 \)

Answer: 11.95

Explanation: Align the decimal points and add:
\( 4.20 + 3.50 + 0.25 + 4.00 = 11.95 \).

14.
\( \$100.00 - \$31.53 \)

Answer: $68.47

Explanation: Subtract using standard regrouping algorithms:
\( 100.00 - 31.53 = 68.47 \).

15.
\( 251,546 - 37,156 \)

Answer: 214,390

Explanation: Subtract matching place values carefully:
\( 251,546 - 37,156 = 214,390 \).

16.
\( N + 423 = 618 \)

Answer: N = 195

Explanation: Use subtraction to isolate \( N \):
\( N = 618 - 423 = 195 \).

17.
\( \$3.46 \times 7 \)

Answer: $24.22

Explanation: Multiply normally, then place the decimal two spots from the right:
\( 3.46 \times 7 = 24.22 \).

18.
\( 96 \times 30 \)

Answer: 2,880

Explanation: Multiply \( 96 \times 3 = 288 \), then attach a zero at the end:
\( 288 \times 10 = 2880 \).

19.
\( \$0.59 \times 8 \)

Answer: $4.72

Explanation: \( 59 \times 8 = 472 \). Add the decimal point two spaces in: \( \$4.72 \).

20.
\( 633 \div 7 \)

Answer: 90 R 3

Explanation: 7 goes into 63 exactly 9 times. Drop the 3. 7 goes into 3 zero times.
Quotient is 90, remainder is 3.

21.
\( 98 \div 5 \)

Answer: 19 R 3

Explanation: \( 5 \times 19 = 95 \).
\( 98 - 95 = 3 \). Thus, 19 with a remainder of 3.

22.
\( 150 \div 3 \)

Answer: 50

Explanation: 3 goes into 15 exactly 5 times. Attach the 0.
\( 3 \times 50 = 150 \).

23.
\( 329 \div 6 \)

Answer: 54 R 5

Explanation: \( 6 \times 50 = 300 \), leaving 29.
\( 6 \times 4 = 24 \).
\( 29 - 24 = 5 \). Result: 54 Remainder 5.

24.
\( 274 \div 4 \)

Answer: 68 R 2

Explanation: 4 goes into 27 six times (\(4 \times 6 = 24\)), remainder 3. Bring down the 4 to make 34.
4 goes into 34 eight times (\(4 \times 8 = 32\)), remainder 2. Result: 68 R 2.

25.
\( 247 \div 8 \)

Answer: 30 R 7

Explanation: \( 8 \times 30 = 240 \).
\( 247 - 240 = 7 \). Result: 30 Remainder 7.

26.
\( \sqrt{25} \times M = 135 \)

Answer: M = 27

Explanation: The square root of 25 is 5. So the equation becomes:
\( 5 \times M = 135 \)
Divide both sides by 5:
\( M = 135 \div 5 = 27 \).

27.
\( Z - 476 = 325 \)

Answer: Z = 801

Explanation: Use addition to solve for \( Z \):
\( Z = 325 + 476 = 801 \).

28.
\( 6A = 12 + 6 \)

Answer: A = 3

Explanation: First, simplify the right side of the equation:
\( 6A = 18 \)
Divide both sides by 6:
\( A = 18 \div 6 = 3 \).

29.
Segment \( AB \) is 2.3 cm long. Segment \( BC \) is 3.5 cm long. How long is segment \( AC \)? Write a decimal addition problem and find the answer.

Answer: 5.8 cm

Explanation: The total length of segment \( AC \) is the sum of segments \( AB \) and \( BC \):
\( 2.3 \text{ cm} + 3.5 \text{ cm} = 5.8 \text{ cm} \).

30.
Which transformation would position \( \Delta ABC \) on \( \Delta ABD \)?

Answer: Reflection (or Flip)

Explanation: The two triangles share the side \( AB \) as an axis of symmetry. To map triangle \( ABC \) exactly onto triangle \( ABD \), you perform a reflection across the line segment \( AB \).
