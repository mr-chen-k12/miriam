Written Practice

1. [Formulate] There are two hundred fifteen pages in the book. Kande has read eighty-six pages. How many more pages are left to read? Write and solve an equation.
Answer:

Equation: $215 - 86 = p$ (or $86 + p = 215$)

Solution: $p = 129\text{ pages}$

Explanation: Subtract the number of pages Kande has read ($86$) from the total number of pages ($215$): $215 - 86 = 129$.

2. Use the digits 7, 8, and 9 to make an even number greater than 800. Use each digit only once.
Answer:

$978$

Explanation: To make the number even, the last digit must be $8$ (the only even digit available). To make the number greater than $800$ using the remaining digits ($7$ and $9$), the hundreds digit must be $9$. Thus, the number is $978$.

3. [Compare] Use digits and a comparison symbol to show that four hundred eighty-five is less than six hundred ninety.
Answer:

$485 < 690$

Explanation: "Four hundred eighty-five" is written as $485$, "six hundred ninety" is written as $690$, and the symbol for "less than" is $<$.

4. [Conclude] This is a sequence of square numbers. What are the next three numbers in the sequence? How do you know?
$$1, 4, 9, 16, \underline{\quad}, \underline{\quad}, \underline{\quad}, \dots$$
Answer:

$25, 36, 49$

Explanation: Square numbers are obtained by multiplying a counting number by itself ($1^2=1, 2^2=4, 3^2=9, 4^2=16$). The next three numbers are $5^2 = 25$, $6^2 = 36$, and $7^2 = 49$.

5. One evening Jermaine finished washing the dishes at the time shown on the clock. What time did Jermaine finish washing the dishes?
Answer:

$8:05\text{ p.m.}$

Explanation: The hour hand points slightly past $8$, and the minute hand points directly at the $1$ mark, representing $5$ minutes past the hour. Since it is in the evening, it is $\text{p.m.}$

6. [Represent] Write 729 in expanded form and use words to write the number.

7. [Connect] Change this addition problem to a multiplication problem. Then find the product on the multiplication table.
$$6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 6$$
Answer:

Multiplication Problem: $7 \times 6$ (or $6 \times 7$)

Product: $42$

Explanation: There are $7$ groups of $6$, which equals $7 \times 6 = 42$.

8. Is the value of three nickels and two dimes an even number of cents or an odd number of cents?
Answer:

An odd number of cents

Explanation: $3 \text{ nickels} = 3 \times 5\phi = 15\phi$. $2 \text{ dimes} = 2 \times 10\phi = 20\phi$. Total $= 15\phi + 20\phi = 35\phi$. Since $35$ ends in $5$, it is an odd number.

10.
a. Use a metric ruler to measure the length of each side of this square in centimeters.
b. What is the perimeter of the square?
Answer:

a. Length of side: $2\text{ cm}$

b. Perimeter: $8\text{ cm}$

Explanation: Each side measures $2\text{ cm}$. The perimeter of a square is $4 \times \text{side length} = 4 \times 2\text{ cm} = 8\text{ cm}$.

11. [Analyze] Which two uppercase letters are formed with only two perpendicular line segments?
Answer:

L and T

Explanation: Both capital letters L and T consist of exactly two line segments meeting at right angles ($90^\circ$).

12. If $62 - w = 48$, then what is the value of $w$?
Answer:

$w = 14$

Explanation: Subtract $48$ from $62$: $w = 62 - 48 = 14$.

13. What fraction of this rectangle is shaded?
Answer:

$\frac{5}{10}$ (or $\frac{1}{2}$)

Explanation: The rectangle is divided into $10$ equal vertical sections, and $5$ of them are shaded. $\frac{5}{10}$ simplifies to $\frac{1}{2}$.

15. [Represent] The number represented by point B is how much greater than the number represented by point A?
Answer:

$75$

Explanation: The scale from $0$ to $100$ is divided into $4$ equal intervals of $25$. Point A is at $50$, and Point B is at $125$. The difference is $125 - 50 = 75$.

$64$

26. [Multiple Choice] Jacob saw an array of freshly baked rolls on a pan. There were four rows of rolls with four rolls in each row. How many rolls will be left on the pan if he eats one roll?
A) 3     B) 7     C) 12     D) 15
Answer:

D) 15

Explanation: Total rolls $= 4 \times 4 = 16$. Subtract $1$ roll eaten: $16 - 1 = 15$.

27. Which property of multiplication does this story illustrate?
"Twenty-four desks were arranged in 4 rows with 6 desks in each row. Then they were moved into 6 rows with 4 desks in each row."
Answer:

Commutative Property of Multiplication

Explanation: The story shows that $4 \times 6 = 6 \times 4 = 24$. Changing the order of factors does not change the product.

28. [Formulate] In 2000, a professional baseball pitcher struck out 347 batters and another professional pitcher struck out 284 batters. Write and solve an equation to find the total number of batters the two pitchers struck out.
Answer:

Equation: $347 + 284 = t$

Solution: $t = 631\text{ batters}$

Explanation: Add the two strikeout totals together: $347 + 284 = 631$.

29. [Estimate] The average depth of the East China Sea is 620 feet. The average depth of the Yellow Sea is 121 feet. Estimate the difference between the two average depths. Explain why your estimate is reasonable.
Answer:

Estimate: $\approx 500\text{ feet}$

Explanation: Round $620\text{ ft}$ to $600\text{ ft}$ and $121\text{ ft}$ to $100\text{ ft}$. The estimated difference is $600 - 100 = 500\text{ feet}$. (Alternatively, rounding to the nearest ten gives $620 - 120 = 500\text{ feet}$).

30. [Predict] Write the sixth term of each pattern:
a. $11, 22, 33, 44, 55, \dots$
b. $12, 24, 36, 48, 60, \dots$
Answer:

a. $66$ ($11 \times 6 = 66$)

b. $72$ ($12 \times 6 = 72$)

Explanation: Pattern (a) increases by $11$ each time (multiples of 11). Pattern (b) increases by $12$ each time (multiples of 12).
