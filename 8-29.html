Question 1

Eight feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply the number of feet by 12:

8 feet × 12 inches = 96 inches

Question 2

Maya handed the cashier a $50 bill to buy a backpack that cost $31.65. How much change did she receive back?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the cost of the backpack from $50.00:

$50.00 − $31.65 = $18.35

Question 3

Olivia is 7 years younger than Ethan. Ethan is six times as old as Noah. If Ethan is 30 years old, how old are Olivia and Noah?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Olivia: 30 − 7 = 23 years old.

2. Noah: 30 ÷ 6 = 5 years old.

Olivia is 23 and Noah is 5.

(c) 3 out of 8 students chose apples.

Teacher Answer Key

Question 5

One-fourth of the 36 students in Ms. Lee's class brought lunch from home. How many students brought lunch?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Divide the total by 4:

36 ÷ 4 = 9 students

Question 6

Looking back at Question 5, how many students did not bring lunch from home?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the 9 students from the total:

36 − 9 = 27 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 93 and 46 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then find the exact difference.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Estimate: 93 → 90 and 46 → 50, so 90 − 50 = 40.

2. Exact: 93 − 46 = 47.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:
