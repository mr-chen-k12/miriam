Problem 1
Mark is 4 years older than Lily. Lily is three times as old as Sam. Sam is 4 years old. How old is Mark?
Answer: 16 years old
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • First, determine Sam's age, which is given as 4.
  • Lily is three times as old as Sam: $$4 \times 3 = 12 \text{ (Lily's age)}$$
  • Mark is 4 years older than Lily: $$12 + 4 = 16 \text{ (Mark's age)}$$

Problem 4
Fifty-two books were placed as equally as possible on 6 shelves.
(a) How many shelves had exactly 8 books?
(b) How many shelves had 9 books?
Answer: (a) 2 shelves  |  (b) 4 shelves
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Divide the total number of books by the number of shelves to find the base number: $$52 \div 6 = 8 \text{ with a remainder of } 4$$
  • The remainder means 4 extra books are distributed one by one to the shelves. So, 4 shelves will have an extra book (making them shelves of 9).
  • The remaining shelves keep the base number of 8 books: $$6 \text{ total shelves} - 4 \text{ shelves of nine} = 2 \text{ shelves of eight}$$
