Math Practice Solutions (1-30)

Use the following information for problems 1-3: Imagine a line graph titled "Books Read by Sarah". Month 1 is at 3 books, Month 2 is at 4 books, Month 3 is at 5 books, Month 4 is at 2 books, and Month 5 is at 6 books.
1. How many books did Sarah read in the first month?
Answer: 3 books
Looking at the graph context, the data point corresponding to Month 1 aligns with 3 on the vertical axis (Number of Books).
2. If every book Sarah read in Month 5 was exactly 50 pages long, how many total pages did she read that month?
Answer: 300 pages
According to the graph data, Sarah read 6 books in Month 5. Multiplying the number of books by the pages per book gives $6 \times 50 = 300$ pages.
3. How many more books did Sarah read in Month 5 than in Month 2?
Answer: 2 books
In Month 5, she read 6 books. In Month 2, she read 4 books. The difference is $6 - 4 = 2$ books.
4. A 4-pound bag of apples costs $2.60. What is the cost per pound?
Answer: $0.65 or 65¢
Divide the total cost by the number of pounds: $\$2.60 \div 4 = \$0.65$.
5. A skateboarder spun halfway around in the air. The skateboarder turned about how many degrees?
Answer: 180°
A full circle or a complete spin is 360 degrees. Half of a spin is $360 \div 2 = 180$ degrees.
6. Mark bought a shirt priced at $24.50. The sales tax was $1.50. Mark gave the cashier a $50.00 bill. How much money should he get back?
Answer: $24.00
First, find the total cost by adding the price and the tax: $\$24.50 + \$1.50 = \$26.00$. Then subtract the total cost from the amount Mark paid: $\$50.00 - \$26.00 = \$24.00$.
7. If the perimeter of an equilateral triangle is 1 yard, how many inches long is each side?
Answer: 12 inches
First, convert the perimeter into inches. 1 yard = 36 inches. Since an equilateral triangle has 3 sides of equal length, divide the total perimeter by 3: $36 \div 3 = 12$ inches per side.
8. A standard paperclip weighs about 1 gram. Use this information to estimate the number of paperclips it would take to weigh 2 kilograms.
Answer: 2000 paperclips
The prefix "kilo-" means 1,000. Therefore, 2 kilograms is equal to 2,000 grams. If one paperclip weighs 1 gram, it takes 2,000 paperclips to reach 2,000 grams.
9. One fifth of the 50 students in the band wear glasses. How many students in the band wear glasses?
Answer: 10 students
To find one fifth of a number, divide the number by 5. $50 \div 5 = 10$.
10. What percent of the students in problem 9 do NOT wear glasses?
Answer: 80%
If $\frac{1}{5}$ of the students *do* wear glasses, then the remaining $\frac{4}{5}$ do *not*. As a percentage, $\frac{4}{5}$ translates to $80\%$.
11. A movie is 2 and a half hours long. If the movie starts at 7:15 PM, what time will it end?
Answer: 9:45 PM
Two hours after 7:15 PM is 9:15 PM. Adding the remaining half hour (30 minutes) brings the time to 9:45 PM.
12. A teacher has 35 boxes of markers. Each box contains 12 markers. How many total markers does the teacher have?
Answer: 420 markers
Multiply the number of boxes by the number of markers in each box: $35 \times 12 = 420$.
13. An auto shop has 120 tires. This is enough to put a full set of new tires on how many 4-wheel cars?
Answer: 30 cars
Since each car requires 4 tires, divide the total number of tires by 4: $120 \div 4 = 30$.
14. Triangle ABC is identical to Triangle DEF, but Triangle DEF is positioned 5 inches straight to the right. Which transformation moves $\triangle ABC$ to $\triangle DEF$?
Answer: Translation (or Slide)
A movement that shifts a shape straight in a given direction without rotating or flipping it is called a translation.
15. Which of these words best describes a triangle that has three sides that are all 5 cm long? A. scalene B. obtuse C. equilateral D. right
Answer: C. equilateral
A triangle with all three sides of equal length is classified as equilateral.
16. $0.850 - (0.3 + 0.25)$
Answer: 0.300 (or 0.3)
By order of operations, evaluate the parentheses first: $0.30 + 0.25 = 0.55$. Then perform the subtraction: $0.850 - 0.550 = 0.300$.
17. $89 \times 1000$
Answer: 89000
When multiplying a whole number by 1000, you simply append three zeros to the end of the number.
18. $417 \times 5$
Answer: 2085
$5 \times 7 = 35$. Write 5, carry 3.
$5 \times 1 = 5$. $5 + 3 = 8$. Write 8.
$5 \times 4 = 20$. Result is 2085.
19. $54 \times 26$
Answer: 1404
$54 \times 6 = 324$.
$54 \times 20 = 1080$.
Adding the partial products: $324 + 1080 = 1404$.
20. $38 \times 45$
Answer: 1710
$38 \times 5 = 190$.
$38 \times 40 = 1520$.
Adding the partial products: $190 + 1520 = 1710$.
21. $7 \overline{) 5218}$
Answer: 745 R 3
7 goes into 52 seven times (49), remainder 3. Bring down 1 (31). 7 goes into 31 four times (28), remainder 3. Bring down 8 (38). 7 goes into 38 five times (35), remainder 3. The quotient is 745 with a remainder of 3.
22. $4 \overline{) \$21.20}$
Answer: $5.30
4 goes into 21 five times (20), remainder 1. Bring down the 2 (12). 4 goes into 12 three times (12), remainder 0. Bring down the 0. 4 goes into 0 zero times. Ensure you keep the decimal directly above its original place.
23. $8 \overline{) 4865}$
Answer: 608 R 1
8 goes into 48 six times (48), remainder 0. Bring down 6. 8 goes into 6 zero times. You must record this zero! Bring down 5 (65). 8 goes into 65 eight times (64), remainder 1.
24. $535 \div 5$
Answer: 107
5 goes into 5 one time, remainder 0. Bring down 3. 5 goes into 3 zero times, remainder 3. Bring down 5 (35). 5 goes into 35 seven times (35), remainder 0.
25. $3618 \div 6$
Answer: 603
6 goes into 36 six times (36), remainder 0. Bring down 1. 6 goes into 1 zero times, remainder 1. Bring down 8 (18). 6 goes into 18 three times (18), remainder 0.
26. $80 \times 400$
Answer: 32000
Multiply the non-zero digits first: $8 \times 4 = 32$. Then, count the total number of zeros in the problem (one in 80, two in 400 = three zeros) and append them to 32.
27. Draw and shade circles to show that $3 \frac{1}{4}$ equals $\frac{13}{4}$.
Answer: Draw four total circles. Shade three entirely, and one a quarter of the way.
If you draw 4 circles and cut each into four equal pieces (quarters), you have 16 quarters total. To show $3 \frac{1}{4}$, you fully shade 3 circles (which covers 12 quarters), and shade 1 quarter of the final circle. $12 + 1 = 13$ quarters, proving it equals $\frac{13}{4}$.
28. $15 + 22 + 8 + 10 + N = 75$
Answer: N = 20
First, add all the known numbers on the left side: $15 + 22 = 37$; $37 + 8 = 45$; $45 + 10 = 55$.
Now the equation is $55 + N = 75$. Subtract 55 from 75 to solve for N: $75 - 55 = 20$.
29. Segment AC is 5.2 cm long. If segment AB is 2.1 cm long, how many centimeters long is segment BC?
Answer: 3.1 cm
Segment AC is made up of segment AB and segment BC combined. To find the missing segment BC, subtract AB from the total length: $5.2\text{ cm} - 2.1\text{ cm} = 3.1\text{ cm}$.
30. Write a decimal addition problem that is illustrated by the lengths of segments AB, BC, and AC in problem 29.
Answer: 2.1 + 3.1 = 5.2
The length of segment AB plus the length of segment BC equals the total length AC. Putting in the decimal values: $2.1\text{ cm} + 3.1\text{ cm} = 5.2\text{ cm}$.
