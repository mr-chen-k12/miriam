Cumulative Test 17B Solutions

1. It takes Jennifer 20 minutes to walk to school. At what time should Jennifer start walking to school if she wants to arrive at 8:05 a.m.?
Answer: 7:45 a.m.
Explanation: You need to count backwards 20 minutes from 8:05 a.m. Subtracting 5 minutes takes you to 8:00 a.m. Subtracting the remaining 15 minutes takes you to 7:45 a.m.
2. Jennifer can walk one mile in 15 minutes. At that rate how far can she walk in an hour?
Answer: 4 miles
Explanation: There are 60 minutes in one hour. To find out how many 15-minute segments fit into an hour, divide 60 by 15. $60 \div 15 = 4$. Therefore, she can walk 4 miles.
3. Vinh bought 4 hamburgers for $1.89 each. The sales tax was 53¢.
a. What was the total price?
b. Vinh paid with a $10 bill. How much change should he have gotten back?
Answer: a. $8.09   b. $1.91
Explanation:
Part a: Multiply the cost of one hamburger by 4: $\$1.89 \times 4 = \$7.56$. Then add the sales tax: $\$7.56 + \$0.53 = \$8.09$. Note: The handwritten answer in the image ($7.91) is incorrect.
Part b: Subtract the total cost from the $\$10$ bill: $\$10.00 - \$8.09 = \$1.91$.
4. Use the information given in the bar graph to answer problem 4. How many students have dogs or cats as pets?
Graph Data:
Dogs: 9
Cats: 8
Other: 7
Answer: 17 students
Explanation: Look at the bar graph to find the values for Dogs and Cats. The bar for Dogs aligns with 9, and the bar for Cats aligns with 8. Add them together: $9 + 8 = 17$.
5. Tom bought a rectangular piece of land that was 3 miles long and 2 miles wide. Half of the land could be farmed. How many square miles could not be farmed?
Answer: 3 square miles
Explanation: First, calculate the total area of the rectangle by multiplying length by width: $3 \times 2 = 6$ square miles. If exactly half of the land *could* be farmed, then the other half *could not* be farmed. Half of 6 is 3 ($6 \div 2 = 3$).
6. Which angle appears to be a 45° angle?
A. (Acute angle)
B. (Right angle)
C. (Obtuse angle)
D. (Straight line)
Answer: A
Explanation: A right angle (Choice B) is 90°. A 45° angle is exactly half of a 90° angle. Choice A shows an acute angle that appears to be half of a right angle.
7. Round five thousand, five hundred fifty-five to the nearest thousand.
Answer: 6,000
Explanation: The number is 5,555. To round to the nearest thousand, look at the hundreds digit. The hundreds digit is 5. Because the rule for rounding is "5 or more, round up", the 5 in the thousands place becomes a 6, resulting in 6,000.
8. The airplane weighs twenty tons. Twenty tons is how many pounds?
Answer: 40,000 lbs
Explanation: There are 2,000 pounds in 1 ton. To convert 20 tons to pounds, multiply by 2,000: $20 \times 2000 = 40,000$.
9. One third of the 18 flowers are roses. How many flowers are roses?
Answer: 6 roses
Explanation: To find one third ($\frac{1}{3}$) of a number, divide the total number by 3. $18 \div 3 = 6$.
10. Write $\frac{625}{1000}$ as a decimal number.
Answer: 0.625
Explanation: The fraction is "six hundred twenty-five thousandths". In decimal form, the thousandths place is three spaces to the right of the decimal point, which writes exactly as 0.625.
11. Write 0.123 as a fraction.
Answer: $\frac{123}{1000}$
Explanation: The decimal extends to three decimal places (tenths, hundredths, thousandths). This translates to a fraction with a denominator of 1000.

13. $$ \begin{array}{r} 31.428 \\ + 17.680 \\ \hline \end{array} $$
Answer: 49.108
Explanation: Align decimals and add from right to left.
8 + 0 = 8.
2 + 8 = 10 (write 0, carry 1).

1 + 7 + 1 = 9.

17. $2100 \div (12 \div 4)$
Answer: 700
Explanation: According to the order of operations (PEMDAS), evaluate the expression inside the parentheses first. $12 \div 4 = 3$. Then divide the outside number by that result: $2100 \div 3 = 700$.
18. $7c = 420$
Answer: c = 60
Explanation: "7c" means 7 multiplied by $c$. To isolate $c$, perform the inverse operation by dividing both sides by 7. $420 \div 7 = 60$.
19. $$ \begin{array}{r} 6 \\ 14 \\ n \\ 17 \\ 25 \\ + 110 \\ \hline 200 \end{array} $$
Answer: n = 28
Explanation: First, add all the known numbers together: $6 + 14 + 17 + 25 + 110 = 172$. You know the grand total is 200. To find the missing number $n$, subtract the sum of the knowns from the total: $200 - 172 = 28$.
20. $$ \begin{array}{r} 23 \\ \times 50 \\ \hline \end{array} $$
Answer: 1,150
Explanation: You can break this down by multiplying 23 by 5 first. $23 \times 5 = 115$. Because you are actually multiplying by 5 tens (50), attach a zero to the end. Final answer is 1150.
