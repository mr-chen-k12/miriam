Math Worksheet Review

1. It took four spoonfuls to make one batch. How many spoonfuls were required to make 40 batches?

Answer: 160 spoonfuls

Explanation: To find the total number of spoonfuls, multiply the number of spoonfuls per batch by the total number of batches: \( 4 \times 40 = 160 \).

2. Find the perimeter and area of this rectangle:

(6 units wide by 4 units high)

Answer: Perimeter = 20 units, Area = 24 sq units

Explanation:
Perimeter is the distance around the outside: \( 6 + 4 + 6 + 4 = 20 \) units.
Area is the space inside (length \(\times\) width): \( 6 \times 4 = 24 \) square units.

3. Angela ran 100 meters in twelve and fourteen hundredths seconds. Use digits to write her time for 100 meters.

Answer: 12.14

Explanation: "Twelve" is the whole number. "And" represents the decimal point. "Fourteen hundredths" is written as 0.14. Putting it together gives 12.14.

4. Maura ran \( \frac{3}{5} \) of the course but walked the rest of the way. What fraction of the course did she walk?

Answer: \( \frac{2}{5} \)

Explanation: The entire course represents \( \frac{5}{5} \) (or 1 whole). Subtract the fraction she ran from the total: \( \frac{5}{5} - \frac{3}{5} = \frac{2}{5} \).

5. In problem 4, did Maura run more than 50% of the course or less than 50% of the course?

Answer: More than 50%

Explanation: She ran \( \frac{3}{5} \) of the course. To convert \( \frac{3}{5} \) to a percentage, multiply numerator and denominator by 20 to get \( \frac{60}{100} \), which is 60%. 60% is greater than 50%.

6. Jimmy drew an octagon and a pentagon. What was the total number of sides in the two polygons?

Answer: 13 sides

Explanation: An octagon has 8 sides. A pentagon has 5 sides. \( 8 + 5 = 13 \).

7. To what mixed number is the arrow pointing?

Answer: \( 7 \frac{5}{6} \)

Explanation: Between the numbers 7 and 8, there are 6 spaces (meaning it is divided into sixths). The arrow points to the 5th mark after the 7, making it \( 7 \frac{5}{6} \).

8. Mount Rainier stands four thousand, three hundred ninety-two meters above sea level. Use digits to write that number of meters.

Answer: 4,392

Explanation: Translating the words into standard numerical digits gives 4,392.

9. Abigail could make 35 prizes in 7 minutes. How many prizes could she make in 1 minute?

Answer: 5 prizes

Explanation: Divide the total number of prizes by the total minutes: \( 35 \div 7 = 5 \).

10. Estimate the sum of 6810 and 9030 by rounding each number to the nearest thousand before adding.

Answer: 16,000

Explanation:
6810 rounded to the nearest thousand is 7,000.
9030 rounded to the nearest thousand is 9,000.
\( 7,000 + 9,000 = 16,000 \).

11. $20 - ($8.95 + 75¢)

Answer: $10.30

Explanation: First, convert 75¢ to $0.75. Then, solve the parentheses: \( \$8.95 + \$0.75 = \$9.70 \). Finally, subtract from twenty dollars: \( \$20.00 - \$9.70 = \$10.30 \).

12. \( 5^2 - 4^2 \)

Answer: 9

Explanation: Square the numbers first: \( 5^2 = 25 \) and \( 4^2 = 16 \). Then subtract: \( 25 - 16 = 9 \).

21. \( 5 \overline{) 96 } \)

Answer: 19 R 1

Explanation: Using long division: 5 goes into 9 one time (1 x 5 = 5), 9 - 5 = 4. Bring down the 6 to make 46. 5 goes into 46 nine times (5 x 9 = 45), 46 - 45 = 1. The quotient is 19 with a remainder of 1.

22. \( 7 \overline{) 156 } \)

Answer: 22 R 2

Explanation: 7 goes into 15 two times (7 x 2 = 14), 15 - 14 = 1. Bring down the 6 to make 16. 7 goes into 16 two times (7 x 2 = 14), 16 - 14 = 2. Quotient is 22 with a remainder of 2.

28. Which word makes the following sentence untrue?

All squares are ____________.
A. polygons      B. rectangles
C. similar         D. congruent

Answer: D. congruent

Explanation: Congruent means identical in shape and size. Since squares can be different sizes (e.g., a \( 2 \times 2 \) square vs a \( 5 \times 5 \) square), they are not all congruent. They are, however, all polygons, rectangles, and similar to one another.

29. Compare: 2 liters \( \bigcirc \) \( \frac{1}{2} \) gallon

Answer: 2 liters > \( \frac{1}{2} \) gallon

Explanation: 1 gallon is approximately 3.785 liters. Half of a gallon is approximately 1.89 liters. Since 2 liters is more than 1.89 liters, 2 liters > \( \frac{1}{2} \) gallon.

30. Draw an octagon. An octagon has how many vertices?

Answer: 8 vertices

Explanation: The prefix "octa-" means eight. Therefore, an octagon is a polygon with 8 sides and 8 vertices (corners). A stop sign is a common example of a regular octagon.
