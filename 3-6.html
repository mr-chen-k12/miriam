Math Problem Solutions

1. Three feet equals 1 yard. A car that is 15 feet long is how many yards long?
Answer: 5 yards
To convert feet to yards, divide the total number of feet by 3.
$15 \div 3 = 5$.
2. Write four multiplication/division facts using the numbers 3, 10, and 30.
Answer: $3 \times 10 = 30$, $10 \times 3 = 30$, $30 \div 10 = 3$, $30 \div 3 = 10$
These make up the "fact family" for these three numbers, showing the inverse relationship between multiplication and division.
3. Roberta had six quarters, three dimes, and fourteen pennies. How much money did she have in all?
Answer: $1.94
Calculate the value of each coin type, then sum them up:
Quarters: $6 \times \$0.25 = \$1.50$
Dimes: $3 \times \$0.10 = \$0.30$
Pennies: $14 \times \$0.01 = \$0.14$
Total: $\$1.50 + \$0.30 + \$0.14 = \$1.94$
4. What is the sum of the even numbers that are greater than 10 but less than 20?
Answer: 60
First, identify the even numbers between 10 and 20: 12, 14, 16, and 18.
Then, find their sum: $12 + 14 + 16 + 18 = 60$.
5. Estimate the sum of 715 and 594 by rounding the numbers to the nearest hundred and then adding.
Answer: 1300
Round 715 to the nearest hundred: 700.
Round 594 to the nearest hundred: 600.
Add the rounded numbers: $700 + 600 = 1300$.
6. Erin opened a 1-gallon bottle of milk and began filling glasses. Each glass held 1 cup of milk. Two cups equals a pint. Two pints equals a quart. Four quarts equals a gallon. How many glasses could Erin fill?
Answer: 16 glasses
Work backwards to find out how many cups are in a gallon:
1 gallon = 4 quarts.
4 quarts = $4 \times 2 = 8$ pints.
8 pints = $8 \times 2 = 16$ cups.
Since 1 glass holds 1 cup, she can fill 16 glasses.
7. To what mixed number is the arrow pointing? (Number line shows an arrow at the 2nd tick mark out of 4 between 6 and 7).
Answer: $6 \frac{2}{4}$ (or $6 \frac{1}{2}$)
The space between 6 and 7 is divided into 4 equal segments, meaning each mark represents one-fourth ($\frac{1}{4}$). The arrow points to the second mark past 6, which is $6 \frac{2}{4}$. This simplifies to $6 \frac{1}{2}$.
8. The cake was cut into 12 equal pieces. Seven of the pieces were eaten. What fraction of the cake was left?
Answer: $\frac{5}{12}$
Subtract the eaten pieces from the total pieces: $12 - 7 = 5$. There are 5 pieces left out of the original 12, making the fraction $\frac{5}{12}$.
9. The product of 4 and 3 is how much greater than the sum of 4 and 3?
Answer: 5
Calculate the product: $4 \times 3 = 12$.
Calculate the sum: $4 + 3 = 7$.
Find the difference: $12 - 7 = 5$.
10. What is the sum of $9^2$ and $\sqrt{9}$?
Answer: 84
Calculate nine squared: $9^2 = 81$.
Calculate the square root of nine: $\sqrt{9} = 3$.
Add them together: $81 + 3 = 84$.
11. (a) What is the name of this polygon? (b) Each side is the same length. What is the perimeter of this polygon? (Image shows a regular 6-sided shape with a ruler measuring one side as 1 cm)
Answer: (a) Hexagon (b) 6 cm
(a) A polygon with 6 sides is called a hexagon.
(b) The ruler indicates one side is 1 cm long. Since all 6 sides are the same length, the perimeter is $6 \times 1\text{ cm} = 6\text{ cm}$.
12. Roger could pick 56 flowers in 8 minutes. At that rate, how many flowers could he pick in 1 minute?
Answer: 7 flowers
Divide the total number of flowers by the total number of minutes to find the unit rate: $56 \div 8 = 7$.
13. Sarah could pick 11 flowers in 1 minute. At that rate, how many flowers could she pick in 5 minutes?
Answer: 55 flowers
Multiply the rate per minute by the number of minutes: $11 \times 5 = 55$.
14. $\$40.00 - D = \$2.43$
Answer: $D = \$37.57$
To isolate $D$, subtract the result from the total: $\$40.00 - \$2.43 = \$37.57$.
15. $5 \times N = 15 + \sqrt{25}$
Answer: $N = 4$
First, evaluate the square root: $\sqrt{25} = 5$.
Substitute back into the equation: $5 \times N = 15 + 5$.
Simplify: $5 \times N = 20$.
Divide both sides by 5: $N = 4$.
16. $6 \times 4 \times 10$
Answer: 240
Multiply left to right: $6 \times 4 = 24$. Then multiply by 10: $24 \times 10 = 240$.
17. $5^3$
Answer: 125
An exponent of 3 means multiplying the base number by itself three times: $5 \times 5 \times 5 = 125$.
18. $3.5 + 2.45$
Answer: 5.95
Align the decimal points by adding a zero to 3.5 to make it 3.50. Then add: $3.50 + 2.45 = 5.95$.
19. $1.95 - 0.4$
Answer: 1.55
Align the decimal points by adding a zero to 0.4 to make it 0.40. Then subtract: $1.95 - 0.40 = 1.55$.
20. $\$1.00 - (\$0.36 + \$0.57)$
Answer: $0.07
Following order of operations, solve parentheses first: $\$0.36 + \$0.57 = \$0.93$.
Subtract that sum from one dollar: $\$1.00 - \$0.93 = \$0.07$.
21. $349 \times 8$
Answer: 2792
Multiply: $349 \times 8 = 2792$.
22. $\$7.60 \times 7$
Answer: $53.20
Multiply the values ignoring the decimal first ($760 \times 7 = 5320$), then place the decimal two spots from the right: $\$53.20$.
23. $34 \div 6$ (From long division symbol $6\overline{)34}$)
Answer: 5 R 4 (or $5 \frac{2}{3}$)
6 goes into 34 five times ($6 \times 5 = 30$). Subtracting 30 from 34 leaves a remainder of 4.
24. $62 \div 8$ (From long division symbol $8\overline{)62}$)
Answer: 7 R 6 (or $7 \frac{3}{4}$)
8 goes into 62 seven times ($8 \times 7 = 56$). Subtracting 56 from 62 leaves a remainder of 6.
25. $24 \div 5$ (From long division symbol $5\overline{)24}$)
Answer: 4 R 4 (or $4 \frac{4}{5}$)
5 goes into 24 four times ($5 \times 4 = 20$). Subtracting 20 from 24 leaves a remainder of 4.
26. $\frac{63}{7}$
Answer: 9
A fraction bar represents division. $63 \div 7 = 9$.
27. If 60% of the flowers that Sarah picked were red, what percent of the flowers that Sarah picked were not red?
Answer: 40%
The total amount is always 100%. Subtract the percentage of red flowers to find the remaining percent: $100\% - 60\% = 40\%$.
28. Which of these numbers is a factor of 10?
A. 3    B. 5    C. 15    D. 40
Answer: B. 5
A factor is a number that divides evenly into another number without a remainder. 5 divides evenly into 10 ($10 \div 5 = 2$).
29. (a) What fraction of the large square is shaded? (b) What percent of the large square is not shaded? (Image shows a 10x10 grid with 3 full columns shaded)
Answer: (a) $\frac{3}{10}$ (b) 70%
(a) The grid has 100 small squares. 3 full columns are shaded, meaning 30 squares are shaded. The fraction is $\frac{30}{100}$, which simplifies to $\frac{3}{10}$.
(b) If 30 out of 100 squares (30%) are shaded, then the unshaded portion is $100\% - 30\% = 70\%$.
