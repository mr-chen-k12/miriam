Math Skills Practice Test

1. Two thousand, four hundred sixty-five is how much greater than three hundred forty-seven?

Explanation: Subtract the smaller number from the larger number to find the difference.

\(2465 - 347 = 2118\)

Answer: 2118

2. If each crate can hold eight boxes, how many boxes can fit in forty-two crates?

Explanation: Multiply the number of crates by the capacity of each crate.

\(42 \times 8 = 336\)

Answer: 336 boxes

3. Liam ran 4 miles in 36 minutes. On average, how many minutes did it take Liam to run each mile?

Explanation: Divide the total time by the total distance.

\(36 \text{ minutes} \div 4 \text{ miles} = 9 \text{ minutes per mile}\)

Answer: 9 minutes

4. A bakery used 63 pounds of flour in 9 days. On average, how many pounds of flour did they use each day?

Explanation: Divide the total flour by the total days.

\(63 \div 9 = 7\)

Answer: 7 pounds

5. A train traveled for 6 hours at an average speed of 75 miles per hour. How far did the train travel?

Explanation: Distance = Speed \(\times\) Time.

\(75 \text{ mph} \times 6 \text{ hours} = 450 \text{ miles}\)

Answer: 450 miles

6. A rectangle is divided into 10 equal parts. If 7 parts are shaded, what fraction of the rectangle is shaded?

Explanation: The shaded parts represent the numerator, and the total parts represent the denominator.

\(\frac{7}{10}\)

Answer: 7/10

7. How many decades are in three centuries?

Explanation: One century = 100 years. Three centuries = 300 years. Since one decade = 10 years, divide 300 by 10.

\(300 \div 10 = 30\)

Answer: 30 decades

8. Compare these fractions using circles to show the comparison: \(\frac{1}{4} \bigcirc \frac{2}{5}\)

Explanation: Convert to decimals: \(\frac{1}{4} = 0.25\) and \(\frac{2}{5} = 0.40\).

Answer: \(\frac{1}{4} < \frac{2}{5}\)

9. Estimate the sum of 582 and 419 by rounding to the nearest hundred.

Explanation: 582 rounds to 600. 419 rounds to 400.

\(600 + 400 = 1000\)

Answer: 1000

10. If a circle has a radius of 5 cm, what is the length of its diameter?

Explanation: The diameter is twice the length of the radius.

\(5 \text{ cm} \times 2 = 10 \text{ cm}\)

Answer: 10 cm

11. Find the eighth multiple of six. Then subtract 15. What is the answer?

Explanation: \(8 \times 6 = 48\). Then, \(48 - 15 = 33\).

Answer: 33

12. \( \$82.55 + \$14.96 \)

Explanation: Line up decimals and add:
\(\hphantom{+}82.55\)
\(\underline{+14.96}\)
\(\hphantom{+}97.51\)

Answer: $97.51

13. \( 72,403 - 35,168 \)

Explanation: Subtract carefully, borrowing where necessary.
\(\hphantom{-}72,403\)
\(\underline{-35,168}\)
\(\hphantom{-}37,235\)

Answer: 37,235

14. \( 9.25 - (2.5 + 3.12) \)

Explanation: Parentheses first: \(2.5 + 3.12 = 5.62\).
Then subtract: \(9.25 - 5.62 = 3.63\).

Answer: 3.63

15. \( 500 - (15 \times 4) \)

Explanation: Multiply first: \(15 \times 4 = 60\).
Then subtract: \(500 - 60 = 440\).

Answer: 440

16. \( 420 \times 8 \)

Explanation: \(420 \times 8 = 3360\)

Answer: 3360

17. \( 258 \times 4 \)

Explanation: \(258 \times 4 = 1032\)

Answer: 1032

18. \( \sqrt{81} \div 3 \)

Explanation: \(\sqrt{81} = 9\). Then, \(9 \div 3 = 3\).

Answer: 3

19. \( 45 \div 7 \)

Explanation: \(7 \times 6 = 42\). The remainder is \(45 - 42 = 3\).

Answer: 6 R 3

20. Find the missing factor: \( 8x = 72 \)

Explanation: Divide 72 by 8.

\(x = 72 \div 8 = 9\)

Answer: x = 9
