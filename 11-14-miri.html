g.

Ten is a multiple of which whole numbers?

Answer: $1,\; 2,\; 5,$ and $10$

Step-by-Step Solution

$$1 \times 10 = 10 \quad \text{and} \quad 2 \times 5 = 10$$

Therefore, $10$ is a multiple of $1, 2, 5,$ and $10$.

j.

List the factors of $5$.

Answer: $1$ and $5$

Step-by-Step Solution

The only whole numbers that multiply to give $5$ are $1 \times 5 = 5$. Thus, $5$ has only two factors: $1$ and $5$.

k.

Write all the prime numbers less than $10$.

Answer: $2,\; 3,\; 5,\; 7$

Step-by-Step Solution

A prime number is a counting number greater than $1$ that has exactly two factors ($1$ and itself):

  • $2$: factors $1, 2$ → Prime
  • $3$: factors $1, 3$ → Prime
  • $4$: factors $1, 2, 4$ → Composite
  • $5$: factors $1, 5$ → Prime
  • $6$: factors $1, 2, 3, 6$ → Composite
  • $7$: factors $1, 7$ → Prime
  • $8$: factors $1, 2, 4, 8$ → Composite
  • $9$: factors $1, 3, 9$ → Composite
l.

True or False: If a counting number is greater than $1$ and is not prime, then it is composite.

Answer: True

Step-by-Step Solution

Every counting number greater than $1$ has at least two factors ($1$ and itself). If it has only those two factors, it is prime; if it has more than two factors (not prime), then by definition it is a composite number.

Formulate Write and solve an equation: Raimi bought a toy for $\$1.85$ and sold it for $75\text{¢}$ more. For what price did he sell the toy?

*4.

Sixty percent of the students in the class were boys. Were there more girls or more boys in the class?

Answer: More boys

Step-by-Step Solution

The entire class represents $100\%$. Subtract the percentage of boys to find the percentage of girls:

$$100\% - 60\% = 40\% \text{ girls}$$

Since $60\% > 40\%$, there were more boys in the class.

Draw a rectangle that is $4\text{ cm}$ long and $3\text{ cm}$ wide.

a. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?

b. What is the area of the rectangle?

Answer: a. $14\text{ cm}$  •  b. $12\text{ cm}^2$

Step-by-Step Solution

a. Perimeter: Add the lengths of all four sides:

$$P = 4\text{ cm} + 3\text{ cm} + 4\text{ cm} + 3\text{ cm} = 2 \times (4\text{ cm} + 3\text{ cm}) = 14\text{ cm}$$

b. Area: Multiply length by width:

$$A = 4\text{ cm} \times 3\text{ cm} = 12\text{ cm}^2$$

Explain Describe the order of operations in this expression and find the number it equals:

$$\sqrt{64} + (4 + 4)$$

Answer: $16$

Step-by-Step Solution

  1. First, perform the operation inside the parentheses: $4 + 4 = 8$.

*26.

Justify One gallon is equal to $128$ fluid ounces. Garrett estimates that four gallons is about $500$ fluid ounces. Is Garrett’s estimate reasonable? Explain why or why not.

Answer: Yes, Garrett’s estimate is reasonable.

Step-by-Step Solution

$128$ fluid ounces is very close to the compatible number $125$. Multiplying $125$ by $4$ gives:

$$4 \times 125 = 500\text{ fluid ounces}$$

Comparing with the exact product, $4 \times 128 = 512\text{ fluid ounces}$, Garrett’s estimate of $500\text{ fluid ounces}$ is very close and therefore reasonable.

*29.

Multiple Choice Which of these numbers is a composite number and not a prime number?

A $2$ B $3$ C $4$ D $5$
Answer: $\text{C } 4$

Step-by-Step Solution

Check the factors of each choice:

  • $2 = 1 \times 2$ (only $2$ factors → prime)
  • $3 = 1 \times 3$ (only $2$ factors → prime)
  • $4 = 1 \times 4$ and $2 \times 2$ ($3$ factors: $1, 2, 4$ → composite)
  • $5 = 1 \times 5$ (only $2$ factors → prime)

Therefore, choice C ($4$) is a composite number.
