Similar to Question 29: Estimation Word Problems
A large bag of flour weighs 5 pounds, which is about 80 ounces. Estimate the number of 9-ounce portions you can make. Explain your thinking.
Answer: Estimate 9 portions.
Explanation: 80 rounds nicely to 81, and \( 81 \div 9 = 9 \).
A car travels 312 miles on a full tank of gas. The tank holds about 10 gallons. Estimate the car's miles per gallon (mpg). Explain your thinking.
Answer: Estimate 31 mpg.
Explanation: 312 rounds down to 310, and \( 310 \div 10 = 31 \).
A bookshelf is 73 inches tall. Estimate the number of 12-inch tall boxes you can stack next to it. Explain your thinking.
Answer: Estimate 6 boxes.
Explanation: 73 rounds to 72, and \( 72 \div 12 = 6 \).
