Question 1

Six feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply the number of feet by 12:

6 feet × 12 inches = 72 inches

Question 2

Maya handed the cashier a $50 bill to buy a backpack that cost $31.75. How much change did she receive back?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the cost of the backpack from $50.00:

$50.00 − $31.75 = $18.25

Question 3

Noah is 7 years younger than Oliver. Oliver is three times as old as Emma. If Oliver is 21 years old, how old are Noah and Emma?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Find Noah's age: Noah is 7 years younger than Oliver → 21 − 7 = 14 years old.

2. Find Emma's age: Oliver is 3 times as old as Emma → 21 ÷ 3 = 7 years old.

Noah is 14 and Emma is 7.

Question 5

One-third of the 27 students in Mr. Hall's class play basketball. How many students play basketball?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-third of a group, divide the total number of students by 3:

27 ÷ 3 = 9 students

Question 6

Looking back at Question 5, how many students do not play basketball?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract basketball players from the total class:

27 − 9 = 18 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 93 and 46 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then, find the exact difference.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Estimate: Round 93 down to 90. Round 46 up to 50. Subtract: 90 − 50 = 40.

2. Exact Difference: Subtract the original numbers: 93 − 46 = 47.

Question 14

A soccer game started at 10:15 AM and finished at 12:45 PM. How long did the soccer game last?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. From 10:15 AM to 12:15 PM is 2 hours.

2. From 12:15 PM to 12:45 PM is 30 minutes.

Total time: 2 hours and 30 minutes (or 150 minutes).

Question 17

Question 20

Ava has 3 one-dollar bills, 2 quarters, 4 dimes, 1 nickel, and 3 pennies. What is her total amount of money?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

• 3 Dollar Bills = $3.00

• 2 Quarters = 2 × 25¢ = 50¢ ($0.50)

• 4 Dimes = 4 × 10¢ = 40¢ ($0.40)

• 1 Nickel = 5¢ ($0.05)

• 3 Pennies = 3¢ ($0.03)

Total: $3.00 + $0.50 + $0.40 + $0.05 + $0.03 = $3.98

Question 26

(a) XVI

(b) XXIX

(c) LXXV

Teacher Answer Key

Answers:

(a) XVI = 10 + 5 + 1 = 16

(b) XXIX = 10 + 10 + (10 − 1) = 29

(c) LXXV = 50 + 10 + 10 + 5 = 75

Question 28

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

2. Add the 8 ounces: 96 + 8 = 104 ounces.

Question 29

A zoo had 610 visitors in the morning. By noon, 140 visitors left, and in the afternoon 85 new visitors entered. How many visitors were in the zoo by the end of the day?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Subtract visitors who left: 610 − 140 = 470 visitors.

2. Add new visitors: 470 + 85 = 555 visitors.
