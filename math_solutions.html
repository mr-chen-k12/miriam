Cumulative Test Solutions (1 - 20)

1. Marie faces south and then turns 90° clockwise. What direction is Marie facing after the turn?
A. north    B. south    C. east    D. west
Answer: D. west
Imagine a compass. If you are facing South (down), a clockwise turn means turning to your right. A 90-degree turn to the right from South points you directly West.
2. Nectarines cost 57¢ per pound. What is the price for 4 pounds of nectarines?
Answer: $2.28
Multiply the cost per pound by the number of pounds:
\( 57\text{¢} \times 4 = 228\text{¢} \).
Since 100 cents equal $1.00, 228 cents is written as $2.28.
3. In bowling, the sum of Amber's score and Beth's score was equal to Consuela's score. If Consuela's score was 111 and Beth's score was 48, what was Amber's score?
Answer: 63
We can set up the equation: \( \text{Amber} + \text{Beth} = \text{Consuela} \).
Substituting the known values: \( \text{Amber} + 48 = 111 \).
To find Amber's score, subtract Beth's score from Consuela's score: \( 111 - 48 = 63 \).
4. One third of the 78 students were assigned to each room. How many students were assigned to each room?
Answer: 26
To find one third of a number, you divide that number by 3.
\( 78 \div 3 = 26 \).
Therefore, 26 students were assigned to each room.

6. What fraction of the letters in the word ALABAMA are not As?
Answer: \( \frac{3}{7} \)
First, count the total number of letters in ALABAMA: 7 letters.
Next, count the letters that are NOT 'A': L, B, and M. That is 3 letters.
The fraction is the number of non-A letters over the total number of letters: 3/7.
7. The whale weighs 3 tons. How many pounds is 3 tons?
Answer: 6,000 pounds
Recall the conversion factor: \( 1 \text{ ton} = 2,000 \text{ pounds} \).
To find the weight of 3 tons, multiply by 3: \( 3 \times 2,000 = 6,000 \).
8. Which is the more reasonable measure of weight for a box of cereal?
A. 300 g    B. 300 kg
Answer: A. 300 g
A gram (g) is a small unit of mass, whereas a kilogram (kg) is much heavier (1 kg ≈ 2.2 pounds). 300 kg would weigh over 660 pounds, which is impossible for a standard box of cereal. 300 grams (a little less than a pound) is a very reasonable weight.
9. Which triangle appears to be an equilateral triangle?
Answer: Triangle B
An equilateral triangle has three sides that are all equal in length, and all three internal angles are 60°. Triangle B is the only shape provided that demonstrates equal side lengths. (A is isosceles/scalene, C is a right triangle, D is obtuse).
10. Fifty-two thousand, eight hundred thirty-four dollars is how much greater than forty-five thousand, nine hundred eighty-six dollars?
Answer: $6,848
First, write both numbers in standard form:
Number 1: 52,834
Number 2: 45,986
Subtract the smaller amount from the larger amount:
\( 52,834 - 45,986 = 6,848 \).
11. Estimate the sum of 396 and 512.
Answer: 900
To estimate, round each number to the nearest hundred.
396 rounds up to 400.
512 rounds down to 500.
Add the rounded numbers together: \( 400 + 500 = 900 \).
12. Michelle could load 105 packages in 3 hours. How many packages could Michelle load in 1 hour?
Answer: 35
To find the hourly rate, divide the total number of packages by the total number of hours.
\( 105 \div 3 = 35 \).
She can load 35 packages per hour. (Note: the student incorrectly wrote 40).

16. \( 6464 \div 8 \)
Answer: 808
Using long division:
1. 8 goes into 64 eight times. (\(8 \times 8 = 64\))
2. Bring down the 6. 8 goes into 6 zero times. This is an important placeholder!
3. Bring down the 4 making it 64. 8 goes into 64 eight times.
Result: 808. (The student missed the zero placeholder and got 88).
17. \( 1234 \div 5 \)
Answer: 246 R 4 (or 246.8)
1. 5 goes into 12 two times (10), remainder 2.
2. Bring down 3 to make 23. 5 goes into 23 four times (20), remainder 3.
3. Bring down 4 to make 34. 5 goes into 34 six times (30), remainder 4.
The final answer is 246 with a remainder of 4.
18. Find the missing number: \( 3b = 201 \)
Answer: \( b = 67 \)
To solve for \( b \), perform the inverse operation. Since \( b \) is multiplied by 3, divide both sides by 3:
\( 201 \div 3 = 67 \).
19. Find the missing number:
2473 1286 +   n 4000
Answer: \( n = 241 \)
First, combine the numbers that are given: \( 2473 + 1286 = 3759 \).
Now the equation is \( 3759 + n = 4000 \).
To find \( n \), subtract 3759 from 4000: \( 4000 - 3759 = 241 \).
(The student incorrectly subtracted the two top numbers instead of solving for the missing addend).
20. Use a symbol to compare the number of cups in 1 pint to the number of cups in 1 quart.
Answer: < (Less than)
There are exactly 2 cups in 1 pint.
There are exactly 4 cups in 1 quart.
Comparing the two: 2 cups < 4 cups. Therefore, the correct symbol is <.
