Problem 1
Raimi bought a toy for $1.85 and sold it for $0.75 more. For what price did he sell the toy?

Equation: \( \text{Selling Price} = \text{Buying Price} + \text{Profit} \)

\( \text{Price} = 1.85 + 0.75 \)

Answer: $2.60

Problem 2
Two thousand people entered the contest. Only seven will win prizes. How many entrants will not win prizes?

Subtract the winners from the total:

\( 2000 - 7 = 1993 \)

Answer: 1,993 entrants

Problem 3
A recent census in Arkansas showed that 11,003 people live in Scott County and 8,484 people live in Newton County. How many more people live in Scott County than in Newton County?

Subtract the population of Newton County from Scott County:

\( 11,003 - 8,484 \)

\(\begin{array}{r} 11,003 \\ - \phantom{0}8,484 \\ \hline 2,519 \end{array}\)

Answer: 2,519 more people

Problem 4
Sixty percent of the students in the class were boys. Were there more girls or more boys in the class?

If 60% are boys, then the remaining percentage are girls.

\( 100\% - 60\% = 40\% \) (Girls)

Since 60% > 40%, there are more boys.

Answer: More boys

Problem 5
Draw a rectangle that is 4 cm long and 3 cm wide.
a. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?
b. What is the area of the rectangle?
Rectangle (Not to scale)
4 cm (Length) x 3 cm (Width)

a. Perimeter: \( 2 \times (Length + Width) \)

\( 2 \times (4 + 3) = 2 \times 7 = 14 \text{ cm} \)

b. Area: \( Length \times Width \)

\( 4 \times 3 = 12 \text{ sq cm} \)

Problem 8
Brenda arrived home from school 30 minutes before the time shown on the clock. What time did Brenda arrive home from school?
(Clock shows hour hand near 4, minute hand on 8)

The clock shows 3:40 (The minute hand is on the 8, which is 40 minutes).

Subtract 30 minutes from 3:40.

\( 40 \text{ min} - 30 \text{ min} = 10 \text{ min} \)

Answer: 3:10

Problem 26

Problem 29
Which of these numbers is a composite number and not a prime number?
A 2   B 3   C 4   D 5

A composite number has factors other than 1 and itself.

  • 2 is prime.
  • 3 is prime.
  • 4 is composite (\(2 \times 2\)).
  • 5 is prime.

Answer: C (4)
