Math Practice Worksheet

1. A baker makes 6 batches of cookies. If each batch contains 24 cookies, how many cookies did the baker make in total?

Answer: 144 cookies

Explanation: Multiply the number of batches by the number of cookies in each batch: 6 × 24 = 144.

2. Find the perimeter and area of this rectangle:

(5 units wide by 3 units high)

Answer: Perimeter = 16 units, Area = 15 sq units

Explanation:
Perimeter is the total outside distance: 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16 units.
Area is the space inside (length × width): 5 × 3 = 15 square units.

3. Write the following number using digits: "Forty-five and twenty-three hundredths."

Answer: 45.23

Explanation: "Forty-five" is the whole number. The word "and" acts as the decimal point. "Twenty-three hundredths" translates to 0.23.

4. A pizza is cut into 8 equal slices. Sarah eats 3 slices. What fraction of the pizza is left?

Answer: \(\frac{5}{8}\)

Explanation: The whole pizza is \(\frac{8}{8}\). Subtract the slices eaten: \(\frac{8}{8} - \frac{3}{8} = \frac{5}{8}\).

5. In problem 4, is the fraction of the pizza left more than 50% or less than 50%?

Answer: More than 50%

Explanation: Exactly half (50%) of the pizza would be \(\frac{4}{8}\). Since \(\frac{5}{8}\) is greater than \(\frac{4}{8}\), more than 50% is left.

6. A hexagon and a triangle are drawn on a page. What is the total number of sides of both polygons combined?

Answer: 9 sides

Explanation: A hexagon has 6 sides and a triangle has 3 sides. 6 + 3 = 9.

7. To what mixed number is the arrow pointing?

Answer: \( 4 \frac{1}{4} \)

Explanation: The space between 4 and 5 is divided into 4 equal sections (fourths). The arrow points to the first mark after the 4, making it \( 4 \frac{1}{4} \).

8. The peak of Mount Everest is twenty-nine thousand, thirty-two feet above sea level. Use digits to write this height.

Answer: 29,032

Explanation: Translating the word format into numbers: "Twenty-nine thousand" is 29,000, and "thirty-two" is 32. Combined, it is 29,032.

9. A copy machine can print 42 pages in 6 minutes. How many pages can it print in 1 minute?

Answer: 7 pages

Explanation: To find the per-minute rate, divide the total pages by the total minutes: 42 ÷ 6 = 7.

10. Estimate the sum of 4,195 and 3,820 by rounding each number to the nearest thousand before adding.

Answer: 8,000

Explanation:
4,195 rounded to the nearest thousand is 4,000.
3,820 rounded to the nearest thousand is 4,000.
4,000 + 4,000 = 8,000.

11. $50.00 - ($12.50 + $3.25)

Answer: $34.25

Explanation: Always solve the parentheses first: $12.50 + $3.25 = $15.75. Then subtract that sum from fifty dollars: $50.00 - $15.75 = $34.25.

12. \( 6^2 - 3^2 \)

Answer: 27

Explanation: Find the squares first: \( 6^2 = 36 \) and \( 3^2 = 9 \). Then subtract: 36 - 9 = 27.

13. 45.12 - 12.87

Answer: 32.25

Explanation: Align the decimals and subtract, carrying over as needed to get 32.25.

14. 55¢ × 6

Answer: 330¢ or $3.30

Explanation: Multiply 55 by 6 to get 330. Since the unit is cents, 330 cents converts to 3 dollars and 30 cents ($3.30).

15. $4.15 × 4

Answer: $16.60

Explanation: Multiply as if there is no decimal: 415 × 4 = 1660. Place the decimal back in two spots from the right: $16.60.

16. $6.30 × 5

Answer: $31.50

Explanation: 630 × 5 = 3150. Moving the decimal two places to the left gives $31.50.

17. Rewrite this addition problem as a multiplication problem and find the answer:

    45 + 45 + 45 + 45

Answer: 4 × 45 = 180

Explanation: The number 45 is being added 4 times. Repeated addition is the same as multiplication: 4 × 45 = 180.

18. 56 × 40

Answer: 2,240

Explanation: Multiply 56 by 4 to get 224, then attach the zero from the 40 to the end to get 2,240.

19. \( 6 \overline{) 145 } \)

Answer: 24 R 1

Explanation: 6 goes into 14 two times (12), leaving a remainder of 2. Bring down the 5 to make 25. 6 goes into 25 four times (24), leaving a remainder of 1. The quotient is 24 with a remainder of 1.

20. Draw a hexagon. A hexagon has how many vertices?

Answer: 6 vertices

Explanation: A hexagon is a polygon with 6 straight sides. Where those sides meet, they form 6 corners or "vertices."
