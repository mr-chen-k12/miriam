1 (Inv. 4)

Represent The large square represents 1. Write the shaded part of the square

a. as a fraction.    b. as a decimal number.    c. using words.

Answer: a. \(\frac{30}{100}\) (or \(\frac{3}{10}\))   b. 0.30   c. thirty hundredths

Solution: a. 3 of the 10 columns are shaded, so \(\frac{30}{100}\) of the square is shaded (\(\frac{30}{100} = \frac{3}{10}\)). b. \(\frac{30}{100} = 0.30\). c. Thirty hundredths.

2 (35)

Takeshi had a dime, a quarter, and a penny. Write this amount using a dollar sign and a decimal point.

Answer: $0.36

Solution: A dime is \$0.10, a quarter is \$0.25, and a penny is \$0.01: \(0.10 + 0.25 + 0.01 = 0.36\), so \$0.36\.

3 (40)

Donna opened a 1-gallon container of milk and poured 1 quart of milk into a pitcher. How many quarts of milk were left in the 1-gallon container?

Answer: 3 quarts

Solution: One gallon equals 4 quarts. After pouring out 1 quart, \(4 - 1 = 3\) quarts remain.

4 (3)

Generalize Describe the rule for this sequence and find the next three numbers:

\(\ldots, 4200,\ 4300,\ 4400,\) __, __, __, \(\ldots\)

Answer: Rule: count up by hundreds (add 100 each time). Next: 4500, 4600, 4700

Solution: Each number is 100 more than the one before it, so the rule is “add 100.” Continuing: \(4400 + 100 = 4500\), \(4500 + 100 = 4600\), \(4600 + 100 = 4700\).

5 (Inv. 4)

Connect Use digits and a comparison symbol to show that the decimal number five tenths equals the fraction one half.

Answer: \(0.5 = \frac{1}{2}\)

Solution: Five tenths is written 0.5, and one half is written \(\frac{1}{2}\). Since \(0.5 = \frac{5}{10} = \frac{1}{2}\), we write \(0.5 = \frac{1}{2}\).

6 (27)

Anando fell asleep last night at the time shown on the clock. His alarm clock was set to ring eight hours later. What time was Anando's alarm clock set to ring?

Answer: 6:15

Solution: The clock shows 10:15 (hour hand just past 10, minute hand on the 3). Eight hours after 10:15 is 6:15.

9 (35)

What mixed number is illustrated by these shaded triangles?

Answer: \(1\frac{3}{4}\)

Solution: The first triangle is fully shaded (1 whole). The second triangle is divided into 4 equal parts with 3 shaded (\(\frac{3}{4}\)). Together: \(1 + \frac{3}{4} = 1\frac{3}{4}\).

10 (23)

Which letter below has no right angles?

F   E   Z   L

Answer: Z

Solution: F, E, and L are all made with square corners (right angles). Z is made only with slanted and horizontal strokes, so it has no right angles.

Solution: The amount \$1.25 is added 4 times, so the multiplication form is \(4 \times \$1.25\) (which equals \$5.00).

13 (Inv. 2)

A meter equals how many centimeters?

Answer: 100 centimeters

Solution: The prefix “centi-” means one hundredth, so \(1\text{ m} = 100\text{ cm}\).

14 (36)

a. Five dimes are what fraction of a dollar?

b. Write the value of five dimes using a dollar sign and a decimal point.

Answer: a. \(\frac{1}{2}\) of a dollar   b. $0.50

Solution: A dime is \(\frac{1}{10}\) of a dollar, so five dimes \(= 5 \times \frac{1}{10} = \frac{5}{10} = \frac{1}{2}\) of a dollar \(= 5 \times \$0.10 = \$0.50\).

15 (Inv. 4) Compare

Compare:   a. \(0.5 \bigcirc 0.50\)      b. \(\frac{1}{2} \bigcirc \frac{1}{4}\)

Answer: a. \(0.5 = 0.50\)   b. \(\frac{1}{2} > \frac{1}{4}\)

Solution: a. Zeros at the end of a decimal do not change its value: \(0.5 = 0.50\). b. One half of a whole is more than one fourth: \(\frac{1}{2} > \frac{1}{4}\).

24 (Inv. 1)

Conclude What are the next four numbers in this counting sequence?

\(\ldots, 28,\ 21,\ 14,\) __, __, __, __, \(\ldots\)

Answer: 7, 0, \(-7\), \(-14\)

Solution: Each number is 7 less than the one before it (counting down by sevens): \(14 - 7 = 7\), \(7 - 7 = 0\), \(0 - 7 = -7\), \(-7 - 7 = -14\).

26 (Inv. 3)

Multiple Choice If the area of a square is 36 square inches, then how long is each side of the square?

  1. 6 in.
  2. 9 in.
  3. 12 in.
  4. 18 in.

Answer: A — 6 in.

Solution: The side length is the square root of the area: \(\sqrt{36} = 6\), since \(6 \times 6 = 36\).

28 (Inv. 4)

Nieve quickly started and stopped a stopwatch four times. Write these times in order from fastest to slowest:

0.27 second, 0.21 second, 0.24 second, 0.20 second.

Answer: 0.20 second, 0.21 second, 0.24 second, 0.27 second

Solution: Fastest means the smallest time. Comparing hundredths: \(0.20 < 0.21 < 0.24 < 0.27\).

29 (13)

Formulate Justify The Washington Monument is 153 feet taller than the City Center building in Nashville, Tennessee, which is 402 feet tall. How tall is the Washington Monument? Explain why your answer is reasonable.

Answer: 555 feet

Solution: Let \(w\) = the monument's height. Equation: \(w = 402 + 153\), so \(w = 555\) feet. This is reasonable because adding a positive amount (153) to 402 must give a number greater than 402, and 555 is a sensible sum — it also matches the monument's well-known height of about 555 feet.

30 (25, 41)

Formulate Explain The Panther waterfall in Alberta is 600 feet tall. The Fall Creek waterfall in Tennessee is 256 feet tall. How many feet taller is the Panther waterfall? Explain how you found your answer.

Answer: 344 feet taller

Solution: Let \(d\) = how many feet taller. Equation: \(256 + d = 600\), so \(d = 600 - 256 = 344\) feet. I subtracted the shorter waterfall's height from the taller one's height to find the difference.
