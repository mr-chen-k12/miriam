*3.
Mrs. Rushing had a package of lined cards. She used seventy-five cards in class last week. She has forty-seven cards left. How many cards were in the package before last week?
Answer: 122 cards
Explanation: To find the original total number of cards, add the cards used to the cards remaining:
$75 + 47 = 122$ cards.
4.
There are 12 months in a year. How many months are in half of a year?
Answer: 6 months
Explanation: Divide total months by 2:
$12 \div 2 = 6$ months.

*8.
If the diameter of a wheel on Joshua's bike is 20 inches, then what is the radius of the wheel?
Answer: 10 inches
Explanation: The radius is half of the diameter ($r = d \div 2$):
$20 \div 2 = 10$ inches.
*9.
The last recess of the afternoon at Taft Elementary School begins at the time shown on the clock. The recess ends at 1:35 p.m. How long is the last recess of the afternoon?
Answer: 20 minutes
Explanation: The clock shows 1:15 p.m. From 1:15 p.m. to 1:35 p.m. is $35 - 15 = 20$ minutes.
*10. Conclude
Which street is perpendicular to Elm?
Answer: Broadway
Explanation: Perpendicular lines intersect at right angles ($90^\circ$). Broadway crosses Elm at a right angle.
*11.
What fraction of this shape is shaded?
Answer: $\frac{3}{12}$ (or $\frac{1}{4}$)
Explanation: There are 12 total square parts, and 3 of them are shaded. So the fraction is $\frac{3}{12}$, which simplifies to $\frac{1}{4}$.
*12.
Draw a square whose sides are 4 cm long. What is the perimeter of the square?
Answer: 16 cm
Explanation: The perimeter of a square is the sum of its four sides:
$4\text{ cm} + 4\text{ cm} + 4\text{ cm} + 4\text{ cm} = 16\text{ cm}$ (or $4 \times 4\text{ cm} = 16\text{ cm}$).

*26. Multiple Choice
Which of these measurements is not equivalent to one meter?

A 1000 mm      B 100 cm      C 1000 km      D 1 m
Answer: C (1000 km)
Explanation: 1 meter equals 1000 millimeters, 100 centimeters, or 1 meter. 1000 kilometers equals 1,000,000 meters, which is much larger than 1 meter.

28.
The Illinois River and the Potomac River have a combined length of 803 miles. The Illinois River is 420 miles long. Write and solve an equation to find the length of the Potomac River.
Answer: Equation: $420 + p = 803$; $p = 383$ miles
Explanation: Let $p$ be the length of the Potomac River.
Equation: $420 + p = 803$
Solve: $p = 803 - 420 = 383$ miles.
29.
At a school supply store, pencil erasers cost 59¢ each. A drawing pad costs $3.39. What is a reasonable estimate of the total cost of a drawing pad and an eraser? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.
Answer: About $4.00
Explanation: 59¢ rounds up to $0.60, and $3.39 rounds up to $3.40. Adding $0.60 + $3.40 = $4.00. This is a reasonable estimate because both numbers were rounded to the nearest ten cents for easy mental addition.
30.
In Bismarck, North Dakota, the average high temperature in January is $21^\circ\text{F}$. The average low temperature is $-1^\circ\text{F}$. How many degrees warmer is a temperature of $21^\circ\text{F}$ than a temperature of $-1^\circ\text{F}$?
Answer: $22^\circ\text{F}$ warmer
Explanation: From $-1^\circ\text{F}$ to $0^\circ\text{F}$ is 1 degree, and from $0^\circ\text{F}$ to $21^\circ\text{F}$ is 21 degrees.
Total difference: $21 - (-1) = 22^\circ\text{F}$.
Real-World Connection
There were 119 third grade students, 121 fourth grade students and 135 fifth grade students in the auditorium. One hundred eighty-seven of the students returned to class. Which number is greater, the number of students still in the auditorium, or the number of students who returned to class? How do you know?
Answer: The number of students still in the auditorium (188) is greater.
Explanation: 1. Total students in auditorium: $119 + 121 + 135 = 375$ students.
2. Students remaining in auditorium: $375 - 187 = 188$ students.
3. Compare: $188 \text{ (remaining)} > 187 \text{ (returned)}$. Therefore, more students remained in the auditorium.
