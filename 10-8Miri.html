Written Practice

Formulate: write and solve equations for problems 1–3.

1

A group of fish is called a school. There are twenty-five fish in the small school. There are one hundred twelve fish in the big school. How many fewer fish are in the small school?

Answer: 87 fewer fish

Solution: Let \(f\) = how many fewer. Equation: \(112 - 25 = f\). Then \(f = 87\).

2

A piece of ribbon that measured 1 yard was cut into two pieces. If one piece was 12 inches long, how many inches long was the other piece?

Answer: 24 inches

Solution: 1 yard = 36 inches. Let \(p\) = the other piece. Equation: \(12 + p = 36\), so \(p = 36 - 12 = 24\) inches.

3

Mrs. Green took forty-seven digital pictures in Hawaii. Her husband took sixty-two digital pictures in Hawaii. Her son took seventy-five digital pictures. In all, how many digital pictures did the Greens take?

Answer: 184 digital pictures

Solution: Let \(t\) = total pictures. Equation: \(47 + 62 + 75 = t\). Then \(t = 109 + 75 = 184\).

4 Represent

Write the number 7,500,000 in expanded form. Then use words to write the number.

Answer: Expanded form: \(7{,}000{,}000 + 500{,}000\). In words: seven million, five hundred thousand.

Solution: 7,500,000 has 7 millions and 5 hundred-thousands, so \(7{,}500{,}000 = 7{,}000{,}000 + 500{,}000\).

5

Which digit in 27,384,509 is in the thousands place?

Answer: 4

Solution: Reading place values from the right: ones 9, tens 0, hundreds 5, thousands 4, ten-thousands 8, hundred-thousands 3, millions 7, ten-millions 2.

6 Connect

Use a dollar sign and a decimal point to write the value of three dollars, two quarters, one dime, and two nickels. Then write that amount of money using words.

Answer: $3.70; in words: three dollars and seventy cents

Solution: \(\$3.00 + 2(\$0.25) + \$0.10 + 2(\$0.05) = 3.00 + 0.50 + 0.10 + 0.10 = \$3.70\).

7

A gallon of milk is how many quarts of milk?

Answer: 4 quarts

Solution: There are 4 quarts in 1 gallon.

8

How many squares are shaded?

Answer: \(4\frac{1}{2}\) squares

Solution: Four squares are fully shaded, and the first square is cut by a diagonal with half shaded. Total: \(4 + \frac{1}{2} = 4\frac{1}{2}\).

9 Estimate

Use a ruler to find the length of the line segment below to the nearest quarter inch.

Answer: About \(1\frac{1}{2}\) inches

Solution: Line up the 0 mark of a ruler with one end of the segment and read the other end to the nearest quarter-inch mark. The printed segment measures about \(1\frac{1}{2}\) inches. (Sizes can vary slightly between printings — measure the segment in your book.)

10 Connect

Printed on the label of the milk container were these words and numbers:

1 gal (3.78 L)

Use this information to compare the following:

1 gallon \(\bigcirc\) 3 liters

Answer: 1 gallon \(>\) 3 liters

Solution: The label says 1 gallon = 3.78 liters, and \(3.78 > 3\), so 1 gallon is greater than 3 liters.

11

Destiny began reading a book last night at the time shown on the clock. She read until midnight. How much time did Destiny spend reading last night?

Answer: 1 hour 50 minutes

Solution: The clock shows 10:10 (hour hand just past 10, minute hand at the 2). From 10:10 to midnight is 1 hour 50 minutes.

12

a. (Multiple Choice) What type of angle is formed by the hands of the clock shown in problem 11?

  1. acute
  2. right
  3. obtuse
  4. straight

b. (Justify) How do you know that your answer to part a is correct?

Answer: a. C — obtuse

Solution: a. At 10:10 the hands form an angle of about 115°. b. An obtuse angle measures between 90° and 180°, and 115° falls in that range — the hands are more than a quarter-turn apart but less than a half-turn.

13 Compare

a. \(-29 \bigcirc -32\)

b. \(\$0.75 \bigcirc \frac{3}{4}\) of a dollar

Answer: a. \(-29 > -32\)   b. \(\$0.75 = \frac{3}{4}\) of a dollar

Solution: a. On the number line, \(-29\) is to the right of (closer to zero than) \(-32\), so \(-29 > -32\). b. \(\frac{3}{4}\) of a dollar \(= 3 \times \$0.25 = \$0.75\).

Answer: ten and one tenth

Solution: Write the whole-number part ("ten"), then "and", then the fraction part ("one tenth").

25

a. (Connect) Two quarters are what fraction of a dollar?

b. Write the value of two quarters using a dollar sign and a decimal point.

Answer: a. \(\frac{1}{2}\) of a dollar   b. $0.50

Solution: A quarter is \(\frac{1}{4}\) of a dollar, so two quarters \(= 2 \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}\) of a dollar \(= 2 \times \$0.25 = \$0.50\).

26 Multiple Choice

A rectangle has an area of 24 square inches. Which of these could be the length and width of the rectangle?

  1. 6 in. by 6 in.
  2. 12 in. by 12 in.
  3. 8 in. by 4 in.
  4. 8 in. by 3 in.

Answer: D — 8 in. by 3 in.

Solution: Check each: A. \(6 \times 6 = 36\); B. \(12 \times 12 = 144\); C. \(8 \times 4 = 32\); D. \(8 \times 3 = 24\) ✓.

27 Represent

Tarik measured the width of his notebook paper and said that the paper was \(8\frac{2}{4}\) inches wide. What is another way to write \(8\frac{2}{4}\)?

Answer: \(8\frac{1}{2}\) (eight and one half)

Solution: Reduce the fraction part: \(\frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}\), so \(8\frac{2}{4} = 8\frac{1}{2}\).

28 Justify

A gardener plans to build a fence around his 24-by-12-foot rectangular vegetable garden. Fencing for the garden can be purchased in 50-foot, 75-foot, or 100-foot rolls. Which roll of fencing should the gardener buy? Explain why.

Answer: The 75-foot roll

Solution: The garden's perimeter is \(2(24 + 12) = 72\) feet. The 50-foot roll is too short, and the 100-foot roll wastes 28 feet. The 75-foot roll is the smallest roll that covers the 72 feet needed (with 3 feet to spare).

29 Estimate

At a gardening center, one pair of gardening gloves costs $12.00, not including a sales tax of 66¢. What is a reasonable estimate of the cost of two pairs of gloves? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.

Answer: About $26

Solution: Two pairs cost \(2 \times \$12.00 = \$24.00\) plus about \(2 \times \$0.66 = \$1.32\) in tax, for an exact total of \$25.32. Rounding up to \$26 is a reasonable estimate because it is close to the exact total and leaves a small cushion.

30 Analyze

How many different three-digit numbers can you write using the digits 6, 2, and 0? Each digit may be used only once, and the digit 0 may not be used in the hundreds place. Label the numbers you write as even or odd.

Answer: 4 numbers: 620 (even), 602 (even), 260 (even), 206 (even)

Solution: The hundreds digit can only be 6 or 2 (not 0). Listing each once: with 6 first: 620, 602; with 2 first: 260, 206. That is 4 different numbers. All end in 0, 2, or 6, so all are even.
