Use this information to answer problems 1-3:
Freeman rode his bike 2 miles from his house to Didi's house. Together they rode 4 miles to the lake. Didi caught 8 fish. At 3:30 p.m. they rode back to Didi's house. Then Freeman rode home.
1. Altogether, how far did Freeman ride his bike?
▶ Review Answer & Explanation
Answer: 12 miles
Explanation:

We need to add up all the distances Freeman traveled:

  • From his house to Didi's house: $2$ miles
  • From Didi's house to the lake: $4$ miles
  • From the lake back to Didi's house: $4$ miles
  • From Didi's house back to his own house: $2$ miles

Total distance = $2 + 4 + 4 + 2 = 12$ miles.

2. It took Freeman an hour and a half to get home from the lake. At what time did he get home?
▶ Review Answer & Explanation
Answer: 5:00 p.m.
Explanation:

Freeman left the lake at 3:30 p.m. An hour and a half is equal to 1 hour and 30 minutes.

  • Add 1 hour to 3:30 p.m. $\rightarrow$ 4:30 p.m.
  • Add 30 minutes to 4:30 p.m. $\rightarrow$ 5:00 p.m.
3. Didi caught twice as many fish as Freeman. How many fish did Freeman catch?
▶ Review Answer & Explanation
Answer: 4 fish
Explanation:

We know from the information box that Didi caught $8$ fish. If Didi caught twice as many as Freeman, we divide Didi's amount by 2 to find Freeman's amount.
$8 \div 2 = 4$.

4. Shep bought some feed priced at $12.97. Tax was 91¢. He paid with a $20 bill. How much money should he get back?
▶ Review Answer & Explanation
Answer: $6.12
Explanation:

First, calculate the total cost by adding the price of the feed and the tax:
$\$12.97 + \$0.91 = \$13.88$

Next, subtract the total cost from the amount he paid ($\$20.00$) to find his change:
$\$20.00 - \$13.88 = \$6.12$

5. Estimate the sum of 4876 and 3149 by rounding each number to the nearest thousand before adding.
▶ Review Answer & Explanation
Answer: 8000
Explanation:

Round each number to the nearest thousand:

  • $4876$ rounds up to $5000$ (because the hundreds digit, 8, is 5 or greater).
  • $3149$ rounds down to $3000$ (because the hundreds digit, 1, is less than 5).

Sum of estimates: $5000 + 3000 = 8000$.

▶ Review Answer & Explanation
Answer: Angle D (or $\angle D$)
Explanation:

An angle of $110^\circ$ is an obtuse angle (greater than $90^\circ$). Looking at the quadrilateral:

  • Angles A and C on the left side appear to be roughly right angles ($90^\circ$).
  • Angle B (top right) is an acute angle (less than $90^\circ$) because the right side slants inward.
  • Angle D (bottom right) leans outward, making it the only obtuse angle ($> 90^\circ$) in the figure.
