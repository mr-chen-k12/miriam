Lesson 65 Solutions

1. The chef uses 3 eggs for each omelette. How many omelettes can he make with 24 eggs?
Answer: 8 omelettes

Explanation: Divide the total number of eggs by the number of eggs needed for one omelette.
\[ 24 \div 3 = 8 \]
2. Seventy-two young knights met peril in the forest. Twenty-seven fought bravely, but the others fled. How many young knights fled?
Answer: 45 knights

Explanation: Subtract the number of knights who fought from the total number of knights.
\[ 72 - 27 = 45 \]
3. Armando wore braces for 3 years. For how many months did he wear braces?
Answer: 36 months

Explanation: There are 12 months in a year. Multiply the number of years by 12.
\[ 3 \times 12 = 36 \]
4. Freddy bought a book for $12.89 and a folder for $3.95. Estimate how much money Freddy spent by rounding each amount to the nearest dollar before adding.
Answer: $17

Explanation: Round $12.89 to $13 (since .89 is closer to the next dollar) and $3.95 to $4. Then add the estimated amounts.
\[ \$13 + \$4 = \$17 \]
5. Fanga ran 28 miles in 4 hours. She ran at a rate of how many miles per hour?
Answer: 7 miles per hour

Explanation: Divide the total distance by the total time to find the unit rate.
\[ 28 \div 4 = 7 \]
6. Mallory bought ballet shoes for $18.95 and tap shoes for $42.85. How much did she pay for both pairs of shoes?
Answer: $61.80

Explanation: Add the exact cost of both items together.
\[ \$18.95 + \$42.85 = \$61.80 \]
7. What fraction of this hexagon is not shaded? (Image shows a hexagon divided into 6 equal triangles, 1 is shaded)
Answer: \( \frac{5}{6} \)

Explanation: The hexagon is divided into 6 equal parts. 1 part is shaded, meaning 5 parts are not shaded.
8. Each side of the hexagon in problem 7 is 1 cm long. What is its perimeter?
Answer: 6 cm

Explanation: A hexagon has 6 sides. If each side is 1 cm long, the total perimeter is:
\[ 6 \times 1\text{ cm} = 6\text{ cm} \]
9. Jim started walking early in the morning and did not stop until later that morning. How much time did Jim spend walking? (Clocks show start at 7:40 a.m. and stop at 10:20 a.m.)
Answer: 2 hours and 40 minutes

Explanation: The first clock shows 7:40. The second clock shows 10:20.
From 7:40 to 9:40 is 2 hours. From 9:40 to 10:20 is 40 minutes. Total time is 2 hours and 40 minutes.
10. Nigel drew a circle with a radius of 18 inches. What was the diameter of the circle?
Answer: 36 inches

Explanation: The diameter is twice the length of the radius.
\[ 2 \times 18 = 36 \]
11. How long is segment BC? (Ruler shows B at 5 cm and C at 11 cm)
Answer: 6 cm

Explanation: Subtract the starting point of the segment from the ending point.
\[ 11\text{ cm} - 5\text{ cm} = 6\text{ cm} \]
12. Which of these words names the answer to a division problem? A. product B. dividend C. divisor D. quotient
Answer: D. quotient

Explanation: A quotient is the result of division. (Product is for multiplication, dividend is the number being divided, and divisor is the number you divide by).
13. Compare: \( 27 + 3^2 \bigcirc 27 + \sqrt{9} \)
Answer: >

Explanation: Simplify both sides first.
Left side: \( 27 + 3^2 = 27 + 9 = 36 \)
Right side: \( 27 + \sqrt{9} = 27 + 3 = 30 \)
Since 36 is greater than 30, the correct symbol is >.

Answer: 840

27. Dooley awoke to a cold morning. He glanced out the window at the thermometer. What temperature is shown on this thermometer?
Answer: 12°F

Explanation: Look closely at the thermometer. Between 10°F and 20°F there are 5 intervals (4 tick marks). This means each tick mark represents 2 degrees (\(10 \div 5 = 2\)). The liquid level is exactly one tick mark above the 10°F mark.
\( 10 + 2 = 12^\circ\text{F} \)
28. (a) Three quarters are what fraction of a dollar?
(b) Three quarters are what percent of a dollar?
Answer: (a) \( \frac{3}{4} \)    (b) 75%

Explanation:
(a) There are 4 quarters in a dollar. Therefore, 3 quarters represent 3 out of 4 parts, or \( \frac{3}{4} \).
(b) A whole dollar is 100%. One quarter is 25%. Three quarters is \( 25\% \times 3 = 75\% \).
29. Draw a quadrilateral. A quadrilateral has how many vertices?
Answer: 4 vertices

Explanation: The prefix "quad-" means four. A quadrilateral is any 2D shape with 4 straight sides (like a square, rectangle, or rhombus), and it therefore has exactly 4 vertices (corners).
