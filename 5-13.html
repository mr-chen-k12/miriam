1. Peter packed 6 table-tennis balls in each package. There were 100 table-tennis balls to pack.
(a) How many packages could he fill?
(b) How many table-tennis balls were left over?
Answer: (a) 16 packages    (b) 4 left over
Explanation: Divide the total number of balls by the amount in each package: $100 \div 6$.
$100 \div 6 = 16$ with a remainder of $4$. He can fill 16 complete packages, leaving 4 balls leftover.
2. One hundred twenty-three is how much less than three hundred twenty-one?
Answer: 198
Explanation: Set up a subtraction problem: $321 - 123$.
$321 - 123 = 198$.
3. Brooke bought four giant pretzels priced at 59¢ each. The sales tax was 16¢. What was the total cost of the pretzels?
Answer: $2.52
Explanation: First, find the subtotal of the pretzels: $4 \times \$0.59 = \$2.36$.
Next, add the sales tax to the subtotal: $\$2.36 + \$0.16 = \$2.52$.
4. Twenty-four inches is how many feet?
Answer: 2 feet
Explanation: There are 12 inches in 1 foot. Divide total inches by 12: $24 \div 12 = 2$.
5. (a) Segment YZ is how many millimeters long?
(b) Segment YZ is how many centimeters long?
(Based on ruler image: Point Y is at 40 mm, Point Z is at 80 mm)
Answer: (a) 40 mm    (b) 4 cm
Explanation:
(a) To find the length, subtract the starting point from the ending point: $80\text{ mm} - 40\text{ mm} = 40\text{ mm}$.
(b) There are 10 millimeters in 1 centimeter. Divide by 10: $40 \div 10 = 4\text{ cm}$.
6. It is morning. What time will it be 5 hours 20 minutes from now?

Add 20 minutes: $12:05\text{ PM} + 20\text{ minutes} = 12:25\text{ PM}$.

20. $\$33.00 \div 6$
Answer: $5.50
Explanation:
$33 \div 6 = 5$ with a remainder of $3$. Bring down the $0$ to make $30$.
$30 \div 6 = 5$. Bring down the final $0$.
$0 \div 6 = 0$. Total: $\$5.50$.

25. $7 + 7 + N = 7^2$
Answer: N = 35
Explanation: First, solve the known parts. $7 + 7 = 14$ and $7^2 = 49$.
Rewrite the equation: $14 + N = 49$.
Subtract $14$ from both sides: $N = 49 - 14 \Rightarrow N = 35$.
26. $3 \times N = 6^2$
Answer: N = 12
Explanation: First, simplify $6^2 = 36$.
Rewrite the equation: $3N = 36$.
Divide both sides by 3: $N = 36 \div 3 \Rightarrow N = 12$.

28. The classroom was 40 feet long and 30 feet wide. How many 1-foot square floor tiles were needed to cover the floor?
Answer: 1200 square tiles
Explanation: You need to find the area of the room.
$\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{width}$.
$40\text{ ft} \times 30\text{ ft} = 1200\text{ square feet}$. Since each tile is 1 square foot, you need 1200 tiles.
29. In this polygon, which side appears to be parallel to side AD?

Answer: Side BC
