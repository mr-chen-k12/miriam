Practice Set 6 - 20 Questions

1. Ninety-six hours is how many days?

There are 24 hours in 1 day.

$$96 \div 24 = 4$$

Answer: 4 days

2. List the factors of 20.

Factors are numbers that divide into 20 evenly:

  • \(1 \times 20 = 20\)
  • \(2 \times 10 = 20\)
  • \(4 \times 5 = 20\)

Answer: 1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 20

3. Fifty-six chairs were placed equally into 7 rows. How many chairs were in each row?

Divide the total number of chairs by the number of rows:

$$56 \div 7 = 8$$

Answer: 8 chairs

4. Draw a rectangle and shade \(\frac{1}{4}\) of it.

Divide the rectangle into four equal sections and shade one of them.

Answer: Shade one of four equal sections.

5. Five hundred years equals how many centuries?

1 century = 100 years.

$$500 \div 100 = 5$$

Answer: 5 centuries

6. Find the sixth multiple of 7. Then subtract 10. What is the answer?

Step 1 (6th multiple of 7): \(6 \times 7 = 42\)

Step 2 (Subtract 10): \(42 - 10 = 32\)

Answer: 32

8. Segment AB is 8 cm long. Segment BC is 4 cm long. If B is between A and C, how long is segment AC?

The total length AC is the sum of AB and BC.

\(AB + BC = AC\)

\(8\text{ cm} + 4\text{ cm} = 12\text{ cm}\)

Answer: 12 cm

9. What is the perimeter of a square with a side length of 9 mm?

A square has 4 equal sides. Perimeter = \(4 \times \text{side}\).

\(P = 4 \times 9\)

\(P = 36\)

Answer: 36 mm
