Practice Math Test - Form B

1. Emma has five days to complete a 180-page reading assignment. If she wants to read the same number of pages each day, how many pages should Emma read daily?
Answer: 36 pages
Explanation: To find the daily page count, divide the total number of pages by the total number of days.
$$180 \div 5 = 36$$
2. When Emma has read four ninths of her assignment, what fraction will she still have left to read?
Answer: $\frac{5}{9}$
Explanation: The entire assignment represents 1 whole, which can be written as $\frac{9}{9}$. Subtract the part she has read from the whole.
$$\frac{9}{9} - \frac{4}{9} = \frac{5}{9}$$
Use the information given below to answer problems 3-5.
One hundred forty students voted for their favorite pet. Sixty-five voted for dogs. Cats received twenty fewer votes than dogs. The remaining students voted for fish.
3. How many students chose cats as their favorite pet?
Answer: 45 students
Explanation: The problem states cats received 20 fewer votes than dogs. Dogs received 65 votes.
$$65 - 20 = 45$$
4. How many students chose fish as their favorite pet?
Answer: 30 students
Explanation: First, find the total votes for dogs and cats: $65 + 45 = 110$.
Subtract this from the total number of students (140) to find the fish votes:
$$140 - 110 = 30$$
5. Which pet received the fewest votes?
Answer: Fish
Explanation: Compare the votes for each pet:
Dogs: 65
Cats: 45
Fish: 30
Fish have the lowest number of votes.
6. How many days equal 8 weeks?
Answer: 56 days
Explanation: There are 7 days in a single week. Multiply the number of weeks by 7.
$$8 \times 7 = 56$$
7. In the word MASSACHUSETTS, what fraction of the letters are Ss?
Answer: $\frac{4}{13}$
Explanation: First, count the total number of letters in MASSACHUSETTS, which is 13.
Next, count the number of 'S's in the word, which is 4.
The fraction is the number of 'S's over the total letters: $\frac{4}{13}$.
8. A scientist filled a container with 7 liters of liquid. Seven liters is how many milliliters?
Answer: 7000 milliliters
Explanation: There are 1,000 milliliters in 1 liter. Multiply the number of liters by 1,000.
$$7 \times 1000 = 7000$$
9. Carlos is on a 30-mile bike ride but stopped to rest after completing one fifth of the distance. How many miles did Carlos ride before resting?
Answer: 6 miles
Explanation: To find one fifth of 30, multiply 30 by $\frac{1}{5}$ (or divide 30 by 5).
$$30 \div 5 = 6$$
10. What is the perimeter of this triangle?
Answer: 52 mm
Explanation: The perimeter is the total distance around the outside of the shape. Add the lengths of all three sides together.
$$12 + 18 + 22 = 52$$
11. The length of $\overline{PQ}$ is 45 mm. The length of $\overline{PR}$ is 100 mm. What is the length of $\overline{QR}$?
Answer: 55 mm
Explanation: Point Q lies between P and R, meaning the length of PR is the sum of PQ and QR ($PR = PQ + QR$). To find QR, subtract the length of PQ from PR.
$$100 - 45 = 55$$
12. $\$75 - (\$20.50 + 80\text{¢})$
Answer: $53.70
Explanation: First, convert $80\text{¢}$ to dollars ($0.80). Then, solve the operations inside the parentheses by adding.
$$\$20.50 + \$0.80 = \$21.30$$
Next, subtract this sum from $\$75.00$.
$$\$75.00 - \$21.30 = \$53.70$$

16. $532 \div 6$   (Shown as $6 \overline{) 532}$)
Answer: 88 R 4
Explanation: Perform long division. 6 goes into 5 zero times. 6 goes into 53 eight times ($6 \times 8 = 48$). Subtract 48 from 53 to get 5. Bring down the 2 to make 52. 6 goes into 52 eight times ($6 \times 8 = 48$). Subtract 48 from 52 to leave a remainder of 4.
17. $815 \div 9$   (Shown as $9 \overline{) 815}$)
Answer: 90 R 5
Explanation: Perform long division. 9 goes into 81 exactly 9 times ($9 \times 9 = 81$). Subtract 81 from 81 to get 0. Bring down the 5. 9 goes into 5 zero times ($9 \times 0 = 0$). Subtract 0 from 5. The remainder is 5.
18. $8m = 320$
Answer: $m = 40$
Explanation: Isolate the variable $m$ by performing the inverse operation. Divide both sides by 8.
$$m = 320 \div 8$$ $$m = 40$$
19. Find the missing number $n$:
  310
  260
+   $n$
  800
Answer: $n = 230$
Explanation: First, add the two known numbers together.
$$310 + 260 = 570$$
Next, subtract that sum from the total to find the missing addend.
$$800 - 570 = 230$$
20. Compare: $3 \times 4^2 \bigcirc \sqrt{100} \times \sqrt{36}$
Answer: < (Less than)
Explanation: Evaluate both sides of the comparison.
Left side: Solve the exponent first ($4^2 = 4 \times 4 = 16$), then multiply.
$$3 \times 16 = 48$$
Right side: Find the square roots ($\sqrt{100} = 10$ and $\sqrt{36} = 6$), then multiply.
$$10 \times 6 = 60$$
Since $48 \lt 60$, the correct symbol is $\lt$.
