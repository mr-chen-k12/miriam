Question 1

Verified Solution

Step-by-Step Proof:

1. Identify intermediate baseline scale transformations:

$$1 \text{ gallon} = 4 \text{ quarts}$$ $$1 \text{ quart} = 4 \text{ cups} \implies 1 \text{ gallon} = 16 \text{ cups}$$

$$3 \text{ gallons} \times 16 \text{ cups} = 48 \text{ cups}$$
