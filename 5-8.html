Math Problem Solutions

1. Captain Bluebeard found 40 hidden chests. Each chest contained 250 silver coins. How many coins were there in all?
Answer: 10,000 coins

Explanation: To find the total number of coins, multiply the number of chests by the number of coins in each chest.
$250 \times 40 = 10000$.

2. The school play was 2 hours long. If it started at 2:15 p.m., at what time did it end?
Answer: 4:15 p.m.

Explanation: Add 2 hours to the starting time.
2:15 p.m. + 2 hours = 4:15 p.m.

3. Sarah is reading a 345-page novel. If she has finished page 189, how many pages does she still have to read?
Answer: 156 pages

Explanation: Subtract the number of pages she has read from the total number of pages.
$345 - 189 = 156$.

4. Alex, Ben, Carl, and Dan each scored one fourth of their basketball team's 48 points. They each scored how many points?
Answer: 12 points

Explanation: Divide the total number of points by 4.
$48 \div 4 = 12$.

5. Round 7,512 to the nearest thousand.
Answer: 8,000

Explanation: Look at the hundreds digit (5). Since it is 5 or greater, you round up. The thousands digit becomes 8, and the rest become zeros.

6. A book was priced at $12.50. The tax was $0.75. Emma paid the clerk with a $20 bill. How much money should Emma get back?
Answer: $6.75

Explanation: First, find the total cost by adding the price and the tax: $\$12.50 + \$0.75 = \$13.25$.
Then, subtract the total cost from the amount paid to find the change: $\$20.00 - \$13.25 = \$6.75$.

7. What fraction of the letters in the following word are A's?
MASSACHUSETTS
Answer: 2/13

Explanation: Count the total number of letters in the word, which is 13. Then count the number of "A"s, which is 2. The fraction is $\frac{2}{13}$.

Use the information below to answer problems 8-10:
In the first 10 games of the season, the Springfield baseball team won 7 games and lost 3 games. They won their next game by a score of 5 to 2. The team will play 15 games in all.
8. In the first eleven games of the season, how many games did Springfield win?
Answer: 8 games

Explanation: They won 7 games out of their first 10. They also won their 11th game.
$7 + 1 = 8$ total wins.

9. Springfield won its eleventh game by how many runs?
Answer: 3 runs

Explanation: The score of the eleventh game was 5 to 2. The difference is $5 - 2 = 3$.

10. What is the greatest number of games Springfield could win this season?
Answer: 12 games

Explanation: They have currently played 11 games and won 8 of them. Since the season has 15 games total, there are 4 games remaining ($15 - 11 = 4$). If they win all the remaining games, their maximum possible wins would be $8 + 4 = 12$.

11. Compare: $2 \times 6 \times 7 \bigcirc 7 \times 6 \times 2$
Answer: =

Explanation: Both sides equal 84. The commutative property of multiplication states that changing the order of the numbers does not change the product.

12. $X - 215 = 430$
Answer: X = 645

Explanation: To isolate $X$, add 215 to both sides of the equation.
$X = 430 + 215 = 645$.

13. $Y + 215 = 430$
Answer: Y = 215

Explanation: To isolate $Y$, subtract 215 from both sides of the equation.
$Y = 430 - 215 = 215$.

Answer: 6.05

Explanation: Perform the operation in the parentheses first:

19. $4 \longdiv{844}$
Answer: 211

Explanation: $8 \div 4 = 2$. $4 \div 4 = 1$. $4 \div 4 = 1$. The quotient is 211.

20. $6 \longdiv{735}$
Answer: 122 R 3

Explanation: $7 \div 6 = 1$ (R1). Bring down 3 to make 13. $13 \div 6 = 2$ (R1). Bring down 5 to make 15. $15 \div 6 = 2$ (R3). The quotient is 122 with a remainder of 3.

21. $5 \longdiv{3500}$
Answer: 700

Explanation: $35 \div 5 = 7$. Add the remaining two zeros to get 700.

22. $\$15.90 \div 5$
Answer: $3.18

Explanation: $15 \div 5 = 3$. Bring down 9. $9 \div 5 = 1$ (R4). Bring down 0 to make 40. $40 \div 5 = 8$. Keep the decimal place to get $\$3.18$.

23. $\$21.42 \div 7$
Answer: $3.06

Explanation: $21 \div 7 = 3$. Bring down 4. $4 \div 7 = 0$ (R4). Bring down 2 to make 42. $42 \div 7 = 6$. Keep the decimal place to get $\$3.06$.

24. $\$52.00 \div 8$
Answer: $6.50

Explanation: $52 \div 8 = 6$ (R4). Bring down 0 to make 40. $40 \div 8 = 5$. Bring down 0. $0 \div 8 = 0$. Keep the decimal place to get $\$6.50$.

25. $7^2 = 7 \times K$
Answer: K = 7

Explanation: $7^2$ is the same as $7 \times 7$, which is 49. The equation is $49 = 7 \times K$. Dividing both sides by 7 gives $K = 7$.

26. $4 \times Z = 4 \times 10^3$
Answer: Z = 1,000

Explanation: Calculate $10^3$, which is 1000. The equation simplifies to $4 \times Z = 4 \times 1000$. Divide both sides by 4 to find $Z = 1000$.

27. One fifth of the students in the class wear glasses. What percent of the students wear glasses?
Answer: 20%

Explanation: The fraction $\frac{1}{5}$ represents one fifth. As a percentage, one fifth is equal to $20\%$.

28. What is the perimeter of this rectangle in millimeters?

Answer: 100 mm

Explanation: First, find the perimeter in centimeters. The sides are 3 cm, 2 cm, 3 cm, and 2 cm.
Perimeter = $3 + 2 + 3 + 2 = 10$ cm.
Since there are 10 millimeters in a centimeter, multiply by 10.
$10 \text{ cm} \times 10 = 100 \text{ mm}$.

29. Draw a rectangle that is similar to the rectangle in problem 28 and whose sides are three times as long. What is the perimeter in centimeters of the rectangle you drew?
Answer: 30 cm

Explanation: If the original sides were 3 cm and 2 cm, the new rectangle has sides three times as long: 9 cm and 6 cm.
Perimeter = $9 + 6 + 9 + 6 = 30$ cm.

30. A figure skater spun around two and a half times in the air. How many degrees did the skater turn?
Answer: 900°

Explanation: One full spin (a circle) is 360 degrees. If the skater spun 2.5 times, multiply by 2.5.
$2.5 \times 360 = 900$ degrees.
