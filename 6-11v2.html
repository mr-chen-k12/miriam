Variants for Question 4 (Equal Distribution with Remainders)

Q4.1 Variant 1
Forty-five books were arranged on 7 shelves as evenly as possible.
a. How many shelves had exactly 6 books?
b. How many shelves had 7 books?
Divide total books by shelves: $45 \div 7 = 6 \text{ remainder } 3$.
Every shelf gets at least 6 books. The 3 remaining books are given to 3 separate shelves, making them have 7 books. The rest ($7 - 3 = 4$) keep exactly 6 books.
Answer: a. 4 shelves  |  b. 3 shelves
Q4.2 Variant 2
Twenty-nine students were split into 4 team groups as evenly as possible.
a. How many teams had exactly 7 students?
b. How many teams had 8 students?
Divide students by teams: $29 \div 4 = 7 \text{ remainder } 1$.
Every team gets 7 students. The remaining 1 student joins one team, boosting its size to 8. The other 3 teams stay at 7.
Answer: a. 3 teams  |  b. 1 team
Q4.3 Variant 3
Fifty-eight cookies were packed into 8 gift bags as evenly as possible.
a. How many bags had exactly 7 cookies?
b. How many bags had 8 cookies?
Divide cookies by bags: $58 \div 8 = 7 \text{ remainder } 2$.
Every bag gets 7 cookies. 2 bags get an extra cookie to make 8. The remaining $8 - 2 = 6$ bags have exactly 7.
Answer: a. 6 bags  |  b. 2 bags

Variants for Question 11 (Fraction of a Group)

Q11.1 Variant 1
There are 30 apples in a large basket. Three fifths ($\frac{3}{5}$) of them are red. How many apples are red?
1. Find $\frac{1}{5}$ of 30: $30 \div 5 = 6$.
2. Multiply by the numerator (3): $6 \times 3 = 18$.
Answer: 18 red apples
Q11.2 Variant 2
An art gallery has 36 paintings on display. Three fourths ($\frac{3}{4}$) of them are landscape scenes. How many paintings are landscape scenes?
1. Find $\frac{1}{4}$ of 36: $36 \div 4 = 9$.
2. Multiply by the numerator (3): $9 \times 3 = 27$.
Answer: 27 landscape paintings
Q11.3 Variant 3
There are 42 cars parked in a local lot. Two sevenths ($\frac{2}{7}$) of them are completely electric. How many cars are electric?
1. Find $\frac{1}{7}$ of 42: $42 \div 7 = 6$.
2. Multiply by the numerator (2): $6 \times 2 = 12$.
Answer: 12 electric cars

Variants for Question 12 (Rate Word Problems)

Q12.1 Variant 1
Maya typed 432 words in 8 minutes. If she typed at a steady speed, how many words did she type in one minute?
Divide total words by total minutes to find the rate per minute:
$$432 \div 8 = 54$$
Answer: 54 words per minute
Q12.2 Variant 2
A baker managed to make 252 donuts over a 6-hour shift. On average, how many donuts did the baker make per hour?
Divide total donuts by total hours:
$$252 \div 6 = 42$$
Answer: 42 donuts per hour
Q12.3 Variant 3
A cross-country train traveled 585 miles in 9 hours at a constant speed. How many miles did it travel in one single hour?
Divide total miles by total hours:
$$585 \div 9 = 65$$
Answer: 65 miles per hour

Variants for Question 20 (Missing Addend Equation Strings)

Q20.1 Variant 1
Find the missing addend $x$:
$$3 + 4 + 7 + 8 + x + 6 + 1 + 4 = 42$$
1. Combine all known values: $3 + 4 + 7 + 8 + 6 + 1 + 4 = 33$.
2. Set up equation: $33 + x = 42$.
3. Subtract to solve: $x = 42 - 33 = 9$.
Answer: x = 9
Q20.2 Variant 2
Find the missing addend $m$:
$$5 + 9 + 2 + 6 + m + 7 + 3 + 4 + 2 = 55$$
1. Combine all known values: $5 + 9 + 2 + 6 + 7 + 3 + 4 + 2 = 38$.
2. Set up equation: $38 + m = 55$.
3. Subtract to solve: $m = 55 - 38 = 17$.
Answer: m = 17
Q20.3 Variant 3
Find the missing addend $y$:
$$1 + 8 + 5 + 7 + y + 3 + 6 + 2 = 39$$
1. Combine all known values: $1 + 8 + 5 + 7 + 3 + 6 + 2 = 32$.
2. Set up equation: $32 + y = 39$.
3. Subtract to solve: $y = 39 - 32 = 7$.
Answer: y = 7
