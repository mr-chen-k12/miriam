Math Practice Problems (1-30)

Problem 1
A local library has four hundred twenty-one science fiction books, eight hundred fifteen mystery books, and one thousand thirty-four biographies. How many of these books are there in all?
Answer:
2,270 books
Equation: $421 + 815 + 1034 = 2270$
Problem 2
A baker uses 1 bag of flour to bake 6 cakes. How many bags of flour would he need to bake 3 dozen cakes?
Answer:
6 bags
1 dozen = 12, so 3 dozen = 36 cakes. Equation: $36 \div 6 = 6$ bags.

Problem 4
Seventy-five percent of an hour is how many minutes?
Answer:
45 minutes
An hour has 60 minutes. 75% is three-quarters ($\frac{3}{4}$). $60 \times 0.75 = 45$.
Problem 5
A hot air balloon was flying at an altitude of 4,120 feet. It descended (dropped) 875 feet. What is a reasonable estimate of the balloon's new altitude? Explain your thinking.
Answer:
Approx. 3,200 feet
Estimate by rounding the numbers before subtracting: $4100 - 900 = 3200$ feet. (The exact answer is 3,245).
Problem 6
Which of these numbers is not a multiple of 3?
A 12   B 15   C 23   D 27
Answer:
C. 23
Multiples of 3 can be divided by 3 evenly ($3 \times 4 = 12$, $3 \times 5 = 15$, $3 \times 9 = 27$). 23 cannot.
Problem 7
Write the time "half past four in the afternoon" in digital form.
Answer:
4:30 p.m.
"Half past" means 30 minutes after the hour. Afternoon indicates p.m.
Problem 8
Solve for n: $4n = 4 \times 7$
Answer:
$n = 7$
Simplify the right side: $4 \times 7 = 28$. So, $4n = 28$. Divide by 4 to find $n = 7$. Because both sides are multiplied by 4, $n$ must equal 7.
Problem 9
The product of 8 and 4 is how much greater than the sum of 8 and 4?
Answer:
20
Product: $8 \times 4 = 32$. Sum: $8 + 4 = 12$. Difference: $32 - 12 = 20$.
Problem 10
What is the length of segment MN?
Answer:
5 cm
Point M is at 2 cm and Point N is at 7 cm. $7 - 2 = 5$.
Problem 11
Compare: $(24 \div 6) \div 2 \bigcirc 24 \div (6 \div 2)$
Answer:
$<$ (Less than)
Left side: $(24 \div 6) \div 2 = 4 \div 2 = 2$. Right side: $24 \div (6 \div 2) = 24 \div 3 = 8$. Since $2 < 8$, the correct symbol is $<$.

Problem 21
a. A quarter is what fraction of a dollar?
b. A quarter is what percent of a dollar?
Answer:
a. $\frac{1}{4}$ (or $\frac{25}{100}$)
b. $25\%$
A quarter is 25 cents out of 100 cents. As a fraction, this simplifies to $\frac{1}{4}$. As a percentage, it is $25\%$.
Problem 22
Perfect squares of prime numbers have exactly an odd number of factors (specifically, three). The number 49 is a perfect square. What are the three factors of 49?
Answer:
1, 7, and 49
The numbers that multiply to give 49 are $1 \times 49$ and $7 \times 7$.
Problem 23
Compare: $20\% \bigcirc \frac{1}{4}$
Answer:
$<$ (Less than)
$\frac{1}{4}$ is equivalent to $0.25$ or $25\%$. Therefore, $20\% < 25\%$.
Problem 24
Refer to parallelogram WXYZ to answer parts a and b.
a. What type of angle are angles W and Y?
b. What type of angle are angles X and Z?
Answer:
a. Acute
b. Obtuse
Angles W and Y are narrower than 90 degrees (acute). Angles X and Z are wider than 90 degrees (obtuse).
Problem 25
The rectangular patio is 6 yards long and 3 yards wide.

a. How many yards of fencing are needed to go around the patio?
b. How many square yards of brick are needed to cover the patio floor?
Answer:
a. 18 yards
b. 18 square yards
a. Fencing goes around the perimeter: $P = 2(6 + 3) = 18$ yards.
b. Bricks cover the area: $A = 6 \times 3 = 18$ square yards.
Problem 26
If $n + 8 = 20$, then which of these equations is not true?
A. $n + 2 = 14$
B. $n - 2 = 10$
C. $n + 5 = 15$
D. $n - 5 = 7$
Answer:
C. $n + 5 = 15$
First, find $n$: $n = 20 - 8 = 12$. If $n = 12$, let's test C: $12 + 5 = 17$, not 15. Therefore, equation C is false.
Problem 27
(a) Compare: $12 \div (6 \div 2) \bigcirc (12 \div 6) \div 2$
(b) Does the associative property apply to division based on this problem?
Answer:
a. $>$ (Greater than)
b. No
a. Left side: $12 \div (6 \div 2) = 12 \div 3 = 4$. Right side: $(12 \div 6) \div 2 = 2 \div 2 = 1$. Since $4 > 1$, use $>$.
b. Because moving the parenthesis changed the answer, the associative property does NOT apply to division.
Problem 28
a. Seven pennies are what fraction of a dollar?
b. Seven pennies are what percent of a dollar?
c. Write the value of seven pennies as a decimal part of a dollar.
Answer:
a. $\frac{7}{100}$
b. $7\%$
c. $\$0.07$
A dollar equals 100 pennies, so 7 pennies is $\frac{7}{100}$, which translates directly to $7\%$ and $0.07$.
Problem 29
At the store, Marcus bought a notebook for $\$8.15$, some pens for $\$2.90$, and a calculator for $\$4.85$. Estimate Marcus's total bill.
Answer:
$\$16.00$
Round each item to the nearest dollar: $\$8.00 + \$3.00 + \$5.00 = \$16.00$.
Problem 30
a. Name a segment that is parallel to CD.
b. Name a segment that is perpendicular to HD.
Answer:
a. AB, EF, or HG
b. EH, FG, CD, or AD
a. Parallel segments run in the same direction. AB, EF, and HG run along the same horizontal axis as CD.
b. Perpendicular segments meet at a 90-degree corner. Segments that meet segment HD at a corner (like EH, FG, CD, or AD) are perpendicular to it.
