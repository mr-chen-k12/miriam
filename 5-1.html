Math Worksheet Solutions

Decimal Numbers to Thousandths (Lesson 84)

1. If it is not a leap year, what is the total number of days in January, February, and March?
Answer: 90 days
Explanation:
January has 31 days.
February has 28 days (in a non-leap year).
March has 31 days.
$31 + 28 + 31 = 90$
2. The shoemaker's wife made each of the 12 children a pair of pants and 2 shirts. How many pieces of clothing did she make?
Answer: 36 pieces of clothing
Explanation:
Each child receives 1 pair of pants + 2 shirts = 3 pieces of clothing per child.
There are 12 children.
$12 \times 3 = 36$
3. John did seven more chin-ups than Paloma did. If John did eighteen chin-ups, how many chin-ups did Paloma do?
Answer: 11 chin-ups
Explanation:
Let $P$ be the number of chin-ups Paloma did.
John's chin-ups = $P + 7$
$18 = P + 7$
$P = 18 - 7 = 11$
4. Kadeeja drove 200 miles on 8 gallons of gas. Her car averaged how many miles on each gallon of gas?
Answer: 25 miles per gallon
Explanation:
Divide the total miles by the total gallons of gas to find the average.
$200 \div 8 = 25$
5. Melinda paid the clerk $20.00 for a book that was priced at $8.95. The tax was 54¢. How much money should she get back?
Answer: $10.51
Explanation:
First, calculate the total cost: $\$8.95 + \$0.54 = \$9.49$.
Subtract the total cost from the amount given to the clerk: $\$20.00 - \$9.49 = \$10.51$.
6. The tally for 8 is |||| |||. What is the tally for 9?
Answer: |||| ||||
Explanation:
Tally marks are grouped in fives. The first five is represented by four vertical lines with a diagonal line striking through them. To make 9, you add four individual vertical lines after the group of five.
7. If each side of an octagon is 1 centimeter long, what is the octagon's perimeter in millimeters?
Answer: 80 millimeters
Explanation:
An octagon has 8 sides. Perimeter in cm = $8 \times 1\text{ cm} = 8\text{ cm}$.
Since $1\text{ cm} = 10\text{ mm}$, multiply by 10 to convert: $8 \times 10 = 80\text{ mm}$.
8. One third of the 18 marbles were cat's-eyes. How many of the marbles were cat's-eyes? Draw a picture to help you solve the problem.
Answer: 6 marbles
Explanation:
To find one third of 18, divide 18 by 3.
$18 \div 3 = 6$.
The picture below shows 18 marbles in 3 groups of 6. One group (one third) is shaded green.
9. Robert picked 46 peaches in 1 day. At that rate, how many peaches could he pick in 6 days?
Answer: 276 peaches
Explanation:
Multiply the number of peaches picked per day by the number of days.
$46 \times 6 = 276$
10. Mary picked 3640 peaches in 7 days. She picked an average of how many peaches each day?
Answer: 520 peaches
Explanation:
Divide the total number of peaches by the number of days to find the daily average.
$3640 \div 7 = 520$
11. In a feat of strength, Jack did 1000 push-ups, 129 of them with one arm. What fraction of the 1000 push-ups did Jack do with one arm?
Answer: $\frac{129}{1000}$
Explanation:
The part is 129 and the whole is 1000. Put the part over the whole to make the fraction.
12. Write the answer to problem 11 as a decimal number. Then use words to name the number.
Answer: 0.129; one hundred twenty-nine thousandths
Explanation:
To write $\frac{129}{1000}$ as a decimal, the last digit (9) goes into the thousandths place: $0.129$.
In words, this is read as the numerator value followed by the place value of the last decimal digit: "one hundred twenty-nine thousandths".
13. $4.56 - (2.3 + 1.75)$
Answer: 0.51
Explanation:
According to the order of operations, solve inside the parentheses first:
$2.30 + 1.75 = 4.05$
Then subtract this sum from 4.56:
$4.56 - 4.05 = 0.51$
14. $\sqrt{36} + N = 7 \times 8$
Answer: $N = 50$
Explanation:
First, simplify both sides of the equation:
$\sqrt{36} = 6$ and $7 \times 8 = 56$
The equation becomes: $6 + N = 56$
Subtract 6 from both sides to find N:
$N = 56 - 6 = 50$
15. $3 \times 6 \times 3^2$
Answer: 162
Explanation:
First evaluate the exponent: $3^2 = 9$
Now multiply from left to right:
$3 \times 6 = 18$
$18 \times 9 = 162$
16. $462 \times \sqrt{9}$
Answer: 1386
Explanation:
First calculate the square root: $\sqrt{9} = 3$
Then multiply:
$462 \times 3 = 1386$
17. $7^2 - \sqrt{49}$
Answer: 42
Explanation:
Evaluate the exponent and the square root:
$7^2 = 49$
$\sqrt{49} = 7$
Subtract: $49 - 7 = 42$
18. $36 \times 50$
Answer: 1800
Explanation:
$36 \times 5 = 180$
Add the zero from the 50: $1800$
19. $\$4.76 \times 7$
Answer: $33.32
Explanation:
$4.76 \times 7 = 33.32$
Carry over the decimal two places, yielding $\$33.32$.
20. Column Addition:
4 + 3 + 2 + 7 + 6 + 8 + N = 47
Answer: $N = 17$
Explanation:
First, sum the known numbers:
$4 + 3 + 2 + 7 + 6 + 8 = 30$
Now set up the equation: $30 + N = 47$
Subtract 30 from both sides: $N = 47 - 30 = 17$.
21. $\frac{524}{4}$
Answer: 131
Explanation:
Perform long division or mental division:
$5 \div 4 = 1$ (R1)
$12 \div 4 = 3$
$4 \div 4 = 1$
Result: 131.
22. $6\overline{)4200}$
Answer: 700
Explanation:
$42 \div 6 = 7$
Carry over the two zeros to get 700.
23. $5\overline{)\$26.30}$
Answer: $5.26
Explanation:
$26 \div 5 = 5$ (R1)
Bring down the 3 to make 13. $13 \div 5 = 2$ (R3)
Bring down the 0 to make 30. $30 \div 5 = 6$
Bring the decimal point straight up to get $\$5.26$.
24. $\$3.70 \div 2$
Answer: $1.85
Explanation:
Half of $\$3.00$ is $\$1.50$
Half of $70¢$ is $35¢$
$\$1.50 + \$0.35 = \$1.85$.
25. $786 \div 3$
Answer: 262
Explanation:
$7 \div 3 = 2$ (R1)
Bring down 8 to make 18. $18 \div 3 = 6$
Bring down 6. $6 \div 3 = 2$
Result: 262.
26. $4902 \div 7$
Answer: 700 R2 (or $700\frac{2}{7}$)
Explanation:
$49 \div 7 = 7$
$0 \div 7 = 0$
$2 \div 7 = 0$ with a remainder of 2.
Quotient is 700 with a remainder of 2.
27. Write 0.321 as a fraction.
Answer: $\frac{321}{1000}$
Explanation:
The decimal 0.321 extends to the thousandths place. The numerator is the digits (321) and the denominator is 1000.
28. Find the perimeter and area of this square. (A square with side length 3 yards)
Answer: Perimeter = 12 yards, Area = 9 square yards
Explanation:
A square has 4 equal sides.
Perimeter = $4 \times \text{side} = 4 \times 3\text{ yards} = 12\text{ yards}$.
Area = $\text{side} \times \text{side} = 3\text{ yards} \times 3\text{ yards} = 9\text{ square yards}$.
29. Which transformations would move figure ABCD to position WXYZ?
Answer: Translation (Slide)
Explanation:
The orientation and size of the figure remain exactly the same; it is simply shifted horizontally to the right. Moving a shape without rotating or flipping it is called a translation (or a slide).
30. Which angle in this figure looks like it measures about $45^\circ$?
Answer: Angle C
Explanation:
- Angle A and Angle B appear to be $90^\circ$ right angles.
- Angle D is an obtuse angle (greater than $90^\circ$).
- Angle C is an acute angle and is roughly half of a right angle, making it approximately $45^\circ$.
