1. Jason finished his homework 45 minutes before the time shown on the clock. If the clock shows 5:15, what time did Jason finish his homework?
Answer: B (4:30)
If the clock is at 5:15, subtract 15 minutes to get to 5:00. You still need to subtract 30 more minutes. 5:00 minus 30 minutes is 4:30.
2. On a centimeter ruler, point A is at the 3 cm mark and point B is at the 11 cm mark. What is the length of the segment AB?
Answer: C (8 cm)
To find the length, subtract the starting point from the ending point: \( 11 - 3 = 8 \).
3. A teacher has 22 pencils and wants to put them into boxes that hold 5 pencils each. How many full boxes can be made, and how many pencils will be left over?
Answer: B (4 boxes, 2 left over)
Divide 22 by 5. \( 5 \times 4 = 20 \). Since \( 22 - 20 = 2 \), the answer is 4 with a remainder of 2.
4. Solve the expression using the correct order of operations:
\( (12 - 2) \div \sqrt{25} \)
Answer: A (2)
1. Parentheses: \( 12 - 2 = 10 \)
2. Roots: \( \sqrt{25} = 5 \)
3. Division: \( 10 \div 5 = 2 \)
5. How many different 3-digit numbers can you make using the digits 1, 5, and 9 exactly once in each number?
Answer: C (6)
You can list them to check: 159, 195, 519, 591, 915, 951. That is 6 different numbers.
