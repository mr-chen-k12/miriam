Lesson Practice

5 Represent

To what mixed number is the arrow pointing?

Answer: \(11\frac{1}{2}\)

Solution: Each whole unit on the number line is divided into 4 equal parts, so each small step is \(\frac{1}{4}\). The arrow is 2 quarter-steps past 11: \(11 + \frac{2}{4} = 11\frac{2}{4} = 11\frac{1}{2}\).

6 Represent

Draw a rectangle whose length is 5 cm and whose width is 3 cm. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?

Answer: 16 cm

Solution: Perimeter \(= 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width}) = 2 \times (5 + 3) = 2 \times 8 = 16\) cm. (Equivalently, \(5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16\) cm.)

7

What mixed number is shown by the shaded rectangles?

Answer: \(5\frac{1}{2}\)

Solution: Five whole rectangles are fully shaded, and one more rectangle is half shaded (split on the diagonal). \(5 + \frac{1}{2} = 5\frac{1}{2}\).

8 Represent

Use words to write \(12\frac{3}{10}\).

Answer: twelve and three tenths

Solution: Write the whole-number part in words ("twelve"), then "and" for the fraction bar/decimal point, then the fraction part in words ("three tenths").

9 Represent

Write 7026 in expanded form. Then use words to write the number.

Answer: Expanded form: \(7000 + 20 + 6\). In words: seven thousand, twenty-six.

Solution: 7026 has 7 thousands, 0 hundreds, 2 tens, and 6 ones, so \(7026 = 7000 + 0 + 20 + 6 = 7000 + 20 + 6\). In words, read the thousands part then the rest: "seven thousand, twenty-six."

10

On the morning of an important game, Gail woke up at the time shown on the clock. She wanted to wake up 2 hours 35 minutes later. What time did Gail want to wake up?

Answer: 6:00

Solution: The clock shows 3:25 (hour hand between 3 and 4, minute hand on the 5). Add 2 hours: \(3{:}25 + 2\text{ h} = 5{:}25\). Add 35 minutes: \(5{:}25 + 35\text{ min} = 6{:}00\).

11

a. Three quarters are what fraction of a dollar?

b. Write the value of three quarters using a dollar sign and a decimal point.

Answer: a. \(\frac{3}{4}\) of a dollar   b. $0.75

Solution: A quarter is \(\frac{1}{4}\) of a dollar, so three quarters are \(3 \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\) of a dollar. Since one quarter = $0.25, three quarters = \(3 \times \$0.25 = \$0.75\).

23

a. Conclude What are the next three numbers in this counting sequence?

\[\ldots, 450,\ 460,\ 470,\ 480,\ \underline{\phantom{000}},\ \underline{\phantom{000}},\ \underline{\phantom{000}},\ \ldots\]

b. Generalize What is one rule for this sequence?

Answer: a. 490, 500, 510   b. Add 10 to each number (count by tens).

Solution: Each number is 10 more than the one before it: \(460 - 450 = 10\), \(470 - 460 = 10\), \(480 - 470 = 10\). Continuing: \(480 + 10 = 490\), \(490 + 10 = 500\), \(500 + 10 = 510\). One rule: "add 10 each time" (count by 10s).

24

If the diameter of a circle is one foot, then the radius of the circle is how many inches?

Answer: 6 inches

Solution: One foot = 12 inches, so the diameter is 12 inches. The radius is half the diameter: \(12 \div 2 = 6\) inches.

26 Multiple Choice

One yard does not equal which of the following?

  1. 36 in.
  2. 3 ft
  3. 1 m
  4. 2 ft + 12 in.

Answer: C — 1 m

Solution: One yard = 36 inches = 3 feet. Check each choice: A. 36 in. = 1 yd ✓; B. 3 ft = 1 yd ✓; D. 2 ft + 12 in. = 24 in. + 12 in. = 36 in. = 1 yd ✓. But C. 1 meter ≈ 39 inches, which is not equal to 1 yard.

28 Justify

An adult cheetah weighs about 130 pounds. An adult mountain lion weighs about 170 pounds. A student estimates that a mountain lion weighs about twice as much as a cheetah. Is the estimate reasonable? Explain why or why not.

Answer: No, the estimate is not reasonable.

Solution: Twice the cheetah's weight is \(2 \times 130 = 260\) pounds, but a mountain lion weighs only about 170 pounds — 90 pounds less than double. The mountain lion weighs only about 40 pounds more than the cheetah, not twice as much, so the estimate is not reasonable.

29

For a very short distance, a world-class sprinter can run at a speed of about 23 miles per hour. Round that speed to the nearest ten miles per hour.

Answer: 20 miles per hour

Solution: 23 is between 20 and 30. The ones digit is 3, which is less than 5, so round down to 20.

30

In Barrow, Alaska, the average maximum temperature in July is 47°F. The average minimum temperature is 34°F. How many degrees cooler is a temperature of 34°F compared to a temperature of 47°F?

Answer: 13°F cooler

Solution: Subtract the lower temperature from the higher one: \(47 - 34 = 13\). So 34°F is 13 degrees cooler than 47°F.

Written Practice

Formulate — Write and solve equations for problems 1 and 2.

1

The Pearl River in Mississippi is 411 miles long. The San Juan River in Colorado is 360 miles long. How many miles longer is the Pearl River?

Answer: 51 miles longer

Solution: Let \(m\) = miles longer. Equation: \(360 + m = 411\), so \(m = 411 - 360 = 51\).

2

If the length of the Sabine River in Texas were 50 miles longer, it would be the same length as the Wisconsin River. The Wisconsin River is 430 miles long. How long is the Sabine River?

Answer: 380 miles

Solution: Let \(s\) = length of the Sabine River. Equation: \(s + 50 = 430\), so \(s = 430 - 50 = 380\). Check: \(380 + 50 = 430\) ✓

4 Explain

Leah wants to put square floor tiles that measure one foot on each side in a room that is 9 feet long and 9 feet wide. How many floor tiles will Leah need? Is your answer reasonable? Explain why.

Answer: 81 tiles. Yes, this is reasonable.

Solution: Each tile covers 1 square foot. The floor is a 9 ft × 9 ft square, so the number of tiles is \(9 \times 9 = 81\). This is reasonable because the floor's area is \(9 \times 9 = 81\) square feet, and each 1-foot tile covers exactly 1 square foot — 81 tiles cover the floor exactly with none left over.
