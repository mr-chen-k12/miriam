2 Brain Science: Why Fast Scrolling Shrinks Focus
45 Seconds
Key Points to Remember: • Fast-moving videos train our brains to expect instant rewards.
• This makes long activities like reading books, doing homework, or practicing hobbies feel harder.
Quick Pick:
"When we swipe fast, our brain gets quick bursts of excitement every 15 seconds. Scientists call this 'popcorn brain'! Over time, it makes patient activities—like reading a chapter book—feel boring or slow. But our brains need patience to build real superpower focus!"
3 Solution: The "Stop & Swap" 10-Minute Rule
45 Seconds
Key Points to Remember: • Set a strict 10-minute timer for video watching.
• When the timer rings, swap scrolling for a hands-on real-world activity immediately.
Quick Pick:
"So how can we win against the endless scroll? I use a super simple trick called Stop & Swap. By setting a 10-minute timer, I enjoy a quick laugh without getting trapped in a hours-long video spiral!"
