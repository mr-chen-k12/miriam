Practice Set 3 - 20 Questions

1. Forty-eight hours is how many days?

There are 24 hours in 1 day.

$$48 \div 24 = 2$$

Answer: 2 days

2. List the factors of 18.

Factors are numbers that divide into 18 evenly:

  • \(1 \times 18 = 18\)
  • \(2 \times 9 = 18\)
  • \(3 \times 6 = 18\)

Answer: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18

3. Fifty pencils were packed equally into 5 boxes. How many pencils were in each box?

Divide the total number of pencils by the number of boxes:

$$50 \div 5 = 10$$

Answer: 10 pencils

4. Draw a rectangle and shade \(\frac{1}{3}\) of it.

Divide the rectangle into three equal sections and shade one of them.

Answer: Shade one of three equal sections.

5. Three hundred years equals how many centuries?

1 century = 100 years.

$$300 \div 100 = 3$$

Answer: 3 centuries

6. Find the seventh multiple of 6. Then add 8. What is the answer?

Step 1 (7th multiple of 6): \(7 \times 6 = 42\)

Step 2 (Add 8): \(42 + 8 = 50\)

Answer: 50

7. Compare: \(\frac{1}{2} \bigcirc 50\%\)

\(\frac{1}{2}\) is exactly equal to 50%.

Answer: =

8. Segment XY is 6 cm long. Segment YZ is 9 cm long. If Y is between X and Z, how long is segment XZ?

The total length XZ is the sum of XY and YZ.

\(XY + YZ = XZ\)

\(6\text{ cm} + 9\text{ cm} = 15\text{ cm}\)

Answer: 15 cm

9. What is the perimeter of a square with a side length of 12 cm?

A square has 4 equal sides. Perimeter = \(4 \times \text{side}\).

\(P = 4 \times 12\)

\(P = 48\)

Answer: 48 cm

10. Round 3,499 to the nearest thousand.

The thousands digit is 3. Look at the hundreds digit (4). Since 4 is less than 5, we round down.

Answer: 3,000
