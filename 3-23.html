Cumulative Test 13A

1. Carl took 11 big steps to measure the length of the room. If each step was one yard, then the length of the room was how many feet?
Answer: 33 feet
Explanation: First, find the total length in yards: 11 steps × 1 yard/step = 11 yards. Since there are 3 feet in every yard, multiply the number of yards by 3: \( 11 \times 3 = 33 \). Therefore, the room is 33 feet long.
2. Every fifth student sings in the choir. There are two hundred fifty-five students in all. How many students sing in the choir?
Answer: 51 students
Explanation: "Every fifth student" means \(\frac{1}{5}\) of the total students. To find how many students sing, divide the total number of students by 5: \( 255 \div 5 = 51 \).
3. Two fifths of the students walked to school. What fraction of the students did not walk to school?
Answer: \(\frac{3}{5}\)
Explanation: The total number of students represents a whole, or \(\frac{5}{5}\). Subtract the fraction that walked to find the fraction that did not walk: \(\frac{5}{5} - \frac{2}{5} = \frac{3}{5}\).
4. Compare: 25% of 24 \(\bigcirc\) \(3 \times 2\)
Answer: = (Equal)
Explanation: First, evaluate the left side: 25% is the same as \(\frac{1}{4}\). One-fourth of 24 is \(24 \div 4 = 6\). Next, evaluate the right side: \(3 \times 2 = 6\). Since both sides equal 6, the correct comparison is \(6 = 6\).
5. What is the area of this square?
Answer: 144 sq mm (or \(144 \text{ mm}^2\))
Explanation: The area of a square is calculated by multiplying its side length by itself (\(Area = side \times side\)). Here, \(12 \text{ mm} \times 12 \text{ mm} = 144 \text{ sq mm}\).
6. Estimate the product of 79 and 9.
Answer: 720 (or 800)
Explanation: To estimate, round the numbers to make multiplication easier. Round 79 up to 80. You can leave 9 as is: \(80 \times 9 = 720\). Alternatively, rounding 9 to 10 gives \(80 \times 10 = 800\). Both are acceptable estimates, though 720 is closer to the exact answer (711).
7. The hiker could hike 36 miles in one day. How far could she hike in 9 days?
Answer: 324 miles
Explanation: Multiply the daily distance by the number of days: \(36 \text{ miles/day} \times 9 \text{ days} = 324 \text{ miles}\).
8. Name the polygon.
Answer: Pentagon
Explanation: Count the number of sides on the shape. A polygon with 5 sides is called a pentagon.
9. If each side of the polygon in problem 8 measures 3 units, then what is its perimeter?
Answer: 15 units
Explanation: The perimeter is the total distance around the outside of a shape. Since it is a pentagon (5 sides) and each side is 3 units, multiply: \(5 \text{ sides} \times 3 \text{ units/side} = 15 \text{ units}\).
10. Compare. Draw and shade two congruent squares to show the comparison. \(\frac{1}{2} \bigcirc \frac{5}{8}\)
Answer: < (Less than)
Explanation: To compare them numerically, convert \(\frac{1}{2}\) to an equivalent fraction with a denominator of 8. \(\frac{1}{2} = \frac{4}{8}\). Since \(4 < 5\), we know \(\frac{4}{8} < \frac{5}{8}\), so \(\frac{1}{2} < \frac{5}{8}\). The visual drawing confirms that slightly more of the second square is shaded.
11. If 63% of the students were boys, then what percent of the students were girls?
Answer: 37%
Explanation: The total number of students represents 100%. Subtract the percentage of boys to find the percentage of girls: \(100\% - 63\% = 37\%\).

13. \(\$11 - 9\text{¢}\)
Answer: $10.91
Explanation: First, rewrite 9 cents in dollar format as $0.09. Then subtract it from $11.00. \( \$11.00 - \$0.09 = \$10.91 \).
14. \(7 \times 6 \times 5\)
Answer: 210
Explanation: Multiply left to right. \(7 \times 6 = 42\). Then multiply \(42 \times 5 = 210\). (Alternatively, \(6 \times 5 = 30\), and \(30 \times 7 = 210\)).
15. \(3^2 + 8^2\)
Answer: 73
Explanation: First, evaluate the exponents. \(3^2 = 3 \times 3 = 9\) and \(8^2 = 8 \times 8 = 64\). Then add the results together: \(9 + 64 = 73\).

17. \(322 \div 7\)
Answer: 46
Explanation: Use long division: 7 goes into 32 four times (\(7 \times 4 = 28\)). Subtract 28 from 32 to get 4. Bring down the 2 to make 42. 7 goes into 42 six times exactly (\(7 \times 6 = 42\)). Therefore, the quotient is 46.
18. \(45 \div (45 \div 9)\)
Answer: 9
Explanation: According to the order of operations, perform the operation inside the parentheses first. \(45 \div 9 = 5\). Now substitute 5 back into the equation: \(45 \div 5 = 9\).

20. Find the missing factor: \(4r = 52\)
Answer: 13
Explanation: To isolate the variable \(r\), divide both sides of the equation by 4. \(r = 52 \div 4\). \(r = 13\).
