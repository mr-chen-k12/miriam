Practice Test 15C Solutions

1. David has eight days to read a 400-page book. If he wants to read the same number of pages each day, how many pages should David read each day?
Answer: 50 pages
Explanation: To find the daily page count, divide the total number of pages by the total number of days.
$$ 400 \div 8 = 50 $$
2. When David has read five eighths of the book, what fraction will he still have left to read?
Answer: $ \frac{3}{8} $
Explanation: The entire book represents 1 whole, which can be written as $ \frac{8}{8} $. Subtract the part he has read from the whole.
$$ \frac{8}{8} - \frac{5}{8} = \frac{3}{8} $$
Use the information given below to answer problems 3-5.
One hundred twenty students voted for their favorite foods. Forty-five voted for tacos. Hamburgers received ten fewer votes than tacos. The remaining students voted for pizza.
3. How many students chose hamburgers as their favorite food?
Answer: 35 students
Explanation: The problem states hamburgers received 10 fewer votes than tacos. Tacos received 45 votes.
$$ 45 - 10 = 35 $$
4. How many students chose pizza as their favorite food?
Answer: 40 students
Explanation: First, find the total votes for tacos and hamburgers: $ 45 + 35 = 80 $.
Subtract this from the total number of students (120) to find the pizza votes:
$$ 120 - 80 = 40 $$
5. Which food received the most votes?
Answer: Tacos
Explanation: Compare the votes for each food:
Tacos: 45
Pizza: 40
Hamburgers: 35
Tacos have the highest number of votes.
6. How many days equal 12 weeks?
Answer: 84 days
Explanation: There are 7 days in a single week. Multiply the number of weeks by 7.
$$ 12 \times 7 = 84 $$
7. In the word MISSISSIPPI, what fraction of the letters are Is?
Answer: $ \frac{4}{11} $
Explanation: First, count the total number of letters in MISSISSIPPI, which is 11.
Next, count the number of 'I's in the word, which is 4.
The fraction is the number of 'I's over the total letters: $ \frac{4}{11} $.
8. Sarah ran a 7-kilometer race. Seven kilometers is how many meters?
Answer: 7000 meters
Explanation: There are 1,000 meters in 1 kilometer. Multiply the number of kilometers by 1,000.
$$ 7 \times 1000 = 7000 $$
9. Leo entered a 15-kilometer race but walked one third of the distance. How many kilometers did Leo walk?
Answer: 5 kilometers
Explanation: To find one third of 15, multiply 15 by $ \frac{1}{3} $ (or divide 15 by 3).
$$ 15 \div 3 = 5 $$
10. What is the perimeter of this triangle?
Answer: 60 mm
Explanation: The perimeter is the total distance around the outside of the shape. Add the lengths of all three sides together.
$$ 15 + 20 + 25 = 60 $$
11. The length of $\overline{XY}$ is 35 mm. The length of $\overline{XZ}$ is 90 mm. What is the length of $\overline{YZ}$?
Answer: 55 mm
Explanation: Point Y lies between X and Z, meaning the length of XZ is the sum of XY and YZ ($XZ = XY + YZ$). To find YZ, subtract the length of XY from XZ.
$$ 90 - 35 = 55 $$
12. $\$50 - (\$24.35 + 85\text{¢})$
Answer: $24.80
Explanation: First, convert $85\text{¢}$ to dollars ($0.85). Then, solve the operations inside the parentheses by adding.
$$ \$24.35 + \$0.85 = \$25.20 $$
Next, subtract this sum from $\$50.00$.
$$ \$50.00 - \$25.20 = \$24.80 $$

16. $235 \div 6$   (Shown as $6 \overline{) 235}$)
Answer: 39 R 1
Explanation: Perform long division. 6 goes into 23 three times ($6 \times 3 = 18$). Subtract 18 from 23 to get 5. Bring down the 5 to make 55. 6 goes into 55 nine times ($6 \times 9 = 54$). Subtract 54 from 55 to leave a remainder of 1.

18. $5y = 350$
Answer: $y = 70$
Explanation: Isolate the variable $y$ by performing the inverse operation. Divide both sides by 5.
$$ y = 350 \div 5 $$ $$ y = 70 $$
19. Find the missing number $n$:
  426
  183
+   $n$
  850
Answer: $n = 241$
Explanation: First, add the two known numbers together.
$$ 426 + 183 = 609 $$
Next, subtract that sum from the total to find the missing addend.
$$ 850 - 609 = 241 $$
20. Compare: $2 \times 5^2 \bigcirc \sqrt{100} \times \sqrt{49}$
Answer: < (Less Than)
Explanation: Evaluate both sides of the comparison.
Left side: Solve the exponent first ($5^2 = 25$), then multiply.
$$ 2 \times 25 = 50 $$
Right side: Find the square roots ($\sqrt{100} = 10$ and $\sqrt{49} = 7$), then multiply.
$$ 10 \times 7 = 70 $$
Since $50 < 70$, the correct symbol is $<$.
