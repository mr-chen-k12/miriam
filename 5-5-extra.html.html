Extra Practice: Questions 1, 16, & 19

These practice questions are designed to reinforce the concepts from the problems missed or struggled with on the cumulative test.

Practice for Question 1 (Directions & Rotations)

1a. John is facing north. He turns 90° counter-clockwise. What direction is he facing now?
Answer: West
If you are facing North (up on a compass), turning counter-clockwise means turning to your left. A 90-degree turn to the left from North points you West.
1b. Sarah is facing east. She turns 180°. What direction is she facing now?
Answer: West
A 180-degree turn means doing a complete half-circle, or turning completely around to face the opposite direction. The opposite of East is West.
1c. A boat is heading south. It makes a 90° turn counter-clockwise. What direction is the boat heading?
Answer: East
If the boat is facing South (down), turning counter-clockwise means turning to the left. A 90-degree turn left from South points directly East.

Practice for Question 16 (Long Division with Internal Zeros)

16a. \( 4242 \div 6 \)
Answer: 707
1. 6 goes into 42 seven times (\(6 \times 7 = 42\)).
2. Bring down the 4. 6 goes into 4 zero times. You must put a 0 in the quotient!
3. Bring down the 2 making it 42. 6 goes into 42 seven times.
Result: 707.
16b. \( 3618 \div 6 \)
Answer: 603
1. 6 goes into 36 six times (\(6 \times 6 = 36\)).
2. Bring down the 1. 6 goes into 1 zero times. Place a 0 in the quotient.
3. Bring down the 8 making it 18. 6 goes into 18 three times.
Result: 603.
16c. \( 8181 \div 9 \)
Answer: 909
1. 9 goes into 81 nine times (\(9 \times 9 = 81\)).
2. Bring down the 8. 9 goes into 8 zero times. Put a 0 in the quotient.
3. Bring down the 1 making it 81. 9 goes into 81 nine times.
Result: 909.

Practice for Question 19 (Missing Addends in Column Addition)

19a. Find the missing number:
1520 1340 +   n 4000
Answer: \( n = 1140 \)
First, add the known numbers together: \( 1520 + 1340 = 2860 \).
Now your equation is \( 2860 + n = 4000 \).
To find \( n \), subtract the sum from the total: \( 4000 - 2860 = 1140 \).
