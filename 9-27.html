*3. Represent: On 1-cm grid paper, draw a square that is 4 cm on each side.
a. What is the perimeter of the square?
b. What is the area of the square?
Answer:
  • a. Perimeter: \(16\text{ cm}\)
  • b. Area: \(16\text{ sq cm}\) (or \(16\text{ cm}^2\))

Explanation:

a. \(\text{Perimeter} = 4 \times 4\text{ cm} = 16\text{ cm}\)

b. \(\text{Area} = 4\text{ cm} \times 4\text{ cm} = 16\text{ sq cm}\)

5. Represent: Draw a circle that has a diameter of 4 centimeters. What is the radius of the circle?
Answer:

\(2\text{ centimeters}\)

Explanation: The radius is half of the diameter.

\[\text{Radius} = \frac{\text{Diameter}}{2} = \frac{4\text{ cm}}{2} = 2\text{ cm}\]

6. Jharma arrived home from school at the time shown on the clock and finished her homework 1 hour 35 minutes later. What time did Jharma finish her homework?
Answer:

\(4:55\text{ PM}\)

Explanation:

  1. The clock shows \(3:20\text{ PM}\) (hour hand between 3 and 4, minute hand on 4).
  2. Add 1 hour: \(3:20 + 1\text{ hour} = 4:20\).
  3. Add 35 minutes: \(4:20 + 35\text{ minutes} = 4:55\text{ PM}\).

21. If the radius of a circle is 12 inches, then the diameter of the circle is how many feet?
Answer:

\[2\text{ feet}\]

Explanation:

  1. \(\text{Diameter} = 2 \times \text{radius} = 2 \times 12\text{ inches} = 24\text{ inches}\).
  2. Convert inches to feet: \(24\text{ inches} \div 12 = 2\text{ feet}\).

  • Five squared (\(5^2\)) \(= 25\).

  • Difference: \(25 - 10 = 15\).

*30. Estimate: M'Lisa would like to purchase about 100 balloons for her birthday party. One bag of 25 balloons costs \$2.49. What is a reasonable estimate of M'Lisa's cost to purchase about 100 balloons? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.
Answer:

\[\$10.00\]

Explanation:

  1. Find the number of bags needed: \(100 \div 25 = 4\text{ bags}\).
  2. Round the cost per bag from \(\$2.49\) to \(\$2.50\).
  3. Multiply: \(4 \times \$2.50 = \$10.00\).

This estimate is reasonable because \(\$2.49\) is only one cent away from \(\$2.50\), making the calculation simple and accurate.
