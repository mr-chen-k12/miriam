*1. Problem
Marcos is reading a book with $211$ pages. K'Neesha is reading a book with $272$ pages. How many more pages will K'Neesha read than Marcos? Write and solve an equation.
Answer:
Equation: $272 - 211 = p$
Solution: $p = 61$ pages

Explanation: To find out how many more pages K'Neesha reads, subtract the number of pages Marcos reads from the number of pages K'Neesha reads: $272 - 211 = 61$.

*4. Problem
The square root of $49$ is how much less than four squared?
Answer:
$9$

Explanation:
1. Square root of $49$: $\sqrt{49} = 7$
2. Four squared: $4^2 = 16$
3. Difference: $16 - 7 = 9$.
*5. Represent
On $1\text{-cm}$ grid paper, draw a $6\text{ cm}$ by $2\text{ cm}$ rectangle.
a. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?
b. What is the area of the rectangle?
Answer:
a. Perimeter = $16\text{ cm}$
b. Area = $12\text{ cm}^2$ (or $12\text{ square centimeters}$)

Explanation:
• Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width}) = 2 \times (6 + 2) = 2 \times 8 = 16\text{ cm}$.
• Area: $A = \text{length} \times \text{width} = 6 \times 2 = 12\text{ cm}^2$.

9. Compare
Compare: $9 \times 4 \bigcirc \sqrt{36}$
Answer:
$9 \times 4 > \sqrt{36}$

Explanation: $9 \times 4 = 36$ and $\sqrt{36} = 6$. Since $36 > 6$, the correct symbol is $>$.

*11. Represent
To what number is the arrow pointing on the number line between $2200$ and $2300$?
Answer:
$2260$

Explanation: The segment between $2200$ and $2300$ is divided into $5$ equal intervals of $20$ units each ($2220, 2240, 2260, 2280$). The arrow points to the third tick mark after $2200$, which corresponds to $2200 + (3 \times 20) = 2260$.

*27. Multiple Choice
Look at the sequence below. Which of the following numbers is not in the sequence?
$$1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, \dots$$ a. $64$
b. $49$
c. $80$
d. $100$
Answer:
c. $80$

Explanation: This is a sequence of perfect squares ($1^2, 2^2, 3^2, 4^2, 5^2, 6^2, \dots$).
• $49 = 7^2$
• $64 = 8^2$
• $100 = 10^2$
$80$ is not a perfect square integer, so it is not in the sequence.
*28. Formulate
The state of Kentucky had $189$ public libraries in $2006$. The state of Maryland had $176$ public libraries. Write and solve an equation to find the number of public libraries Kentucky and Maryland had altogether.
Answer:
Equation: $189 + 176 = L$
Solution: $L = 365$ libraries

Explanation: Add Kentucky's libraries ($189$) and Maryland's libraries ($176$): $189 + 176 = 365$.
