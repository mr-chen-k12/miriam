Question 1

Perform the long division calculation: 14.25 ÷ 4

(Work out the decimal division until it terminates cleanly with no remainder.)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. 14 ÷ 4 = 3 with a remainder of 2. Place decimal point after 3.

2. Bring down 2 → 22 ÷ 4 = 5 (20) with a remainder of 2.

3. Bring down 5 → 25 ÷ 4 = 6 (24) with a remainder of 1.

4. Add zero and bring down → 10 ÷ 4 = 2 (8) with a remainder of 2.

5. Add another zero and bring down → 20 ÷ 4 = 5 (20) with 0 remainder.

14.25 ÷ 4 = 3.5625 (Terminating decimal)

Question 2

Perform the long division calculation: 17 ÷ 3

(Express your answer using repeating decimal notation or showing the infinitely repeating digit.)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. 17 ÷ 3 = 5 with a remainder of 2.

2. Add decimal point and trailing zero: 20 ÷ 3 = 6 (18) with a remainder of 2.

3. Add another zero: 20 ÷ 3 = 6 (18) with a remainder of 2.

4. The remainder 2 repeats endlessly, producing a repeating 6.

17 ÷ 3 = 5.666... or 5.6̄ (Repeating decimal)

Question 3

Find the area of a right triangle with a base of 12 cm and a height of 7 cm. (Hint: Area = ½ × Base × Height)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Multiply base × height: 12 cm × 7 cm = 84 sq cm.

2. Divide by 2: 84 ÷ 2 = 42 sq cm.

Question 4

Evaluate the expression using the correct order of operations (PEMDAS):

(18 + 6) ÷ 3 + 4 × 5

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Parentheses first: 18 + 6 = 24.

2. Division and Multiplication from left to right: 24 ÷ 3 = 8, and 4 × 5 = 20.

3. Addition: 8 + 20 = 28.

Question 5

A recipe requires ¾ cup of sugar. If you are making 3 batches of the recipe, how many cups of sugar do you need in total?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Multiply 3 × (3/4) = 9/4 cups.

2. Convert to a mixed number: 9 ÷ 4 = 2 ¼ cups.
