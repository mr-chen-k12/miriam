Cumulative Test 15B Solutions

1. Shannon has six days to read a 300-page book. If she wants to read the same number of pages each day, how many pages should Shannon read each day?
Answer: 50 pages
Explanation: To find the daily page count, divide the total number of pages by the total number of days.
$$ 300 \div 6 = 50 $$
2. When Shannon has read three fifths of the book, what fraction will she still have left to read?
Answer: $ \frac{2}{5} $
Explanation: The entire book represents 1 whole, which can be written as $ \frac{5}{5} $. Subtract the part she has read from the whole.
$$ \frac{5}{5} - \frac{3}{5} = \frac{2}{5} $$
Use the information given below to answer problems 3-5.
Eighty students voted for their favorite foods. Thirty-two voted for pizza. Hot dogs received eight fewer votes than pizza. The remaining students voted for hamburgers.
3. How many students chose hot dogs as their favorite food?
Answer: 24 students
Explanation: The problem states hot dogs received 8 fewer votes than pizza. Pizza received 32 votes.
$$ 32 - 8 = 24 $$
4. How many students chose hamburgers as their favorite food?
Answer: 24 students
Explanation: First, find the total votes for pizza and hot dogs: $ 32 + 24 = 56 $.
Subtract this from the total number of students (80) to find the hamburger votes:
$$ 80 - 56 = 24 $$
5. Which food received the most votes?
Answer: Pizza
Explanation: Compare the votes for each food:
Pizza: 32
Hot dogs: 24
Hamburgers: 24
Pizza has the highest number of votes.
6. How many days equal 8 weeks?
Answer: 56 days
Explanation: There are 7 days in a single week. Multiply the number of weeks by 7.
$$ 8 \times 7 = 56 $$
7. In the word HIPPOPOTAMI, what fraction of the letters are Os?
Answer: $ \frac{2}{11} $
Explanation: First, count the total number of letters in HIPPOPOTAMI, which is 11.
Next, count the number of 'O's in the word, which is 2.
The fraction is the number of 'O's over the total letters: $ \frac{2}{11} $.
8. Mary ran a 5-kilometer race. Five kilometers is how many meters?
Answer: 5000 meters
Explanation: There are 1,000 meters in 1 kilometer. Multiply the number of kilometers by 1,000.
$$ 5 \times 1000 = 5000 $$
9. Takeshi entered a 10-kilometer race but walked one fifth of the distance. How many kilometers did Takeshi walk?
Answer: 2 kilometers
Explanation: To find one fifth of 10, multiply 10 by $ \frac{1}{5} $ (or divide 10 by 5).
$$ 10 \div 5 = 2 $$
10. What is the perimeter of this triangle?
Answer: 73 mm
Explanation: The perimeter is the total distance around the outside of the shape. Add the lengths of all three sides together.
$$ 18 + 25 + 30 = 73 $$
11. The length of $\overline{AB}$ is 42 mm. The length of $\overline{AC}$ is 74 mm. What is the length of $\overline{BC}$?
Answer: 32 mm
Explanation: Point B lies between A and C, meaning the length of AC is the sum of AB and BC ($AC = AB + BC$). To find BC, subtract the length of AB from AC.
$$ 74 - 42 = 32 $$
12. $\$34 - (\$18.61 + 93\text{¢})$
Answer: $14.46
Explanation: First, convert $93\text{¢}$ to dollars ($0.93). Then, solve the operations inside the parentheses by adding.
$$ \$18.61 + \$0.93 = \$19.54 $$
Next, subtract this sum from $\$34.00$.
$$ \$34.00 - \$19.54 = \$14.46 $$

16. $197 \div 4$   (Shown as $4 \overline{) 197}$)
Answer: 49 R 1
Explanation: Perform long division. 4 goes into 19 four times ($4 \times 4 = 16$). Subtract 16 from 19 to get 3. Bring down the 7 to make 37. 4 goes into 37 nine times ($4 \times 9 = 36$). Subtract 36 from 37 to leave a remainder of 1.
17. $545 \div 6$   (Shown as $6 \overline{) 545}$)
Answer: 90 R 5
Explanation: Perform long division. 6 goes into 54 exactly 9 times ($6 \times 9 = 54$). Subtracting gives 0. Bring down the 5. 6 goes into 5 zero times. The remainder is 5.
18. $4w = 240$
Answer: $w = 60$
Explanation: Isolate the variable $w$ by performing the inverse operation. Divide both sides by 4.
$$ w = 240 \div 4 $$ $$ w = 60 $$
19. Find the missing number $n$:
  374
  215

  958
Answer: $n = 369$
Explanation: First, add the two known numbers together.
$$ 374 + 215 = 589 $$
Next, subtract that sum from the total to find the missing addend.
$$ 958 - 589 = 369 $$
20. Compare: $3 \times 4^2 \bigcirc \sqrt{64} \times \sqrt{36}$
Answer: = (Equal)
Explanation: Evaluate both sides of the comparison.
Left side: Solve the exponent first ($4^2 = 16$), then multiply.
$$ 3 \times 16 = 48 $$
Right side: Find the square roots ($\sqrt{64} = 8$ and $\sqrt{36} = 6$), then multiply.
$$ 8 \times 6 = 48 $$
Since $48 = 48$, the correct symbol is $=$.
