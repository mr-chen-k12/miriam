1 Warm-Up: The Morning Announcement
30 Seconds
Key Points to Remember: • Grab your audience's attention right away.
• Announce a fun morning change to set an exciting mood for the day.
Quick Pick:
"Good morning, elementary school! My name is Leo, and today I am Principal for a Day! First thing on my agenda: every morning will kick off by playing superhero theme songs over the loudspeakers! Because school should start with big smiles and great energy!"
2 The Big Upgrade: Recess & Lunch
45 Seconds
Key Points to Remember: • Exercise and healthy fun give students energy to learn.
• Propose a creative change that every kid in school will love.
Quick Pick:
"Next up: lunch and recess! A great school needs great play time. That's why I am adding a giant inflatable obstacle course to the field! When students move their bodies and have fun together, our minds get recharged and ready to tackle hard math and reading lessons!"
3 Creative Learning: Hands-On Classroom Magic
45 Seconds
Key Points to Remember: • Being principal isn't just about fun; it's about making learning exciting.
• Share a hands-on subject or project that makes kids curious.
Quick Pick:
"Now let's talk about the classroom! Learning should feel like an adventure. As principal, I am replacing boring worksheets with building real robots in science class. When we build, create, and experiment with our hands, we learn much faster and have way more fun!"
4 School Culture: Kindness & High Fives
45 Seconds
Key Points to Remember: • A true leader makes sure every single person feels welcomed and valued.
• Introduce a school-wide reward for kindness and teamwork.
Quick Pick:
"Finally, the most important rule of all: kindness! A great principal makes sure no student ever sits alone. I am creating a brand-new tradition by handing out Golden High-Five trophies for helping others. When we support each other, our whole school wins!"
