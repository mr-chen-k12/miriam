Math Worksheet Solutions

1. Chazz pays $7.50 every week for a bus pass. How much does she pay for 4 weeks of bus passes? Write an equation to solve the problem.
Answer: \$30.00
Equation: \( \$7.50 \times 4 = x \)
Explanation: You multiply the weekly cost ($7.50) by the number of weeks (4) to find the total amount paid. \( 7.50 \times 4 = 30 \).

3. Calvin has to get up at 6 a.m. By what time should he go to bed in order to get 8 hours of sleep?
Answer: 10:00 p.m.
Explanation: Count backward 8 hours from 6:00 a.m. Counting back 6 hours gets you to 12:00 a.m. (midnight). Counting back 2 more hours gets you to 10:00 p.m.
4. The store sells paint in quart cans, gallon cans, and 5-gallon cans. The price per quart is lower with larger cans. Hosni needs 8 quarts of paint. What containers of paint should he buy? Explain.
Answer: He should buy two 1-gallon cans.
Explanation: There are 4 quarts in 1 gallon. Therefore, 8 quarts equals 2 gallons (\(8 \div 4 = 2\)). Since larger cans offer a lower price per quart, buying two 1-gallon cans is more cost-effective than buying eight individual quart cans.
5. Write 8402 in expanded form. Then use words to write the number.
Answer:
Expanded Form: \( 8000 + 400 + 2 \)
Word Form: Eight thousand four hundred two
Explanation: Break down the place values: 8 thousands, 4 hundreds, 0 tens, and 2 ones.
6. Find the fourth multiple of 7. Then find the sixth multiple of 6. Add these multiples. What is the square root of the answer?
Answer: 8
Explanation:
1. Fourth multiple of 7: \( 7 \times 4 = 28 \)
2. Sixth multiple of 6: \( 6 \times 6 = 36 \)
3. Add them: \( 28 + 36 = 64 \)
4. Square root: \( \sqrt{64} = 8 \)
7. According to this calendar (September 2042), what is the date of the second Tuesday?
Answer: September 9, 2042
Explanation: Looking at the calendar grid, the Tuesdays fall under the "T" column. The first Tuesday is on the 2nd. The second Tuesday is 7 days later, on the 9th.
8. If \( 5 + n = 23 \), then what number does \( n - 5 \) equal?
Answer: 13
Explanation: First, solve for \( n \) by subtracting 5 from 23, giving \( n = 18 \). Then substitute \( n \) into the second expression: \( 18 - 5 = 13 \).
9. What is the perimeter of this figure? Measurements are in feet. Describe each angle as acute, obtuse, or right.
Answer:
Perimeter = 26 feet.
Angles: A = Acute, B = Right, C = Obtuse, D = Obtuse.
Explanation: The perimeter is the sum of all sides: \( 6 + 6 + 7 + 7 = 26 \). Visually, angle B has a square symbol indicating a 90° Right angle. Angle A is less than 90° (Acute). Angles C and D are wider than 90° (Obtuse).
10. Compare these fractions. Draw and shade two congruent circles to show the comparison: \( \frac{1}{2} \) and \( \frac{2}{4} \)
Answer: They are equal (\( \frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{4} \)).
Explanation: The fractions are equivalent. Simplifying \( \frac{2}{4} \) by dividing the numerator and denominator by 2 yields \( \frac{1}{2} \). The shaded areas in the circles cover the exact same amount of space.
11. To what mixed number is the arrow pointing? (Number line from 7 to 8 with 5 intervals, arrow on the 3rd mark)
Answer: \( 7 \frac{3}{5} \)
Explanation: The space between 7 and 8 is divided into 5 equal sections, meaning each tick mark represents \( \frac{1}{5} \). The arrow points to the 3rd tick mark after 7.
12. Which segment appears to be parallel to AB? (Looking at a 3D rectangular prism)
Answer: Segments DC, EF, and HG.
Explanation: In a rectangular prism, the opposite edges running in the same dimensional direction are parallel. If AB is the top front horizontal edge, the parallel edges are the bottom front (DC), top back (EF), and bottom back (HG).
13. \( 0.47 + 3.62 + 0.85 + 4.54 \)
Answer: 9.48
Explanation: Align the decimals vertically and add. \( 0.47 + 3.62 = 4.09 \). Then \( 4.09 + 0.85 = 4.94 \). Finally, \( 4.94 + 4.54 = 9.48 \).
14. \( \$3 + \$4.39 + \$12.62 \)
Answer: \$20.01
Explanation: Line up the decimal points (treat $3 as $3.00) and add. \( \$3.00 + \$4.39 = \$7.39 \). Then \( \$7.39 + \$12.62 = \$20.01 \).
15. \( 36.47 - (3.5 + 12.6) \)
Answer: 20.37
Explanation: Follow the order of operations (PEMDAS) and calculate the parentheses first: \( 3.5 + 12.6 = 16.1 \). Then subtract that sum from 36.47: \( 36.47 - 16.10 = 20.37 \).
16. \( \$20.00 - (\$0.29 + \$7) \)
Answer: \$12.71
Explanation: Calculate the parentheses first: \( \$0.29 + \$7.00 = \$7.29 \). Then subtract from $20.00: \( \$20.00 - \$7.29 = \$12.71 \).

19. \( \$2.80 \times 4 \)
Answer: \$11.20
Explanation: \( 2.80 \times 4 \): \( 0 \times 4 = 0 \), \( 8 \times 4 = 32 \) (carry 3). \( 2 \times 4 = 8 \), plus 3 is 11. Place the decimal two spots in: \$11.20.

24. How can you find the product of \( 4 \times 100 \) using only mental math?
Answer: 400
Explanation: Using mental math base-10 tricks, multiply the whole numbers (\( 4 \times 1 = 4 \)) and simply attach the two zeros from the 100 to the end of the 4.
25. Two factors of 20 are 1 and 20 because \( 1 \times 20 = 20 \). Find four more factors of 20.
Answer: 2, 4, 5, and 10
Explanation: Factors are numbers that divide evenly into another number. For 20, the pairs are (1, 20), (2, 10), and (4, 5).

29. Which of these even numbers is a prime number?
A 2   B 4   C 6   D 8
Answer: A (2)
Explanation: A prime number has only two factors: 1 and itself. 2 is the only even prime number. Numbers 4, 6, and 8 all have more than two factors (e.g., 4 is divisible by 2).
30. On a road trip across the country, Kwan drove 387 miles the first day and 409 miles the second day. If he drives about the same distance each day, approximately how many miles will Kwan drive in 5 days?
Answer: Approximately 2,000 miles.
Explanation: 387 and 409 both round approximately to 400 miles per day. If he averages 400 miles a day for 5 days: \( 400 \times 5 = 2000 \).
