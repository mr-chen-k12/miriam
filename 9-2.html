Question 1

Nine feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply the number of feet by 12:

9 feet × 12 inches = 108 inches

Question 2

Sophia handed the cashier a $75 bill to buy a backpack that cost $46.38. How much change did she receive?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the cost from $75.00:

$75.00 − $46.38 = $28.62

Question 3

Noah is 7 years younger than Emma. Emma is four times as old as Zoe. If Emma is 32 years old, how old are Noah and Zoe?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Noah: 32 − 7 = 25 years old.

2. Zoe: 32 ÷ 4 = 8 years old.

Noah is 25 and Zoe is 8.

Question 4

(c) 5 out of 9 cookies were eaten.

Teacher Answer Key

Question 5

One-sixth of the 42 students in a grade-level club chose art as their activity. How many students chose art?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-sixth of 42, divide by 6:

42 ÷ 6 = 7 students

Question 6

Looking back at Question 5, how many students did not choose art?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the students who chose art from the total:

42 − 7 = 35 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 83 and 36 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then find the exact difference.

Step-by-Step Solution:

Question 17
