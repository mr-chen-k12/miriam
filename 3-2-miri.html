Practice Set 5 - 20 Questions

1. Five weeks is how many days?

There are 7 days in 1 week.

$$5 \times 7 = 35$$

Answer: 35 days

2. List the factors of 16.

Factors are numbers that divide into 16 evenly:

  • \(1 \times 16 = 16\)
  • \(2 \times 8 = 16\)
  • \(4 \times 4 = 16\)

Answer: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16

3. Sixty cookies were placed equally into 4 jars. How many cookies were in each jar?

Divide the total number of cookies by the number of jars:

$$60 \div 4 = 15$$

Answer: 15 cookies

4. Draw a square and shade 25% of it.

25% is equal to one quarter (\(\frac{1}{4}\)) of the square.

Answer: Shade one of four equal sections.

5. Four hundred years equals how many centuries?

1 century = 100 years.

$$400 \div 100 = 4$$

Answer: 4 centuries

6. Find the fourth multiple of 9. Then add 14. What is the answer?

Step 1 (4th multiple of 9): \(4 \times 9 = 36\)

Step 2 (Add 14): \(36 + 14 = 50\)

Answer: 50

7. Compare: \(25\% \bigcirc \frac{1}{3}\)

25% is equal to \(\frac{1}{4}\).

\(\frac{1}{4}\) is less than \(\frac{1}{3}\).

Answer: <

8. Segment JK is 3 cm long. Segment KL is 8 cm long. If K is between J and L, how long is segment JL?

The total length JL is the sum of JK and KL.

\(JK + KL = JL\)

\(3\text{ cm} + 8\text{ cm} = 11\text{ cm}\)

Answer: 11 cm

9. What is the perimeter of a rectangle that is 20 cm by 10 cm?

Perimeter = Sum of all sides.

\(P = 20 + 10 + 20 + 10\)

\(P = 60\)

Answer: 60 cm

10. Round 6,432 to the nearest thousand.

The thousands digit is 6. Look at the hundreds digit (4). Since 4 is less than 5, we round down.

Answer: 6,000
