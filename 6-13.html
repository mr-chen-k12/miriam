Equal Group Distributions (Concept 4)
Question 4.A
Thirty-eight pencils were distributed into 5 supply boxes as evenly as possible.
a. How many boxes had exactly 7 pencils?
b. How many boxes had 8 pencils?
Divide total units: $38 \div 5 = 7 \text{ remainder } 3$.
Every supply box receives a baseline of 7 pencils. The 3 remaining pencils are distributed one-by-one into 3 boxes, bringing their counts up to 8. The remaining boxes ($5 - 3 = 2$) keep exactly 7.
Answer: a. 2 boxes  |  b. 3 boxes
Question 4.B
Fifty-two chairs were arranged into 6 study rows as evenly as possible.
a. How many rows had exactly 8 chairs?
b. How many rows had 9 chairs?
Divide total units: $52 \div 6 = 8 \text{ remainder } 4$.
Every row receives a base layout of 8 chairs. The 4 remaining chairs are added to 4 rows to make sets of 9. The other rows ($6 - 4 = 2$) remain with exactly 8.
Answer: a. 2 rows  |  b. 4 rows
Question 4.C
Twenty-three tennis balls were placed into 4 training cans as evenly as possible.
a. How many cans had exactly 5 tennis balls?
b. How many cans had 6 tennis balls?
Divide total units: $23 \div 4 = 5 \text{ remainder } 3$.
Every can gets 5 tennis balls. 3 cans receive an extra remainder ball to total 6. The remaining ($4 - 3 = 1$) can has exactly 5.
Answer: a. 1 can  |  b. 3 cans

$$4 + 8 + 2 + 9 + n + 3 + 7 + 1 = 45$$

3. Isolate value: $n = 45 - 34 = 11$.
Answer: n = 11
