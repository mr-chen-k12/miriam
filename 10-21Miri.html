1.(6, 43)
* Conclude Use the numbers 0.5, 0.6, and 1.1 to write two addition facts and two subtraction facts.
Answer
\(0.5 + 0.6 = 1.1;\quad 0.6 + 0.5 = 1.1;\quad 1.1 - 0.5 = 0.6;\quad 1.1 - 0.6 = 0.5\)
Solution
Since \(0.5 + 0.6 = 1.1\), the three numbers form a fact family: two additions (the addends swap places) and two subtractions (start with the sum 1.1).
2.(19)
A whole hour is 60 minutes. How many minutes is half of an hour?
Answer
\(30\text{ minutes}\)
Solution
Half of 60 is \(60 \div 2 = 30\) minutes.
3.(31)
Explain The space shuttle orbited 155 miles above the Earth. The weather balloon floated 15 miles above the Earth. The space shuttle was how much higher than the weather balloon? Explain why your answer is reasonable.
Answer
\(140\text{ miles higher}\)
Solution
\(155 - 15 = 140\) miles. It is reasonable because the shuttle’s height should be a little less than 155 miles (only the balloon’s 15 miles are removed) — 140 is a little less than 155.

7.(27)
The clock shows the time Joe left for work this morning. He ate breakfast 35 minutes before that time. What time did Joe eat breakfast?
Answer
\(5{:}35\text{ a.m.}\)
Solution
The clock shows 6:10 (hour hand just past 6, minute hand on the 2). Count back 35 minutes: \(6{:}10 - 0{:}35 = 5{:}35\). Joe ate breakfast at 5:35 a.m.

13.(45)
Compare: \(12 - (6 - 3) \bigcirc (12 - 6) - 3\)
Answer
\(12 - (6 - 3) > (12 - 6) - 3\)
Solution
Left side: \(12 - (6-3) = 12 - 3 = 9\). Right side: \((12-6) - 3 = 6 - 3 = 3\). Since \(9 > 3\), the left side is greater.

26.(21, 45)
* Multiple Choice Which of the following segments is not a radius of the circle?
A \(\overline{RS}\) B \(\overline{RM}\)
C \(\overline{MT}\) D \(\overline{MS}\)
Answer
\(\text{A — }\overline{RS}\)
Solution
A radius joins the center \(M\) to a point on the circle: \(\overline{RM}\), \(\overline{MT}\), and \(\overline{MS}\) are radii. \(\overline{RS}\) joins two points on the circle and passes through the center — it is a diameter, not a radius.
27.(31)
Formulate Estrella finished the first problem in 34 seconds. She finished the second problem in 28 seconds. The first problem took how much longer to finish than the second problem? Write an equation to solve the problem.
Answer
\(6\text{ seconds}\)
Solution
Equation: \(34 - 28 = d\). \(d = 6\), so the first problem took 6 seconds longer.
28.(45)
* Describe the order of operations in each expression. Then find the number each expression equals.
a. \(12 - (4 - 2)\)
b. \((12 - 4) - 2\)
Answer
\(\text{a. }10\qquad\text{b. }6\)
Solution
Do the parentheses first in both. a. \((4-2) = 2\), then \(12 - 2 = 10\). b. \((12-4) = 8\), then \(8 - 2 = 6\). (Same numbers, different grouping — different answers!)

Answer
\(26\text{ degrees}\)
Solution

30.(25, 42)
* Estimate The population density of Connecticut is 702.9 people per square mile. The population density of Kentucky is 101.7 people per square mile. Round to the nearest hundred to estimate how many more people per square mile live in Connecticut than live in Kentucky.
Answer
\(\text{about }600\text{ more people per square mile}\)
Solution
Round: \(702.9 \to 700\) and \(101.7 \to 100\). Estimate: \(700 - 100 = 600\) more people per square mile.
