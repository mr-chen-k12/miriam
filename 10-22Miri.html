1.(35)
Karim had two dimes, a quarter, and three pennies. Write this amount with a dollar sign and decimal point.
Answer
\(\$0.48\)
Solution
Two dimes = 20¢, a quarter = 25¢, three pennies = 3¢. Total: \(20 + 25 + 3 = 48\) cents \(= \$0.48\).
2.(11)
Findlay is 182 miles west of Youngstown. Akron is 50 miles west of Youngstown. How far is it from Akron to Findlay?
Answer
\(132\text{ miles}\)
Solution
Both cities are west of Youngstown, and Findlay is farther west. Subtract: \(182 - 50 = 132\) miles.
3.(37)
Name the fraction or mixed number marked by the arrow on this number line:
Answer
\(1\dfrac{3}{4}\)
Solution
The spaces between whole numbers are divided into 4 equal parts, so each tick is \(\frac{1}{4}\). The arrow points to the third tick past 1: \(1\frac{3}{4}\).
4.(Inv. 1)
Compare: \(17 + 13 + 29 \bigcirc 7 \times 7\)
Answer
\(17 + 13 + 29 > 7 \times 7\)
Solution
\(17 + 13 + 29 = 59\) and \(7 \times 7 = 49\). Since \(59 > 49\), the left side is greater.

8.(7, Inv. 1)
Use digits and symbols to compare four hundred eighty-one and seven hundred seventy.
Answer
\(481 < 770\)
Solution
Four hundred eighty-one \(= 481\); seven hundred seventy \(= 770\). Since \(481 < 770\): \(481 < 770\).
9.(23)
Which letter below has a right angle?
S L O W
Answer
\(\text{L}\)
Solution
The letter L is formed by a vertical line meeting a horizontal line at a square corner — a right angle (90°). S and O are curves, and W has only sharp (acute) angles.

11.(20)
Round 73 to the nearest ten. Round 77 to the nearest ten. Add the rounded numbers.
Answer
\(150\)
Solution
73 rounds to 70; 77 rounds to 80. \(70 + 80 = 150\).
12.(Inv. 2, 21)
If the radius of a circle is 1 yard, then the diameter is how many feet?
Answer
\(6\text{ feet}\)
Solution
The diameter is twice the radius: \(2 \times 1 = 2\) yards. Convert: \(2 \times 3 = 6\) feet.
13.(35)
What mixed number is shown by the shaded rectangles?
Answer

Solution
One rectangle is fully shaded \(= 1\). The second rectangle is divided into 3 equal parts with 2 shaded \(= \frac{2}{3}\). Total: \(1\frac{2}{3}\).

20.(2)
\(67 + 73 + 31 + 48 + N = 255\)
Answer
\(N = 36\)
Solution
Add the known numbers: \(67 + 73 + 31 + 48 = 219\). Then \(N = 255 - 219 = 36\).
