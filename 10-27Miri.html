1.(31, 41)
FORMULATE Brand A costs two dollars and forty-three cents. Brand B costs five dollars and seven cents. Brand B costs how much more than Brand A? Write an equation and solve this problem.
Answer
\(\$2.43 + n = \$5.07, \quad n = \$2.64\)
Solution
Let \(n\) be how much more Brand B costs. Brand A's price plus the difference equals Brand B's price: \(\$2.43 + n = \$5.07\). Subtract: \(n = \$5.07 - \$2.43 = \$2.64\). Brand B costs $2.64 more than Brand A.
2.(47)
CONNECT The numbers 3, 4, and 12 form a multiplication and division fact family:
\(3 \times 4 = 12\)   \(12 \div 4 = 3\)
\(4 \times 3 = 12\)   \(12 \div 3 = 4\)
Write four multiplication/division facts using the numbers 4, 5, and 20.
Answer
\(4 \times 5 = 20, \quad 5 \times 4 = 20, \quad 20 \div 4 = 5, \quad 20 \div 5 = 4\)
Solution
The two smaller numbers are the factors and the largest is the product: \(4 \times 5 = 20\) and \(5 \times 4 = 20\). Turning them around gives the division facts: \(20 \div 4 = 5\) and \(20 \div 5 = 4\).

4.(10)
CONCLUDE Use the digits 1, 5, 6, and 8 to write an even number greater than 8420. Each digit may be used only once.
Answer
\(8516\)
Solution
An even number must end in 6 or 8, and to beat 8420 the number must start with 8. Trying the remaining digits: 8516 is even and greater than 8420, while 8561 is odd, the 86__ numbers (8615, 8651) are odd, and the 81__ numbers are less than 8420. So 8516 is the only number that works.

6.(Inv. 3)
ANALYZE Carlos will use square floor tiles that measure one foot on each side to cover a hallway that is eight feet long and four feet wide. How many floor tiles will Carlos need?
Answer
\(32\text{ tiles}\)
Solution
Each tile covers 1 square foot, and the hallway's area is \(8 \text{ ft} \times 4 \text{ ft} = 32\) square feet. So Carlos needs 32 tiles (8 tiles along the length in each of 4 rows).

9.(17)
The length of segment \(PQ\) is 2 cm. The length of segment \(PR\) is 11 cm. How long is segment \(QR\)?
Answer
\(9\text{ cm}\)
Solution
Point Q lies between P and R, so \(PQ + QR = PR\). That gives \(2 + QR = 11\), so \(QR = 11 - 2 = 9\) cm.

26.(47)
MULTIPLE CHOICE Which of these does not show 24 divided by 4?
A \(24 \enclose{longdiv}{4}\)   B \(\frac{24}{4}\)   C \(24 \div 4\)   D \(4 \enclose{longdiv}{24}\)
Answer
\(\text{A}\)
Solution
In long-division notation the divisor goes outside and the dividend inside. Choice D, \(4 \enclose{longdiv}{24}\), means 24 ÷ 4, as do B (\(\frac{24}{4}\)) and C (\(24 \div 4\)). Choice A, \(24 \enclose{longdiv}{4}\), means 4 ÷ 24 — the numbers are swapped — so A is the one that does not show 24 divided by 4.

28.(45)
Describe the order of operations in these expressions, and find the number each expression equals.
a. \(12 \div (6 \div 2)\)
b. \((12 \div 6) \div 2\)
Answer
\(\text{a. }4 \qquad \text{b. }1\)
Solution
a. Work inside the parentheses first: \(6 \div 2 = 3\), then \(12 \div 3 = 4\). b. Work inside the parentheses first: \(12 \div 6 = 2\), then \(2 \div 2 = 1\). The two expressions use the same numbers but different groupings, and they give different answers — division is not associative, so the grouping matters.
