Homework Solutions: Lesson 56

1. Drew has fifty-six rolls. Seven rolls will fit on one tray. How many trays does he need? Write an equation.
Answer: 8 trays
Equation: \( 56 \div 7 = 8 \)
Divide the total number of rolls by the capacity of one tray.
2. One gallon is about 3.78 L. About how many liters is two gallons? (Use words)
Answer: Seven and fifty-six hundredths liters.
Math: \( 3.78 \times 2 = 7.56 \)
To find the total, double the value of one gallon.
3. Estimate the sum of $6.87 and $5.92 by rounding to the nearest dollar.
Answer: $7.00 + $6.00 = $13.00
$6.87 rounds up to $7; $5.92 rounds up to $6.
4. Write four multiplication/division facts using 3, 8, and 24.
1. \( 3 \times 8 = 24 \)
2. \( 8 \times 3 = 24 \)
3. \( 24 \div 3 = 8 \)
4. \( 24 \div 8 = 3 \)
5. What are the seven months of the year that have 31 days?
Answer: January, March, May, July, August, October, and December.
6. Find the eighth multiple of six. Add one. What is the square root of the answer?
Step 1: \( 6 \times 8 = 48 \)
Step 2: \( 48 + 1 = 49 \)
Step 3: \( \sqrt{49} = 7 \)
Final Answer: 7
7. Compare \( 1/4 \) and \( 1/6 \) using rectangles.
Answer: \( 1/4 > 1/6 \)
The rectangle divided into 4 pieces has larger individual pieces than the one divided into 6.
8. Round 4,651 to the nearest thousand, hundred, and ten.
Thousand: 5,000
Hundred: 4,700
Ten: 4,650
9. Rectangle with 7 mi length and 4 mi width. Find Perimeter and Area.
a. Perimeter: \( 7+7+4+4 = 22 \text{ miles} \)
b. Area: \( 7 \times 4 = 28 \text{ sq miles} \)
