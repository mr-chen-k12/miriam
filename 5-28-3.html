Problem 1
Ninety-one students are divided as equally as possible among 3 classrooms.
(a) How many classrooms have exactly 30 students?
(b) How many classrooms have 31 students?
Answer: (a) 2 classrooms  |  (b) 1 classroom
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • To divide 91 students into 3 classrooms as equally as possible, perform long division:
    $$91 \div 3 = 30 ext{ R } 1$$
  • The quotient is 30, which represents the baseline number of students per classroom.
  • The remainder is 1, which means 1 extra student must be placed into one of the classrooms.
  • Therefore, 2 classrooms will have exactly 30 students, and 1 classroom will have 30 + 1 = 31 students.
