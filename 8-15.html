Lesson 99: Decimal Numbers and Money

Question 1

Susan B. Anthony divided her 53 suffragettes into groups of 6.

(a) How many groups of 6 could she make?
(b) How many suffragettes were left over?
(c) If the remaining suffragettes formed a group, how many groups were there in all?

Divide 53 by 6:

$$53 \div 6 = 8 \text{ remainder } 5$$

  • (a) She could make 8 groups.
  • (b) There were 5 suffragettes left over.
  • (c) The 8 original groups plus 1 group formed by the leftover suffragettes equals 9 groups in all ($8 + 1 = 9$).
Question 2

Abraham Lincoln was born in 1809 and died in 1865. About how many years did he live?

To find the estimated lifespan, round the years to the nearest ten or find the exact difference and round:

$$\text{Exact: } 1865 - 1809 = 56 \text{ years}$$

$$\text{Estimated: } 1870 - 1810 = 60 \text{ years}$$

Answer: He lived 56 years (or about 60 years if using standard rounding estimation).

Question 3

The parking lot charges $1.25 for the first hour. It charges 75¢ for each additional hour. How much does it cost to park a car in the lot for 3 hours?

Calculate the cost for each component of the 3 hours:

  • First hour: $1.25
  • Remaining hours: $3 - 1 = 2$ hours
  • Cost for remaining hours: $2 \times \$0.75 = \$1.50$

$$\text{Total Cost} = \$1.25 + \$1.50 = \$2.75$$

Answer: $2.75

Question 4

Two thirds of the team's 45 points were scored in the second half. How many points did the team score in the second half? Draw a picture to illustrate the problem.

First, divide the total points by 3 to find the value of one-third:

$$45 \div 3 = 15 \text{ points per third}$$

Then, multiply by 2 to find two-thirds:

$$2 \times 15 = 30 \text{ points}$$

Answer: 30 points

Question 5

Something is wrong with this sign: [ICE CREAM BARS 0.75¢ each]. Draw two different signs that show how to correct the error.

The original sign states "0.75¢", which means seventy-five hundredths of one cent (less than a penny). It should write either seventy-five cents or seventy-five hundredths of a dollar.

Correct Formats: $0.75 or 75¢

Question 6

What is the value of 3 ten-dollar bills, 4 one-dollar bills, 5 dimes, and 2 pennies?

Add the values together:

$$3 \times \$10.00 = \$30.00$$

$$4 \times \$1.00 = \$4.00$$

$$5 \times \$0.10 = \$0.50$$

$$2 \times \$0.01 = \$0.02$$

$$\text{Total} = \$30.00 + \$4.00 + \$0.50 + \$0.02 = \$34.52$$

Answer: $34.52

Question 8

Round 5139 to the nearest thousand. Round 6902 to the nearest thousand. Then add the rounded numbers.

$$5139 \rightarrow 5000$$

$$6902 \rightarrow 7000$$

$$5000 + 7000 = 12000$$

Answer: 12,000

Question 9

James opened a 1-gallon bottle of milk and poured out 1 quart. How many quarts of milk were left in the bottle?

Since $1 \text{ gallon} = 4 \text{ quarts}$:

$$4 \text{ quarts} - 1 \text{ quart} = 3 \text{ quarts}$$

Answer: 3 quarts

Question 10

What percent of the milk in problem 9 was left in the bottle?

Find the fraction left and convert to a percentage:

$$\frac{3 \text{ quarts left}}{4 \text{ quarts total}} = \frac{3}{4} = 75\%$$

Answer: 75%
