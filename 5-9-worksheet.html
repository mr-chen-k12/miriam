Math Problem Solutions - Practice Set 2

1. A baker made 50 trays of cookies. Each tray held 150 cookies. How many cookies were there in all?
Answer: 7,500 cookies

Explanation: To find the total, multiply the number of trays by the number of cookies per tray.
$150 \times 50 = 7500$.

2. The orchestra concert was 2 and a half hours long. If it started at 6:30 p.m., at what time did it end?
Answer: 9:00 p.m.

Explanation: Add 2 hours and 30 minutes to the starting time.
6:30 p.m. + 2.5 hours = 9:00 p.m.

3. Marco is reading a 420-page textbook. If he has finished page 215, how many pages does he still have to read?
Answer: 205 pages

Explanation: Subtract the number of pages he has read from the total number of pages.
$420 - 215 = 205$.

4. Five friends shared a prize of 60 arcade tickets equally. How many tickets did each friend get?
Answer: 12 tickets

Explanation: Divide the total number of tickets by 5.
$60 \div 5 = 12$.

5. Round 8,499 to the nearest thousand.
Answer: 8,000

Explanation: Look at the hundreds digit (4). Since it is less than 5, you round down. The thousands digit stays 8, and the rest become zeros.

6. A toy was priced at $14.25. The tax was $1.15. Leo paid the clerk with a $20 bill. How much money should Leo get back?
Answer: $4.60

Explanation: First, find the total cost by adding the price and the tax: $\$14.25 + \$1.15 = \$15.40$.
Then, subtract the total cost from the amount paid: $\$20.00 - \$15.40 = \$4.60$.

7. What fraction of the letters in the following word are I's?
MISSISSIPPI
Answer: 4/11

Explanation: Count the total number of letters in the word, which is 11. Then count the number of "I"s, which is 4. The fraction is $\frac{4}{11}$.

Use the information below to answer problems 8-10:
In the first 12 games of the season, the Valley High soccer team won 9 games and lost 3 games. They won their next game by a score of 8 to 4. The team will play 18 games in all.
8. In the first thirteen games of the season, how many games did Valley High win?
Answer: 10 games

Explanation: They won 9 games out of their first 12. They also won their 13th game.
$9 + 1 = 10$ total wins.

9. Valley High won its thirteenth game by how many goals?
Answer: 4 goals

Explanation: The score of the thirteenth game was 8 to 4. The difference is $8 - 4 = 4$.

10. What is the greatest number of games Valley High could win this season?
Answer: 15 games

Explanation: They have currently played 13 games and won 10 of them. Since the season has 18 games total, there are 5 games remaining ($18 - 13 = 5$). If they win all the remaining games, their maximum possible wins would be $10 + 5 = 15$.

11. Compare: $3 \times 4 \times 8 \bigcirc 8 \times 3 \times 4$
Answer: =

Explanation: Both sides equal 96. The commutative property of multiplication states that changing the order of the numbers does not change the product.

12. $A - 150 = 300$
Answer: A = 450

Explanation: To isolate $A$, add 150 to both sides of the equation.
$A = 300 + 150 = 450$.

13. $B + 150 = 300$
Answer: B = 150

Explanation: To isolate $B$, subtract 150 from both sides of the equation.
$B = 300 - 150 = 150$.

15. $9.75 - (3.20 + 0.5)$
Answer: 6.05

Explanation: Perform the operation in the parentheses first:
$3.20 + 0.50 = 3.70$.
Next, subtract that sum from 9.75:

16. $324 \times 40$
Answer: 12,960

Explanation: First, multiply $324 \times 4$, which equals $1296$. Then, attach a zero at the end because we are multiplying by 40, resulting in $12960$.

17. $415 \times 6$

18. $\$4.15 \times 9$
Answer: $37.35

Explanation: Ignore the decimal temporarily: $415 \times 9 = 3735$. Place the decimal point two spots from the right to get $\$37.35$.

19. $3 \enclose{longdiv}{966}$
Answer: 322

Explanation: $9 \div 3 = 3$. $6 \div 3 = 2$. $6 \div 3 = 2$. The quotient is 322.

20. $4 \enclose{longdiv}{531}$
Answer: 132 R 3

Explanation: $5 \div 4 = 1$ (R1). Bring down 3 to make 13. $13 \div 4 = 3$ (R1). Bring down 1 to make 11. $11 \div 4 = 2$ (R3). The quotient is 132 with a remainder of 3.

21. $6 \enclose{longdiv}{4200}$
Answer: 700

Explanation: $42 \div 6 = 7$. Add the remaining two zeros to get 700.

22. $\$18.60 \div 6$
Answer: $3.10

Explanation: $18 \div 6 = 3$. Bring down 6. $6 \div 6 = 1$. Bring down 0. $0 \div 6 = 0$. Keep the decimal place to get $\$3.10$.

23. $\$35.42 \div 7$
Answer: $5.06

Explanation: $35 \div 7 = 5$. Bring down 4. $4 \div 7 = 0$ (R4). Bring down 2 to make 42. $42 \div 7 = 6$. Keep the decimal place to get $\$5.06$.

24. $\$63.00 \div 9$
Answer: $7.00

Explanation: $63 \div 9 = 7$. Bring down the two 0s to get 00. Keep the decimal place to get $\$7.00$.

25. $8^2 = 8 \times P$
Answer: P = 8

Explanation: $8^2$ is the same as $8 \times 8$, which is 64. The equation is $64 = 8 \times P$. Dividing both sides by 8 gives $P = 8$.

26. $5 \times Q = 5 \times 10^2$
Answer: Q = 100

Explanation: Calculate $10^2$, which is 100. The equation simplifies to $5 \times Q = 5 \times 100$. Divide both sides by 5 to find $Q = 100$.

27. One fourth of the people at the park are wearing hats. What percent of the people are wearing hats?
Answer: 25%

Explanation: The fraction $\frac{1}{4}$ represents one fourth. As a percentage, one fourth is equal to $25\%$.

28. What is the perimeter of this rectangle in millimeters?

Answer: 120 mm

Explanation: First, find the perimeter in centimeters. The sides are 4 cm, 2 cm, 4 cm, and 2 cm.
Perimeter = $4 + 2 + 4 + 2 = 12$ cm.
Since there are 10 millimeters in a centimeter, multiply by 10.
$12 \text{ cm} \times 10 = 120 \text{ mm}$.

29. Draw a rectangle that is similar to the rectangle in problem 28 and whose sides are twice as long. What is the perimeter in centimeters of the rectangle you drew?
Answer: 24 cm

Explanation: If the original sides were 4 cm and 2 cm, the new rectangle has sides twice as long: 8 cm and 4 cm.
Perimeter = $8 + 4 + 8 + 4 = 24$ cm.

30. A gymnast completed one and a half flips in the air. How many degrees did the gymnast turn?
Answer: 540°

Explanation: One full flip (a circle) is 360 degrees. If the gymnast flipped 1.5 times, multiply by 1.5.
$1.5 \times 360 = 540$ degrees.
