Problem 4
Two fifths of the 25 balloons were yellow. How many balloons were yellow? Draw a picture to illustrate the problem.

Answer: 10 balloons
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Find one fifth of the total group by dividing by 5: \(25 \div 5 = 5\).
  • Multiply by 2 to find two fifths: \(5 \times 2 = 10\).
Problem 5
Tim is 5 years younger than Brad. Brad is 2 years older than Linda. Linda is 11 years old. How old is Tim?
Answer: 8 years old
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Start with Linda's known age: 11 years old.
  • Calculate Brad's age (2 years older than Linda): \(11 + 2 = 13\).
  • Calculate Tim's age (5 years younger than Brad): \(13 - 5 = 8\).

Problem 10

Answer: 36 mm
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The total length (AD) is 89 mm.
  • Subtract the lengths of AB and CD from the total to find the missing segment BC: \(89 - 17 - 36\).
  • \(89 - 17 = 72\). Then \(72 - 36 = 36\). (Note: Your worksheet showed 44mm due to a subtraction error.)
