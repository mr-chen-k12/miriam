Problem 17
Multiply:
$$\begin{array}{r} 67 \\ \times\ 32 \\ \hline \end{array}$$
Answer: 2,144
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiply by the ones place digit (\(2\)):
    $$67 \times 2 = 134$$
  • Multiply by the tens place digit (\(3\), which represents \(30\)):
    $$67 \times 30 = 2010$$
  • Add the partial products together to secure the final answer:
    $$134 + 2010 = 2144$$

Problem 19
Divide: \(625 \div 3\) (or \(3 \overline{)625}\))
Answer: 208 R 1
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Divide the hundreds digit: \(6 \div 3 = 2\) exactly.
  • Divide the tens digit: \(2 \div 3 = 0\) with a remainder of \(2\).
  • Bring down the ones digit (\(5\)) to form \(25\), then divide by \(3\): \(25 \div 3 = 8\) with a remainder of \(1\).
  • Combine terms into standard quotient and remainder notation:
    $$625 \div 3 = 208 \text{ R } 1$$
