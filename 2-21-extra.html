Reviewing Problem 4: Percentages of Time
Practice 4.1
Twenty-five percent of an hour is how many minutes?
Answer:
15 minutes
An hour has 60 minutes. 25% is one-quarter. $60 \times 0.25 = 15$.
Practice 4.2

Answer:
45 minutes

Practice 4.3

Reviewing Problem 9: Comparing Products and Sums
Practice 9.1
The product of 5 and 8 is how much greater than the sum of 5 and 8?
Answer:
27
Product: $5 \times 8 = 40$. Sum: $5 + 8 = 13$. Difference: $40 - 13 = 27$.
Practice 9.2
The product of 9 and 4 is how much greater than the sum of 9 and 4?
Answer:
23
Product: $9 \times 4 = 36$. Sum: $9 + 4 = 13$. Difference: $36 - 13 = 23$.
Practice 9.3
The product of 7 and 3 is how much greater than the sum of 7 and 3?
Answer:
11
Product: $7 \times 3 = 21$. Sum: $7 + 3 = 10$. Difference: $21 - 10 = 11$.

Reviewing Problem 21: Coin Fractions and Percents
Practice 21.1
a. A dime is what fraction of a dollar?
b. A dime is what percent of a dollar?
Answer:
a. $\frac{1}{10}$ (or $\frac{10}{100}$)
b. $10\%$
A dime is 10 cents out of 100. As a fraction, this simplifies to $\frac{1}{10}$. As a percentage, it is $10\%$.
Practice 21.2
a. A quarter is what fraction of a dollar?
b. A quarter is what percent of a dollar?
Answer:
a. $\frac{1}{4}$ (or $\frac{25}{100}$)
b. $25\%$
A quarter is 25 cents out of 100. As a fraction, this simplifies to $\frac{1}{4}$. As a percentage, it is $25\%$.
Practice 21.3
a. Three pennies are what fraction of a dollar?
b. Three pennies are what percent of a dollar?
Answer:
a. $\frac{3}{100}$
b. $3\%$
Three pennies equal 3 cents out of 100 cents. As a fraction, this is $\frac{3}{100}$. As a percentage, it is $3\%$.

Practice 22.1

Reviewing Problem 24: Angle Types
Practice 24.1
An angle measures exactly 120 degrees. What type of angle is this?
Answer:
Obtuse
An obtuse angle is greater than 90 degrees but less than 180 degrees.
Practice 24.2
An angle measures exactly 45 degrees. What type of angle is this?
Answer:
Acute
An acute angle is greater than 0 degrees but less than 90 degrees.
Practice 24.3
An angle measures exactly 90 degrees. What type of angle is this?
Answer:
Right Angle
A 90-degree angle forms a perfect square corner and is called a right angle.
