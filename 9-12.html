Question 1

Eight feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply the number of feet by 12:

8 feet × 12 inches = 96 inches

Question 2

Ethan handed the cashier a $40 bill to buy a board game that cost $23.45. How much change did he receive back?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the cost of the board game from $40.00:

$40.00 − $23.45 = $16.55

Question 3

Lucas is 5 years younger than Sophia. Sophia is twice as old as Liam. If Sophia is 16 years old, how old are Lucas and Liam?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Find Lucas's age: Lucas is 5 years younger than Sophia → 16 − 5 = 11 years old.

2. Find Liam's age: Sophia is twice as old as Liam → 16 ÷ 2 = 8 years old.

Lucas is 11 and Liam is 8.

Question 20

Chloe has 4 one-dollar bills, 3 quarters, 2 dimes, 3 nickels, and 4 pennies. What is her total amount of money?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

• 4 Dollar Bills = $4.00

• 3 Quarters = 3 × 25¢ = 75¢ ($0.75)

• 2 Dimes = 2 × 10¢ = 20¢ ($0.20)

• 3 Nickels = 3 × 5¢ = 15¢ ($0.15)

• 4 Pennies = 4¢ ($0.04)

Total: $4.00 + $0.75 + $0.20 + $0.15 + $0.04 = $5.14

Question 33 (Bonus PEMDAS Challenge)

Solve the following expression using the Order of Operations (PEMDAS):

(15 − 7) × 4 + 18 ÷ 3

(Hint: Remember PEMDAS! First, solve what is inside the parentheses. Next, do all multiplication and division from left to right. Finally, perform addition and subtraction from left to right.)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Parentheses First: (15 − 7) = 8

Rewrite: 8 × 4 + 18 ÷ 3

2. Multiplication & Division (Left to Right):

• 8 × 4 = 32

• 18 ÷ 3 = 6

Rewrite: 32 + 6

3. Addition: 32 + 6 = 38

Final Answer: 38
