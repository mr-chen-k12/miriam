Math Lesson Solutions (Problems 1 - 30)

1. Seven thousand, three hundred ninety-six is how much less than eleven thousand, eight hundred seventy-three?
Answer: 4,477

Explanation: Set up a subtraction problem to find the difference between the two numbers.
\( 11,873 - 7,396 = 4,477 \)
2. Shannon has five days to read a 200-page book. If she wants to read the same number of pages each day, how many pages should she read each day?
Answer: 40 pages

Explanation: Divide the total number of pages by the number of days.
\( 200 \div 5 = 40 \)
3. Julie ordered a book for $6.99, a dictionary for $8.99, and a set of maps for $5.99. What was the price for all three items?
Answer: $21.97

Explanation: Add the prices of the three items together.
\( \$6.99 + \$8.99 + \$5.99 = \$21.97 \)
4. The prince searched 7 weeks for the princess. For how many days did he search?
Answer: 49 days

Explanation: There are 7 days in a week. Multiply the number of weeks by 7.
\( 7 \times 7 = 49 \)
5. One third of the books were placed on the first shelf. What fraction of the books were not placed on the first shelf?
Answer: \( \frac{2}{3} \)

Explanation: The whole collection of books represents 1 (or \( \frac{3}{3} \)). Subtract the fraction placed on the shelf from the whole.
\( 1 - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{3}{3} - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3} \)
6. On a number line, what number is halfway between 2000 and 3000?
Answer: 2500

Explanation: To find the midpoint, add the two numbers together and divide by 2.
\( (2000 + 3000) \div 2 = 5000 \div 2 = 2500 \)
7. In the word HIPPOPOTAMI, what fraction of the letters are P's?
Answer: \( \frac{3}{11} \)

Explanation: Count the total number of letters in the word (there are 11). Then count the number of 'P's (there are 3). The fraction is the number of P's over the total letters.
8. Mary ran a 5-kilometer race. Five kilometers is how many meters?
Answer: 5,000 meters

Explanation: There are 1,000 meters in 1 kilometer. Multiply the number of kilometers by 1,000.
\( 5 \times 1000 = 5000 \)
9. \( 12 + 13 + 5 + N = 9 \times 8 \)
Answer: \( N = 42 \)

Explanation: Simplify both sides of the equation first.
Left side: \( 12 + 13 + 5 = 30 \)
Right side: \( 9 \times 8 = 72 \)
Now solve for N: \( 30 + N = 72 \rightarrow N = 72 - 30 \rightarrow N = 42 \)
10. What is the perimeter of this triangle?
Answer: 60 mm

Explanation: The perimeter is the total distance around the outside of the shape. Add the lengths of all three sides together.
\( 15 + 20 + 25 = 60 \)
11. The length of segment AB is 3.6 cm. The length of segment AC is 11.8 cm. What is the length of segment BC? Write a decimal subtraction problem and find the answer.
Answer: 8.2 cm

Explanation: Segment AC is the total length. Segment AB is a part of it. To find the remaining part (BC), subtract AB from AC.
Problem: \( 11.8 - 3.6 \)
Calculation: \( 11.8 - 3.6 = 8.2 \)
12. \( \$25 - (\$19.71 + 98¢) \)
Answer: $4.31

Explanation: First, convert 98¢ to dollars ($0.98) and solve the expression inside the parentheses.
\( \$19.71 + \$0.98 = \$20.69 \)
Next, subtract that sum from $25.00.
\( \$25.00 - \$20.69 = \$4.31 \)
13. \( 365 + 10^2 + 3^3 \)
Answer: 492

Explanation: First, evaluate the exponents.
\( 10^2 = 10 \times 10 = 100 \)
\( 3^3 = 3 \times 3 \times 3 = 27 \)
Then add all the numbers together: \( 365 + 100 + 27 = 492 \)
14. \( \$5.00 - \$2.92 \)
Answer: $2.08

Explanation: Subtract the decimals, remembering to borrow appropriately.
\( 5.00 - 2.92 = 2.08 \)
15. \( 36.21 - 5.7 \)
Answer: 30.51

Explanation: Align the decimals and add a placeholder zero to 5.7 (making it 5.70), then subtract.
\( 36.21 - 5.70 = 30.51 \)
16. \( 5 \times 6 \times 9 \)
Answer: 270

Explanation: Multiply the first two numbers, then multiply the result by the third number.
\( 5 \times 6 = 30 \)
\( 30 \times 9 = 270 \)
17. \( 50 \times 63 \)
Answer: 3,150

Explanation: Multiply \( 5 \times 63 \), then add a zero at the end.
\( 5 \times 60 = 300 \) and \( 5 \times 3 = 15 \) -> \( 315 \)
Attach the zero: 3,150.
18. \( 478 \times 6 \)
Answer: 2,868

Explanation: Use standard multiplication algorithms.
\( 6 \times 8 = 48 \) (carry 4)
\( 6 \times 7 = 42 + 4 = 46 \) (carry 4)
\( 6 \times 4 = 24 + 4 = 28 \)
Result: 2,868
19. \( 3 \overline{) 435 } \)
Answer: 145

Explanation: Use long division.
\( 4 \div 3 = 1 \) remainder 1.
Bring down 3 making it 13. \( 13 \div 3 = 4 \) remainder 1.
Bring down 5 making it 15. \( 15 \div 3 = 5 \).

Answer: 123 R 6

\( 8 \div 7 = 1 \) R 1
Bring down 6: \( 16 \div 7 = 2 \) R 2
Bring down 7: \( 27 \div 7 = 3 \) R 6.
21. \( 5 \overline{) \$13.65 } \)
Answer: $2.73

Explanation: Divide keeping the decimal in place.
\( 13 \div 5 = 2 \) R 3.
Bring down 6: \( 36 \div 5 = 7 \) R 1.
Bring down 5: \( 15 \div 5 = 3 \).
22. \( 453 \div 6 \)
Answer: 75 R 3 (or 75.5)

Explanation: Long division:
45 divided by 6 is 7 (since \( 6 \times 7 = 42 \)), remainder 3.
Bring down the 3 to make 33. 33 divided by 6 is 5 (\( 6 \times 5 = 30 \)), remainder 3.
23. \( 543 \div 4 \)
Answer: 135 R 3 (or 135.75)

Explanation: Long division:
\( 5 \div 4 = 1 \) R 1
Bring down 4 to make 14. \( 14 \div 4 = 3 \) R 2
Bring down 3 to make 23. \( 23 \div 4 = 5 \) R 3.
24. \( \$4.72 \div 8 \)
Answer: $0.59

Explanation: Place the decimal point above the division bracket. 4 cannot be divided by 8, so look at 47.
\( 47 \div 8 = 5 \) R 7.
Bring down 2 making it 72. \( 72 \div 8 = 9 \).

26. \( 4W = 132 \)
Answer: \( W = 33 \)

Explanation: "4W" means 4 times W. To find W, divide both sides by 4.
\( 132 \div 4 = 33 \)
27. \( 4 + 8 + 7 + 6 + 4 + N + 3 + 6 + 5 = 55 \)
Answer: \( N = 12 \)

Explanation: Add all the known numbers on the left side first.
\( 4 + 8 + 7 + 6 + 4 + 3 + 6 + 5 = 43 \)
The equation becomes: \( 43 + N = 55 \)
Subtract 43 from 55: \( 55 - 43 = 12 \)
28. Mieko was facing east. She heard "Simon says, 'Turn 90° clockwise.'" If Mieko turned correctly, in which direction would she be facing?
Answer: South

Explanation: Clockwise means turning to the right, just like the hands on a clock. A 90° turn from East to the right makes you face South.
29. If the diameter of a playground ball is one foot, then its radius is how many inches?
Answer: 6 inches

Explanation: First, convert the diameter to inches. 1 foot = 12 inches.
The radius is exactly half of the diameter.
\( 12 \text{ inches} \div 2 = 6 \text{ inches} \)
30. Which transformations would move \( \Delta ABC \) to position \( RST \)?
Answer: Translation (slide) and Reflection (flip)

Explanation: Notice the orientation of the triangles. In \( \Delta ABC \), the vertical straight edge is on the right side (line AC). In \( \Delta RST \), the vertical straight edge is on the left side (line RT).
This indicates that the triangle was reflected (flipped horizontally) to mirror its shape, and then translated (slid) to the right to reach the new position.
