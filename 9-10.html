Practice i
Point \(P\) is located at \(4\frac{1}{4}\text{ inches}\) and Point \(Q\) is located at \(8\frac{7}{8}\text{ inches}\) on a ruler. Find the distance \(PQ\).
Final Answer
\(PQ = 4\frac{5}{8}\text{ inches}\)

Subtract the smaller coordinate from the larger coordinate:

$$8\frac{7}{8} - 4\frac{1}{4} = 8\frac{7}{8} - 4\frac{2}{8} = 4\frac{5}{8}\text{ inches}$$

Final Answer
\(\frac{1}{32}\text{ inch}\)

The maximum measurement error is half of the smallest unit mark on the ruler scale:

$$\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{16} = \frac{1}{32}\text{ inch}$$

Problem 17 JUSTIFY
Provide reasons for steps (a) and (b) in solving \(15 + (m + 5) = 40\): $$\begin{align*} 15 + (m + 5) &= 40 && \text{Given equation} \\ 15 + (5 + m) &= 40 && \text{a. }\underline{\quad\quad\quad\quad} \\ (15 + 5) + m &= 40 && \text{b. }\underline{\quad\quad\quad\quad} \end{align*}$$
Final Answer

a. Commutative property of addition

b. Associative property of addition

• Step a reorders \(m + 5\) to \(5 + m\) (Commutative Property).

• Step b changes grouping from \(15 + (5 + m)\) to \((15 + 5) + m\) (Associative Property).

Problem 27
Compare: \(600 - (200 + 100) \bigcirc (600 - 200) + 100\)
Final Answer
\(<\)

Left side: \(600 - (200 + 100) = 600 - 300 = 300\)

Since \(300 < 500\), the correct comparison symbol is \(<\).

Problem 28
Parallelogram \(PQRS\) has two opposite acute angles (\(\angle Q\) and \(\angle S\)) and two opposite obtuse angles (\(\angle P\) and \(\angle R\)). Identify which angles are acute and which are obtuse.
Final Answer

Acute angles: \(\angle Q\) and \(\angle S\)

Obtuse angles: \(\angle P\) and \(\angle R\)

• Angles measuring less than \(90^\circ\) are acute (\(\angle Q, \angle S\)).

• Angles measuring greater than \(90^\circ\) are obtuse (\(\angle P, \angle R\)).

Problem 29
Refer to rectangle \(EFGH\):
a. Name a segment parallel to \(\overline{EF}\).
b. Name a segment perpendicular to \(\overline{EF}\).
Final Answer

a. \(\overline{HG}\) (or \(\overline{GH}\))

b. \(\overline{EH}\) (or \(\overline{FG}\))

a. Opposite sides of a rectangle are parallel (\(\overline{EF} \parallel \overline{HG}\)).

b. Adjacent sides meet at right angles (\(90^\circ\)), making \(\overline{EH}\) and \(\overline{FG}\) perpendicular to \(\overline{EF}\).

Problem 30 EXPLAIN
Explain the difference in mathematical meaning between the notations \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) and \(\overline{AB}\).
Final Answer
\(\overrightarrow{AB}\) represents a ray that originates at point \(A\) and extends infinitely through point \(B\), whereas \(\overline{AB}\) represents a line segment bounded by endpoints \(A\) and \(B\).

• \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) has one endpoint and extends infinitely in one direction.

• \(\overline{AB}\) has fixed endpoints at both ends and a finite, measurable length.

Problem 31 DECIMALS
Divide the terminating decimals: $$14.85 \div 0.6$$ Show all steps to find the exact decimal quotient.
Final Answer
\(24.75\)

Step 1: Make the divisor a whole number by moving the decimal point one position to the right in both the divisor and dividend:

$$14.85 \div 0.6 = 148.5 \div 6$$

Step 2: Divide long hand:

• \(14 \div 6 = 2\) remainder 2

• \(28 \div 6 = 4\) remainder 4 (place decimal point in quotient)

• \(45 \div 6 = 7\) remainder 3

• Bring down a zero: \(30 \div 6 = 5\) remainder 0

The division terminates completely, giving \(24.75\).

Problem 32 DECIMALS
Divide the non-terminating decimals: $$7.1 \div 0.3$$ Write the quotient as a repeating decimal using bar notation.
Final Answer
\(23.\bar{6}\)

Step 1: Shift decimal points one place to the right to make the divisor a whole number:

$$7.1 \div 0.3 = 71 \div 3$$

Step 2: Perform long division:

• \(7 \div 3 = 2\) remainder 1

• \(11 \div 3 = 3\) remainder 2

• Place decimal point and annex zeros: \(20 \div 3 = 6\) remainder 2

• \(20 \div 3 = 6\) remainder 2, repeating infinitely.

Because remainder 2 continually repeats, the digit 6 repeats endlessly. Expressed using bar notation: \(23.\bar{6}\).
