Question 1c Variations: Division with Extra Remainder Group
Question 1c - Variation 1

A tour guide has 34 tourists and can fit up to 6 people in each van. If all the remaining tourists form a final smaller group, how many vans are needed in all?

Divide the total number of tourists by the capacity of each van:

$$34 \div 6 = 5 \text{ full vans remainder } 4 \text{ tourists}$$

The 4 remaining tourists require 1 extra van. Therefore: $$5 + 1 = 6$$

Answer: 6 vans

Question 1c - Variation 2

An organizer places 75 books onto shelves that hold exactly 8 books each. If the leftover books are placed on a final shelf, how many shelves are used in total?

Divide the total books by shelf capacity:

$$75 \div 8 = 9 \text{ full shelves remainder } 3 \text{ books}$$

The 3 leftover books use 1 additional shelf. Therefore: $$9 + 1 = 10$$

Answer: 10 shelves

Question 1c - Variation 3

A camp counselor divides 47 campers into teams of 5. If the remaining campers form their own separate team, how many teams are there in total?

Divide the total campers by team size:

$$47 \div 5 = 9 \text{ teams remainder } 2 \text{ campers}$$

The 2 leftover campers form 1 extra team. Therefore: $$9 + 1 = 10$$

Answer: 10 teams

Question 3 Variations: Base Fee Plus Additional Hourly Rate
Question 3 - Variation 1

A bike rental shop charges \$5.00 for the first hour and \$2.50 for each additional hour. How much does it cost to rent a bike for 4 hours?

Calculate the breakdown for 4 hours:

  • First hour: \$5.00
  • Remaining hours: \(4 - 1 = 3\) hours
  • Cost for remaining hours: \(3 \times \$2.50 = \$7.50\)

$$\text{Total Cost} = \$5.00 + \$7.50 = \$12.50$$

Answer: \$12.50

Question 3 - Variation 2

A boat rental costs \$15.00 for the first hour and \$10.00 for each additional hour. What is the total cost for a 5-hour rental?

Calculate the breakdown for 5 hours:

  • First hour: \$15.00
  • Remaining hours: \(5 - 1 = 4\) hours
  • Cost for remaining hours: \(4 \times \$10.00 = \$40.00\)

$$\text{Total Cost} = \$15.00 + \$40.00 = \$55.00$$

Answer: \$55.00

Question 3 - Variation 3

An internet cafe charges \$2.00 for the first hour and \$1.20 for each additional hour. How much does it cost to use a computer for 3 hours?

Calculate the breakdown for 3 hours:

Question 12 Variations: Fractional Turns in Degrees
Question 12 - Variation 1

A dial is given a half turn clockwise. How many degrees was the dial turned?

A full circle turn is \(360^\circ\). A half turn is:

$$\frac{1}{2} \times 360^\circ = 180^\circ$$

Answer: 180°

Question 12 - Variation 2

A wheel makes a three-quarter turn counterclockwise. How many degrees did it turn?

A three-quarter turn is calculated as:

$$\frac{3}{4} \times 360^\circ = 270^\circ$$

Answer: 270°

Question 12 - Variation 3

An arrow on a game spinner makes a one-third turn clockwise. How many degrees did it turn?

A one-third turn is calculated as:

$$\frac{1}{3} \times 360^\circ = 120^\circ$$

Answer: 120°

Question 26 Variations: Visualizing Mixed Numbers and Improper Fractions
Question 26 - Variation 1

Draw and shade circles to visually demonstrate that \(1\frac{3}{4}\) equals \(\frac{7}{4}\).

Each circle is split into 4 equal quarters. Shading 1 full circle (\(\frac{4}{4}\)) and 3 quarters (\(\frac{3}{4}\)) yields 7 quarters total:

$$\frac{4}{4} + \frac{3}{4} = \frac{7}{4}$$

Question 26 - Variation 2

Draw and shade shapes to visually demonstrate that \(2\frac{1}{2}\) equals \(\frac{5}{2}\).

Each box is split into 2 halves. Shading 2 complete boxes (\(\frac{4}{2}\)) and 1 half (\(\frac{1}{2}\)) yields 5 halves total:

$$\frac{2}{2} + \frac{2}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{2}$$

Question 26 - Variation 3

Draw and shade circles to visually demonstrate that \(1\frac{2}{5}\) equals \(\frac{7}{5}\).

Each circle is split into 5 fifths. Shading 1 whole circle (\(\frac{5}{5}\)) and 2 fifths (\(\frac{2}{5}\)) yields 7 fifths total:

$$\frac{5}{5} + \frac{2}{5} = \frac{7}{5}$$

Question 27 & 28 Variations: Rectangle Dimensions, Perimeter, and Area
Question 27 & 28 - Variation 1

(27) Draw a rectangle that is 4 inches long and 2 inches wide.
(28) Find the perimeter and area of this 4 in. by 2 in. rectangle.

Perimeter: Sum of all sides

$$P = 4\text{ in} + 2\text{ in} + 4\text{ in} + 2\text{ in} = 12\text{ inches}$$

Area: Length \(\times\) Width

$$A = 4\text{ in} \times 2\text{ in} = 8\text{ square inches}$$

Answer: Perimeter = 12 inches; Area = 8 sq. inches
