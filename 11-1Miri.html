29.(49)
ESTIMATE A teacher orders two packs of 28 pens and two packs of 31 pencils. About how many writing tools did the teacher order? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.
Answer
About 120 writing tools
Solution
Round 28 to 30 and 31 to 30. Estimate: 2 × 30 + 2 × 30 = 120. The exact total is 2 × 28 + 2 × 31 = 118, so 120 is close.
30.(11, 17)
ESTIMATE A nature reserve covers 267 acres, and a nearby orchard covers 142 acres. Estimate their combined land area to the nearest hundred acres. Explain why your estimate is reasonable.
Answer
About 400 acres
Solution
Round 267 to 300 and 142 to 100, so 300 + 100 = 400 acres. Exact total = 409 acres, which is only 9 acres from the estimate.

31.(EXTENSION)
DECIMAL DIVISION Divide using long division. Show how you move the decimal point in both numbers. Does the answer terminate?
9.45 ÷ 0.6 = ______
Set up the long-division bracket and show your steps here.
Answer
15.75 — terminating decimal
Solution
Multiply the dividend and the divisor by 10 to make a whole-number divisor: 9.45 ÷ 0.6 = 94.5 ÷ 6. Write 94.50 so the division can continue into hundredths.
The remainder reaches 0. The result 15.75 terminates.
32.(EXTENSION)
REPEATING DECIMAL Divide using long division. Show several decimal places and indicate the repeating digit.
5.2 ÷ 0.3 = ______
Set up the long-division bracket and show your steps here.
Answer
17.3 = 17.3333…
Solution
Multiply both numbers by 10: 5.2 ÷ 0.3 = 52 ÷ 3. Continue the division by bringing down zeros after the decimal point.
The remainder is always 1 after each decimal 3, so the digit 3 repeats forever. The answer is 17.3, not a rounded or terminating decimal.
33.(EXTENSION)
PERCENTAGES & TIPS A restaurant bill is $42.50 before tip. You decide to leave an 18% tip. Find (a) the tip and (b) the total amount you pay, including the tip.
Answer
$7.65 tip; $50.15 total
Solution
Convert 18% to 0.18. Tip = 42.50 × 0.18 = $7.65. Total = $42.50 + $7.65 = $50.15.
34.(EXTENSION)
PEMDAS CHALLENGE Evaluate using the order of operations. Show every step. Include correct handling of negative numbers.
(−2)3 + 4.5 ÷ (32) − (−1.25 × 6)
Answer
2.5
Solution
First exponent: (−2)3 = −8. In parentheses, 32 = 1.5. Then divide: 4.5 ÷ 1.5 = 3. Multiply: −1.25 × 6 = −7.5. Substitute: −8 + 3 − (−7.5) = −8 + 3 + 7.5 = 2.5.
