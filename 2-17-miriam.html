29. A large pitcher holds 2 liters of water, which is about 67.6 fluid ounces. Estimate the number of 8-ounce cups you can fill from the pitcher. Explain your thinking.
About 8 cups.
Explanation: 67.6 rounds to 64 (which is a multiple of 8) or 70. Since \( 64 \div 8 = 8 \), you can fill about 8 cups.
