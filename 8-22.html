Question 1

Four feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply the number of feet by 12:

4 feet × 12 inches = 48 inches

Question 2

Marcus handed the cashier a $20 bill to buy a hardcover book. He received $6.35 back in change. How much did the book cost?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the change from the amount paid:

$20.00 − $6.35 = $13.65

Question 3

Emma is 4 years younger than Jacob. Jacob is twice as old as Liam. If Jacob is 16 years old, how old are Emma and Liam?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Find Emma's age: Emma is 4 years younger than Jacob. So, 16 − 4 = 12 years old.

2. Find Liam's age: Jacob is twice as old as Liam. So, divide Jacob's age by 2:

16 ÷ 2 = 8 years old.

Emma is 12 and Liam is 8.

Question 20

Oliver has 3 quarters, 4 dimes, 2 nickels, and 3 pennies in his pocket. What is the total value of his coins?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

• 3 Quarters = 3 × 25¢ = 75¢ ($0.75)

• 4 Dimes = 4 × 10¢ = 40¢ ($0.40)

• 2 Nickels = 2 × 5¢ = 10¢ ($0.10)

• 3 Pennies = 3 × 1¢ = 3¢ ($0.03)

Total: 75¢ + 40¢ + 10¢ + 3¢ = 128¢ = $1.28

Question 26

(c) XLII

Teacher Answer Key

(c) XLII = (50 − 10) + 1 + 1 = 42

Question 28

A small puppy weighs 2 pounds and 6 ounces. What is the puppy's total weight in ounces? (Hint: 1 pound = 16 ounces)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Convert 2 pounds to ounces: 2 × 16 = 32 ounces.

2. Add the extra 6 ounces: 32 + 6 = 38 ounces.

Question 29

A school library had 450 books. During the week, students checked out 125 books, and 80 books were returned. How many books were in the library at the end of the week?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Subtract checked-out books: 450 − 125 = 325 books.

2. Add returned books: 325 + 80 = 405 books.
