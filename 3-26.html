Cumulative Test 13C

1. David took 12 big steps to measure the length of the patio. If each step was one yard, then the length of the patio was how many feet?
Answer: 36 feet
Explanation: First, find the total length in yards: 12 steps × 1 yard/step = 12 yards. Since there are 3 feet in every yard, multiply the number of yards by 3: \( 12 \times 3 = 36 \). Therefore, the patio is 36 feet long.
2. Every third person in line receives a free hat. There are one hundred fifty-six people in line. How many people will receive a free hat?
Answer: 52 people
Explanation: "Every third person" means \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the total people. To find how many people get a hat, divide the total number of people by 3: \( 156 \div 3 = 52 \).
3. Five eighths of the team wore red jerseys. What fraction of the team did not wear red jerseys?
Answer: \(\frac{3}{8}\)
Explanation: The whole team represents \(\frac{8}{8}\). Subtract the fraction that wore red to find the fraction that did not: \(\frac{8}{8} - \frac{5}{8} = \frac{3}{8}\).
4. Compare: 50% of 18 \(\bigcirc\) \(3 \times 3\)
Answer: = (Equal)
Explanation: Evaluate the left side: 50% is the same as one-half (\(\frac{1}{2}\)). Half of 18 is 9. Evaluate the right side: \(3 \times 3 = 9\). Both sides equal 9, so \(9 = 9\).
5. What is the area of this square?
Answer: 81 sq mm (or \(81 \text{ mm}^2\))
Explanation: The area of a square is its side length multiplied by itself. Here, \(9 \text{ mm} \times 9 \text{ mm} = 81 \text{ sq mm}\).
6. Estimate the product of 68 and 7.
Answer: 490 (or 500)
Explanation: Round 68 to the nearest ten, which is 70. Leave 7 as is: \(70 \times 7 = 490\). The exact answer is 476, making 490 a very close estimate.
7. A train travels 45 miles each hour. How far will it travel in 6 hours?
Answer: 270 miles
Explanation: Multiply the hourly distance by the number of hours: \(45 \text{ miles/hour} \times 6 \text{ hours} = 270 \text{ miles}\).
8. Name the polygon.
Answer: Octagon
Explanation: Count the sides of the shape. A polygon with 8 sides is an octagon.
9. If each side of the regular polygon in problem 8 measures 5 units, then what is its perimeter?
Answer: 40 units
Explanation: Since it is a regular octagon (8 sides) and each side is 5 units, multiply the number of sides by the side length: \(8 \text{ sides} \times 5 \text{ units/side} = 40 \text{ units}\).
10. Compare. Draw and shade two congruent figures to show the comparison. \(\frac{1}{3} \bigcirc \frac{5}{12}\)
Answer: < (Less than)
Explanation: To compare them numerically, convert \(\frac{1}{3}\) to an equivalent fraction with a denominator of 12. \(\frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{12}\). Since \(4 < 5\), we know \(\frac{4}{12} < \frac{5}{12}\), making \(\frac{1}{3} < \frac{5}{12}\).
11. In a pet store, 72% of the animals are dogs. What percent of the animals are not dogs?
Answer: 28%
Explanation: The total number of animals represents 100%. Subtract the percentage of dogs to find the percentage of other animals: \(100\% - 72\% = 28\%\).

14. \(9 \times 4 \times 5\)
Answer: 180
Explanation: You can multiply in any order. Multiplying \(4 \times 5\) first gives 20. Then, \(9 \times 20 = 180\).
15. \(5^2 + 6^2\)
Answer: 61

20. Find the missing factor: \(7y = 91\)
Answer: 13
Explanation: To isolate the variable \(y\), divide both sides by 7. \(y = 91 \div 7\). \(y = 13\).
21. \(5,000 - 2,841\)
Answer: 2,159
Explanation: Borrow across the zeros. The 5 becomes a 4, passing 10 down the line until the ones column becomes 10.
  • Ones: \(10 - 1 = 9\)
  • Tens: \(9 - 4 = 5\)
  • Hundreds: \(9 - 8 = 1\)
  • Thousands: \(4 - 2 = 2\)
22. Identify the type of angle shown below (acute, right, or obtuse).
Answer: Acute

23. A movie started at 8:15 AM and ended at 11:30 AM. How long was the movie?
Answer: 3 hours and 15 minutes
Explanation: Count the hours from 8:15 AM to 11:15 AM, which is 3 hours. From 11:15 AM to 11:30 AM is an additional 15 minutes. Total time: 3 hours and 15 minutes.
24. \(4.56 \times 100\)
Answer: 456
Explanation: When multiplying a decimal by 100, move the decimal point two places to the right. \(4.56 \rightarrow 45.6 \rightarrow 456\).
25. Convert the mixed number \(3 \frac{2}{5}\) to an improper fraction.
Answer: \(\frac{17}{5}\)
Explanation: Multiply the whole number by the denominator: \(3 \times 5 = 15\). Add the numerator: \(15 + 2 = 17\). Place the result over the original denominator to get \(\frac{17}{5}\).
26. What is the area of this rectangle?
Answer: 40 sq in (or \(40 \text{ in}^2\))
Explanation: The area of a rectangle is found by multiplying its length by its width. \(10 \text{ in.} \times 4 \text{ in.} = 40 \text{ sq in}\).
27. If you roll a standard 6-sided number cube (die), what is the probability of rolling an even number? Express your answer as a simplified fraction.
Answer: \(\frac{1}{2}\)
Explanation: A 6-sided die has three even numbers (2, 4, 6) out of six total possible outcomes. The probability is \(\frac{3}{6}\), which simplifies to \(\frac{1}{2}\).
28. Find the average (mean) of the following numbers: 10, 15, and 20.
Answer: 15
Explanation: To find the average, first find the sum of all the numbers: \(10 + 15 + 20 = 45\). Then divide the sum by the count of the numbers (there are 3 numbers): \(45 \div 3 = 15\).
