Student Test Sheet

Question 1

Five yards equals how many feet? (Hint: 1 yard = 3 feet)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 yard = 3 feet.

2. Multiply the number of yards by 3:

5 yards × 3 feet = 15 feet

Question 2

Lucas handed the cashier a $20 bill to buy a board game that cost $14.35. How much change did he receive back?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the purchase cost from $20.00:

$20.00 − $14.35 = $5.65

Question 3

Zoe is 6 years older than Ethan. Ethan is half as old as Mason. If Mason is 18 years old, how old are Ethan and Zoe?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Find Ethan's age: Mason is 18, and Ethan is half as old → 18 ÷ 2 = 9 years old.

2. Find Zoe's age: Zoe is 6 years older than Ethan → 9 + 6 = 15 years old.

Ethan is 9 and Zoe is 15.

Question 5

One-fifth of the 30 students in Mrs. Davis's class ride the yellow school bus. How many students ride the school bus?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-fifth of a group, divide the total number of students by 5:

30 ÷ 5 = 6 students

Question 6

Looking back at Question 5, how many students do not ride the school bus?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the bus riders from the total class size:

30 − 6 = 24 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 73 and 29 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then, find the exact difference.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Estimate: Round 73 down to 70. Round 29 up to 30. Subtract: 70 − 30 = 40.

2. Exact Difference: Subtract the original numbers: 73 − 29 = 44.

Question 20

Ava has 2 one-dollar bills, 3 quarters, 3 dimes, 1 nickel, and 4 pennies. What is her total amount of money?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

• 2 Dollar Bills = $2.00

• 3 Quarters = 3 × 25¢ = 75¢ ($0.75)

• 3 Dimes = 3 × 10¢ = 30¢ ($0.30)

• 1 Nickel = 5¢ ($0.05)

• 4 Pennies = 4¢ ($0.04)

Total: $2.00 + $0.75 + $0.30 + $0.05 + $0.04 = $3.14

Question 26

Write the standard number for each Roman numeral:

(a) XIX

(b) XXXIV

(c) LVI

Teacher Answer Key

Answers:

(a) XIX = 10 + (10 − 1) = 19

(b) XXXIV = 10 + 10 + 10 + (5 − 1) = 34

(c) LVI = 50 + 5 + 1 = 56

Question 28

A shipping package weighs 3 pounds and 8 ounces. What is the total weight in ounces? (Hint: 1 pound = 16 ounces)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Convert 3 pounds to ounces: 3 × 16 = 48 ounces.

2. Add the remaining 8 ounces: 48 + 8 = 56 ounces.

Question 29

A toy store had 520 toy cars in stock. Over the weekend, they sold 180 cars, and then received a new shipment of 95 cars. How many toy cars are in stock now?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Subtract the cars sold: 520 − 180 = 340 cars.

2. Add the new shipment: 340 + 95 = 435 toy cars.
