Written Practice

Formulate: write and solve equations for problems 1–3.

1

A group of fish is called a school. There are thirty-four fish in the small school. There are one hundred twenty-eight fish in the big school. How many fewer fish are in the small school?

Answer: 94 fewer fish

Solution: Let \(f\) = how many fewer. Equation: \(128 - 34 = f\). Then \(f = 94\).

2

A piece of ribbon that measured 1 yard was cut into two pieces. If one piece was 15 inches long, how many inches long was the other piece?

Answer: 21 inches

Solution: 1 yard = 36 inches. Let \(p\) = the other piece. Equation: \(15 + p = 36\), so \(p = 36 - 15 = 21\) inches.

3

Mrs. Green took thirty-eight digital pictures in Hawaii. Her husband took fifty-four digital pictures in Hawaii. Her son took sixty-seven digital pictures. In all, how many digital pictures did the Greens take?

Answer: 159 digital pictures

Solution: Let \(t\) = total pictures. Equation: \(38 + 54 + 67 = t\). Then \(t = 92 + 67 = 159\).

4 Represent

Write the number 4,300,000 in expanded form. Then use words to write the number.

Answer: Expanded form: \(4{,}000{,}000 + 300{,}000\). In words: four million, three hundred thousand.

Solution: 4,300,000 has 4 millions and 3 hundred-thousands, so \(4{,}300{,}000 = 4{,}000{,}000 + 300{,}000\).

5

Which digit in 63,917,204 is in the thousands place?

Answer: 7

Solution: Reading place values from the right: ones 4, tens 0, hundreds 2, thousands 7, ten-thousands 1, hundred-thousands 9, millions 3, ten-millions 6.

6 Connect

Use a dollar sign and a decimal point to write the value of four dollars, one quarter, three dimes, and two nickels. Then write that amount of money using words.

Answer: $4.65; in words: four dollars and sixty-five cents

Solution: \(\$4.00 + \$0.25 + 3(\$0.10) + 2(\$0.05) = 4.00 + 0.25 + 0.30 + 0.10 = \$4.65\).

7

Two gallons of milk are how many quarts of milk?

Answer: 8 quarts

Solution: There are 4 quarts in 1 gallon, so \(2 \times 4 = 8\) quarts in 2 gallons.

8

How many squares are shaded?

Answer: \(3\frac{1}{2}\) squares

Solution: Three squares are fully shaded, and the first square is cut by a diagonal with half shaded. Total: \(3 + \frac{1}{2} = 3\frac{1}{2}\).

9 Estimate

Use a ruler to find the length of the line segment below to the nearest quarter inch.

Answer: About \(2\frac{1}{4}\) inches

Solution: Line up the 0 mark of a ruler with one end of the segment and read the other end to the nearest quarter-inch mark. The printed segment measures about \(2\frac{1}{4}\) inches. (Sizes can vary slightly between printings — measure the segment in your book.)

10 Connect

Printed on the label of the milk container were these words and numbers:

1 gal (3.78 L)

Use this information to compare the following:

1 gallon \(\bigcirc\) 4 liters

Answer: 1 gallon \(<\) 4 liters

Solution: The label says 1 gallon = 3.78 liters, and \(3.78 < 4\), so 1 gallon is less than 4 liters.

11

Destiny began reading a book last night at the time shown on the clock. She read until midnight. How much time did Destiny spend reading last night?

Answer: 3 hours 15 minutes

Solution: The clock shows 8:45 (hour hand near 9, minute hand at the 9). From 8:45 to midnight is 3 hours 15 minutes.

12

a. (Multiple Choice) What type of angle is formed by the hands of the clock shown in problem 11?

  1. acute
  2. right
  3. obtuse
  4. straight

b. (Justify) How do you know that your answer to part a is correct?

Answer: a. A — acute

Solution: a. At 8:45 the hands form an angle of about 7.5°. b. An acute angle measures between 0° and 90°, and 7.5° falls in that range — the hands are very close together.

13 Compare

a. \(-41 \bigcirc -38\)

b. \(\$0.25 \bigcirc \frac{1}{4}\) of a dollar

Answer: a. \(-41 < -38\)   b. \(\$0.25 = \frac{1}{4}\) of a dollar

Solution: a. On the number line, \(-41\) is to the left of (farther from zero than) \(-38\), so \(-41 < -38\). b. \(\frac{1}{4}\) of a dollar \(= \$0.25\).

23 Represent

Draw an array of Xs to show \(6 \times 4\).

Answer: An array of 6 rows with 4 Xs in each row (24 Xs in all), as drawn above

Solution: \(6 \times 4\) means 6 groups of 4. Drawing 6 rows of 4 Xs shows 24 Xs total, and \(6 \times 4 = 24\).

Use words to write \(12\frac{3}{10}\).

Answer: twelve and three tenths

Solution: Write the whole-number part ("twelve"), then "and", then the fraction part ("three tenths").

25

a. (Connect) Three dimes are what fraction of a dollar?

b. Write the value of three dimes using a dollar sign and a decimal point.

Answer: a. \(\frac{3}{10}\) of a dollar   b. $0.30

Solution: A dime is \(\frac{1}{10}\) of a dollar, so three dimes \(= 3 \times \frac{1}{10} = \frac{3}{10}\) of a dollar \(= 3 \times \$0.10 = \$0.30\).

26 Multiple Choice

A rectangle has an area of 30 square inches. Which of these could be the length and width of the rectangle?

  1. 6 in. by 6 in.
  2. 10 in. by 3 in.
  3. 8 in. by 4 in.
  4. 5 in. by 5 in.

Answer: B — 10 in. by 3 in.

Solution: Check each: A. \(6 \times 6 = 36\); B. \(10 \times 3 = 30\) ✓; C. \(8 \times 4 = 32\); D. \(5 \times 5 = 25\).

27 Represent

Tarik measured the width of his notebook paper and said that the paper was \(6\frac{4}{6}\) inches wide. What is another way to write \(6\frac{4}{6}\)?

Answer: \(6\frac{2}{3}\) (six and two thirds)

Solution: Reduce the fraction part: \(\frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3}\), so \(6\frac{4}{6} = 6\frac{2}{3}\).

28 Justify

A gardener plans to build a fence around his 30-by-15-foot rectangular vegetable garden. Fencing for the garden can be purchased in 50-foot, 75-foot, or 100-foot rolls. Which roll of fencing should the gardener buy? Explain why.

Answer: The 100-foot roll

Solution: The garden's perimeter is \(2(30 + 15) = 90\) feet. The 50-foot and 75-foot rolls are both too short. The 100-foot roll is the smallest roll that covers the 90 feet needed (with 10 feet to spare).

29 Estimate

At a gardening center, one pair of gardening gloves costs $15.00, not including a sales tax of 75¢. What is a reasonable estimate of the cost of two pairs of gloves? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.

Answer: About $32

Solution: Two pairs cost \(2 \times \$15.00 = \$30.00\) plus about \(2 \times \$0.75 = \$1.50\) in tax, for an exact total of \$31.50. Rounding up to \$32 is a reasonable estimate because it is close to the exact total and leaves a small cushion.

30 Analyze

How many different three-digit numbers can you write using the digits 5, 3, and 0? Each digit may be used only once, and the digit 0 may not be used in the hundreds place. Label the numbers you write as even or odd.

Answer: 4 numbers: 530 (even), 503 (odd), 350 (even), 305 (odd)

Solution: The hundreds digit can only be 5 or 3 (not 0). Listing each once: with 5 first: 530, 503; with 3 first: 350, 305. That is 4 different numbers. Numbers ending in 0 are even; numbers ending in 3 or 5 are odd.
