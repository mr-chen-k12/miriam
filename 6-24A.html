Question 1

Six gallons equals how many quarts? (Hint: 1 gallon = 4 quarts)

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Know the rule: 1 gallon = 4 quarts.

2. Multiply the number of gallons by 4:

6 gallons × 4 quarts = 24 quarts

Question 2

Question 3

Chloe is 5 years older than Lucas. Lucas is 3 times as old as Ryan. If Chloe is 14 years old, how old is Ryan?

(a) Choose the expression that can help you find Lucas's age.

(b) After finding Lucas's age, choose the expression that can help you find Ryan's age.

(c) How old is Ryan? Show or explain your thinking.

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

(a) Chloe is 5 years older than Lucas, which means Lucas is 5 years younger than Chloe. The helpful expression is 14 − 5.

14 − 5 = 9, so Lucas is 9 years old.

(b) Lucas is 3 times as old as Ryan, which means we divide Lucas's age by 3 to find Ryan's age. The helpful expression is 9 ÷ 3.

(c) 9 ÷ 3 = 3, so Ryan is 3 years old.

Question 5

One-third of the 30 students in Mr. Davis's class voted to play kickball at recess. How many students voted to play kickball?

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-third of a group, divide the total number of students by 3:

30 ÷ 3 = 10 students

Question 6

Looking back at Question 5, how many students did not vote to play kickball?

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the students who voted for kickball from the total class size:

30 − 10 = 20 students

Question 9

Estimate the sum of 53 and 38 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then, find the exact sum.

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Estimate: Round 53 down to 50. Round 38 up to 40. Add them: 50 + 40 = 90.

2. Exact Sum: Add the original numbers together: 53 + 38 = 91.

Question 10

Ella tracked the number of books she checked out from the library over the last few weeks: 6, 4, 6, 9, 5.

(a) Which number of books shows up the most often? This is called the mode.

(b) How many books did she check out in all?

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Answers:

(a) Mode: 6. It appears twice, which is more than any other number.

(b) Total: 6 + 4 + 6 + 9 + 5 = 30 books.
