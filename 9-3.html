Question 1

Eight feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply the number of feet by 12:

8 feet × 12 inches = 96 inches

Question 2

Mia handed the cashier a $20 bill to buy a book that cost $14.35. How much change did she receive back?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the cost of the book from $20.00:

$20.00 − $14.35 = $5.65

Question 3

Mason is 4 years younger than Liam. Liam is two times as old as Chloe. If Liam is 16 years old, how old are Mason and Chloe?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Find Mason's age: Mason is 4 years younger than Liam → 16 − 4 = 12 years old.

2. Find Chloe's age: Liam is 2 times as old as Chloe → 16 ÷ 2 = 8 years old.

Mason is 12 and Chloe is 8.

Question 5

One-fifth of the 35 students in Mrs. Reed's class play soccer. How many students play soccer?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-fifth of a group, divide the total number of students by 5:

35 ÷ 5 = 7 students

Teacher Answer Key

Subtract soccer players from the total class:

35 − 7 = 28 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 76 and 32 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then, find the exact difference.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Question 14

A science field trip started at 8:45 AM and buses returned to school at 1:15 PM. How long did the trip last?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Question 17

Teacher Answer Key

Question 20

Oliver has 4 one-dollar bills, 1 quarter, 3 dimes, 2 nickels, and 6 pennies. What is his total amount of money?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

• 4 Dollar Bills = $4.00

• 1 Quarter = 25¢ ($0.25)

• 3 Dimes = 3 × 10¢ = 30¢ ($0.30)

• 2 Nickels = 2 × 5¢ = 10¢ ($0.10)

• 6 Pennies = 6¢ ($0.06)

Total: $4.00 + $0.25 + $0.30 + $0.10 + $0.06 = $4.71
