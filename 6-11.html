Q1 Age Word Problem
Fran is four years older than Eloise. Eloise is twice as old as Hector. If Hector is 5 years old, how old is Fran?
1. Start with Hector's age: 5 years old.
2. Find Eloise's age (twice Hector's age): $5 \times 2 = 10\text{ years old}$.
3. Find Fran's age (four years older than Eloise): $10 + 4 = 14\text{ years old}$.
Answer: 14 years old

Q3 Money / Stamps
It will cost $1.83 to mail the package. Maura put three 39-cent stamps on the package. How much more postage does the package need?
1. Calculate current postage cost: $3 \times 0.39 = \$1.17$.
2. Subtract current value from total required cost: $\$1.83 - \$1.17 = \$0.66$.
Answer: $0.66 (or 66¢)
Q4 Division with Remainders
Thirty-three desks were arranged in 6 rows as evenly as possible.
a. How many rows had exactly 5 desks?
b. How many rows had 6 desks?
1. Divide total desks by rows: $33 \div 6 = 5 \text{ R } 3$.
2. Base amount per row is 5 desks. The remainder of 3 means 3 rows get 1 extra desk ($5 + 1 = 6$).
3. Total rows with 6 desks = 3. Remaining rows ($6 - 3 = 3$) stay with 5 desks.
Answer:
a. 3 rows
b. 3 rows

Q6 Grid Decimals
a. What decimal number names the shaded part of this square?
b. What decimal number names the part that is not shaded?
The grid consists of 100 total small squares.
1. Counting from the visual, 4 full rows and 7 additional blocks are shaded gray: $40 + 7 = 47$ out of 100.
2. Unshaded portion is $100 - 47 = 53$ out of 100.
Answer:
a. 0.47

Q9 Metric Conversions
Beth bought a meter of ribbon and used about one fourth of it to make a bow. About how many millimeters of ribbon did Beth use to make the bow?
1. 1 meter is exactly equivalent to 1,000 millimeters.
2. Find one-fourth ($\frac{1}{4}$) of 1,000 mm: $$1,000 \div 4 = 250 \text{ mm}$$
Answer: About 250 mm
Q10 Area of a Rectangle
What is the area of this rectangle? (Dimensions: 80 ft by 50 ft)
$$\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{width}$$ $$\text{Area} = 80\text{ ft} \times 50\text{ ft} = 4,000\text{ sq ft}$$
Answer: 4,000 sq ft (or 4,000 ft²)
Q11 Fractions of a Group
There are 24 students in the class. Two thirds of them are reading books. How many students are reading books?
1. Find one-third ($\frac{1}{3}$) of 24 first: $24 \div 3 = 8$.
2. Double that amount to find two-thirds ($\frac{2}{3}$): $8 \times 2 = 16$.
Answer: 16 students

Q20 Missing Addend Equation
Find the missing addend:
$$2 + 5 + 6 + 9 + n + 5 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 49$$
1. Combine all known numbers: $$2 + 5 + 6 + 9 + 5 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 32$$ 2. Set up the simplified algebra equation: $$32 + n = 49$$ 3. Solve for $n$: $$n = 49 - 32 = 17$$
Answer: n = 17
