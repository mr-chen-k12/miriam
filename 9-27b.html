*1. Problem
Sophia is reading a novel with $185$ pages. Liam is reading a novel with $312$ pages. How many more pages will Liam read than Sophia? Write and solve an equation.
Answer:
Equation: $312 - 185 = p$
Solution: $p = 127$ pages

Explanation: Subtract Sophia's page count from Liam's page count: $312 - 185 = 127$.

*4. Problem
The square root of $64$ is how much less than five squared?
Answer:
$17$

Explanation:
1. Square root of $64$: $\sqrt{64} = 8$
2. Five squared: $5^2 = 25$
3. Difference: $25 - 8 = 17$.
*5. Represent
On $1\text{-cm}$ grid paper, draw a $7\text{ cm}$ by $3\text{ cm}$ rectangle.
a. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?
b. What is the area of the rectangle?
Answer:
a. Perimeter = $20\text{ cm}$
b. Area = $21\text{ cm}^2$ (or $21\text{ square centimeters}$)

Explanation:
• Perimeter: $P = 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width}) = 2 \times (7 + 3) = 2 \times 10 = 20\text{ cm}$.
• Area: $A = \text{length} \times \text{width} = 7 \times 3 = 21\text{ cm}^2$.

9. Compare
Compare: $8 \times 3 \bigcirc \sqrt{81}$
Answer:
$8 \times 3 > \sqrt{81}$

Explanation: $8 \times 3 = 24$ and $\sqrt{81} = 9$. Since $24 > 9$, the correct symbol is $>$.

*11. Represent
A number line between $3100$ and $3200$ has major intervals of $20$ units each ($3120, 3140, 3160, 3180$). To what number is an arrow pointing if it points to the second tick mark after $3100$?
Answer:
$3140$

Explanation: Each tick mark adds $20$ units. Starting at $3100$: two tick marks correspond to $3100 + (2 \times 20) = 3140$.

23. Represent
Draw a circle with a radius of $3\text{ inches}$. What is the diameter of the circle? Explain how you know.
Answer:
Diameter = $6\text{ inches}$

Explanation: The diameter of a circle is double its radius ($d = 2r$). Since $r = 3\text{ inches}$, $d = 2 \times 3\text{ inches} = 6\text{ inches}$.

*27. Multiple Choice
Look at the sequence below. Which of the following numbers is not in the sequence?
$$1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, \dots$$ a. $49$
b. $75$
c. $81$
d. $100$
Answer:
b. $75$

Explanation: The sequence consists of perfect squares ($1^2, 2^2, 3^2, 4^2, 5^2, 6^2, \dots$).
• $49 = 7^2$
• $81 = 9^2$
• $100 = 10^2$
$75$ is not a perfect square, so it is not part of this sequence.
*28. Formulate

Answer:
Equation: $214 + 148 = L$
Solution: $L = 362$ libraries

Explanation: Add Oregon's libraries ($214$) and Nevada's libraries ($148$): $214 + 148 = 362$.
