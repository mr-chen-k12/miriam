Test 7B

1

It is two hundred twenty miles from Lillian's house to her grandmother's house. Lillian has already driven one hundred fifteen of those miles. How many miles does Lillian have left to drive?

Answer: 105 miles

Solution: Subtract the miles driven from the total: \(220 - 115 = 105\) miles left to drive.

2

Use the digits 4, 8, and 7 once each to make an even number greater than 800.

Answer: 874

Solution: To be greater than 800, the hundreds digit must be 8 (a 7 or 4 in the hundreds place would make the number less than 800). To be even, the ones digit must be 4 (the only remaining even digit). That leaves 7 for the tens place: 874.

3

Draw a pattern of dots to show the multiplication of 3 and 4.

Answer: 3 rows of 4 dots (12 dots in all), as drawn above

Solution: \(3 \times 4\) means 3 groups of 4. Drawing 3 rows with 4 dots in each row shows \(3 \times 4 = 12\).

4

Seth wrote his birth date as 3/20/96.

(a) In what month was Seth born?

(b) In what year was Seth born?

Answer: (a) March   (b) 1996

Solution: In the form month/day/year, the first number 3 is the 3rd month (March), and 96 stands for the year 1996.

5

Draw two perpendicular lines.

Answer: Two lines that cross at a right angle (90°), as drawn above

Solution: Perpendicular lines meet to form square corners. The small square at the intersection marks the right angle.

6

This rectangle is 4 cm long and 2 cm wide. What is the area of the rectangle?

Answer: 8 square centimeters

Solution: Area = length × width = \(4 \times 2 = 8\). Counting the unit squares in the figure also gives 8.

7

What fraction of this circle is shaded?

Answer: \(\frac{1}{4}\)

Solution: The circle is divided into 8 equal wedges and 2 are shaded: \(\frac{2}{8} = \frac{1}{4}\).

8

Change this addition problem to a multiplication problem: \(7 + 7 + 7 + 7 + 7\)

Answer: \(5 \times 7\)

Solution: The number 7 is added 5 times, so the addition is the same as 5 groups of 7: \(5 \times 7 = 35\).

9

Round 64 to the nearest ten. Round 87 to the nearest ten. Then add the rounded numbers.

Answer: 150

Solution: 64 rounds to 60 (the ones digit 4 is less than 5). 87 rounds to 90 (the ones digit 7 is 5 or more). Then \(60 + 90 = 150\).

10

Is the value of 3 nickels and 4 dimes an even number of cents or an odd number of cents?

Answer: An odd number of cents (55¢)

Solution: 3 nickels = \(3 \times 5¢ = 15¢\); 4 dimes = \(4 \times 10¢ = 40¢\). Total: \(15 + 40 = 55\) cents, and 55 is odd.

11

The arrow is pointing to what number on this number line?

Answer: 50

Solution: Each small interval on the number line is 25 (0, 25, 50, 75, 100, …). The arrow points to the second mark after 0, which is 50 — halfway between 0 and 100.

12

(a) \(2 \times 2\)    (b) \(7 \times 7\)    (c) \(8 \times 8\)

Answer: (a) 4   (b) 49   (c) 64

Solution: \(2 \times 2 = 4\), \(7 \times 7 = 49\), \(8 \times 8 = 64\).

13

Forty-five is how much less than fifty-four?

Answer: 9 less

Solution: \(54 - 45 = 9\).

14

Find the square root: \(\sqrt{49}\)

Answer: 7

Solution: \(\sqrt{49}\) asks: what number times itself equals 49? Since \(7 \times 7 = 49\), \(\sqrt{49} = 7\).

15

Compare: \(67 + 22 \bigcirc 62 + 27\)

Answer: \(67 + 22 = 62 + 27\)

Solution: \(67 + 22 = 89\) and \(62 + 27 = 89\). Since \(89 = 89\), the two sides are equal.

16

Find each missing number: \(18 + W = 99\)

Answer: \(W = 81\)

Solution: \(W = 99 - 18 = 81\). Check: \(18 + 81 = 99\) ✓

17

\(\begin{array}{r} 656 \\ -\;\; X \\ \hline 303 \end{array}\)

Answer: \(X = 353\)

Solution: \(X = 656 - 303 = 353\). Check: \(656 - 353 = 303\) ✓

18

Use words to write \(4\frac{2}{3}\).

Answer: four and two thirds

Solution: Write the whole-number part ("four"), then "and", then the fraction ("two thirds").

19

Use digits to write six million.

Answer: 6,000,000

Solution: Six million = 6 followed by six zeros: 6,000,000.

20

\(\begin{array}{r} 922 \\ -\; 628 \\ \hline \end{array}\)

Answer: 294

Solution: \(\begin{array}{r} 922 \\ -\; 628 \\ \hline 294 \end{array}\) — ones: \(2 - 8\) needs borrowing, \(12 - 8 = 4\); tens: \(1 - 2\) needs borrowing, \(11 - 2 = 9\); hundreds: \(8 - 6 = 2\).
