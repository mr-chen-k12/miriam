Problem 9
What is the perimeter of the rectangle:
(a) in millimeters?
(b) in centimeters?
Answer: (a) 80 mm  |  (b) 8 cm
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • (a) Millimeters: Perimeter is the sum of all external linear side lengths:
    $$P = 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width}) = 2 \times (30\text{ mm} + 10\text{ mm}) = 2 \times 40\text{ mm} = 80\text{ mm}$$
  • (b) Centimeters: Convert the scale knowing that \(10\text{ mm} = 1\text{ cm}\):
    $$80\text{ mm} \div 10 = 8\text{ cm}$$
Problem 10
What is the area of the rectangle:
(a) in square millimeters?
(b) in square centimeters?
Answer: (a) 300 mm²  |  (b) 3 cm²
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • (a) Square Millimeters: Multiply length by width to evaluate interior space grid fields:
    $$\text{Area} = 30\text{ mm} \times 10\text{ mm} = 300\text{ mm}^2$$
  • (b) Square Centimeters: Convert dimensions into centimeters prior to multiplication:
    • \(30\text{ mm} = 3\text{ cm}\)
    • \(10\text{ mm} = 1\text{ cm}\)
    $$\text{Area} = 3\text{ cm} \times 1\text{ cm} = 3\text{ cm}^2$$
    (Note: Since \(1\text{ cm}^2 = 100\text{ mm}^2\), \(300\text{ mm}^2 \div 100 = 3\text{ cm}^2\).)
Problem 11
Santos figured the trip would take seven and a half hours. He left at 7 a.m. At what time did he think he would arrive?
Answer: 2:30 p.m.
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Break down the planned addition interval time block of 7 hours and 30 minutes starting from 7:00 a.m.
  • Add the 5 hours needed to reach the midday benchmark boundary line:
    $$7:00\text{ a.m.} + 5\text{ hours} = 12:00\text{ p.m. (Noon)}$$
  • Apply the remaining 2 hours and 30 minutes past noon:
    $$12:00\text{ p.m.} + 2\text{ hours } 30\text{ minutes} = 2:30\text{ p.m.}$$
Problem 12
What is the average number of days per month in the first three months of a common year?
Answer: 30 days
Step-by-Step Explanation:

    • February = \(28\) days (common year standard)
    • March = \(31\) days
  • Calculate the cumulative sum across these tracking fields:
    $$31 + 28 + 31 = 90\text{ days}$$
  • Divide by the total count of track items (\(3\)) to establish the average value metric:
    $$90 \div 3 = 30\text{ days per month}$$

Problem 27
What is the mode of the scores?
Answer: 7
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The **mode** is the value that appears most frequently in a dataset.
  • Count how often each score appears:
    • 6 occurs 1 time
    • 7 occurs 3 times
    • 8 occurs 2 times
    • 9 occurs 1 time
    • 10 occurs 2 times
  • Because 7 occurs most often, it is the mode.
Problem 28
What is the median of the scores?
Answer: 8
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The **median** is the middle value when a data set is ordered from least to greatest.
  • Arrange the 9 scores in order:
    $$6, \ 7, \ 7, \ 7, \ \mathbf{[8]}, \ 8, \ 9, \ 10, \ 10$$
  • The 5th value is the exact center, leaving 4 scores on either side. That middle value is 8.
Problem 29
What is the range of the scores?
Answer: 4
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The **range** is the difference between the highest and lowest values in a data set.
  • Identify the extremes from the sorted scores:
    • Maximum value = \(10\)
    • Minimum value = \(6\)
  • Subtract the minimum from the maximum:
    $$\text{Range} = 10 - 6 = 4$$
Problem 30
What is the mean of the scores?
Answer: 8
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The **mean** is the average value, calculated by dividing the sum of all elements by the total count of elements.
  • Find the sum of the 9 scores:
    $$6 + 7 + 7 + 7 + 8 + 8 + 9 + 10 + 10 = 72$$
  • Divide the sum by the total number of quiz items (\(9\)):
    $$\text{Mean} = 72 \div 9 = 8$$
