New Numbers – Same Concepts
Problem 1
Three hundred twenty pages were read in the first week. Altogether eight hundred fifty pages were read over two weeks. How many pages were read in the second week?

Subtract the first week's pages from the total.

$$ 850 - 320 $$

Calculation: $0-0=0$, $5-2=3$, $8-3=5$.

Answer: 530 pages
Problem 2
Jason paid $10.00 for a toy that cost $6.28. How much money should Jason get back?

Subtract cost from payment.

$$ \$10.00 - \$6.28 $$

Regrouping required across zeros:

  • $10 - 8 = 2$
  • $9 - 2 = 7$
  • $9 - 6 = 3$
Answer: $3.72

Problem 6
It is evening. What time was it 4 hours and 15 minutes ago according to this clock?

The clock shows 2:45 PM (Evening implies PM range, though typically afternoon/evening transition).

  1. Subtract 4 hours: $2:45 \rightarrow 1:45 \rightarrow 12:45 \rightarrow 11:45 \rightarrow 10:45$. (10:45 AM).
  2. Subtract 15 minutes: $10:45 - 15 = 10:30$.
Answer: 10:30 AM
Problem 7
To the nearest quarter inch, how long is the red line segment?

The line goes past 1 inch. It lands on the first quarter mark after the 1.

Answer: \( 1\frac{1}{4} \) inches
Problem 8
To what mixed number is the arrow pointing?

The scale is between 3 and 4. There are 8 spaces. The arrow points to the 7th mark.

Answer: \( 3\frac{7}{8} \)
Problem 9
Store A sold 450 items. Store B sold 920 items. How many fewer items did Store A sell?

Find the difference: $920 - 450$.

$0-0=0$. Borrow for tens: $12-5=7$. $8-4=4$.

Answer: 470 items
Problem 10
Find the missing number: $$ \begin{aligned} n \\ - \underline{125} \\ 475 \end{aligned} $$

Add the bottom two numbers to find the top number (minuend).

$$ 475 + 125 = 600 $$
Answer: 600
Problem 11
Find the missing number: $$ \begin{aligned} 600 \\ - \underline{\;\;s\;\;} \\ 240 \end{aligned} $$

Subtract the answer from the top number.

$$ 600 - 240 = 360 $$
Answer: 360
Problem 12
Solve for n: \( 8n = 72 \)

This asks: 8 times what equals 72?

$$ n = 72 \div 8 $$
Answer: 9
Problem 13

Answer: $4.87
Problem 14

Problem 15
Calculate: \( 45 \times 3 \)
  1. $3 \times 5 = 15$ (carry 1)
  2. $3 \times 4 = 12$. Add 1 = 13.
Answer: 135
Problem 16
Calculate: \( 30 + (7 \times 5) \)

Parentheses first: $7 \times 5 = 35$.

$$ 30 + 35 = 65 $$
Answer: 65
Problem 17
Calculate: \( 4 \times (6 + 4) \)

Parentheses first: $6 + 4 = 10$.

$$ 4 \times 10 = 40 $$
Answer: 40
Problem 18
Calculate: \( \$2.50 + 35\text{¢} + \$6 \)

Convert cents to dollars: $0.35$.

$$ 2.50 + 0.35 + 6.00 $$ $$ 2.85 + 6.00 = 8.85 $$
Answer: $8.85
Problem 19
Calculate: \( 0.90 - 0.45 \)

Subtract decimals.

$$ \begin{aligned} 0.90 \\ - \underline{0.45} \\ 0.45 \end{aligned} $$
Answer: 0.45
Problem 20
Calculate: \( \sqrt{81} + \sqrt{25} \)

$\sqrt{81} = 9$ (because $9 \times 9 = 81$)
$\sqrt{25} = 5$ (because $5 \times 5 = 25$)

$$ 9 + 5 = 14 $$
Answer: 14
