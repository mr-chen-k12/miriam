Cumulative Test 13B

1. Sarah took 14 big steps to measure the length of the hallway. If each step was one yard, then the length of the hallway was how many feet?
Answer: 42 feet
Explanation: First, find the total length in yards: 14 steps × 1 yard/step = 14 yards. Since there are 3 feet in every yard, multiply the number of yards by 3: \( 14 \times 3 = 42 \). Therefore, the hallway is 42 feet long.
2. Every fourth student plays an instrument. There are two hundred twelve students in all. How many students play an instrument?
Answer: 53 students
Explanation: "Every fourth student" means \(\frac{1}{4}\) of the total students. To find how many students play an instrument, divide the total number of students by 4: \( 212 \div 4 = 53 \).
3. Three sevenths of the students brought their lunch. What fraction of the students did not bring their lunch?
Answer: \(\frac{4}{7}\)
Explanation: The total number of students represents a whole, or \(\frac{7}{7}\). Subtract the fraction that brought lunch to find the fraction that did not: \(\frac{7}{7} - \frac{3}{7} = \frac{4}{7}\).
4. Compare: 20% of 40 \(\bigcirc\) \(2 \times 4\)
Answer: = (Equal)
Explanation: First, evaluate the left side: 20% is the same as \(\frac{1}{5}\). One-fifth of 40 is \(40 \div 5 = 8\). Next, evaluate the right side: \(2 \times 4 = 8\). Since both sides equal 8, the correct comparison is \(8 = 8\).
5. What is the area of this square?
Answer: 225 sq cm (or \(225 \text{ cm}^2\))
Explanation: The area of a square is calculated by multiplying its side length by itself (\(Area = side \times side\)). Here, \(15 \text{ cm} \times 15 \text{ cm} = 225 \text{ sq cm}\).
6. Estimate the product of 88 and 8.
Answer: 720 (or 900)
Explanation: To estimate, round the numbers to make multiplication easier. Round 88 up to 90. You can leave 8 as is: \(90 \times 8 = 720\). Alternatively, rounding 8 to 10 gives \(90 \times 10 = 900\). Both are acceptable estimates, though 720 is closer to the exact answer (704).
7. A cyclist rode 42 miles in one day. How far could he ride in 7 days?
Answer: 294 miles
Explanation: Multiply the daily distance by the number of days: \(42 \text{ miles/day} \times 7 \text{ days} = 294 \text{ miles}\).
8. Name the polygon.
Answer: Hexagon
Explanation: Count the number of sides on the shape. A polygon with 6 sides is called a hexagon.
9. If each side of the polygon in problem 8 measures 4 units, then what is its perimeter?
Answer: 24 units
Explanation: The perimeter is the total distance around the outside of a shape. Since it is a hexagon (6 sides) and each side is 4 units, multiply: \(6 \text{ sides} \times 4 \text{ units/side} = 24 \text{ units}\).
10. Compare. Draw and shade two congruent squares to show the comparison. \(\frac{1}{4} \bigcirc \frac{3}{8}\)
Answer: < (Less than)
Explanation: To compare them numerically, convert \(\frac{1}{4}\) to an equivalent fraction with a denominator of 8. \(\frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{8}\). Since \(2 < 3\), we know \(\frac{2}{8} < \frac{3}{8}\), so \(\frac{1}{4} < \frac{3}{8}\). The visual drawing confirms that more of the second square is shaded.
11. If 58% of the vehicles in a parking lot were cars, then what percent of the vehicles were not cars?
Answer: 42%
Explanation: The total number of vehicles represents 100%. Subtract the percentage of cars to find the percentage of other vehicles: \(100\% - 58\% = 42\%\).

13. \(\$15 - 7\text{¢}\)
Answer: $14.93
Explanation: First, rewrite 7 cents in dollar format as $0.07. Then subtract it from $15.00. \( \$15.00 - \$0.07 = \$14.93 \).
14. \(8 \times 5 \times 4\)
Answer: 160
Explanation: Multiply left to right. \(8 \times 5 = 40\). Then multiply \(40 \times 4 = 160\).
15. \(4^2 + 7^2\)
Answer: 65
Explanation: First, evaluate the exponents. \(4^2 = 4 \times 4 = 16\) and \(7^2 = 7 \times 7 = 49\). Then add the results together: \(16 + 49 = 65\).

17. \(432 \div 6\)
Answer: 72
Explanation: Use long division: 6 goes into 43 seven times (\(6 \times 7 = 42\)). Subtract 42 from 43 to get 1. Bring down the 2 to make 12. 6 goes into 12 two times exactly (\(6 \times 2 = 12\)). Therefore, the quotient is 72.
18. \(56 \div (56 \div 8)\)
Answer: 8
Explanation: According to the order of operations, perform the operation inside the parentheses first. \(56 \div 8 = 7\). Now substitute 7 back into the equation: \(56 \div 7 = 8\).

20. Find the missing factor: \(6m = 78\)
Answer: 13
Explanation: To isolate the variable \(m\), divide both sides of the equation by 6. \(m = 78 \div 6\). \(m = 13\).
