Proportions & Rates
Question 1
If 3 apples cost $2.00, how much would 9 apples cost?
Answer:
$6.00
9 apples is 3 times as many as 3 apples. So, multiply the cost by 3: $2.00 \times 3 = $6.00.
Question 2
A runner completes 2 miles in 16 minutes. At this steady pace, how many minutes will it take her to run 5 miles?
Answer:
40 minutes
First, find the time for 1 mile: $16 \div 2 = 8$ minutes per mile. For 5 miles: $8 \times 5 = 40$ minutes.

Fractions & Percentages of Time
Question 5
Twenty-five percent ($25\%$) of an hour is how many minutes?
Answer:
15 minutes
$25\%$ is one-fourth ($\frac{1}{4}$) of a whole. One-fourth of 60 minutes is $60 \div 4 = 15$ minutes.
Question 6
Seventy-five percent ($75\%$) of an hour is how many minutes?
Answer:
45 minutes
$75\%$ is three-fourths ($\frac{3}{4}$). Since $\frac{1}{4}$ is 15 minutes, $\frac{3}{4}$ is $15 \times 3 = 45$ minutes.

Multiples
Question 9
Which of these numbers is not a multiple of 6?
A. 18   B. 24   C. 32   D. 42
Answer:
C. 32
18 ($6 \times 3$), 24 ($6 \times 4$), and 42 ($6 \times 7$) are multiples. 32 is not divisible by 6 evenly.
Question 10
Which of these numbers is not a multiple of 8?
A. 16   B. 32   C. 48   D. 54
Answer:
D. 54
16 ($8 \times 2$), 32 ($8 \times 4$), and 48 ($8 \times 6$) are multiples. 54 is not divisible by 8 evenly.

Products vs. Sums
Question 13
The product of 8 and 4 is how much greater than the sum of 8 and 4?
Answer:
20
Product: $8 \times 4 = 32$. Sum: $8 + 4 = 12$. Difference: $32 - 12 = 20$.
Question 14
The product of 7 and 5 is how much greater than the sum of 7 and 5?
Answer:
23
Product: $7 \times 5 = 35$. Sum: $7 + 5 = 12$. Difference: $35 - 12 = 23$.

Comparing Expressions
Question 17
Compare: $(24 \div 4) \div 2 \bigcirc 24 \div (4 \div 2)$
Answer:
$<$ (Less than)
Left side: $(24 \div 4) \div 2 = 6 \div 2 = 3$. Right side: $24 \div (4 \div 2) = 24 \div 2 = 12$. Since $3 < 12$, use $<$.
Question 18
Compare: $(48 \div 8) \div 2 \bigcirc 48 \div (8 \div 2)$
Answer:
$<$ (Less than)
Left side: $(48 \div 8) \div 2 = 6 \div 2 = 3$. Right side: $48 \div (8 \div 2) = 48 \div 4 = 12$. Since $3 < 12$, use $<$.

Question 26
If $w - 2 = 8$, then which of these equations is not true?
A. $w + 2 = 12$
B. $w - 10 = 0$
C. $w - 5 = 6$
Answer:
C. $w - 5 = 6$
First, solve for $w$: $w = 8 + 2 = 10$. Test option C: $10 - 5 = 5$, not 6. Therefore, C is false.
