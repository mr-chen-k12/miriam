Question 1

Three feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Know the rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply the number of feet by 12:

3 feet × 12 inches = 36 inches

Question 2

Sarah handed the cashier a $10 bill to buy some stickers. She received $2.25 back in change. How much did the stickers cost?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the change from the money Sarah gave the cashier:

$10.00 − $2.25 = $7.75

Question 3

Leo is 5 years younger than Sam. Sam is three times as old as Max. If Sam is 15 years old, how old are Leo and Max?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Find Leo's age: Leo is 5 years younger than Sam. So, Leo is 15 − 5 = 10 years old.

2. Find Max's age: Sam is 3 times as old as Max. So, divide Sam's age by 3:

15 ÷ 3 = 5 years old (Max is 5 years old).

Leo is 10 and Max is 5.

Question 4

Write a fraction to show each of the following descriptions:

(a) 2 out of 6 slices of a cake eaten.

(b) 4 out of 7 apples picked.

(c) 7 out of 10 blocks used.

Teacher Answer Key

Answers:

(a) 2/6 (or 1/3)

(b) 4/7

(c) 7/10

Question 5

One-fourth of the 24 students in Mrs. Green's class voted to play tag at recess. How many students voted to play tag?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-fourth of a group, divide the total number of students by 4:

24 ÷ 4 = 6 students

Question 6

Looking back at Question 5, how many students did not vote to play tag?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the students who voted for tag from the total class size:

24 − 6 = 18 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 82 and 28 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then, find the exact difference.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Estimate: Round 82 down to 80. Round 28 up to 30. Subtract them: 80 - 30 = 50.

2. Exact Difference: Subtract the original numbers: 82 - 28 = 54.

Question 10

Noah tracked the number of laps he ran each day: 2, 4, 4, 3, 5.

(a) Which number of laps shows up the most often (the mode)?

(b) How many laps did he run in all?

Teacher Answer Key

Answers:

(a) Mode: 4 (It appears twice, which is more than any other number).

(b) Total: 2 + 4 + 4 + 3 + 5 = 18 laps.

Question 13

Calculate: $50.00 − ($15.25 + $5.75)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Always solve the numbers inside the parentheses first: $15.25 + $5.75 = $21.00.

2. Subtract that amount from $50.00:

$50.00 − $21.00 = $29.00
