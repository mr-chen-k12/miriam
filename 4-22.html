Practice Math Test

1. Sarah has seven days to complete a 210-page reading assignment. If she wants to read the same number of pages each day, how many pages should Sarah read daily?
Answer: 30 pages
Explanation: To find the daily page count, divide the total number of pages by the total number of days.
$$210 \div 7 = 30$$
2. When Sarah has read two fifths of her assignment, what fraction will she still have left to read?
Answer: $\frac{3}{5}$
Explanation: The entire assignment represents 1 whole, which can be written as $\frac{5}{5}$. Subtract the part she has read from the whole.
$$\frac{5}{5} - \frac{2}{5} = \frac{3}{5}$$
Use the information given below to answer problems 3-5.
Two hundred students voted for their favorite sport. Ninety voted for basketball. Soccer received thirty fewer votes than basketball. The remaining students voted for baseball.
3. How many students chose soccer as their favorite sport?
Answer: 60 students
Explanation: The problem states soccer received 30 fewer votes than basketball. Basketball received 90 votes.
$$90 - 30 = 60$$
4. How many students chose baseball as their favorite sport?
Answer: 50 students
Explanation: First, find the total votes for basketball and soccer: $90 + 60 = 150$.
Subtract this from the total number of students (200) to find the baseball votes:
$$200 - 150 = 50$$
5. Which sport received the fewest votes?
Answer: Baseball
Explanation: Compare the votes for each sport:
Basketball: 90
Soccer: 60
Baseball: 50
Baseball has the lowest number of votes.
6. How many hours equal 5 days?
Answer: 120 hours
Explanation: There are 24 hours in a single day. Multiply the number of days by 24.
$$5 \times 24 = 120$$
7. In the word MISSISSIPPI, what fraction of the letters are Is?
Answer: $\frac{4}{11}$
Explanation: First, count the total number of letters in MISSISSIPPI, which is 11.
Next, count the number of 'I's in the word, which is 4.
The fraction is the number of 'I's over the total letters: $\frac{4}{11}$.
8. A recipe calls for 8 liters of water. Eight liters is how many milliliters?
Answer: 8000 milliliters
Explanation: There are 1,000 milliliters in 1 liter. Multiply the number of liters by 1,000.
$$8 \times 1000 = 8000$$
9. Leo entered a 36-mile biking trail but stopped to rest after completing one third of the distance. How many miles did Leo bike before resting?
Answer: 12 miles
Explanation: To find one third of 36, multiply 36 by $\frac{1}{3}$ (or divide 36 by 3).
$$36 \div 3 = 12$$
10. What is the perimeter of this triangle?
Answer: 60 mm
Explanation: The perimeter is the total distance around the outside of the shape. Add the lengths of all three sides together.
$$15 + 20 + 25 = 60$$
11. The length of $\overline{AB}$ is 35 mm. The length of $\overline{AC}$ is 100 mm. What is the length of $\overline{BC}$?
Answer: 65 mm
Explanation: Point B lies between A and C, meaning the length of AC is the sum of AB and BC ($AC = AB + BC$). To find BC, subtract the length of AB from AC.
$$100 - 35 = 65$$
12. $\$80 - (\$22.50 + 75\text{¢})$
Answer: $56.75
Explanation: First, convert $75\text{¢}$ to dollars ($0.75). Then, solve the operations inside the parentheses by adding.
$$\$22.50 + \$0.75 = \$23.25$$
Next, subtract this sum from $\$80.00$.
$$\$80.00 - \$23.25 = \$56.75$$

16. $532 \div 5$   (Shown as $5 \overline{) 532}$)
Answer: 106 R 2
Explanation: Perform long division. 5 goes into 5 one time ($5 \times 1 = 5$). Subtract 5 from 5 to get 0. Bring down the 3. 5 goes into 3 zero times ($5 \times 0 = 0$). Bring down the 2 to make 32. 5 goes into 32 six times ($5 \times 6 = 30$). Subtract 30 from 32 to leave a remainder of 2.
17. $850 \div 7$   (Shown as $7 \overline{) 850}$)
Answer: 121 R 3
Explanation: Perform long division. 7 goes into 8 one time ($7 \times 1 = 7$). Subtract 7 from 8 to get 1. Bring down the 5 to make 15. 7 goes into 15 two times ($7 \times 2 = 14$). Subtract 14 from 15 to get 1. Bring down the 0 to make 10. 7 goes into 10 one time ($7 \times 1 = 7$). Subtract 7 from 10. The remainder is 3.
18. $5m = 350$
Answer: $m = 70$
Explanation: Isolate the variable $m$ by performing the inverse operation. Divide both sides by 5.
$$m = 350 \div 5$$ $$m = 70$$
19. Find the missing number $n$:
  320
  145
+   $n$
  800
Answer: $n = 335$
Explanation: First, add the two known numbers together.
$$320 + 145 = 465$$
Next, subtract that sum from the total to find the missing addend.
$$800 - 465 = 335$$
20. Compare: $5 \times 2^4 \bigcirc \sqrt{400} \times \sqrt{25}$
Answer: < (Less than)
Explanation: Evaluate both sides of the comparison.
Left side: Solve the exponent first ($2^4 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 16$), then multiply.
$$5 \times 16 = 80$$
Right side: Find the square roots ($\sqrt{400} = 20$ and $\sqrt{25} = 5$), then multiply.
$$20 \times 5 = 100$$
Since $80 \lt 100$, the correct symbol is $\lt$.
