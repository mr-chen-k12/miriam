Math Lesson 55: Problem Set

1. Sarah has 72 stickers. She wants to place them on pages with 8 stickers on each page. How many pages will she fill?
Equation: $72 \div 8 = p$
Solution: $p = 9$ pages.
Explanation: Dividing the total stickers by the stickers per page gives the number of pages. [cite: 3]
2. There are 45 apples in a crate. The grocer wants to put them into bags of 5. How many bags can the grocer make?
Equation: $45 \div 5 = b$
Solution: $b = 9$ bags.
Explanation: Dividing total apples by the size of each bag. [cite: 3]
3. Write four multiplication/division facts using the numbers 6, 9, and 54.
1. $6 \times 9 = 54$
2. $9 \times 6 = 54$
3. $54 \div 6 = 9$
4. $54 \div 9 = 6$ [cite: 3]
4. How many hours are there in exactly 2 days?
Answer: 48 hours.
Explanation: There are 24 hours in 1 day, so $24 \times 2 = 48$. [cite: 3]
5. Sequence: 32,000, 33,000, 34,000, 35,000...
a. Write a rule. b. Next term?
a. Rule: Add 1,000 to the previous term.
b. Next Term: 36,000 [cite: 3]
6. a. Round 8,247 to nearest hundred. b. Round to nearest ten.
a. Nearest Hundred: 8,200 (since 47 is less than 50).
b. Nearest Ten: 8,250 (since 7 is 5 or greater). [cite: 3]
7. Write the time "half past six in the evening" in digital form.
Answer: 6:30 P.M. [cite: 3]
8. One side of a square is 6 yards long. a. Perimeter? b. Area?
a. Perimeter: $6 \times 4 = 24$ yards.
b. Area: $6 \times 6 = 36$ square yards. [cite: 3]
9. How many circles are shaded?
Answer: $2 \frac{3}{4}$ (or 2.75) circles.
Explanation: Two circles are fully shaded, and the third is divided into four parts with three shaded.
10. Order of operations: $\sqrt{100} + (18 \div 2)$
Steps:
1. Parentheses: $18 \div 2 = 9$
2. Root: $\sqrt{100} = 10$
3. Addition: $10 + 9 = 19$
Result: 19

26. Product is 20. Which could NOT be the numbers?
Answer: C (4 and 6)
Explanation: $4 \times 6 = 24$, not 20. All other options equal 20.

30. Empire State (1,250 ft) vs Chrysler (1,046 ft). Difference?
Answer: 204 feet.
Explanation: $1,250 - 1,046 = 204$. I subtracted the height of the shorter building from the taller one.
