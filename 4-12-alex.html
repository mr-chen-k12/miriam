Simplify the following:
a. $-4 + -2 - -6 + -1$
b. $-5 + (+4) - (+2) - (-8)$
c. $+2 + -5 - +3 + +8 - -4$
d. $5 + (-2) - (-8) - (+6) + (+3)$
e. $7 - -2 + -6 - +4 + +1$
f. $(-15) - (+25) - (-35) + (-45)$

Explanations: Convert subtractions to additions of the opposite, then combine like signs.

  • a. $-4 - 2 + 6 - 1 = -7 + 6 = $ $-1$
  • b. $-5 + 4 - 2 + 8 = -7 + 12 = $ $5$
  • c. $2 - 5 - 3 + 8 + 4 = 14 - 8 = $ $6$
  • d. $5 - 2 + 8 - 6 + 3 = 16 - 8 = $ $8$
  • e. $7 + 2 - 6 - 4 + 1 = 10 - 10 = $ $0$
  • f. $-15 - 25 + 35 - 45 = -85 + 35 = $ $-50$

Written Practice

1. A pyramid with a pentagonal base has how many more edges than vertices?

Explanation: A pentagonal pyramid has 6 vertices (5 at the base, 1 at the apex) and 10 edges (5 around the base, 5 reaching to the apex). The difference is $10 - 6 = 4$.

Answer: 4

2. James weighed 6 lb 10 oz when he was born and 11 lb 4 oz at 3 months. How many pounds and ounces did James gain in 3 months?

Explanation: Subtract the birth weight from the 3-month weight.
$11\text{ lb } 4\text{ oz} - 6\text{ lb } 10\text{ oz}$. Since 4 oz is less than 10 oz, borrow 1 lb (16 oz) from the 11 lbs.
$10\text{ lb } (4+16)\text{ oz} = 10\text{ lb } 20\text{ oz}$.
Now subtract: $10\text{ lb } 20\text{ oz} - 6\text{ lb } 10\text{ oz} = 4\text{ lb } 10\text{ oz}$.

Answer: 4 lb 10 oz

3. There are 8 cats and 12 dogs at the animal shelter. What is the ratio of cats to dogs?

Explanation: The ratio is $\frac{\text{Cats}}{\text{Dogs}} = \frac{8}{12}$. Reduce the fraction by dividing by 4 to get $\frac{2}{3}$.

Answer: 2/3

4. A team's win-loss ratio was 4 to 3. If the team had played 28 games without a tie, how many games had it won?

Explanation: Total ratio parts = $4 \text{ (wins)} + 3 \text{ (losses)} = 7 \text{ parts}$.
Total games = 28. Divide total games by total parts: $\frac{28}{7} = 4 \text{ games per part}$.
Multiply by the win parts: $4 \times 4 = 16 \text{ wins}$.

Answer: 16 games

5. If Carlos tosses a coin and rolls a standard number cube, what is the probability of the coin landing tails up and the number cube stopping with a 5 on top?

Explanation: These are independent events. Multiply their probabilities.
$P(\text{Tails}) = \frac{1}{2}$. $P(5) = \frac{1}{6}$.
$P(\text{Tails and } 5) = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{12}$.

Answer: 1/12

6. a. What is the perimeter of this parallelogram?
b. What is the area of this parallelogram?

Explanation:
a. Perimeter is the distance around the outside. $P = 2(\text{base}) + 2(\text{side}) = 2(10) + 2(8) = 20 + 16 = 36\text{ cm}$.
b. Area is base $\times$ height. $A = 10\text{ cm} \times 7\text{ cm} = 70\text{ cm}^2$.

Answer: a. 36 cm; b. 70 cm$^2$

7. If each acute angle of a parallelogram measures $65^\circ$, then what is the measure of each obtuse angle?

Explanation: Consecutive angles in a parallelogram are supplementary (they add up to $180^\circ$).
$180^\circ - 65^\circ = 115^\circ$.

Answer: $115^\circ$

8. The center of this circle is the origin. The circle passes through (3, 0).
a. Estimate the area of the circle in square units by counting squares.
b. Calculate the area of the circle by using 3.14 for $\pi$.

Explanation:
a. Counting full and partial squares inside the circle roughly yields 28 to 29 squares.
b. The radius is 3 units. Area $= \pi r^2 \approx 3.14 \times (3)^2 = 3.14 \times 9 = 28.26$.

Answer: a. ~28-29 sq units; b. 28.26

9. Which ratio forms a proportion with $\frac{3}{5}$?
A $\frac{3}{10}$     B $\frac{6}{10}$     C $\frac{5}{3}$     D $\frac{9}{20}$

Explanation: A proportion is an equation stating two ratios are equal. Multiply the numerator and denominator of $\frac{3}{5}$ by 2 to get $\frac{6}{10}$. Option B is the equivalent fraction.

Answer: B

10. Complete this proportion: $\frac{4}{10} = \frac{a}{25}$

Explanation: Cross-multiply to solve for $a$:
$10 \times a = 4 \times 25$
$10a = 100$
$a = 10$

Answer: 10

11. What is the perimeter of the hexagon at right? Dimensions are in centimeters.

Explanation: Find the missing side lengths.
The unknown inner vertical side + 6 (right vertical side) = 15 (total left vertical side). So the inner vertical side is $15 - 6 = 9$.
The unknown inner horizontal side + 8 (top horizontal) = 18 (bottom horizontal). So the inner horizontal side is $18 - 8 = 10$.
Perimeter $= 15 \text{ (left)} + 8 \text{ (top)} + 9 \text{ (inner vertical)} + 10 \text{ (inner horizontal)} + 6 \text{ (right)} + 18 \text{ (bottom)} = 66\text{ cm}$.

Answer: 66 cm

Sale Price = $0.70 \times 8.50 = 5.95$.

16. Between which two consecutive whole numbers is $\sqrt{180}$?

Explanation: Find perfect squares near 180.
$13^2 = 169$ and $14^2 = 196$. Since 180 is between 169 and 196, $\sqrt{180}$ is between 13 and 14.

Answer: 13 and 14

17. Compare: $\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2 \bigcirc$ the probability of guessing the correct answer on two consecutive multiple-choice questions, each with 3 options.

Explanation:
Left side: $\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{9}$.
Right side: $P(\text{2 correct guesses}) = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{9}$.
They are equal.

Answer: $=$

18. Divide 0.835 by 0.04 and round the quotient to the nearest whole number.

Explanation: $0.835 \div 0.04 = 83.5 \div 4 = 20.875$.
Rounding to the nearest whole number gives 21.

Answer: 21

19. The average of four numbers is 15. What is the sum of the four numbers?

Explanation: Average $= \frac{\text{Sum}}{\text{Count}}$.
Therefore, $\text{Sum} = \text{Average} \times \text{Count} = 15 \times 4 = 60$.

Answer: 60

20. Write the prime factorization of 600 using exponents.

Explanation: Create a factor tree for 600.
$600 = 6 \times 100$
$6 = 2 \times 3$
$100 = 10 \times 10 = (2 \times 5) \times (2 \times 5)$
Combine all prime factors: $2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5 \times 5 = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5^2$.

Answer: $2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 5^2$

21. $-4 + -6 - -3 - +5$

Explanation: Change subtractions to adding the opposite.
$-4 - 6 + 3 - 5 = -10 + 3 - 5 = -7 - 5 = -12$.

Answer: $-12$

22. $4^3 + 3^2 \times 5 - \sqrt{144} \times 2^2$

Explanation: Follow order of operations.
Exponents and roots first: $64 + 9 \times 5 - 12 \times 4$.
Multiplication: $64 + 45 - 48$.
Addition and Subtraction: $109 - 48 = 61$.

23. How many blocks 1 inch on each edge would it take to fill a storage box that is 10 inches long, 8 inches wide, and 4 inches tall?

Explanation: Find the volume of the box in cubic inches.
$V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height} = 10 \times 8 \times 4 = 320$. Since each block is 1 cubic inch, it takes 320 blocks.

Answer: 320 blocks

24. Two fifths of the 50 students in the band were absent. How many students were present?

Explanation: If $\frac{2}{5}$ were absent, then $\frac{3}{5}$ were present.
$\frac{3}{5} \text{ of } 50 = (50 \div 5) \times 3 = 10 \times 3 = 30$.

Answer: 30 students

25. The distance a car travels can be found by multiplying the speed of the car by the amount of time the car travels at that speed. How far would a car travel in 5 hours at 75 kilometers per hour?
$\frac{75 \text{ km}}{1 \text{ hr}} \times \frac{5 \text{ hr}}{1}$

Explanation: Multiply the given fractions, canceling the "hr" units.
$75 \times 5 = 375\text{ km}$.

Answer: 375 km

26. Use the figure on the right to answer a - c.
a. What is the area of the shaded rectangle?
b. What is the area of the unshaded rectangle?
c. What is the combined area of the two rectangles?

27. A student measured the circumference and diameter of four circles. Then she divided the circumference by the diameter of each circle. Here are her answers:
3.15, 3.11, 3.18, 3.12
Find the average of the student's answers. Round the average to the nearest hundredth.

Explanation: Add the numbers and divide by 4.
$3.15 + 3.11 + 3.18 + 3.12 = 12.56$.
$12.56 \div 4 = 3.14$.
The number is already at the hundredths place.

Answer: 3.14

28. Elena was thinking of a three-digit counting number, and she asked Marco to guess the number. Describe how you can find the probability that Marco will guess correctly on his first try.

Explanation: The three-digit counting numbers go from 100 to 999. To find the total amount, subtract the lowest from the highest and add 1 ($999 - 100 + 1 = 900$). There are 900 possible numbers. Since Marco makes exactly 1 guess, his chance is 1 out of the 900 possibilities.

Answer: The probability is 1/900. (Find total numbers from 100 to 999, which is 900, and set the guess as 1 favorable outcome over 900).

29. The coordinates of three vertices of a triangle are (2, 4), (-2, 4), and (-2, -1). What is the area of the triangle?

Explanation: Plotting these points reveals a right triangle.
The base connects (-2, 4) and (2, 4). Length $= 2 - (-2) = 4$ units.
The height connects (-2, 4) and (-2, -1). Length $= 4 - (-1) = 5$ units.
$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 5 = 10\text{ sq units}$.

Answer: 10 square units

30. $\frac{3 \text{ gal}}{1} \times \frac{4 \text{ qt}}{1 \text{ gal}} \times \frac{2 \text{ pt}}{1 \text{ qt}}$

Explanation: Cancel out the units that appear in both the numerator and the denominator (gallons and quarts).
You are left with $3 \times 4 \times 2 \text{ pt} = 24 \text{ pints}$.
