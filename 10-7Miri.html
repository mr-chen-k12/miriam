Written Practice

1 Explain

Trinity is twelve years old. Trinity's mother is thirty-five years old. Trinity's mother is how many years older than Trinity? Describe this type of word problem.

Answer: 23 years older; it is a "how-many-more" (comparison/difference) problem.

Solution: \(35 - 12 = 23\). This is a comparison word problem — it asks for the difference between two amounts ("how many more/older"), so it is solved by subtraction.

2

Four hundred sixty-eight thousand, five hundred two boxes were in the warehouse. Use digits to write that number of boxes.

Answer: 468,502

Solution: "Four hundred sixty-eight thousand" = 468,000; "five hundred two" = 502. Together: 468,502.

3 Represent

Write the number 3905 in expanded form. Then use words to write the number.

Answer: Expanded form: \(3000 + 900 + 5\). In words: three thousand, nine hundred five.

Solution: 3905 has 3 thousands, 9 hundreds, 0 tens, and 5 ones, so \(3905 = 3000 + 900 + 0 + 5 = 3000 + 900 + 5\).

4 Verify

J'Maresh collected two hundred forty-three aluminum cans while volunteering at the recycling center. Leilani collected three hundred sixty-four aluminum cans. Was the total number of cans collected an even number or an odd number?

Answer: Odd

Solution: Total \(= 243 + 364 = 607\), which is odd. (Shortcut: odd + even = odd, and 243 is odd while 364 is even.)

Answer: one hundred and one hundredth

Solution: Write the whole-number part ("one hundred"), then "and", then the fraction part ("one hundredth").

6 Represent

Use digits and symbols to show that negative nineteen is greater than negative ninety.

Answer: \(-19 > -90\)

Solution: On the number line, \(-19\) is to the right of \(-90\) (closer to zero), so \(-19\) is greater: \(-19 > -90\).

7 Connect

Use a dollar sign and a decimal point to write the value of two dollars, one quarter, two dimes, and three nickels.

Answer: $2.60

Solution: \(\$2.00 + \$0.25 + 2(\$0.10) + 3(\$0.05) = 2.00 + 0.25 + 0.20 + 0.15 = \$2.60\).

8

The clock shows the time that Brayden arrived at school. School begins at 8:15 a.m. Was Brayden early or late for school? How many minutes early or late was he?

Answer: 2 minutes late

Solution: The clock shows 8:17 (hour hand just past 8, minute hand just past the 3). School begins at 8:15, so \(8{:}17 - 8{:}15 = 2\) minutes — Brayden was 2 minutes late.

9 Connect

a. Nine dimes are what fraction of a dollar?

b. Write the value of nine dimes using a dollar sign and a decimal point.

Answer: a. \(\frac{9}{10}\) of a dollar   b. $0.90

Solution: A dime is \(\frac{1}{10}\) of a dollar, so nine dimes \(= 9 \times \frac{1}{10} = \frac{9}{10}\) of a dollar \(= 9 \times \$0.10 = \$0.90\).

10

Haruto lives about 1 kilometer from school. One kilometer is how many meters?

Answer: 1000 meters

Solution: The prefix "kilo-" means 1000, so 1 kilometer = 1000 meters.

11

How many of these circles are shaded?

Answer: \(2\frac{2}{3}\)

Solution: Two whole circles are fully shaded. The third circle is divided into 3 equal parts with 2 parts shaded, so it shows \(\frac{2}{3}\). Together: \(2 + \frac{2}{3} = 2\frac{2}{3}\).

This rectangle illustrates eight squared. What multiplication fact is illustrated by the rectangle?

Answer: \(8 \times 8 = 64\)

Solution: "Eight squared" means \(8^2 = 8 \times 8\). The square is an 8-by-8 array: 8 rows of 8, which is 64 small squares in all.

24 Conclude

Write the next three numbers in this counting sequence:

\[\ldots,\ 470,\ 480,\ 490,\ 500,\ \underline{\phantom{000}},\ \underline{\phantom{000}},\ \underline{\phantom{000}},\ \ldots\]

Answer: 510, 520, 530

Solution: Each number is 10 more than the one before: \(480 - 470 = 10\), and so on. Continuing: \(500 + 10 = 510\), \(510 + 10 = 520\), \(520 + 10 = 530\).

26 Multiple Choice

Which of these does not equal \(9 + 9\)?

  1. \(2 \times 9\)
  2. \(9 \times 2\)
  3. \(3 \times 6\)
  4. nine squared

Answer: D — nine squared

Solution: \(9 + 9 = 18\). Check each choice: A. \(2 \times 9 = 18\) ✓; B. \(9 \times 2 = 18\) ✓; C. \(3 \times 6 = 18\) ✓; D. nine squared \(= 9^2 = 81 \neq 18\) ✗.

27

A realtor was writing an advertisement about houses for sale in town. The prices of five houses are listed below. Show how the realtor would arrange the prices in order from most expensive to least expensive.

$385,900    $189,000    $1,280,000    $476,000    $299,000

Answer: $1,280,000; $476,000; $385,900; $299,000; $189,000

Solution: Compare from the leftmost digit: $1,280,000 (millions) is greatest. Then compare hundred-thousands: $476,000 > $385,900 > $299,000 > $189,000.

28 Justify

Lake Huron is 206 miles long. Lake Superior is 350 miles long. Anastacia estimates that Lake Superior is about 140 miles longer than Lake Huron. Is Anastacia's estimate reasonable? Explain why or why not.

Answer: Yes, the estimate is reasonable.

Solution: The actual difference is \(350 - 206 = 144\) miles. Anastacia's estimate of 140 miles is only 4 miles away from the exact answer, so it is reasonable.

29 Analyze

Kyle wants to form rectangles using straws. He has two straws 6 inches long, two straws 4 inches long, and two straws 2 inches long. Using four straws attached at the ends, how many different rectangles can Kyle form? What are the perimeters of the rectangles?

Answer: 3 different rectangles; perimeters 20 in., 16 in., and 12 in.

Solution: A rectangle needs two pairs of equal sides. From the three lengths, Kyle can pair them as (6, 4), (6, 2), or (4, 2) — 3 different rectangles. Perimeters: \(2(6+4) = 20\) in.; \(2(6+2) = 16\) in.; \(2(4+2) = 12\) in.
