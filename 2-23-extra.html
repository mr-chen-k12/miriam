Reviewing Problem 2: Proportions & Rates
Practice 2.1
A recipe calls for 2 cups of sugar to make 1 batch of cookies. How many cups of sugar are needed to make 4 batches of cookies?
Answer:
8 cups
Equation: $2 \text{ cups} \times 4 \text{ batches} = 8 \text{ cups}$.
Practice 2.2
A car travels 40 miles on 1 gallon of gas. How many gallons of gas would the car need to travel 120 miles?
Answer:
3 gallons
Equation: $120 \div 40 = 3$.

Reviewing Problem 4: Percentages of Time
Practice 4.1
Fifty percent of an hour is how many minutes?
Answer:
30 minutes
50% is half. Half of 60 minutes is 30 minutes.
Practice 4.2
Ten percent of an hour is how many minutes?
Answer:
6 minutes
10% is one-tenth ($\frac{1}{10}$). $60 \div 10 = 6$.
Reviewing Problem 5: Estimation
Practice 5.1
A plane was flying at an altitude of 3,850 feet. It climbed an additional 1,120 feet. What is a reasonable estimate of the plane's new altitude?
Answer:
Approx. 5,000 feet
Round to the nearest hundred or thousand: $3900 + 1100 = 5000$. (Exact is 4,970).
Practice 5.2
A submarine is 1,950 feet below sea level. It dives another 880 feet deeper. Estimate the submarine's new depth.
Answer:
Approx. 2,900 feet
Round before adding the depths: $2000 + 900 = 2900$ feet below sea level. (Exact is 2,830).
Reviewing Problem 6: Multiples
Practice 6.1
Which of these numbers is not a multiple of 4?
A. 12   B. 18   C. 24   D. 32
Answer:
B. 18
Multiples of 4 are 12 ($4 \times 3$), 24 ($4 \times 6$), and 32 ($4 \times 8$). 18 is not divisible by 4 evenly.
Practice 6.2
Which of these numbers is not a multiple of 5?
A. 15   B. 25   C. 35   D. 42
Answer:
D. 42
Multiples of 5 always end in 0 or 5. 42 does not.
Reviewing Problem 8: Simple Equations
Practice 8.1
Solve for $x$: $5x = 5 \times 9$
Answer:
$x = 9$
Because both sides are multiplied by 5, $x$ must be 9. ($5x = 45 \rightarrow x = 9$).
Practice 8.2
Solve for $y$: $8y = 8 \times 3$
Answer:
$y = 3$
Because both sides are multiplied by 8, $y$ must be 3. ($8y = 24 \rightarrow y = 3$).
Reviewing Problem 9: Products vs. Sums
Practice 9.1
The product of 6 and 3 is how much greater than the sum of 6 and 3?
Answer:
9
Product: $6 \times 3 = 18$. Sum: $6 + 3 = 9$. Difference: $18 - 9 = 9$.
Practice 9.2
The product of 10 and 5 is how much greater than the sum of 10 and 5?
Answer:
35
Product: $10 \times 5 = 50$. Sum: $10 + 5 = 15$. Difference: $50 - 15 = 35$.

Reviewing Problem 11: Comparing Expressions
Practice 11.1
Compare: $(16 \div 4) \div 2 \bigcirc 16 \div (4 \div 2)$
Answer:
$<$ (Less than)
Left side: $(16 \div 4) \div 2 = 4 \div 2 = 2$. Right side: $16 \div (4 \div 2) = 16 \div 2 = 8$. Since $2 < 8$, use $<$.
Practice 11.2
Compare: $(36 \div 6) \div 3 \bigcirc 36 \div (6 \div 3)$
Answer:
$<$ (Less than)
Left side: $(36 \div 6) \div 3 = 6 \div 3 = 2$. Right side: $36 \div (6 \div 3) = 36 \div 2 = 18$. Since $2 < 18$, use $<$.
Reviewing Problem 21: Coin Fractions & Percents
Practice 21.1
a. A nickel is what fraction of a dollar?
b. A nickel is what percent of a dollar?
Answer:
a. $\frac{1}{20}$ (or $\frac{5}{100}$)
b. $5\%$
A nickel is 5 cents out of 100. As a fraction, this simplifies to $\frac{1}{20}$. As a percentage, it is $5\%$.
Practice 21.2
a. A half-dollar is what fraction of a dollar?
b. A half-dollar is what percent of a dollar?
Answer:
a. $\frac{1}{2}$ (or $\frac{50}{100}$)
b. $50\%$
A half-dollar is 50 cents out of 100. As a fraction, this simplifies to $\frac{1}{2}$. As a percentage, it is $50\%$.

Practice 22.1

1, 5, and 25

1, 3, and 9

Reviewing Problem 24: Angle Types
Practice 24.1
An angle measures exactly 135 degrees. What type of angle is this?
Answer:
Obtuse
An obtuse angle is greater than 90 degrees but less than 180 degrees.
Practice 24.2
An angle measures exactly 15 degrees. What type of angle is this?
Answer:
Acute
An acute angle is greater than 0 degrees but less than 90 degrees.
Reviewing Problem 26: Evaluating Equations
Practice 26.1
If $x - 5 = 10$, then which of these equations is not true?
A. $x + 2 = 17$
B. $x - 10 = 5$
C. $x + 5 = 25$
Answer:
C. $x + 5 = 25$
First find $x$: $x = 10 + 5 = 15$. Test option C: $15 + 5 = 20$, not 25. Therefore, C is false.
Practice 26.2
If $y + 4 = 12$, then which of these equations is not true?
A. $y - 4 = 4$
B. $y + 2 = 10$
C. $y - 2 = 8$
Answer:
C. $y - 2 = 8$
First find $y$: $y = 12 - 4 = 8$. Test option C: $8 - 2 = 6$, not 8. Therefore, C is false.

Reviewing Problem 30: Parallel and Perpendicular Segments
Practice 30.1 & 30.2
Refer to the rectangular prism below:

Practice 30.1: Name a segment that is parallel to segment AD.
Practice 30.2: Name a segment that is perpendicular to segment CG.
Answers:
30.1 Answer: BC, EH, or FG.
30.2 Answer: BC, DC, FG, or HG.
