Problem 1
There were 43 parrots in the flock. Some flew away. Then there were 27 parrots in the flock. How many parrots flew away?
Answer:

16 parrots flew away.

Equation: $43 - p = 27$
Solve: $p = 43 - 27 = 16$

Problem 4
On Saturday morning, Brady awoke at the time shown on the clock. Three hours later, he left home to go to softball practice. What time did Brady leave home?
Answer:

10:05 a.m.

1. The clock shows 7:05 a.m. (hour hand points just past 7, minute hand points to 1).
2. Adding 3 hours: $7:05 + 3\text{ hours} = 10:05\text{ a.m.}$

Problem 7
The rectangle shown at right is 4 cm long and 2 cm wide.
a. What is the perimeter?
b. What is the area?
Answer:

a. 12 cm
b. 8 sq cm (or $8\text{ cm}^2$)

a. $\text{Perimeter} = 4\text{ cm} + 2\text{ cm} + 4\text{ cm} + 2\text{ cm} = 12\text{ cm}$
b. $\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{width} = 4\text{ cm} \times 2\text{ cm} = 8\text{ sq cm}$

Problem 11
The number represented by point A is how much less than the number represented by point B?
Answer:

25 less

1. Each tick mark on the number line represents 5 units (since there are 5 intervals between 75 and 100).
2. Point A is at 85 ($75 + 10$).
3. Point B is at 110 ($100 + 10$).
4. Difference: $110 - 85 = 25$.

Problem 29
Estimate: The land area of Aztec Ruins National Monument in New Mexico is 318 acres. The land area of Casa Grande Ruins National Monument in Arizona is 473 acres. What is a reasonable estimate of the total acreage of these two national monuments? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.
Answer:

Estimated Total: 800 acres

Explanation:
Round each number to the nearest hundred:
• 318 rounds to 300.
• 473 rounds to 500.
$300 + 500 = 800\text{ acres}$.

This estimate is reasonable because 318 is slightly above 300 and 473 is slightly below 500, making 800 a very accurate estimate close to the exact sum ($318 + 473 = 791$).
Problem 30
Classify: Name each figure:
a. Vertical and horizontal rays forming a square corner.
b. Two straight slanted lines with arrows extending side-by-side.
c. Horizontal line segment intersecting a vertical line segment forming right angles.
d. Angle open wider than a right angle.
Answer:

a. Right angle
b. Parallel lines
c. Perpendicular line segments
d. Obtuse angle

• a: A $90^\circ$ angle is a right angle.
• b: Lines that stay equal distance apart and never intersect are parallel.
• c: Segments that intersect to form square ($90^\circ$) angles are perpendicular.
• d: An angle measuring greater than $90^\circ$ and less than $180^\circ$ is obtuse.
