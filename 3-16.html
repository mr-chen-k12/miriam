1. Seventy-five beans were equally divided into five pots. How many beans were in each pot?
Answer: 15 beans
Explanation: To find out how many beans go into each pot, you divide the total number of beans by the number of pots. $$75 \div 5 = 15$$
2. Find the perimeter and area of this rectangle. Remember to label your answer with "units" or "square units."
(Image shows a rectangle that is 8 units wide and 3 units high)
Answer: Perimeter = 22 units; Area = 24 square units
Explanation:
- Perimeter is the distance around the outside. Add all four sides: $8 + 3 + 8 + 3 = 22 \text{ units}$.
- Area is the space inside. Multiply length by width: $8 \times 3 = 24 \text{ square units}$.
3. The server placed a full pitcher of water on the table. Which of the following is a reasonable estimate of the amount of water in the pitcher?
A. 2 gallons    B. 2 quarts    C. 2 cups    D. 2 ounces
Answer: B. 2 quarts
Explanation: - 2 gallons is too large (like a large bucket).
- 2 cups is too small (about the size of one large drinking glass).
- 2 ounces is very small (like a shot glass).
- 2 quarts (half a gallon) is a standard and reasonable size for a restaurant water pitcher.
4. Which of these numbers is not a factor of 12?
A. 6    B. 5    C. 4    D. 3
Answer: B. 5
Explanation: Factors of 12 are numbers that divide evenly into 12. The factors of 12 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 12. You cannot multiply 5 by a whole number to get 12, so 5 is not a factor.
5. The starting time was before dawn. The stopping time was in the afternoon. What was the difference in the two times?
(Starting clock: ~5:15 AM. Stopping clock: ~1:35 PM)
Answer: 8 hours and 20 minutes
Explanation: First, read the clocks:
- Starting time (before dawn) is 5:15 AM.
- Stopping time (afternoon) is 1:35 PM.
Next, calculate the time elapsed:
- From 5:15 AM to 12:00 PM (noon) is 6 hours and 45 minutes.
- From 12:00 PM to 1:35 PM is 1 hour and 35 minutes.
- Add them together: $6\text{ hrs } 45\text{ mins} + 1\text{ hr } 35\text{ mins} = 7\text{ hrs } 80\text{ mins}$.
- Since 60 minutes = 1 hour, $7\text{ hrs } 80\text{ mins}$ becomes 8 hours and 20 minutes.
6. One square mile is 3,097,600 square yards. Use words to write that number of square yards.
Answer: Three million, ninety-seven thousand, six hundred
Explanation: Break the number 3,097,600 into its period blocks separated by commas: - Millions period: 3 -> "Three million" - Thousands period: 097 -> "ninety-seven thousand" - Ones period: 600 -> "six hundred"
7. What fraction of this pentagon is not shaded?
(Image shows a regular pentagon split into 5 triangles. 2 are shaded, 3 are unshaded.)
Answer: $\frac{3}{5}$
Explanation: The pentagon is divided into 5 equal parts. Looking closely at the image, 2 parts are shaded gray and 3 parts are left blank (unshaded). Therefore, the fraction that is NOT shaded is 3 out of 5, or $\frac{3}{5}$.
8. Is the shaded part of this pentagon more than 50% or less than 50% of the pentagon?
Answer: Less than 50%
Explanation: 2 out of the 5 sections are shaded. As a fraction, this is $\frac{2}{5}$. To convert to a percentage, multiply by 20: $\frac{2 \times 20}{5 \times 20} = \frac{40}{100} = 40\%$. Since 40% is less than 50%, the shaded part is less than 50% of the pentagon.

Answer: July 27, 2019
Explanation: Look at the column labeled "S" for Saturday at the far right of the calendar provided. The dates listed in that column are 6, 13, 20, and 27. The last number in that column is 27.
10. To what mixed number is the arrow pointing?
(Number line from 7 to 8 with 8 segments. Arrow points to the 5th tick mark after 7.)
Answer: $7 \frac{5}{8}$
Explanation: First, count the spaces between the whole numbers 7 and 8. There are 8 equal spaces, meaning each tick mark represents $\frac{1}{8}$. The arrow is pointing to the 5th tick mark past 7, so the mixed number is $7 \frac{5}{8}$.
11. Estimate the product of 78 and 4 by rounding 78 to the nearest ten before multiplying by 4.
Answer: 320
Explanation: First, round 78 to the nearest ten, which is 80. Then multiply that estimate by 4. $$80 \times 4 = 320$$
12. Compare: $2^3 \bigcirc 2 \times 3$
Answer: $>$
Explanation: First evaluate both sides:
$2^3$ means $2 \times 2 \times 2$, which equals 8.
$2 \times 3$ equals 6.
Because 8 is greater than 6, the correct symbol is $>$. ($8 > 6$).

27. Which two figures are congruent?
A. (Equilateral-looking triangle)    B. (Small right triangle)    C. (Right triangle)    D. (Right triangle, flipped)
Answer: C and D
Explanation: Congruent figures must be exactly the same size and shape (though they can be rotated or reflected). Looking at the options, triangles C and D are identical in size and shape, with D just being rotated/flipped compared to C.
28. How much money is $\frac{1}{4}$ of a dollar?
Answer: $\$0.25$ (or 25 cents)
Explanation: One dollar is equal to 100 cents. To find $\frac{1}{4}$ of a dollar, divide 100 by 4. $$100 \div 4 = 25$$ So, it is 25 cents, which is commonly represented by a quarter coin.
29. Draw a hexagon. A hexagon has how many vertices?
Answer: 6 vertices
Explanation: A hexagon is a polygon with 6 straight sides. Because it has 6 sides, it also has exactly 6 corners, which are called vertices.
30. Sean is using a line graph to keep a record of his test scores. There are 20 questions on each test. According to Sean's line graph, how many correct answers did he have on Test 3?
Answer: 16
Explanation: Locate "Test 3" on the horizontal (bottom) axis. Move straight up to where the point is plotted on the line graph. Then, look horizontally to the left to read the value on the vertical ("Number of Correct Answers") axis. The point aligns perfectly with the line for 16.
