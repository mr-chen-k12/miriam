Math Problem Set

1. Seventy-two children attend the morning session at a preschool. Forty-two children attend the afternoon session. How many children attend those sessions altogether?
Answer: \( 72 + 42 = 114 \)
2. Sherri needs \( \$35 \) to buy a baseball glove. She has saved \( \$18 \). How much more does she need?
Answer: \( \$35 - \$18 = \$17 \)
3. Draw a rectangle that is 4 cm long and 3 cm wide. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?
Answer: \( 4 + 3 + 4 + 3 = 14 \text{ cm} \)
4. Find the missing numbers: \( \dots, 12, \dots, \dots, 30, 36, \dots, \dots \)
Answer: Multiples of 6: \( 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48 \)
5. Find the missing numbers: \( \dots, 36, \dots, \dots, 24, 20, \dots, \dots \)
Answer: Decreasing by 4: \( 40, 36, 32, 28, 24, 20, 16, 12 \)
6. Change this addition problem to a multiplication problem: \( 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 \).
Answer: \( 7 \times 6 = 42 \)
7. a. Round 28 to the nearest ten.
b. Round \(\$12.29\) to the nearest dollar.
c. Round \(\$12.29\) to the nearest 25 cents.
Answer: a. \(30\), b. \(\$12\), c. \(\$12.25\)
8. A right triangle has one right angle. Draw a right triangle. Draw the two perpendicular sides 3 cm long and 4 cm long.
Answer: Draw a triangle with a 90-degree angle between a 3cm side and a 4cm side.
9. On Saturday morning, Mason went to the public library at the time shown on the clock (10:15). He arrived home 90 minutes later. What time did Mason arrive home from the library?
Answer: 10:15 + 1 hour 30 mins = \(11:45\)
10. What fraction of this group is shaded? (5 out of 12 circles)
Answer: \( \frac{5}{12} \)
11. Write 417 in expanded form. Then use words to write the number.
Answer: \( 400 + 10 + 7 \), Four hundred seventeen
12. What temperature is shown on this thermometer (-8°C)? If it increases by 10 degrees, what will it be?
Answer: a. \( -8^\circ\text{C} \), b. \( 2^\circ\text{C} \)
13. \( 76 - 29 \)
Answer: \( 47 \)
14. \( \$286 + \$388 \)
Answer: \( \$674 \)
15. \( \$73 - \$39 \)
Answer: \( \$34 \)
16. \( \$5.87 + \$2.43 \)
Answer: \( \$8.30 \)
17. \( 46 - c = 19 \)
Answer: \( c = 27 \)
18. \( n + 48 = 87 \)
Answer: \( n = 39 \)
19. \( 29 + y = 57 \)
Answer: \( y = 28 \)
20. \( d - 14 = 37 \)
Answer: \( d = 51 \)
21. \( 78 - 43 \)
Answer: \( 35 \)
22. \( 77 - 17 \)
Answer: \( 60 \)
23. \( 53 - 19 \)
Answer: \( 34 \)
24. Find each product:
a. \( 8 \times 11 \)
b. \( 7 \times 10 \)
c. \( 5 \times 12 \)
Answer: a. \( 88 \), b. \( 70 \), c. \( 60 \)
25. Compare: 1 yard \( \bigcirc \) 1 meter
Answer: \( 1 \text{ yard} < 1 \text{ meter} \)
26. Which of the following shows 3 ones and 4 hundreds?
A 304
B 403
C 4003
D 3400
Answer: B 403
27. The product of 9 and 3 is 27. How many times does this product appear in this lesson's multiplication table? What property of multiplication does this show?
Answer: Twice. Commutative property
28. Cheetah runs 70 mph. Elk runs 45 mph. Write and solve a subtraction equation to find the difference.
Answer: \( 70 - 45 = 25 \text{ mph} \)
29. D'Wayne bid \(\$37\) for one item and \(\$54\) for another. Reasonable estimate of total cost?
Answer: \( \$40 + \$50 = \$90 \). Rounding makes it easy to add mentally.
30. What is the tenth number in this counting sequence? \( 90, 80, 70, 60, 50, \dots \)
Answer: \( 0 \)
