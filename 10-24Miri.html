33.(Special · Percentages)
Percent A school library has 100 books on a display shelf. If 25% of the books are mystery books:
a. Express 25% as both a fraction in simple form and as a decimal.
b. How many mystery books are on the display shelf?
Answer
\(\text{a. Fraction: }\dfrac{1}{4},\quad \text{Decimal: }0.25\qquad\text{b. }25\text{ mystery books}\)
Solution
a. Percent means “out of 100.” \(25\% = \dfrac{25}{100} = \dfrac{1}{4} = 0.25\).
b. \(25\%\text{ of }100 = \dfrac{25}{100} \times 100 = 25\text{ mystery books}\).
34.(Special · PMDAS / Order of Operations)
Order of Operations Evaluate the expression step by step using PMDAS (Parentheses, Multiplication/Division, Addition/Subtraction): \[ \left(\frac{3}{4} + 0.25\right) \times 6 + (-2) \]
Answer
\(4\)
Solution
Follow PMDAS step by step:
Step 1 (Parentheses): Convert \(\frac{3}{4}\) to decimal \(0.75\). Then \(0.75 + 0.25 = 1.0\).
Step 2 (Multiplication): Multiply the result by 6: \(1.0 \times 6 = 6\).
Step 3 (Addition with Negative Number): Add \(-2\): \(6 + (-2) = 6 - 2 = 4\).
35.(Special · Multi-Step Application)
Application Maya bought 3 notebooks that cost \$1.25 each and 1 pack of pencils for \$2.25.
a. What was the total cost of her items?
b. If she paid with a \$10.00 bill, how much change did she receive?
Answer
\(\text{a. }\$6.00\qquad\text{b. }\$4.00\)
Solution
a. Cost of notebooks: \(3 \times \$1.25 = \$3.75\).
Total cost: \(\$3.75 + \$2.25 = \$6.00\).
b. Change received: \(\$10.00 - \$6.00 = \$4.00\).
