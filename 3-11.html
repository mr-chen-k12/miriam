Cumulative Test 12B Solutions

1. One thousand, two hundred eighty-two is how much greater than two hundred nineteen?

Explanation: To find how much greater one number is than another, subtract the smaller number from the larger number.

\(1282 - 219 = 1063\)

Answer: 1063

2. How many people could ride in thirty-five carts if seven people could ride in each cart?

Explanation: Multiply the number of carts by the number of people per cart.

\(35 \times 7 = 245\)

Answer: 245 people

3. Dora rode her bike 5 miles to her grandmother's house. The ride took 30 minutes. On average, how many minutes did it take Dora to ride each mile?

Explanation: Divide the total time by the total distance to find minutes per mile.

\(30 \text{ minutes} \div 5 \text{ miles} = 6 \text{ minutes/mile}\)

Answer: 6 minutes

4. The Gilbreth family drank 48 quarts of milk in 6 days. On average, how many quarts of milk did the Gilbreths drink each day?

Explanation: Divide the total quarts by the total number of days.

\(48 \div 6 = 8\)

Answer: 8 quarts

5. Andreas drove for 8 hours at an average speed of 45 miles per hour. How far did Andreas drive?

Explanation: Multiply the speed by the time to get the distance.

\(45 \text{ mph} \times 8 \text{ hours} = 360 \text{ miles}\)

Answer: 360 miles

6. What fraction of this rectangle is shaded?

Explanation: Count the shaded squares and divide by the total number of squares. There are 5 shaded squares out of 12 total squares.

\(\frac{5}{12}\)

Answer: 5/12

7. Ten decades is the same as how many centuries?

Explanation: One decade is 10 years, so 10 decades is \(10 \times 10 = 100\) years. One century is 100 years.

Answer: 1 century

8. Compare these fractions. Draw and shade two congruent circles to show the comparison: \(\frac{2}{3} \bigcirc \frac{1}{2}\)

Explanation: \(\frac{2}{3}\) (approx. 0.66) is greater than \(\frac{1}{2}\) (0.5).

Answer: \(\frac{2}{3} > \frac{1}{2}\)

9. Estimate the sum of 608 and 237 by rounding each number to the nearest hundred before adding.

Explanation: Round 608 down to 600. Round 237 down to 200. Add the rounded numbers.

\(600 + 200 = 800\)

Answer: 800

10. Which segment in this circle is a diameter?

Explanation: A diameter is a straight line segment that passes through the center of the circle and whose endpoints lie on the circle. In the image, segment WY passes through the center M.

Answer: Segment WY (or YW)

11. Find the seventh multiple of seven. Then subtract 19. What is the answer?

Explanation: The seventh multiple of seven is \(7 \times 7 = 49\). Subtracting 19 gives \(49 - 19 = 30\).

Answer: 30

\(\underline{-48,281}\)
\(\hphantom{-0}8,088\)

14. \( 6.48 - (1.6 + 2.44) \)

Explanation: Follow order of operations (PEMDAS) and calculate the parentheses first.
\(1.6 + 2.44 = 4.04\)
Then subtract from 6.48:
\(6.48 - 4.04 = 2.44\)

Answer: 2.44

15. \( 1000 - (12 \times 8) \)

Explanation: Calculate the parentheses first: \(12 \times 8 = 96\).
Then subtract: \(1000 - 96 = 904\).

Answer: 904

16. \( 760 \times 5 \)

Explanation: \(760 \times 5 = 3800\)

Answer: 3800

17. \( 377 \times 3 \)

Explanation: \(377 \times 3 = 1131\)

Answer: 1131

18. \( \sqrt{64} \div 4 \)

Explanation: First find the square root of 64, which is 8. Then divide by 4.
\(8 \div 4 = 2\)

Answer: 2

19. \( 59 \div 6 \)

Explanation: Divide 59 by 6. \(6 \times 9 = 54\).
Subtract 54 from 59 to find the remainder: \(59 - 54 = 5\).
The answer is 9 with a remainder of 5, or \(9 \frac{5}{6}\).

Answer: 9 R 5

20. Find the missing factor: \( 9r = 63 \)

Explanation: To find the missing factor \(r\), divide both sides by 9.
\(r = 63 \div 9\)
\(r = 7\)

Answer: r = 7
