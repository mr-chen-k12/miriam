1.
Rafael placed two 1-foot rulers end to end. What was the total length of the two rulers in inches?
Answer: 24 inches
Explanation:
One foot equals \(12\text{ inches}\).
Since there are two 1-foot rulers placed end to end: \[ 12\text{ inches} + 12\text{ inches} = 24\text{ inches} \]
2.
During the one-hour television show, there were 12 minutes of commercials. How many minutes of the hour were not commercials? Write an equation.
Answer: Equation: \(60 - 12 = m\); \(48\text{ minutes}\)
Explanation:
There are \(60\text{ minutes}\) in 1 hour.
Subtracting the commercial time yields: \[ 60 - 12 = 48\text{ minutes} \]
3. Multiple Choice
All the students lined up in two equal rows. Which could not be the total number of students?
  • A. 36
  • B. 45
  • C. 60
  • D. 24
Answer: B. 45
Explanation:
If students form two equal rows, the total number of students must be an even number (divisible by 2).
36, 60, and 24 are even numbers, but \(45\) is an odd number.

Explanation:

Answer: About 2 meters tall
Explanation:
\(1\text{ meter} \approx 3.28\text{ feet}\). A height of \(2\text{ meters}\) is approximately \(6\text{ feet } 7\text{ inches}\), which is a typical height for a professional basketball player.
9.
Jeong and her friends attended an evening movie that began at the time shown on the clock. The movie ended at 9:05 p.m. How long was the movie?
Answer: 1 hour 50 minutes (or 110 minutes)
Explanation:
The clock shows start time: **7:15 p.m.**
From 7:15 p.m. to 9:15 p.m. is \(2\text{ hours}\) (\(120\text{ minutes}\)).
9:05 p.m. is \(10\text{ minutes}\) before 9:15 p.m.: \[ 120\text{ minutes} - 10\text{ minutes} = 110\text{ minutes} = 1\text{ hour } 50\text{ minutes} \]
10. Conclude
Which street is parallel to Elm?
Answer: Oak (or Oak Street)
Explanation:
Parallel lines run in the same direction and never intersect. Elm and Oak both run horizontally, so Oak is parallel to Elm.

27. Explain
How is a segment different from a line?
Answer: A line segment has two distinct endpoints, while a line continues endlessly in both directions.
Explanation:
A **line** extends without end in both directions (has no endpoints). A **segment** (or line segment) is part of a line between two specific endpoints.
28. Estimate
Concert tickets cost $18 each, not including a $4.25 transaction fee for each ticket. What is a reasonable estimate of the cost to purchase two concert tickets? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.
Answer: About $44 (or $48 depending on rounding strategy)
Explanation:
* **Method 1:** Round \(\$18\) to \(\$20\) and \(\$4.25\) to \(\$4\). Cost per ticket \(\approx \$24\). Two tickets \(\approx 2 \times \$24 = \$48\).
* **Method 2:** Combine ticket + fee \(\$18 + \$4.25 = \$22.25\). Round to \(\$22\). Two tickets \(\approx 2 \times \$22 = \$44\).
Both estimates are reasonable because they replace exact values with numbers that are easy to calculate mentally.
