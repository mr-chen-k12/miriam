Cumulative Test 14B - Solutions

1. Farmer McGregor had 84 heads of cabbage in his garden. Peter picked one third of the heads of cabbage. How many heads of cabbage did Peter pick?

Answer: $28$ heads of cabbage

Explanation: To find one third of $84$, divide the total number by $3$.
$$84 \div 3 = 28$$

2. Thirty-five percent of the lights in the house were on. What percent of the lights were off?

Answer: $65\%$

Explanation: The total amount of lights represents $100\%$. Subtract the percentage of lights that are on to find the percentage that are off.
$$100\% - 35\% = 65\%$$

3. Each bagel contains an average of seven raisins. How many raisins are in 105 bagels?

Answer: $735$ raisins

Explanation: Multiply the number of bagels by the average number of raisins per bagel.
$$105 \times 7 = 735$$

4. Estimate the sum of 7362 and 8824 by rounding each number to the nearest thousand before adding.

Answer: $16,000$

Explanation:
Step 1: Round $7362$ to the nearest thousand $\rightarrow 7000$
Step 2: Round $8824$ to the nearest thousand $\rightarrow 9000$
Step 3: Add the rounded numbers:
$$7000 + 9000 = 16000$$

5. What is the value of 8 pennies, 4 dimes, 3 quarters, and 3 nickels? Write the answer using a dollar sign and decimal point.

Answer: $\$1.38$

Explanation: Convert each coin type to its monetary value and add them together:
8 pennies = $\$0.08$
4 dimes = $\$0.40$
3 quarters = $\$0.75$
3 nickels = $\$0.15$
$$\$0.08 + \$0.40 + \$0.75 + \$0.15 = \$1.38$$

6. One fourth of the students rode to school on the bus. There were 60 students in all. How many students rode the bus to school?

Answer: $15$ students

Explanation: Find one fourth of the total amount of students by dividing $60$ by $4$.
$$60 \div 4 = 15$$

7. What number is indicated on the number line below?

Answer: $-3$

Explanation: The arrow points to the 3rd tick mark to the left of $0$. Since each tick mark represents $1$ unit, the number is $-3$.

8. Katana has a hexagon and a quadrilateral. What is the total number of sides on the two polygons?

Answer: $10$ sides

Explanation: A hexagon has $6$ sides. A quadrilateral has $4$ sides.
$$6 + 4 = 10$$

9. a. The line segment shown below is how many centimeters long?
b. The segment is how many millimeters long?

Answer:
a. $4$ cm
b. $40$ mm

Explanation:
a. The segment starts at $0$ and ends exactly at the $4$ mark on the cm scale.
b. There are $10$ millimeters in $1$ centimeter. Multiply the cm measurement by $10$: $4 \times 10 = 40$ mm.

10. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?

Answer: $16$ cm

Explanation: The perimeter is the distance around the outside of the shape. Add all four sides together (two lengths of 5 cm, two widths of 3 cm).
$$5 + 5 + 3 + 3 = 16 \text{ cm}$$

11. What is the area of the rectangle in problem 10?

Answer: $15$ sq cm

Explanation: Area is calculated by multiplying length by width.
$$\text{Area} = 5 \times 3 = 15 \text{ sq cm}$$

15. $24 \times 20$

Answer: $480$

Explanation: You can multiply $24 \times 2 = 48$, and then append the zero for the tens place to get $480$.

16. $4^2 - \sqrt{4}$

Answer: $14$

Explanation: First, solve the exponents and roots.
$4^2 = 4 \times 4 = 16$.
$\sqrt{4} = 2$.
Then, subtract: $$16 - 2 = 14$$

17. $279 \div 4$

Answer: $69 \text{ R } 3$ (or $69.75$, or $69 \frac{3}{4}$)

Explanation: Use long division. $4$ goes into $27$ six times ($4 \times 6 = 24$), remainder $3$. Bring down the $9$ to make $39$. $4$ goes into $39$ nine times ($4 \times 9 = 36$), leaving a remainder of $3$.

18. $9 \times 7 \times 4$

Answer: $252$

Explanation: Multiply from left to right.
First: $9 \times 7 = 63$
Next: $63 \times 4 = 252$

19. $6.27 - 4.3$

Answer: $1.97$

Explanation: Append a zero to $4.3$ to align decimal places, making it $4.30$.
$$6.27 - 4.30 = 1.97$$

20. Find the missing factor: $7n = 98$

Answer: $n = 14$

Explanation: To isolate $n$, divide both sides of the equation by $7$.
$$n = 98 \div 7 = 14$$
