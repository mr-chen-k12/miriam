Question 4.1: Forty-five books were arranged on seven shelves as evenly as possible.
a. How many shelves had exactly 6 books?
b. How many shelves had 7 books?
Remaining Books: 45
Unassigned Stack
(45 Books)

The Math Explained Visually:

When you divide 45 books evenly among 7 shelves, you are calculating: $$\mathbf{45 \div 7 = 6 \text{ remainder } 3}$$

  • Round 1 through 6: Every single shelf gets an equal base share of 6 books ($7 \times 6 = 42$ books placed).
  • The Remainder: You are left with a remainder of 3 extra books. Because we must distribute as evenly as possible, these 3 books are placed one-by-one onto Shelves 1, 2, and 3.

Final Answer:
• (a) Shelves with exactly 6 books: 4 shelves (Shelves 4, 5, 6, and 7)
• (b) Shelves with 7 books: 3 shelves (Shelves 1, 2, and 3)
