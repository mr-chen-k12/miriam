Lesson 98: Geometric Solids & Mixed Practice

Context for Problems 1–3:
"In the Jones family there are 3 children. John is 10 years old. James is 2 years younger than Jill. James is 4 years older than John."
Problem 1
How old is James?

Answer: 14 years old

Explanation: The problem states that John is 10 years old and James is 4 years older than John. Therefore, $\text{James} = 10 + 4 = 14$ years old.

Problem 2
How old is Jill?

Answer: 16 years old

Explanation: We know from Problem 1 that James is 14 years old. The text states that James is 2 years younger than Jill, which means Jill is 2 years older than James: $\text{Jill} = 14 + 2 = 16$ years old.

Problem 3
When James is 16 years old, how old will Jill be?

Answer: 18 years old

Explanation: The age difference between siblings remains constant over time. Since Jill is always 2 years older than James, when James turns 16, Jill will be $16 + 2 = 18$ years old.

Problem 4
Denise bought an artichoke and 6 pounds of carrots for $2.76. If the artichoke cost 84¢, how much was 1 pound of carrots? (Hint: First find the cost of all the carrots.)

Answer: $0.32 (or 32¢) per pound

Explanation: First, find the total cost of all 6 pounds of carrots by subtracting the artichoke's price from the total expense:
$$\$2.76 - \$0.84 = \$1.92$$
Next, divide the cost of the carrots by 6 to find the price per pound:
$$\$1.92 \div 6 = \$0.32 \text{ (or } 32\text{¢)}$$

Problem 5
Write each mixed number as a decimal:
(a) $5 \frac{31}{1000}$      (b) $16 \frac{7}{10}$      (c) $5 \frac{7}{100}$

Answer: (a) 5.031    (b) 16.7    (c) 5.07

Explanation:
(a) $\frac{31}{1000}$ represents 31 thousandths, yielding $5.031$.
(b) $\frac{7}{10}$ represents 7 tenths, yielding $16.7$.
(c) $\frac{7}{100}$ represents 7 hundredths, yielding $5.07$.

Problem 6
Three fifths of the team's 40 points were scored in the first half. How many points did the team score in the first half? Draw a picture to illustrate the problem.

Answer: 24 points

Explanation: To find three-fifths ($\frac{3}{5}$) of 40, first compute one-fifth by dividing 40 by 5:
$$40 \div 5 = 8 \text{ points per fifth}$$
Now multiply by 3 to find three-fifths:
$$8 \times 3 = 24 \text{ points}$$

Problem 7
One fifth is 20%. What percent is three fifths?

Answer: 60%

Explanation: Since $\frac{1}{5} = 20\%$, three fifths ($\frac{3}{5}$) is three times that value:
$$3 \times 20\% = 60\%$$

Problem 8
Use words to write 7.68.

Answer: Seven and sixty-eight hundredths

Explanation: The integer part is 'seven', the decimal point is expressed as 'and', and the decimal positions terminate in the hundredths column ($68/100$).

Problem 9
Use words to write 76.8.

Answer: Seventy-six and eight tenths

Explanation: The integer part is 'seventy-six', followed by 'and', and the single decimal space represents tenths ($\frac{8}{10}$).

Problem 10
Estimate the product of 78 and 91.

Answer: 7200

Explanation: Round each value to the nearest ten before multiplying:
* 78 rounds up to 80
* 91 rounds down to 90
Now multiply the rounded components: $80 \times 90 = 7200$.

Problem 11
Name the number of shaded squares below:
(a) as a mixed number.
(b) as a decimal.

Answer: (a) $1 \frac{2}{10}$ (or $1 \frac{1}{5}$)    (b) 1.2

Explanation: The diagrams represent grids composed of 10 total subdivisions each. One entire box is fully colored in ($1$), and the adjacent block contains exactly 2 shaded components out of 10 ($\frac{2}{10}$).

Problem 12
There were 24 people in one line and 16 people in the other line. What was the average number of people per line?

Answer: 20 people per line

Explanation: Find the average by summing the total population and dividing by the count of lines:
$$\text{Total People} = 24 + 16 = 40$$
$$\text{Average} = 40 \div 2 = 20$$

Problem 13
It is evening. What time will it be 5 hours 20 minutes from now?
(Clock shows 8:40)

Answer: 2:00 AM

Explanation: The clock reads 8:40. Since it is evening, it is 8:40 PM.
* Adding 5 hours: $8:40\text{ PM} \rightarrow 1:40\text{ AM}$ (crossing midnight).
* Adding the remaining 20 minutes: $1:40\text{ AM} + 20\text{ mins} = 2:00\text{ AM}$.

Problem 14
Mr. Toto could bake 27 pizzas in 3 hours.
(a) How many pizzas could he bake in 1 hour?
(b) How many pizzas could he bake in 5 hours? (Hint: Multiply the answer to part (a) by 5.)

Answer: (a) 9 pizzas    (b) 45 pizzas

Explanation:
(a) Calculate the rate per hour: $27 \div 3 = 9$ pizzas/hour.
(b) Scale the hourly volume over 5 hours: $9 \times 5 = 45$ pizzas.

Problem 15
Find the sum: $3.65 + 4.2 + 0.625$

Answer: 8.475

Explanation: Carefully align the decimal placeholders vertically to perform addition:
$$\begin{array}{r@{\quad}l} 3.650\\ 4.200\\ +\;0.625\\ \hline 8.475 \end{array}$$

Problem 16
Evaluate: $\$13.70 - \$6.85$

Answer: $6.85

Explanation: Subtract line by line: $\$13.70 - \$6.85 = \$6.85$ (Note that $\$6.85 \times 2 = \$13.70$).

Problem 17
Evaluate: $26 \times 100$

Answer: 2600

Explanation: Multiplying a whole number by 100 is equivalent to shifting the base value left by two place orders, effectively appending two zeros to the end.

Problem 18
Evaluate: $9 \times 87\text{¢}$

Answer: 783¢ or $7.83

Explanation: Multiply the units straight across:
$$9 \times 87 = 783\text{¢}$$
Converting to standard currency form yields $\$7.83$.

Problem 19
Evaluate: $14 \times 16$

Answer: 224

Explanation: Can be structured via partial products or differences of squares:
$$14 \times 16 = (15 - 1)(15 + 1) = 15^2 - 1 = 225 - 1 = 224$$

Problem 20
Evaluate: $15^2$

Answer: 225

Explanation: Squaring a value means multiplying it by itself:
$$15 \times 15 = 225$$

Problem 21
Evaluate: $\frac{456}{6}$

Answer: 76

Explanation: Using division rules:
* 6 goes into 45 exactly 7 times ($7 \times 6 = 42$), leaving a remainder of 3.
* Combine 3 with the 6 to make 36. 6 goes into 36 exactly 6 times ($6 \times 6 = 36$).
Resulting Quotient = 76.

Problem 22
Evaluate:
$$\begin{array}{r} 47 \\ \times \quad 60 \\ \end{array}$$

Answer: 2820

Explanation: Drop down the zero from the 60 multiplier, then calculate $47 \times 6$:
$$6 \times 7 = 42 \text{ (write 2, carry 4)}$$
$$6 \times 4 = 24 + 4 = 28$$
Assembling the digits results in 2820.

Problem 23
Divide: $6 \overline{) 4248}$

Answer: 708

Explanation: Perform step-by-step long division:
1. $42 \div 6 = 7$.
2. Bring down the 4. 6 goes into 4 exactly 0 times. Place a 0 above.
3. Bring down the 8 to make 48. $48 \div 6 = 8$.
Total Quotient = 708.

Problem 24
Divide: $1 \overline{) 163}$

Answer: 163

Explanation: Any identity value divided by 1 retains its baseline structural numerical magnitude: $163 \div 1 = 163$.

Problem 25
Divide: $5 \overline{) \$49.00}$

Answer: $9.80

Explanation: Divide sequentially:
* $49 \div 5 = 9$ with a remainder of 4.
* Bring past decimal, making 40. $40 \div 5 = 8$.
* Concluding position yields 0. Result: $\$9.80$.

Problem 26
Solve for $P$: $1 + 3 + 5 + P + 7 + 3 + 2 + 3 = 44$

Answer: P = 20

Explanation: Group the known integers first and sum them:
$$1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 3 + 2 + 3 = 24$$
The simplified statement reads: $24 + P = 44$. Isolate variable $P$ by performing subtraction:
$$P = 44 - 24 = 20$$

Problem 27
How many floor tiles that are one-foot square are needed to cover the floor of a room that is 15 feet long and 10 feet wide?

Answer: 150 tiles

Explanation: Compute total area of the floor space using the rectangle property:
$$\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{width} = 15\text{ ft} \times 10\text{ ft} = 150\text{ sq ft}$$
Since every single tile perfectly occupies exactly $1\text{ sq ft}$, 150 individual tiles are required.

Problem 28
Find the median and mode of this set of numbers:
1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13

Answer: Median = 3, Mode = 1

Explanation:
* Median (Middle Value): The data set is already sorted sequentially. With 7 items present, the exact center location is the 4th element, which is 3.
* Mode (Most Frequent): The value that presents itself with the highest occurrence frequency is 1 (appearing twice).

Problem 29
What geometric shape is a globe?

Answer: Sphere

Explanation: A globe is a perfect three-dimensional round representation of Earth, which corresponds geometrically to a sphere.

Problem 30
(a) What is the geometric name for this solid? (Image shows a 6-sided die)
(b) How many faces does this solid have?

Answer: (a) Cube    (b) 6 faces

Explanation: A regular standard 3D block with equal square dimensions on all edges is classified geometrically as a cube, possessing a total of 6 faces.
