Problem 3
When James is 16 years old, how old will Jill be?
Answer: 18 years old
Step-by-Step Explanation:

Problem 4
Denise bought an artichoke and 6 pounds of carrots for $2.76. If the artichoke cost 84¢, how much was 1 pound of carrots? (Hint: First find the cost of all the carrots.)
Answer: $0.32 (or 32¢)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Subtract the cost of the artichoke from the total cost to find the total cost of just the 6 pounds of carrots:
    $$\$2.76 - \$0.84 = \$1.92$$
  • Divide the remaining amount by 6 to find the price per pound:
    $$\$1.92 \div 6 = \$0.32$$

Problem 26
Solve for $P$: $1 + 3 + 5 + P + 7 + 3 + 2 + 3 = 44$
Answer: 20
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Add all the known numbers on the left side of the equation:
    $$1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 3 + 2 + 3 = 24$$
  • Rewrite the simplified equation:
    $$24 + P = 44$$
  • Subtract 24 from 44 to solve for $P$:
    $$P = 44 - 24 = 20$$
