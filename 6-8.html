Geometric Solids & Review (Problems 1–30)

Problem 1
Use this information to answer problems 1-3:
In the Jones family there are 3 children. John is 10 years old. James is 2 years younger than Jill. James is 4 years older than John.
How old is James?
Answer: 14 years old
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • We are given that John is 10 years old.
  • The problem states James is 4 years older than John:
    $$10 + 4 = 14 \text{ years old}$$
Problem 2
How old is Jill?
Answer: 16 years old
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • From Problem 1, we established that James is 14 years old.
  • The problem states James is 2 years younger than Jill. This means Jill is 2 years older than James:
    $$14 + 2 = 16 \text{ years old}$$
Problem 3
When James is 16 years old, how old will Jill be?
Answer: 18 years old
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The age gap between siblings never changes. Jill is always 2 years older than James.
  • When James reaches 16, calculate Jill's age by adding 2:
    $$16 + 2 = 18 \text{ years old}$$
Problem 4
Denise bought an artichoke and 6 pounds of carrots for $2.76. If the artichoke cost 84¢, how much was 1 pound of carrots? (Hint: First find the cost of all the carrots.)
Answer: $0.32 (or 32¢)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Subtract the cost of the artichoke from the total to find the cost of just the carrots:
    $$\$2.76 - \$0.84 = \$1.92$$
  • Divide the total cost of carrots by the number of pounds (6) to find the price per pound:
    $$\$1.92 \div 6 = \$0.32$$
Problem 5
Write each mixed number as a decimal:
(a) $5 \frac{31}{1000}$      (b) $16 \frac{7}{10}$      (c) $5 \frac{7}{100}$
Answer: (a) 5.031  |  (b) 16.7  |  (c) 5.07
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • (a) The fraction is in thousandths, meaning 3 decimal places: $5 + 0.031 = 5.031$
  • (b) The fraction is in tenths, meaning 1 decimal place: $16 + 0.7 = 16.7$
  • (c) The fraction is in hundredths, meaning 2 decimal places: $5 + 0.07 = 5.07$
Problem 6
Three fifths of the team's 40 points were scored in the first half. How many points did the team score in the first half? Draw a picture to illustrate the problem.

Answer: 24 points
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Divide the total points (40) into 5 equal parts to find the value of one fifth:
    $$40 \div 5 = 8 \text{ points}$$
  • Multiply by 3 to find the value of three fifths:
    $$8 \times 3 = 24 \text{ points}$$
Problem 7
One fifth is 20%. What percent is three fifths?
Answer: 60%
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • If one fifth ($\frac{1}{5}$) is equal to 20%, you multiply this base percentage by 3 to find three fifths ($\frac{3}{5}$):
    $$20\% \times 3 = 60\%$$

Problem 11
Name the number of shaded squares below: (a) as a mixed number. (b) as a decimal.

Answer: (a) $1 \frac{3}{10}$  |  (b) 1.3
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Each large grid is divided into 10 smaller identical rectangles.
  • The left grid is completely shaded (representing 1 whole).

Problem 12
There were 24 people in one line and 16 people in the other line. What was the average number of people per line?
Answer: 20 people
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • First, find the total number of people across both lines:
    $$24 + 16 = 40 \text{ people}$$
  • Divide the total by the number of lines (2) to find the average:
    $$40 \div 2 = 20 \text{ people per line}$$
Problem 13
It is evening. What time will it be 5 hours 20 minutes from now?

Answer: 12:40 a.m.
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The clock shows the time as 7:20. Since it is evening, the current time is 7:20 p.m.
  • Add 5 hours to the current time:
    $$\text{7:20 p.m.} + 5\text{ hours} = \text{12:20 a.m. (midnight passes)}$$
  • Add the remaining 20 minutes:
    $$\text{12:20 a.m.} + \text{20 minutes} = \text{12:40 a.m.}$$
Problem 14
Mr. Toto could bake 27 pizzas in 3 hours.
(a) How many pizzas could he bake in 1 hour?
(b) How many pizzas could he bake in 5 hours? (Hint: Multiply the answer to part (a) by 5.)
Answer: (a) 9 pizzas  |  (b) 45 pizzas
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • (a) Divide total pizzas by total hours to find the unit rate:
    $$27 \div 3 = 9 \text{ pizzas per hour}$$
  • (b) Multiply the unit rate by 5 hours:
    $$9 \times 5 = 45 \text{ pizzas}$$

Problem 23
Divide: $4248 \div 6$
Answer: 708
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Perform standard long division left to right:
    $$42 \div 6 = 7$$
    $$4 \div 6 = 0 \text{ (remainder 4)}$$
    $$48 \div 6 = 8$$
  • The combined quotient is 708.

Problem 25
Divide: $\$49.00 \div 5$
Answer: $9.80
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Divide the whole number component first: $49 \div 5 = 9$ with a remainder of 4.
  • Carry the 4 to the tenths place (creating 40): $40 \div 5 = 8$.
  • Combine the decimal correctly:
    $$\$49.00 \div 5 = \$9.80$$
Problem 26
Find $P$: $1 + 3 + 5 + P + 7 + 3 + 2 + 3 = 44$
Answer: 20
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Sum all the known numbers on the left side of the equation:
    $$1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 3 + 2 + 3 = 24$$
  • Substitute the combined sum back into the equation:
    $$24 + P = 44$$
  • Subtract 24 from 44 to isolate $P$:
    $$P = 44 - 24 = 20$$
Problem 27
How many floor tiles that are one-foot square are needed to cover the floor of a room that is 15 feet long and 10 feet wide?
Answer: 150 tiles
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Calculate the total area of the floor using the area formula for rectangles ($\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{width}$):
    $$\text{Area} = 15 \text{ ft} \times 10 \text{ ft} = 150 \text{ sq ft}$$
  • Since each tile strictly covers exactly 1 square foot, 150 tiles are required.
Problem 28
Find the median and mode of this set of numbers: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13
Answer: Median = 3  |  Mode = 1
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Mode: The mode is the number that appears most frequently in a dataset. In this set, only the number 1 repeats. So, the mode is 1.
  • Median: The median is the middle value in a sorted list. This list has 7 values, so the 4th value lies directly in the center. That value is 3.
