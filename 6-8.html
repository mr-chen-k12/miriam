Use this information to answer problems 1-3:
In the Jones family there are 3 children. John is 10 years old. James is 2 years younger than Jill. James is 4 years older than John.
Problem 1
How old is James?
Answer: 14 years old
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • We know John is 10 years old.
  • James is 4 years older than John. Add 4 to John's age:
    $$10 + 4 = 14$$
Problem 2
How old is Jill?
Answer: 16 years old
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • James is 14 years old (from Problem 1).
  • James is 2 years younger than Jill, meaning Jill is 2 years older than James:
    $$14 + 2 = 16$$
Problem 3
When James is 16 years old, how old will Jill be?
Answer: 18 years old
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Jill is always 2 years older than James.
  • When James is 16, Jill will be $16 + 2 = 18$.
Problem 4
Denise bought an artichoke and 6 pounds of carrots for $2.76. If the artichoke cost 84¢, how much was 1 pound of carrots? (Hint: First find the cost of all the carrots.)
Answer: $0.32 (or 32¢)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Subtract the cost of the artichoke to find the cost of the 6 pounds of carrots:
    $$\$2.76 - \$0.84 = \$1.92$$
  • Divide the remaining amount by 6 to find the price per pound:
    $$\$1.92 \div 6 = \$0.32$$
Problem 5
Write each mixed number as a decimal:

(a) $5 \frac{31}{1000}$      (b) $16 \frac{7}{10}$      (c) $5 \frac{7}{100}$
Answer: (a) 5.031  |  (b) 16.7  |  (c) 5.07
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • (a) The fraction is thousandths, so 31 must end in the third decimal place: 5.031
  • (b) The fraction is tenths, so 7 goes in the first decimal place: 16.7
  • (c) The fraction is hundredths, so 7 must be in the second decimal place: 5.07

Answer: 24 points

    $$40 \div 5 = 8$$
  • Multiply by 3 to find three-fifths:
    $$8 \times 3 = 24$$

Problem 9
$\overline{AB}$ is $30 \text{ mm}$. $\overline{CD}$ is $45 \text{ mm}$. $\overline{AD}$ is $100 \text{ mm}$. Find $\overline{BC}$.

Answer: 25 mm
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The total length from $A$ to $D$ is $100 \text{ mm}$.
  • The sum of the segments is $\overline{AB} + \overline{BC} + \overline{CD} = \overline{AD}$.
    $$30 + \overline{BC} + 45 = 100$$
  • Combine the known lengths: $75 + \overline{BC} = 100$.
  • Subtract 75 from 100: $\overline{BC} = 25 \text{ mm}$.
