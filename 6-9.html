Lesson 99 Practice Set & Written Practice

Complete Solutions for Problems 1–30

Use this information to answer problems 1-2:
Five eighths of the 40 answers were correct.
Problem 1
How many answers were correct? Draw a picture to illustrate the problem.

Answer: 25 answers
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • First, find the size of one eighth by dividing the total number of answers by 8:
    $$40 \div 8 = 5 \text{ answers per eighth}$$
  • The problem states that five eighths ($\frac{5}{8}$) were correct. Multiply the value of one eighth by 5:
    $$5 \times 5 = 25 \text{ correct answers}$$
Problem 2
What percent of the answers were correct?
Answer: $62\frac{1}{2}\%$ (or $62.5\%$)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • To find the percentage corresponding to $\frac{5}{8}$, multiply the fraction by $100\%$:
    $$\frac{5}{8} \times 100\% = \frac{500\%}{8}$$
  • Divide 500 by 8:
    $$500 \div 8 = 62.5\% = 62\frac{1}{2}\%$$
Problem 3
The grocery bill was $12.45. Tax was 60¢. What was the total bill?
Answer: $13.05
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Align the decimal values properly before adding. Note that $60\text{¢} = \$0.60$:
    $$\begin{array}{r} \$12.45 \\ +\quad \$0.60 \\ \hline \$13.05 \end{array}$$
Problem 4
Write the decimal number twelve and three hundredths.
Answer: 12.03
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The whole number before the word "and" is twelve (12).
  • The word "and" indicates the position of the decimal point.
  • The phrase "three hundredths" places the digit 3 in the second space after the decimal point (the hundredths column), requiring a placeholder zero in the tenths column: 12.03
Problem 5
Which digit in 123.456 is in the hundredths place?
Answer: 5
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Analyze the decimal place values starting to the right of the decimal point:
    • 4 is in the tenths place.
    • 5 is in the hundredths place.
    • 6 is in the thousandths place.
Problem 6
The perimeter of a square is 24 inches. What is its area?
Answer: 36 sq. in.
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • A square has 4 equal sides. Find the length of one side by dividing the total perimeter by 4:
    $$\text{Side} = 24 \text{ in.} \div 4 = 6 \text{ in.}$$
  • Calculate area by squaring the side length ($A = s^2$):
    $$\text{Area} = 6 \text{ in.} \times 6 \text{ in.} = 36 \text{ sq. in.}$$
Problem 7
What is the volume of a cube with edges 3 inches long?
Answer: 27 cu. in. (or $\text{in.}^3$)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The formula for the volume of a cube is $V = s^3$, where $s$ represents the length of an edge.
  • Multiply the edge measurement three times over:
    $$V = 3 \text{ in.} \times 3 \text{ in.} \times 3 \text{ in.} = 27 \text{ cu. in.}$$
Problem 8
List the factors of 15.
Answer: 1, 3, 5, 15
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Find all whole numbers that divide evenly into 15 by identifying factor pairs:
    • $1 \times 15 = 15$
    • $3 \times 5 = 15$
  • Arranged in numerical order, the unique factors are 1, 3, 5, 15.
Problem 9
The length of segment $AB$ is $3.4\text{ cm}$. The length of segment $BC$ is $4.8\text{ cm}$. What is the length of segment $AC$?

Answer: 8.2 cm
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Segment $AC$ is made up of segments $AB$ and $BC$ connected end-to-end ($\overline{AC} = \overline{AB} + \overline{BC}$).
  • Add the values together, aligning the decimal point:
    $$3.4\text{ cm} + 4.8\text{ cm} = 8.2\text{ cm}$$
Problem 10
Round 12.345 to the nearest whole number.
Answer: 12
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Look at the first digit to the right of the decimal point (the tenths place), which is 3.
  • Since 3 is less than 5, leave the whole number component unchanged and drop the decimal digits: 12
Problem 11
What is the probability of rolling a 6 with a single dot cube?
Answer: $\frac{1}{6}$
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • A standard dot cube (die) contains 6 total faces, numbered 1 through 6.
  • There is exactly one face with a value of 6.
  • Probability is defined as the number of favorable outcomes divided by total outcomes: $\frac{1}{6}$.
Problem 12
Add: $3.45 + 12.6 + 0.78$
Answer: 16.83
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Write the numbers vertically while keeping the decimals aligned, adding a trailing zero to 12.6 to make calculation simpler:
    $$\begin{array}{r} 3.45 \\ 12.60 \\ +\quad 0.78 \\ \hline 16.83 \end{array}$$
Problem 13
Subtract: $12.34 - 5.67$
Answer: 6.67
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Align the decimal points vertically and perform regrouping across values:
    $$\begin{array}{r} 12.34 \\ -\quad 5.67 \\ \hline 6.67 \end{array}$$
Problem 14
Multiply: $0.12 \times 0.15$
Answer: 0.018
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiply the values as if they were whole numbers:
    $$12 \times 15 = 180$$
  • Count the total number of decimal positions in the original factors. Both $0.12$ and $0.15$ have 2 places, for a total of 4 decimal places.
  • Shift the decimal point to the left 4 steps starting from the end of 180, adding a placeholder zero: 0.0180 = 0.018
Problem 15
Multiply: $1.2 \times 3.4$
Answer: 4.08
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiply without matching decimals initially:
    $$12 \times 34 = 408$$
  • Count the total number of decimal places in the factors: $1.2$ has one and $3.4$ has one, meaning the final product requires 2 decimal places.
  • Position the decimal to get 4.08.
Problem 16
Divide: $0.24 \div 6$
Answer: 0.04
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Set up long division, keeping the decimal point in the quotient directly above its position in the dividend:
    $$6 \overline{) 0.24 }$$
  • $0 \div 6 = 0$; $2 \div 6 = 0$. Carry the 2 to the next place value, making 24.
  • $24 \div 6 = 4$. This results in a final quotient of 0.04.
Problem 17
Divide: $3.6 \div 0.06$
Answer: 60
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • To divide by a decimal, shift the decimal point in the divisor 2 steps to the right to make it a whole number ($0.06 \rightarrow 6$).
  • Shift the decimal point in the dividend the same number of spaces to the right, adding a trailing zero ($3.6 \rightarrow 360$).
  • Complete the whole number division step:
    $$360 \div 6 = 60$$
Problem 18
Multiply: $\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{4}{5}$
Answer: $\frac{3}{5}$
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiply the numerators and the denominators across:
    $$\frac{3 \times 4}{4 \times 5} = \frac{12}{20}$$
  • Simplify the resulting fraction by dividing the numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor, 4:
    $$\frac{12 \div 4}{20 \div 4} = \frac{3}{5}$$
  • Alternative Method: Cancel out the 4 from the numerator and denominator before multiplying directly to get $\frac{3}{5}$.
Problem 19
Divide: $\frac{2}{3} \div \frac{1}{2}$
Answer: $1\frac{1}{3}$
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • To divide by a fraction, multiply by its reciprocal (invert the second fraction):
    $$\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{2}{1} = \frac{4}{3}$$
  • Convert the improper fraction into a mixed number:
    $$\frac{4}{3} = 1\frac{1}{3}$$
Problem 20
Add: $3\frac{1}{3} + 1\frac{2}{3}$
Answer: 5
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Add the whole numbers and fractional components separately:
    $$\text{Whole numbers: } 3 + 1 = 4$$
    $$\text{Fractions: } \frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{3} = \frac{3}{3} = 1$$
  • Combine the sums together:
    $$4 + 1 = 5$$
Problem 21
Subtract: $4\frac{1}{4} - 1\frac{3}{4}$
Answer: $2\frac{1}{2}$
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Since you cannot subtract $\frac{3}{4}$ from $\frac{1}{4}$, regroup 1 from the whole number 4:
    $$4\frac{1}{4} = 3 + 1 + \frac{1}{4} = 3 + \frac{4}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = 3\frac{5}{4}$$
  • Perform the subtraction using the regrouped fraction:
    $$3\frac{5}{4} - 1\frac{3}{4} = 2\frac{2}{4}$$
  • Reduce the fractional component to simplest terms:
    $$2\frac{2}{4} = 2\frac{1}{2}$$
Problem 22
Simplify: $2^3 + \sqrt{16}$
Answer: 12
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Evaluate the exponent term ($2^3$ means $2 \times 2 \times 2$):
    $$2^3 = 8$$
  • Evaluate the radical term (find the non-negative number that multiplied by itself equals 16):
    $$\sqrt{16} = 4$$
  • Add the simplified expressions together:
    $$8 + 4 = 12$$
Problem 23
Solve for $x$: $6x = 4.2$
Answer: 0.7
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Isolate the variable $x$ by dividing both sides of the equation by 6:
    $$x = \frac{4.2}{6}$$
  • Perform the decimal division steps:
    $$4.2 \div 6 = 0.7$$
Problem 24
Solve for $w$: $\frac{w}{4} = 1.2$
Answer: 4.8
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Isolate the variable $w$ by multiplying both sides of the equation by 4:
    $$w = 1.2 \times 4$$
  • Calculate the final value:
    $$1.2 \times 4 = 4.8$$
Problem 25
Reduce: $\frac{12}{16}$
Answer: $\frac{3}{4}$
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Find the greatest common factor (GCF) of 12 and 16, which is 4.
  • Divide both numbers by 4 to reduce the fraction to lowest terms:
    $$\frac{12 \div 4}{16 \div 4} = \frac{3}{4}$$
Problem 26
The diameter of a bicycle tire is 24 inches. What is its radius?
Answer: 12 inches
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • The radius of a circle is exactly half the length of its diameter ($r = \frac{d}{2}$).
  • Divide the diameter by 2:
    $$24 \text{ in.} \div 2 = 12 \text{ in.}$$
Problem 27
What geometric solid is an unsharpened pencil?
Answer: Cylinder (or hexagonal prism)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • An unsharpened round pencil has two parallel circular bases and a smooth curved side, which fits the definition of a cylinder.
  • Note: Many standard wooden pencils feature flat sides, making them a hexagonal prism, but in basic geometry instruction, they are typically used to model a cylinder.
Problem 28
This rectangular prism is constructed of 1-inch cubes.
(a) How many cubes are in each layer?
(b) How many layers are there?
(c) What is the volume of the prism?
Answers: (a) 12 cubes  |  (b) 2 layers  |  (c) 24 cubic inches
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • (a) Count or calculate the surface base dimension of a single layer. The block is 4 cubes long and 3 cubes wide:
    $$4 \times 3 = 12 \text{ cubes per layer}$$
  • (b) Look at the vertical height of the prism structure. It stands 2 layers tall.
  • (c) Multiply the number of blocks per layer by the total number of layers to find volume ($V = l \times w \times h$):
    $$12 \times 2 = 24 \text{ cubic inches (or in.}^3\text{)}$$
Problem 29
The coordinates of three vertices of a rectangle are $(0,0)$, $(4,0)$, and $(0,3)$. What are the coordinates of the fourth vertex?

Answer: (4,3)
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Plotting the three known coordinate pairs shows:
    • $(0,0)$ sits directly at the origin point.
    • $(4,0)$ is located 4 units out along the horizontal x-axis.
    • $(0,3)$ is located 3 units straight up along the vertical y-axis.
  • To form a true rectangle with perpendicular lines, the final missing corner must align horizontally with $(0,3)$ and vertically with $(4,0)$. This intersects directly at coordinate $(4,3)$.
Problem 30
Find the mean, median, mode, and range of this set of scores:
80, 90, 100, 80, 100, 80, 70
Answers: Mean = $85\frac{5}{7}$  |  Median = 80  |  Mode = 80  |  Range = 30
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • First, arrange the 7 scores in order from smallest to largest:
    $$\mathbf{70, 80, 80, 80, 90, 100, 100}$$
  • Mean: Add all values together and divide by the total count (7):
    $$70 + 80 + 80 + 80 + 90 + 100 + 100 = 600$$
    $$600 \div 7 = 85\frac{5}{7} \approx 85.71$$
  • Median: The middle score in the ordered list of 7 items is the 4th value: 80.
  • Mode: The value that appears most often is 80 (it occurs 3 times).
  • Range: Compute the difference between the highest and lowest scores:
    $$100 - 70 = 30$$
