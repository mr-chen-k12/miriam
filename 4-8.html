Math Worksheet Solutions (1-30)

1. Jamaal bought apples at 5 cents per pound at the sale. He spent 95 cents. How many pounds of apples did Jamaal buy?

Answer: 19 pounds

Explanation: To find the total pounds, divide the total cost by the cost per pound.
$95 \div 5 = 19$.

2. Laura placed 243 paint cans on the shelf. Ninety-five of the cans fell during the earthquake. How many paint cans stayed on the shelf?

Answer: 148 paint cans

Explanation: Subtract the number of cans that fell from the initial total.
$243 - 95 = 148$.

3. Pamela listened to half of a 90-minute tape. How many minutes of the tape did she hear?

Answer: 45 minutes

Explanation: Divide the total minutes by 2 to find half.
$90 \div 2 = 45$.

4. One fourth of the guests gathered in the living room. What fraction of the guests did not gather in the living room? What percent of the guests did not gather in the living room?

Answer: Fraction: $\frac{3}{4}$, Percent: $75\%$

Explanation: The whole group is represented by $1$. The fraction that did not gather is $1 - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}$. To convert $\frac{3}{4}$ to a percentage, multiply by 100: $\frac{3}{4} \times 100 = 75\%$.

5. If one side of an equilateral triangle is 3 centimeters long, then what is its perimeter in (a) centimeters? (b) millimeters?

Answer: (a) $9$ cm, (b) $90$ mm

Explanation: An equilateral triangle has 3 equal sides.
(a) Perimeter in cm: $3 + 3 + 3 = 9$ cm.
(b) Since $1$ cm $= 10$ mm, $9 \text{ cm} \times 10 = 90$ mm.

6. Which of these arrows could be pointing to 2750? (Given a number line from 2000 to 3000 with arrows A, B, C spaced evenly)

Answer: Arrow C

Explanation: The number line starts at 2000 and ends at 3000. 2750 is exactly three-quarters of the way between 2000 and 3000. Arrow C is in the 3/4 position.

7. Half of a gallon is a half gallon. Half of a half gallon is a quart. Half of a quart is a pint. Half of a pint is a cup. A cup is what fraction of a quart?

Answer: $\frac{1}{4}$

Explanation: Let's work backwards from a quart. Half of a quart is a pint ($\frac{1}{2}$ of a quart). Half of a pint is a cup ($\frac{1}{2}$ of $\frac{1}{2}$ of a quart). $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}$.

8. Isabel weighed 3 kilograms when she was born. How many grams is that?

Answer: 3000 grams

Explanation: There are 1000 grams in a kilogram. $3 \times 1000 = 3000$ grams.

9. Estimate the product of $396$ and $7$.

Answer: 2800

Explanation: To estimate, round 396 to the nearest hundred, which is 400. Multiply $400 \times 7 = 2800$.

10. It is afternoon. What time was it 12 hours ago? (Clock face shows 5:56)

Answer: 5:56 a.m.

Explanation: Since it is afternoon, the time on the clock is 5:56 p.m. Exactly 12 hours prior to a PM time is the identical AM time: 5:56 a.m.

11. Compare: $\frac{3}{4} \bigcirc \frac{4}{5}$. Draw and shade two congruent rectangles to show the comparison.

Answer: $\frac{3}{4} < \frac{4}{5}$

Explanation: Getting a common denominator of 20, we see $\frac{3}{4} = \frac{15}{20}$ and $\frac{4}{5} = \frac{16}{20}$. Therefore, $\frac{3}{4}$ is smaller.

16. $567 \div 2$

Answer: $283 \text{ R } 1$

Explanation:
$5 \div 2 = 2$ remainder $1$.
Bring down 6 to make $16$. $16 \div 2 = 8$ remainder $0$.
Bring down 7. $7 \div 2 = 3$ remainder $1$.

17. $\$34.56 \div 6$

Answer: $\$5.76$

Explanation:
$34 \div 6 = 5$ remainder $4$.
Bring down 5 making $45$. $45 \div 6 = 7$ remainder $3$.
Bring down 6 making $36$. $36 \div 6 = 6$.

19. $398 \times 6$

Answer: $2388$

Explanation:
$6 \times 8 = 48$ (write 8, carry 4).
$6 \times 9 = 54$; $54 + 4 = 58$ (write 8, carry 5).
$6 \times 3 = 18$; $18 + 5 = 23$. Result is 2388.

20. $47 \times 60$

Answer: $2820$

Explanation: Multiply $47$ by $6$ and then attach a zero at the end.
$6 \times 7 = 42$ (write 2, carry 4).
$6 \times 4 = 24$; $24 + 4 = 28$. Result is 282. Append the 0 to get 2820.

25. $8N = 120$

Answer: $N = 15$

Explanation: Isolate $N$ by dividing both sides by 8.

26. $F \times 3^2 = 108$

Answer: $F = 12$

Explanation: First solve the exponent: $3^2 = 9$. The equation becomes $F \times 9 = 108$. Divide both sides by 9:
$108 \div 9 = 12$.

27. $7 + 8 + 5 + 4 + N + 2 + 7 + 3 = 54$

Answer: $N = 18$

Explanation: Combine all the known numbers:
$7+8+5+4+2+7+3 = 36$.
The equation becomes $36 + N = 54$. Subtract 36 from 54 to get $N = 18$.

28. Find the perimeter and area of this rectangle: (Grid showing length 8 units, height 4 units)

Answer: Perimeter = $24$ units, Area = $32$ units

Explanation:
The rectangle has side lengths of 8 and 4.
$\text{Perimeter} = 2 \times (\text{Length} + \text{Width}) = 2 \times (8 + 4) = 2 \times 12 = 24$ units.
$\text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Width} = 8 \times 4 = 32$ square units.

29. Name the transformation(s) that would move $\Delta ABC$ to position $WXY$.

Answer: Reflection

Explanation: The triangle has been flipped horizontally like a mirror image, indicating a reflection across a vertical axis. It preserves the upwards orientation but reverses left-to-right.

30. The first four multiples of 18 are 18, 36, 54, 72. What are the first four multiples of 180?

Answer: 180, 360, 540, 720

Explanation: Since 180 is ten times 18, its multiples are simply ten times the multiples of 18. Append a zero to the multiples of 18.
