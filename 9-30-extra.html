1.

Thirty-five boys sat in the first row. Forty-six boys sat in the second row. Ninety boys sat in the third row. Altogether how many boys sat in the first three rows?

Answer: \(171\) boys.

Explanation: Add the three rows: \(35 + 46 + 90 = 81 + 90 = 171\).

2.

Sixty marbles were in the bag. Mia took some marbles from the bag. Thirty-five marbles were left in the bag. How many marbles did Mia take from the bag?

Answer: \(25\) marbles.

Explanation: Subtract what is left from what was there: \(60 - 35 = 25\).

3.

The board game costs forty-five dollars. Sofia has saved twenty-seven dollars. How much more money does Sofia need in order to buy the board game?

Answer: \(\$18\) more.

Explanation: \(45 - 27 = 18\). Sofia needs 18 more dollars.

4.

Write 506 in expanded form. Then use words to write the number.

Answer: \(500 + 6\); "five hundred six."

Explanation: 506 = 5 hundreds + 0 tens + 6 ones, so expanded form is \(500 + 6\). In words: five hundred six.

5.

Use digits and symbols to write "Nine times four equals six times six."

Answer: \(9 \times 4 = 6 \times 6\).

Explanation: "Nine" \(= 9\), "times" \(= \times\), "four" \(= 4\), "equals" \(= =\), "six" \(= 6\). (Both sides equal 36.)

6.

A crayon is measured with a centimeter ruler. One end of the crayon is at 0 cm and the other end is at about 9 cm. About how long is the crayon?

Answer: About \(9\) cm.

Explanation: The crayon spans from 0 to about the 9-cm mark on the ruler, so it is about 9 centimeters long.

7.

Draw a circle and shade \(\frac{1}{3}\) of it.

Answer: A circle with one third shaded.

Explanation: Draw a circle, divide it into 3 equal parts, and shade exactly 1 of the 3 parts.

8.

Round \(\$24.75\) to the nearest dollar.

Answer: \(\$25\).

Explanation: \(\$24.75\) is between \(\$24\) and \(\$25\). The cents part, \(75\text{¢}\), is more than \(50\text{¢}\), so round up to \(\$25\).

9.

Find the missing numbers in this counting sequence: \(63, \text{___}, \text{___}, 45, 39, \text{___}, \ldots\)

Answer: \(63, 57, 51, 45, 39, 33, \ldots\)

Explanation: The pattern counts down by 6: \(63 - 6 = 57\), \(57 - 6 = 51\), \(51 - 6 = 45\), \(45 - 6 = 39\), \(39 - 6 = 33\).

10.

The clock shows 7:25. If it is morning, what time will it be in 2 hours and 50 minutes?

Answer: 10:15 AM.

Explanation: Start at 7:25 AM. Adding 2 hours gives 9:25 AM; adding 50 more minutes gives 10:15 AM.

11.

What is the perimeter of a triangle with sides 7 cm, 7 cm, and 9 cm?

Answer: \(23\) cm.

Explanation: Add the three sides: \(7 + 7 + 9 = 23\) cm.

12.

Change this addition problem to a multiplication problem: \(4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4\)

Answer: \(6 \times 4\) (which equals \(24\)).

Explanation: The number 4 is added 6 times, so the repeated addition becomes the multiplication \(6 \times 4 = 24\).

13.

The rope is eight meters long. How many centimeters is that?

Answer: \(800\) cm.

Explanation: There are 100 centimeters in a meter, so \(8 \times 100 = 800\) cm.

14.

(a) \(4 \times 6\)    (b) \(7 \times 5\)    (c) \(8 \times 5\)

Answer: (a) \(24\)    (b) \(35\)    (c) \(40\).

Explanation: (a) \(4 \times 6 = 24\). (b) \(7 \times 5 = 35\). (c) \(8 \times 5 = 40\).

15.

\(740 - 320\)

Answer: \(420\).

Explanation: \(740 - 320 = 420\).

16.

\(\begin{array}{r} 95 \\ -\;38 \\ \hline \end{array}\)

Answer: \(57\).

Explanation: \(95 - 38 = 57\) (borrow 1 ten: \(15 - 8 = 7\), \(8 - 3 = 5\)).

17.

\(\begin{array}{r} 486 \\ +\;257 \\ \hline \end{array}\)

Answer: \(743\).

Explanation: \(486 + 257 = 743\) (ones: \(6+7=13\), carry 1; tens: \(8+5+1=14\), carry 1; hundreds: \(4+2+1=7\)).
