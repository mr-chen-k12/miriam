1.(49)
a. There are eight nests with five eggs in each nest. How many eggs are there in all?
b. There are six aquariums with nine fish in each aquarium. How many fish are there in all?
c. There are nine shelves with four books on each shelf. How many books are there in all?
Answers
a. \(40\) eggs
b. \(54\) fish
c. \(36\) books
Solutions
a. \(8 \times 5 = 40\) eggs.
b. \(6 \times 9 = 54\) fish.
c. \(9 \times 4 = 36\) books.
2.(Inv. 2, 21)
a. If the radius of a circle is 6 in., then its diameter is
A.  3 in.   B.  12 ft   C.  1 ft   D.  36 in.
b. If the radius of a circle is 9 in., then its diameter is
A.  18 yd   B.  \(\frac{1}{2}\) yd   C.  \(4\frac{1}{2}\) in.   D.  3 ft
c. If the radius of a circle is 12 in., then its diameter is
A.  2 ft   B.  6 in.   C.  24 yd   D.  1 ft
Answers
a. C. 1 ft
b. B. \(\frac{1}{2}\) yd
c. A. 2 ft
Solutions
a. Diameter \(= 2 \times 6\) in. \(= 12\) in., and \(12\) inches \(= 1\) foot.
b. Diameter \(= 2 \times 9\) in. \(= 18\) in., and \(18\) inches \(= \frac{1}{2}\) yard.
c. Diameter \(= 2 \times 12\) in. \(= 24\) in., and \(24\) inches \(= 2\) feet.

19.(Inv. 1, 45)
a. Compare: \(8 - (2 \times 3)\)  ◱  \((8 - 2) \times 3\)
b. Compare: \((15 - 5) \times 2\)  ◱  \(15 - (5 \times 2)\)
c. Compare: \(3 \times (6 - 2)\)  ◱  \((3 \times 6) - (3 \times 2)\)
Answers
a. <
b. >
c. =
Solutions
a. Left: \(8 - 6 = 2\). Right: \(6 \times 3 = 18\). So \(2 < 18\).
b. Left: \(10 \times 2 = 20\). Right: \(15 - 10 = 5\). So \(20 > 5\).
c. Left: \(3 \times 4 = 12\). Right: \(18 - 6 = 12\). So the two sides are equal.
