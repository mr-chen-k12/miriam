Lesson 64 Solutions

1. A square mile is twenty-seven million, eight hundred seventy-eight thousand, four hundred square feet. Use digits to write that number of square feet.
Answer: 27,878,400

Explanation: Break down the words into their numerical groups: "twenty-seven million" is $27,000,000$. "Eight hundred seventy-eight thousand" is $878,000$. "Four hundred" is $400$. Combine them with commas separating the periods.

2. Apollo's target was one hundred thirteen paces away. If each pace was 3 feet, how many feet away was the target?
Answer: 339 feet

Explanation: Multiply the number of paces by the length of each pace: $113 \times 3 = 339$.

3. Tracy's baseball card album will hold five hundred cards. Tracy has three hundred eighty-four cards. How many more cards will fit in the album?
Answer: 116 cards

Explanation: Subtract the number of cards Tracy currently has from the total capacity: $500 - 384 = 116$.

4. The trip lasted 21 days. How many weeks did the trip last?
Answer: 3 weeks

Explanation: Since there are 7 days in a week, divide the total days by 7: $21 \div 7 = 3$.

5. A stop sign has the shape of an octagon. How many sides do seven stop signs have?
Answer: 56 sides

Explanation: An octagon has 8 sides. Multiply the sides per sign by the number of signs: $8 \times 7 = 56$.

6. Find the length of this hairpin to the nearest quarter inch:
Answer: $2 \frac{1}{4}$ inches

Explanation: Looking at the ruler provided in the image, the right edge of the hairpin ends exactly at the first quarter mark past the 2-inch line.

7. Write 406,912 in expanded form. Then use words to write the number.
Answer:

Expanded form: $400,000 + 6,000 + 900 + 10 + 2$

Words: Four hundred six thousand, nine hundred twelve.

Explanation: Identify the place value of each non-zero digit. 4 is in the hundred-thousands place, 6 is in the thousands, 9 is in the hundreds, 1 in the tens, and 2 in the ones.

8. One foot equals 12 inches. If each side of a square is 1 foot long, then what is the perimeter of the square in inches?
Answer: 48 inches

Explanation: A square has 4 equal sides. Each side is 1 foot, which is 12 inches. The perimeter is $4 \times 12 = 48$ inches.

9. Estimate the sum of 586 and 797 by rounding the numbers to the nearest hundred before adding.
Answer: 1,400

Explanation: 586 rounds up to 600. 797 rounds up to 800. Add the rounded numbers: $600 + 800 = 1400$.

10. Compare: $\frac{3}{6} \bigcirc \frac{1}{2}$. Draw and shade two congruent circles to show the comparison.
Answer: $\frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$

Explanation: Both fractions represent the exact same proportion of a whole. $\frac{3}{6}$ simplifies to $\frac{1}{2}$.

11. Compare: $50\% \text{ of } 100 \bigcirc \sqrt{100}$
Answer: $50\% \text{ of } 100 > \sqrt{100}$

Explanation: Calculate the left side: $50\%$ of $100$ is $50$. Calculate the right side: the square root of $100$ is $10$. Since $50$ is greater than $10$, the symbol is $>$.

12. Some birds sat on the wire at sunup. After 47 more birds came there were 112 birds sitting on the wire. How many birds sat on the wire at sunup?
Answer: 65 birds

Explanation: Let $x$ be the initial number of birds. The equation is $x + 47 = 112$. To find $x$, subtract 47 from 112: $112 - 47 = 65$.

13. $\$32.47 + \$67.54$
Answer: $100.01

Explanation: Align the decimals and add: $32.47 + 67.54 = 100.01$. Don't forget the dollar sign.

15. $53.6 + 2.9 + 97.4 + 8.8 + 436.1$
Answer: 598.8

Explanation: Align the decimal points and add up the columns. The tenths column adds up to $28$ (write down $8$, carry $2$). Resulting sum is $598.8$.

16. $75 \div 5$ (Written as $5\overline{)75}$)
Answer: 15

Explanation: Using long division, 5 goes into 7 one time (with 2 left over). Bring down the 5 to make 25. 5 goes into 25 five times exactly.

17. $84 \div 3$ (Written as $3\overline{)84}$)
Answer: 28

Explanation: 3 goes into 8 two times ($3 \times 2 = 6$), leaving a remainder of 2. Bring down the 4 to make 24. 3 goes into 24 eight times exactly ($3 \times 8 = 24$).

18. $92 \div 4$ (Written as $4\overline{)92}$)
Answer: 23

Explanation: 4 goes into 9 two times ($4 \times 2 = 8$), remainder 1. Bring down the 2 to make 12. 4 goes into 12 three times ($4 \times 3 = 12$).

19. $58 \div 6$ (Written as $6\overline{)58}$)
Answer: 9 R 4 (or $9 \frac{2}{3}$)

Explanation: 6 goes into 58 nine times ($6 \times 9 = 54$). $58 - 54 = 4$. So the answer is 9 with a remainder of 4.

20. $257 \times 5$
Answer: 1,285

Explanation: $5 \times 7 = 35$ (carry 3). $5 \times 5 = 25$, plus 3 is 28 (carry 2). $5 \times 2 = 10$, plus 2 is 12.

21. $\$7.09 \times 3$
Answer: $21.27

Explanation: $3 \times 9 = 27$ (carry 2). $3 \times 0 = 0$, plus 2 is 2. $3 \times 7 = 21$. Maintain the decimal point for money.

22. $\$334 \times 9$
Answer: $3,006

Explanation: $9 \times 4 = 36$ (carry 3). $9 \times 3 = 27$, plus 3 is 30 (carry 3). $9 \times 3 = 27$, plus 3 is 30. Therefore $\$3006$.

23. $36 \div 2$ (Written as $2\overline{)36}$)
Answer: 18

Explanation: Half of 36 is 18. Or by division: 2 goes into 3 once, remainder 1. Bring down 6 to make 16. 2 goes into 16 eight times.

24. $4N = 36$
Answer: $N = 9$

Explanation: Divide both sides by 4 to solve for N. $36 \div 4 = 9$.

25. $4^2 + 2^3$
Answer: 24

Explanation: Evaluate the exponents first. $4^2 = 4 \times 4 = 16$. $2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8$. Add them together: $16 + 8 = 24$.

26. $3.5 - (2.4 - 1.3)$
Answer: 2.4

Explanation: According to the order of operations, solve parentheses first. $2.4 - 1.3 = 1.1$. Then subtract that from the outside number: $3.5 - 1.1 = 2.4$.

27. Segments $AB$ and $BC$ are each $1 \frac{1}{2}$ in. long. How long is segment $AC$?
Answer: 3 inches

Explanation: Segment $AC$ is the sum of $AB$ and $BC$. Add $1 \frac{1}{2} + 1 \frac{1}{2}$.
First add the whole numbers: $1 + 1 = 2$.
Next add the fractions: $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = 1$.
Total: $2 + 1 = 3$ inches.

28. Three fourths of the game was over. What fraction of the game remained?
Answer: $\frac{1}{4}$

Explanation: A whole game is represented by $1$, or $\frac{4}{4}$. Subtract the portion that is over: $\frac{4}{4} - \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{4}$.

29.
(a) What fraction of the large square is shaded?
(b) What percent of the large square is not shaded?
(c) What decimal number is represented by the shaded part of the square?
Answer:

(a) $\frac{1}{2}$ (or $\frac{50}{100}$)

(b) $50\%$

(c) $0.5$ (or $0.50$)

Explanation: The grid is a $10 \times 10$ square, totaling $100$ small squares. Exactly half of the columns (5 out of 10) are shaded, meaning $50$ out of $100$ are shaded. $\frac{50}{100}$ reduces to $\frac{1}{2}$. The remaining unshaded squares are the other 50, so $50\%$. The decimal equivalent for $\frac{50}{100}$ is $0.50$.
