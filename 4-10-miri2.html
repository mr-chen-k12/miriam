Math Worksheet - Practice Set B

1. Marcus bought flour at 4 cents per pound at the bulk store. He spent 84 cents. How many pounds of flour did Marcus buy?
Answer: 21 pounds

Explanation: Divide the total amount spent by the cost per pound.
\( 84 \div 4 = 21 \)
2. The library had 315 science fiction books on the shelves. One hundred twenty-eight of the books were checked out on Monday. How many science fiction books stayed on the shelf?
Answer: 187 books

Explanation: Subtract the number of books checked out from the total.
\( 315 - 128 = 187 \)
3. Carlos watched a third of a 120-minute movie before falling asleep. How many minutes of the movie did he watch?
Answer: 40 minutes

Explanation: Divide the total minutes by 3 to find one third.
\( 120 \div 3 = 40 \)
4. One fifth of the students take the bus to school. What fraction of the students do not take the bus? What percent of the students do not take the bus?
Answer: \( \frac{4}{5} \) and 80%

Explanation: Subtract the fraction that takes the bus from 1 (the whole group).
\( 1 - \frac{1}{5} = \frac{4}{5} \)
To convert \( \frac{4}{5} \) to a percentage, multiply by 100: \( \frac{4}{5} \times 100 = 80\% \)
5. If one side of a square is 5 centimeters long, then what is its perimeter in (a) centimeters? (b) millimeters?
Answer: (a) 20 cm (b) 200 mm

Explanation: A square has 4 equal sides.
(a) Perimeter in cm = \( 5 \times 4 = 20 \text{ cm} \)
(b) Since 1 cm = 10 mm, multiply by 10: \( 20 \times 10 = 200 \text{ mm} \)
6. Draw a number line from 5000 to 6000 with tick marks every 250 units. If an arrow points exactly halfway between 5000 and 6000, what number is it pointing to?
Answer: 5500

Explanation: The total distance between 5000 and 6000 is 1000. Half of 1000 is 500. Adding 500 to the starting point (5000) gives 5500.
7. There are 2 pints in a quart, and 4 quarts in a gallon. A pint is what fraction of a gallon?
Answer: \( \frac{1}{8} \)

Explanation:
1 gallon = 4 quarts
Since 1 quart = 2 pints, 4 quarts = \( 4 \times 2 = 8 \) pints.
Therefore, 1 pint is \( \frac{1}{8} \) of a gallon.
8. A puppy weighed 4.5 kilograms at its checkup. How many grams is that?
Answer: 4500 grams

Explanation: There are 1000 grams in 1 kilogram. Multiply the kilograms by 1000.
\( 4.5 \times 1000 = 4500 \)
9. Estimate the product of 498 and 6.
Answer: 3000

Explanation: Round 498 up to the nearest hundred, which is 500. Multiply by 6.
\( 500 \times 6 = 3000 \)
10. It is morning. What time was it 12 hours ago if the clock reads 8:15?
Answer: 8:15 p.m.

Explanation: Because it is morning, the time is 8:15 a.m. Exactly 12 hours before any a.m. time is the same time in p.m.
11. Compare: \( \frac{2}{3} \bigcirc \frac{3}{5} \).
Answer: \( \frac{2}{3} > \frac{3}{5} \)

Explanation: Find a common denominator (15).
\( \frac{2}{3} = \frac{10}{15} \)
\( \frac{3}{5} = \frac{9}{15} \)
Since 10 > 9, \( \frac{2}{3} > \frac{3}{5} \).

26. \( Y \times 2^3 = 96 \)
Answer: \( Y = 12 \)

Explanation: Evaluate the exponent first: \( 2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8 \).
The equation becomes \( Y \times 8 = 96 \).
Divide by 8: \( Y = \frac{96}{8} = 12 \).
27. \( 5 + 6 + 9 + N + 3 + 8 = 45 \)
Answer: \( N = 14 \)

Explanation: Add the known values: \( 5 + 6 + 9 + 3 + 8 = 31 \).
Substitute back: \( 31 + N = 45 \).
Subtract 31 from both sides: \( N = 14 \).
28. Find the perimeter and area of a rectangle that is 7 units wide and 5 units high.
Answer: Perimeter = 24 units, Area = 35 square units

Explanation:
Perimeter = \( 2 \times (7 + 5) = 2 \times 12 = 24 \) units.
Area = \( 7 \times 5 = 35 \) square units.
29. A triangle is shifted 4 units to the right and 2 units up without twisting or flipping. What is the name of this geometric transformation?
Answer: Translation

Explanation: A translation (or slide) moves a shape in a specific direction without rotating, resizing, or reflecting it.
30. The first four multiples of 15 are 15, 30, 45, and 60. What are the first four multiples of 150?
Answer: 150, 300, 450, 600

Explanation: Because 150 is 10 times 15, its multiples will simply be 10 times the multiples of 15.
\( 15 \times 10 = 150 \)
\( 30 \times 10 = 300 \)
\( 45 \times 10 = 450 \)
\( 60 \times 10 = 600 \)
