*1. Subtraction Word Problem

Mr. Sterling had $142$ science fair project boards. He handed out $88$ boards to 4th grade students. How many project boards does he have left?

Answer: 54 project boards
Explanation: Subtract the number of boards handed out from the initial amount:
$$142 - 88 = 54\text{ project boards}$$
2. Time & Division

A full day has $24$ hours. How many hours are in one-quarter ($\frac{1}{4}$) of a day?

Answer: 6 hours
Explanation: Divide the total number of hours in a day by $4$:
$$24 \div 4 = 6\text{ hours}$$

*6. Geometry - Circles

The radius of a circular park fountain is $9$ feet. What is the diameter of the fountain?

Answer: 18 feet
Explanation: The diameter ($d$) is twice the length of the radius ($r$):
$$d = 2 \times r = 2 \times 9\text{ ft} = 18\text{ feet}$$
*7. Time Elapsed & Visual Clock

An afternoon art class begins at the time shown on the clock below. The art class ends at 3:10 p.m. How long is the art class?

Answer: 50 minutes
Explanation: The clock displays 2:20 p.m.
From 2:20 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. is $40$ minutes.
From 3:00 p.m. to 3:10 p.m. is $10$ minutes.
$$40\text{ min} + 10\text{ min} = 50\text{ minutes}$$

*10. Perimeter

Calculate the perimeter of a rectangle that is $6\text{ cm}$ long and $3\text{ cm}$ wide. What is its perimeter?

Answer: 18 cm
Explanation: A rectangle has two lengths of $6\text{ cm}$ and two widths of $3\text{ cm}$.
$$\text{Perimeter} = 6 + 3 + 6 + 3 = 2 \times (6 + 3) = 18\text{ cm}$$

*24. Multiple Choice - Metric Conversion

Which of these measurements is NOT equivalent to $1\text{ kilometer}$?

A $1,000\text{ m}$
B $100,000\text{ cm}$
C $1,000,000\text{ mm}$
D $100\text{ m}$
Answer: D ($100\text{ m}$)
Explanation: $1\text{ kilometer} = 1,000\text{ meters} = 100,000\text{ centimeters} = 1,000,000\text{ millimeters}$. $100\text{ m}$ is only equal to $0.1\text{ km}$ (one-tenth of a kilometer).
*25. Geometry Concepts

How are parallel lines different from perpendicular lines?

Answer: Geometric Difference
Explanation: Parallel lines run in the same direction, remain the same distance apart, and never cross or intersect. Perpendicular lines intersect or cross each other at right angles ($90^\circ$).
26. Multi-Step River Miles Equation

The Red River and Blue River have a combined length of $940$ miles. The Red River is $510$ miles long. Write and solve an equation to find the length of the Blue River.

Answer: Equation: $510 + b = 940$; $b = 430$ miles
Explanation: Let $b$ represent the length of the Blue River.
Equation: $$510 + b = 940$$ Solve for $b$: $$b = 940 - 510 = 430\text{ miles}$$
27. Estimation Word Problem

At a school bookstore, a notebook costs $\$2.89$ and a pack of pens costs $\$1.19$. What is a reasonable estimate of the total cost? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.

Answer: About $4.00 to $4.10
Explanation: Rounding $\$2.89$ to the nearest dollar gives $\$3.00$ (or $\$2.90$ to nearest 10 cents), and $\$1.19$ rounds to $\$1.00$ (or $\$1.20$).
$$\$3.00 + \$1.00 = \$4.00 \quad \text{or} \quad \$2.90 + \$1.20 = \$4.10$$ This estimate is reasonable because each price was rounded to a convenient nearby amount for quick mental calculation.
28. Integers & Temperature Differences

In Minneapolis, the high temperature on a winter day was $14^\circ\text{F}$ and the low temperature was $-6^\circ\text{F}$. How many degrees warmer was the high temperature than the low temperature?

Answer: $20^\circ\text{F}$ warmer
Explanation: Subtract the low temperature from the high temperature:
$$14 - (-6) = 14 + 6 = 20^\circ\text{F}$$ Alternatively, from $-6^\circ\text{F}$ up to $0^\circ\text{F}$ is $6$ degrees, and from $0^\circ\text{F}$ up to $14^\circ\text{F}$ is $14$ degrees. $6 + 14 = 20^\circ\text{F}$.

Real-World Connection

Three elementary school classes collected canned food for a food drive. Class A collected $134$ cans, Class B collected $148$ cans, and Class C collected $122$ cans. The school donated $260$ cans to a local shelter. Are there more cans left over or were more cans donated? How do you know?

Answer: More cans were donated ($260$ donated vs $144$ remaining).

Step 1: Calculate the total number of cans collected:
$$134 + 148 + 122 = 404\text{ total cans}$$

Step 2: Subtract the donated cans ($260$) to find how many cans remain:
$$404 - 260 = 144\text{ cans remaining}$$

Step 3: Compare the donated cans ($260$) with the remaining cans ($144$):
$$260\text{ (donated)} > 144\text{ (remaining)}$$

Since $260 > 144$, more cans were donated than left over.
