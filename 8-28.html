Question 1

Seven feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply the number of feet by 12:

7 feet × 12 inches = 84 inches

Question 2

Charlotte handed the cashier a $50 bill to buy a bicycle helmet that cost $28.45. How much change did she receive back?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the cost of the helmet from $50.00:

$50.00 − $28.45 = $21.55

Question 3

Benjamin is 8 years younger than James. James is five times as old as Chloe. If James is 25 years old, how old are Benjamin and Chloe?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Find Benjamin's age: Benjamin is 8 years younger than James → 25 − 8 = 17 years old.

2. Find Chloe's age: James is 5 times as old as Chloe → 25 ÷ 5 = 5 years old.

Benjamin is 17 and Chloe is 5.

Question 5

One-fifth of the 35 students in Mr. Taylor's science class play soccer. How many students play soccer?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-fifth of a group, divide the total number of students by 5:

35 ÷ 5 = 7 students

Question 6

Teacher Answer Key

35 − 7 = 28 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 76 and 27 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then, find the exact difference.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Question 14

A baseball game started at 1:10 PM and finished at 3:40 PM. How long did the baseball game last?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. From 1:10 PM to 3:10 PM is 2 hours.

2. From 3:10 PM to 3:40 PM is 30 minutes.

Total time: 2 hours and 30 minutes (or 150 minutes).

Question 20

Lucas has 5 one-dollar bills, 1 quarter, 5 dimes, 2 nickels, and 6 pennies. What is his total amount of money?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

• 5 Dollar Bills = $5.00

• 1 Quarter = 25¢ ($0.25)

• 5 Dimes = 5 × 10¢ = 50¢ ($0.50)

• 2 Nickels = 2 × 5¢ = 10¢ ($0.10)

• 6 Pennies = 6¢ ($0.06)

Total: $5.00 + $0.25 + $0.50 + $0.10 + $0.06 = $5.91

Question 29

Teacher Answer Key

2. Add new visitors: 425 + 90 = 515 visitors.
