Question 1

Six feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply the number of feet by 12:

6 feet × 12 inches = 72 inches

Question 2

Daniel handed the cashier a $50 bill to buy a backpack that cost $36.80. How much change did he receive back?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the cost of the backpack from $50.00:

$50.00 − $36.80 = $13.20

Question 3

Sophia is 7 years younger than Mason. Mason is four times as old as Eli. If Mason is 20 years old, how old are Sophia and Eli?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Find Sophia's age: Sophia is 7 years younger than Mason → 20 − 7 = 13 years old.

2. Find Eli's age: Mason is 4 times as old as Eli → 20 ÷ 4 = 5 years old.

Sophia is 13 and Eli is 5.

Question 5

One-sixth of the 36 students in Mr. Clark's music class play the violin. How many students play the violin?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-sixth of a group, divide the total number of students by 6:

36 ÷ 6 = 6 students

Question 6

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract violin players from the total class:

36 − 6 = 30 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 84 and 49 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then, find the exact difference.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Estimate: Round 84 down to 80. Round 49 up to 50. Subtract: 80 − 50 = 30.

2. Exact Difference: Subtract the original numbers: 84 − 49 = 35.

Question 20

Caleb has 4 one-dollar bills, 2 quarters, 4 dimes, 3 nickels, and 7 pennies. What is his total amount of money?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

• 4 Dollar Bills = $4.00

• 2 Quarters = 2 × 25¢ = 50¢ ($0.50)

• 4 Dimes = 4 × 10¢ = 40¢ ($0.40)

• 3 Nickels = 3 × 5¢ = 15¢ ($0.15)

• 7 Pennies = 7¢ ($0.07)

Total: $4.00 + $0.50 + $0.40 + $0.15 + $0.07 = $5.12

Question 26

Write the standard number for each Roman numeral:

(a) XVI

(b) XXIX

(c) LXVIII

Teacher Answer Key

Answers:

(a) XVI = 10 + 5 + 1 = 16

(b) XXIX = 10 + 10 + (10 − 1) = 29

(c) LXVIII = 50 + 10 + 5 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 68

Question 28

A sack of whole wheat flour weighs 4 pounds and 5 ounces. What is its total weight in ounces? (Hint: 1 pound = 16 ounces)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Convert 4 pounds to ounces: 4 × 16 = 64 ounces.

2. Add the 5 ounces: 64 + 5 = 69 ounces.

Question 29

A bird sanctuary had 310 songbirds. In the morning, 65 birds flew outside to feed, and in the afternoon 40 new birds arrived. How many songbirds were in the sanctuary by the evening?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Subtract birds that left: 310 − 65 = 245 birds.

2. Add the new arrivals: 245 + 40 = 285 songbirds.
