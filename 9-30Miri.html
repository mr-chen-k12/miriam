1. (1)

Twenty-four girls sat in the first row. Thirty-seven girls sat in the second row. Eighty girls sat in the third row. Altogether how many girls sat in the first three rows?

Answer: \(141\) girls.

Solution: Add the three rows: \(24 + 37 + 80 = 61 + 80 = 141\).

2. (25)

Fifty seashells were in the jar. Jeremy took some seashells from the jar. Twenty-four seashells were left in the jar. How many seashells did Jeremy take from the jar?

Answer: \(26\) seashells.

Solution: Subtract what is left from what was there: \(50 - 24 = 26\).

3. (11)

The portable radio costs thirty-eight dollars. Adam has saved nineteen dollars. How much more money does Adam need in order to buy the portable radio?

Answer: \(\$19\) more.

Solution: \(38 - 19 = 19\). Adam needs 19 more dollars.

4. (7, 16)

Write 407 in expanded form. Then use words to write the number.

Answer: \(400 + 7\); "four hundred seven."

Solution: 407 = 4 hundreds + 0 tens + 7 ones, so expanded form is \(400 + 7\). In words: four hundred seven.

5. (Inv. 1, 29)

Use digits and symbols to write "Eight times zero equals five times zero."

Answer: \(8 \times 0 = 5 \times 0\).

Solution: "Eight" \(= 8\), "times" \(= \times\), "zero" \(= 0\), "equals" \(= =\), "five" \(= 5\). (Both sides equal 0.)

6. (Inv. 2)

Leon found this metal object in the scrap yard. About how long is it? (Measured against the centimeter ruler shown.)

Answer: About \(7\) cm.

Solution: Line the left end of the object up with 0 on the cm ruler; the right end reaches about the 7-cm mark, so the object is about 7 centimeters long.

7. (26)

Draw a circle and shade \(\frac{1}{4}\) of it.

Answer: A circle with one quarter shaded.

Solution: Draw a circle, divide it into 4 equal parts (for example, with a vertical and a horizontal line through the center), and shade exactly 1 of the 4 parts.

8. (20)

Round \(\$15.25\) to the nearest dollar.

Answer: \(\$15\).

Solution: \(\$15.25\) is between \(\$15\) and \(\$16\). The cents part, \(25\text{¢}\), is less than \(50\text{¢}\), so round down to \(\$15\).

9. (3)

Find the missing numbers in this counting sequence: \(48, \text{___}, \text{___}, 30, 24, \text{___}, \ldots\)

Answer: \(48, 42, 36, 30, 24, 18, \ldots\)

Solution: The pattern counts down by 6: \(48 - 6 = 42\), \(42 - 6 = 36\), \(36 - 6 = 30\), \(30 - 6 = 24\), \(24 - 6 = 18\).

10. (27)

If it is afternoon, what time will it be in 3 hours and 45 minutes according to this clock? (The clock shows 4:15.)

Answer: 8:00 PM.

Solution: The clock shows 4:15 (hour hand just past 4, minute hand on 3). In the afternoon that is 4:15 PM. Adding 3 hours gives 7:15 PM; adding 45 more minutes gives 8:00 PM.

11. (Inv. 2)

What is the perimeter of this triangle? (Sides: 5 cm, 5 cm, 6 cm.)

Answer: \(16\) cm.

Solution: Add the three sides: \(5 + 5 + 6 = 16\) cm.

12. (27)

Change this addition problem to a multiplication problem: \(3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3\)

Answer: \(8 \times 3\) (which equals \(24\)).

Solution: The number 3 is added 8 times, so the repeated addition becomes the multiplication \(8 \times 3 = 24\).

13. (Inv. 2)

The boat is five meters long. How many centimeters is that?

Answer: \(500\) cm.

Solution: There are 100 centimeters in a meter, so \(5 \times 100 = 500\) cm.

14. (29)

(a) \(3 \times 5\)    (b) \(5 \times 6\)    (c) \(9 \times 5\)

Answer: (a) \(15\)    (b) \(30\)    (c) \(45\).

Solution: (a) \(3 \times 5 = 15\). (b) \(5 \times 6 = 30\). (c) \(9 \times 5 = 45\).

17. (13)

\(\begin{array}{r} 325 \\ +\;869 \\ \hline \end{array}\)

Answer: \(1194\).

Solution: \(325 + 869 = 1194\) (ones: \(5+9=14\), carry 1; tens: \(2+6+1=9\); hundreds: \(3+8=11\)).
