Order of Rays — smallest to longest wavelength

  • 1. Gamma rays
  • 2. X-rays
  • 3. Ultraviolet Light
  • 4. Infrared Light
  • 5. Microwaves
  • 6. Radio waves

Morse Code

  • Invented by Samuel Morse in the 1830s
  • Uses dots and dashes to represent the letters of the alphabet
  • The device that delivers the codes is called a telegraph
  • A telegraph uses sound waves

✅ Helpful Radiation

  • Cell phones / Wi-Fi use Bluetooth
  • Two-way radios help with communication on ships, planes, and space shuttles
  • Microwaves are used in GPS systems

⚠️ Harmful Radiation

  • Sunburns due to Ultraviolet light exposure
  • Too much exposure to X-rays can cause cancers
  • Gamma rays can cause tissue damage
