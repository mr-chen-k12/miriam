1 (11)

a. It is 345 miles from Lillian's house to her grandmother's house. Lillian has already driven 178 of those miles. How many miles does Lillian have left to drive?

b. It is 512 miles from Lillian's house to her grandmother's house. Lillian has already driven 236 of those miles. How many miles does Lillian have left to drive?

c. It is 804 miles from Lillian's house to her grandmother's house. Lillian has already driven 457 of those miles. How many miles does Lillian have left to drive?

Answer: a. 167 miles   b. 276 miles   c. 347 miles

Solution: Subtract miles driven from the total: a. \(345 - 178 = 167\); b. \(512 - 236 = 276\); c. \(804 - 457 = 347\).

2 (10)

Use the given digits once each to make an even number greater than the target.

a. Digits 3, 9, 6 — make an even number greater than 900.

b. Digits 2, 5, 7 — make an even number greater than 700.

c. Digits 1, 6, 8 — make an even number greater than 800.

Answer: a. 936   b. 752   c. 816

Solution: a. Hundreds digit must be 9 (to top 900); ones digit must be even, so 6; tens digit 3: 936. b. Hundreds digit 7; ones digit 2; tens digit 5: 752. c. Hundreds digit 8; ones digit 6; tens digit 1: 816. Each is the only arrangement that works.

3 (Inv. 3)

Draw a pattern of dots to show each multiplication.

a. 2 and 6    b. 3 and 5    c. 4 and 4

Answer: a. 2 rows of 6 dots (12)   b. 3 rows of 5 dots (15)   c. 4 rows of 4 dots (16), as drawn

Solution: a. \(2 \times 6 = 12\). b. \(3 \times 5 = 15\). c. \(4 \times 4 = 16\).

4 (5)

Seth wrote his birth date in month/day/year form.

a. 7/04/08 — (i) In what month was Seth born? (ii) In what year?

b. 11/25/11 — (i) In what month was Seth born? (ii) In what year?

c. 2/14/99 — (i) In what month was Seth born? (ii) In what year?

Answer: a. (i) July (ii) 2008   b. (i) November (ii) 2011   c. (i) February (ii) 1999

Solution: The first number is the month and the last is the year: a. 7 = July, 08 = 2008; b. 11 = November, 11 = 2011; c. 2 = February, 99 = 1999.

Draw two perpendicular lines.

Answer: Two lines that cross at a right angle (90°), as drawn

Solution: Perpendicular lines meet to form square corners. The small square at the intersection marks the right angle.

6 (Inv. 3)

Find the area of each rectangle.

a. 5 cm long and 3 cm wide    b. 6 cm long and 4 cm wide    c. 7 cm long and 3 cm wide

Answer: a. 15 square centimeters   b. 24 square centimeters   c. 21 square centimeters

Solution: Area = length × width: a. \(5 \times 3 = 15\); b. \(6 \times 4 = 24\); c. \(7 \times 3 = 21\).

7 (22)

What fraction of each circle is shaded? (Give each answer in lowest terms.)

Answer: a. \(\frac{3}{8}\)   b. \(\frac{1}{2}\)   c. \(\frac{3}{4}\)

Solution: a. 3 of 8 wedges: \(\frac{3}{8}\). b. 4 of 8 wedges: \(\frac{4}{8} = \frac{1}{2}\). c. 6 of 8 wedges: \(\frac{6}{8} = \frac{3}{4}\).

8 (27)

Change each addition problem to a multiplication problem.

a. \(9 + 9 + 9 + 9\)    b. \(6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 6\)    c. \(8 + 8 + 8\)

Answer: a. \(4 \times 9\)   b. \(6 \times 6\)   c. \(3 \times 8\)

Solution: Count how many times the number is added: a. four 9's \(= 4 \times 9\); b. six 6's \(= 6 \times 6\); c. three 8's \(= 3 \times 8\).

9 (20)

Round each number to the nearest ten. Then add the rounded numbers.

a. 73 and 46    b. 58 and 94    c. 37 and 68

Answer: a. 120   b. 150   c. 110

Solution: a. \(73 \to 70\), \(46 \to 50\); \(70 + 50 = 120\). b. \(58 \to 60\), \(94 \to 90\); \(60 + 90 = 150\). c. \(37 \to 40\), \(68 \to 70\); \(40 + 70 = 110\).

10 (10, 35)

Is each value an even number of cents or an odd number of cents?

a. 4 nickels and 3 dimes    b. 5 nickels and 2 dimes    c. 2 nickels and 5 dimes

Answer: a. even (50¢)   b. odd (45¢)   c. even (60¢)

Solution: a. \(4 \times 5¢ + 3 \times 10¢ = 20 + 30 = 50¢\) — even. b. \(25 + 20 = 45¢\) — odd. c. \(10 + 50 = 60¢\) — even.

11 (Inv. 1)

The arrow is pointing to what number on each number line?

Answer: a. 75   b. 125   c. 175

Solution: Each small interval is 25. a. The third mark after 0 is 75. b. The fifth mark after 0 (one past 100) is 125. c. The seventh mark after 0 (three past 100) is 175.

16 (14)
