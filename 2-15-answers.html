1. Write and solve equations for problems 1 and 2.
Marcus baked 48 cookies. He wanted to pack them into boxes with 6 cookies in each box. How many boxes will he need?
Answer: \( 48 \div 6 = b \). Marcus will need 8 boxes.
2. A classroom has 35 desks arranged in rows. There are 5 rows of desks. If each row has the same number of desks, how many desks are in each row?
Answer: \( 35 \div 5 = d \). There are 7 desks in each row.
3. Write four multiplication/division facts using the numbers 6, 8, and 48.
Answer:
\( 6 \times 8 = 48 \)
\( 8 \times 6 = 48 \)
\( 48 \div 6 = 8 \)
\( 48 \div 8 = 6 \)
4. How many days are in a leap year?
Answer: 366 days.
5. Consider this sequence: \( \dots, 24,000, 25,000, 26,000, 27,000, \dots \)
a. Write a rule that describes how to find the next term of the sequence.
b. What is the next term of the sequence?
Answer:
a. Add 1,000 to the previous term.
b. 28,000

9. How many squares are shaded?
Answer: \( 3 \frac{1}{4} \) or 3.25 squares.
10. Describe the order of operations in this expression and find the number it equals.
\[ \sqrt{81} + (35 \div 5) \]
Answer: First, divide inside the parentheses (\(35 \div 5 = 7\)) and find the square root (\(\sqrt{81} = 9\)). Then, add the results together (\(9 + 7 = 16\)). The answer is 16.
