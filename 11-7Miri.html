Problem 1
In the soccer league there were five teams. Each team had nine players. How many players were on all five teams?
Write an equation: the total number of players is \(5\) teams times \(9\) players on each team. \[ p = 5 \times 9 = 45 \] 45 players
Problem 2
There were \(8\) nickels in each stack. There were \(7\) stacks of nickels. How many nickels were there in all?
Write an equation: multiply the number of stacks by the nickels in each stack. \[ n = 7 \times 8 = 56 \] 56 nickels
Problem 3
Maya ran the first lap in \(58.6\) seconds and the second lap in \(62.3\) seconds. Maya ran the first lap how much faster than the second lap?
Write an equation: subtract the faster (smaller) time from the slower time. \[ 62.3 - 58.6 = 3.7 \] 3.7 seconds faster

The numbers \(8\), \(9\), and \(72\) form a fact family: \[ 8 \times 9 = 72 \qquad 9 \times 8 = 72 \qquad 72 \div 8 = 9 \qquad 72 \div 9 = 8 \] 8 × 9 = 72; 9 × 8 = 72; 72 ÷ 8 = 9; 72 ÷ 9 = 8

Problem 26
The length of \(\overline{AB}\) is \(8\) cm. The length of \(\overline{AC}\) is \(15\) cm. How long is \(\overline{BC}\)?
Point \(B\) lies on \(\overline{AC}\), so \(AB + BC = AC\). Subtract: \[ BC = 15 \text{ cm} - 8 \text{ cm} = 7 \text{ cm} \] 7 cm
Problem 27
If three-fourths of the students passed the test, what percent of the students passed?
Three-fourths expressed as a fraction is \(\frac{3}{4}\). As a percentage: \[ \frac{3}{4} = \frac{75}{100} = 75\% \] 75%
Problem 28
Connect  If \(8y = 0\), then what does \(9y\) equal?
If \(8y = 0\), then \(y\) must be \(0\) (since any non-zero number multiplied by 8 gives a non-zero product). Therefore: \[ 9y = 9 \times 0 = 0 \] 0

Problem 30
Explain  In 1990, the median age of a resident in a town was \(32.8\) years. By 2010, the median age had increased by \(4.4\) years. What was the median age of a resident in 2010? Explain why your answer is reasonable.
Add the increase to the 1990 median age: \[ 32.8 + 4.4 = 37.2 \] The answer is reasonable because \(4.4\) is close to \(4\), and \(32.8 + 4 = 36.8\), which is very close to \(37.2\).
37.2 years
Problem 31
Decimal Division (Terminating)  Divide: \(17.55 \div 0.45\). Show your work.
Shift the decimal point two places to the right in both the divisor and dividend to divide by a whole number: \[ 17.55 \div 0.45 = 1755 \div 45 \] Dividing: \[ 1755 \div 45 = 39 \] Since there is no remainder, this is a terminating decimal solution.
39
Problem 32
Decimal Division (Repeating)  Divide: \(4.6 \div 0.3\). Write your answer using repeating decimal notation (a bar over the repeating digit).
Shift the decimal point one place to the right for both numbers: \[ 4.6 \div 0.3 = 46 \div 3 \] Perform long division: \[ 46 \div 3 = 15 \text{ R } 1 = 15.3333... = 15.\bar{3} \] The digit \(3\) repeats infinitely.
\(15.\bar{3}\)
Problem 33
Percentages & Tips  Maya's dinner bill at a restaurant came to \(\$48.00\). She wants to leave a \(15\%\) tip for her server.
a. How much money is the tip?
b. What is the total amount Maya will pay including the tip?
a. Convert \(15\%\) to a decimal (\(0.15\)) and multiply by the bill amount: \[ \text{Tip} = 0.15 \times \$48.00 = \$7.20 \] b. Add the tip to the original bill to find the total: \[ \text{Total} = \$48.00 + \$7.20 = \$55.20 \] a. $7.20 tip  ·  b. $55.20 total

4. Addition & Subtraction (left to right): \[ 9 + 1.125 - (-2.8) = 10.125 + 2.8 = 12.925 \] 12.925
