Math Problem Solutions

1. Blackbeard brought home 30 bags. Each bag contained 320 gold coins. How many coins were there in all?
Answer: 9,600 coins

Explanation: To find the total number of coins, multiply the number of bags by the number of coins in each bag.
$320 \times 30 = 9600$.

2. The movie was 3 hours long. If it started at 11:10 a.m., at what time did it end?
Answer: 2:10 p.m.

Explanation: Add 3 hours to the starting time.
11:10 a.m. + 1 hour = 12:10 p.m.
12:10 p.m. + 2 more hours = 2:10 p.m.

3. Jeremy is reading a 212-page book. If he has finished page 135, how many pages does he still have to read?
Answer: 77 pages

Explanation: Subtract the number of pages he has read from the total number of pages.
$212 - 135 = 77$.

4. Brad, Jan, and Jordan each scored one third of the team's 42 points. They each scored how many points?
Answer: 14 points

Explanation: Divide the total number of points by 3.
$42 \div 3 = 14$.

5. Round 4286 to the nearest thousand.
Answer: 4,000

Explanation: Look at the hundreds digit (2). Since 2 is less than 5, you round down. The thousands digit remains 4, and the rest become zeros.

6. The shirt was priced at $16.98. The tax was $1.02. Sam paid the clerk $20. How much money should Sam get back?
Answer: $2.00

Explanation: First, find the total cost by adding the price and the tax: $\$16.98 + \$1.02 = \$18.00$.
Then, subtract the total cost from the amount paid to find the change: $\$20.00 - \$18.00 = \$2.00$.

7. What fraction of the letters in the following word are I's?
SUPERCALIFRAGILISTICEXPIALIDOCIOUS
Answer: 7/34

Explanation: Count the total number of letters in the word, which is 34. Then count the number of "I"s, which is 7. The fraction is $\frac{7}{34}$.

Use the information below to answer problems 8-10:
In the first 8 games of this season, the Rio Hondo football team won 6 games and lost 2 games. They won their next game by a score of 24 to 20. The team will play 12 games in all.
8. In the first nine games of the season, how many games did Rio Hondo win?
Answer: 7 games

Explanation: They won 6 games out of their first 8. They also won their 9th game.
$6 + 1 = 7$ total wins.

9. Rio Hondo won its ninth game by how many points?
Answer: 4 points

Explanation: The score of the ninth game was 24 to 20. The difference is $24 - 20 = 4$.

10. What is the greatest number of games Rio Hondo could win this season?
Answer: 10 games

Explanation: They have currently played 9 games and won 7 of them. Since the season has 12 games total, there are 3 games remaining ($12 - 9 = 3$). If they win all the remaining games, their maximum possible wins would be $7 + 3 = 10$.

11. Compare: $3 \times 4 \times 5 \bigcirc 5 \times 4 \times 3$
Answer: =

Explanation: Both sides equal 60. The commutative property of multiplication states that changing the order of the numbers does not change the product.

12. $M - 137 = 257$
Answer: M = 394

Explanation: To isolate $M$, add 137 to both sides of the equation.
$M = 257 + 137 = 394$.

13. $N + 137 = 257$
Answer: N = 120

Explanation: To isolate $N$, subtract 137 from both sides of the equation.
$N = 257 - 137 = 120$.

Answer: 2.13

19. $3 \longdiv{921}$
Answer: 307

Explanation: $9 \div 3 = 3$. $2 \div 3 = 0$. Bring down the 1 to make 21. $21 \div 3 = 7$. The quotient is 307.

20. $5 \longdiv{678}$
Answer: 135 R 3

Explanation: $6 \div 5 = 1$ (R1). Bring down 7 to make 17. $17 \div 5 = 3$ (R2). Bring down 8 to make 28. $28 \div 5 = 5$ (R3). The quotient is 135 with a remainder of 3.

21. $4 \longdiv{2400}$
Answer: 600

Explanation: $24 \div 4 = 6$. Add the remaining two zeros to get 600.

22. $\$12.60 \div 5$
Answer: $2.52

Explanation: $12 \div 5 = 2$ (R2). Bring down 6 to make 26. $26 \div 5 = 5$ (R1). Bring down 0 to make 10. $10 \div 5 = 2$. Keep the decimal place to get $\$2.52$.

24. $\$46.00 \div 8$
Answer: $5.75

Explanation: $46 \div 8 = 5$ (R6). Bring down 0 to make 60. $60 \div 8 = 7$ (R4). Bring down 0 to make 40. $40 \div 8 = 5$. Keep the decimal place to get $\$5.75$.

25. $9^2 = 9 \times N$
Answer: N = 9

Explanation: $9^2$ is the same as $9 \times 9$, which is 81. The equation is $81 = 9 \times N$. Dividing both sides by 9 gives $N = 9$.

26. $5 \times W = 5 \times 10^2$
Answer: W = 100

Explanation: Calculate $10^2$, which is 100. The equation simplifies to $5 \times W = 5 \times 100$. Divide both sides by 5 to find $W = 100$.

27. The names of one fourth of the months begin with the letter J. What percent of the months begin with the letter J?
Answer: 25%

Explanation: The fraction $\frac{1}{4}$ represents one quarter. As a percentage, one quarter is equal to $25\%$.

28. What is the perimeter of this rectangle in millimeters?

Answer: 60 mm

Explanation: First, find the perimeter in centimeters. The sides are 2 cm, 1 cm, 2 cm, and 1 cm.
Perimeter = $2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 6$ cm.
Since there are 10 millimeters in a centimeter, multiply by 10.
$6 \text{ cm} \times 10 = 60 \text{ mm}$.

29. Draw a rectangle that is similar to the rectangle in problem 28 and whose sides are twice as long. What is the perimeter in centimeters of the rectangle you drew?
Answer: 12 cm

Explanation: If the original sides were 2 cm and 1 cm, the new rectangle has sides twice as long: 4 cm and 2 cm.
Perimeter = $4 + 2 + 4 + 2 = 12$ cm.

30. Kurt spun around three times and then fell down dizzy. How many degrees did Kurt turn?
Answer: 1080°

Explanation: One full spin (a circle) is 360 degrees. If he spun three times, multiply by 3.
$3 \times 360 = 1080$ degrees.
