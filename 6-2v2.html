Problem 1 (Two-Step Money)
Max gave the clerk a \$10 bill to pay for a notebook that cost \$3.15 and a pack of colored pencils that cost \$4.65. How much change should he get back?
Correct Answer: B) \$2.20
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Step 1: Find the combined total cost of both items:
    $$\$3.15 + \$4.65 = \$7.80$$
  • Step 2: Subtract that total cost from the \$10.00 bill to find the change:
    $$\$10.00 - \$7.80 = \$2.20$$
Problem 2 (Fractional Groups)
Sixty birds were resting in a large tree. One fourth of them were robins, and the remaining birds were sparrows. How many sparrows were in the tree?
Correct Answer: C) 45 sparrows
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Step 1: Calculate how many birds make up one-fourth (\(\frac{1}{4}\)) of 60 to find the robins:
    $$60 \div 4 = 15 \text{ robins}$$
  • Step 2: Subtract the robins from the total group count to find the sparrows:
    $$60 - 15 = 45 \text{ sparrows}$$
Problem 3 (Equal Groups)
An orchard worker sorted apples into 7 crates. If each crate holds exactly 24 apples, how many total apples were sorted?
Correct Answer: A) 168 apples
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Multiply the total number of crates by the quantity of items packed inside each crate:
    $$7 \times 24 = 168 \text{ apples}$$
Problem 4 (Division Word Problem)
Five identical storage boxes cost a business a total of 185 rubles. How many rubles did each individual storage box cost?
Correct Answer: C) 37 rubles
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Divide the total monetary valuation by the equivalent group quantities purchased:
    $$185 \div 5 = 37 \text{ rubles}$$
Problem 5 (Fractions & Decimals)
If exactly 13 small squares out of a 100-square grid are shaded, how would you write the shaded value as a fraction and as a decimal number?
Correct Answer: B) \(\frac{13}{100}\) and 0.13
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • A grid containing 100 total equal pieces serves as the base denominator (100).
  • The 13 shaded units represent the numerator, producing the structural fraction: \(\frac{13}{100}\).
  • Converting hundredths into standard place values places the final digit in the hundredths row, yielding: \(0.13\).
Problem 6 (Estimation vs. Exact)
Estimate the product of 29 and 52 by rounding to the nearest ten. Then find the exact product. Which option is correct?
Correct Answer: A) Estimated = 1,500; Exact = 1,508
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Estimation: Round 29 up to 30, and round 52 down to 50. Multiply the estimates:
    $$30 \times 50 = 1,500$$
  • Exact Calculation: Multiply the precise values without changes:
    $$29 \times 52 = 1,508$$
Problem 7 (Geometric Attributes)
Which of the following geometric statements is completely true?
Correct Answer: B) Every square is also a rectangle.
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • To classify as a rectangle, a shape only requires four right (90°) angles.
  • Because every square possesses four right angles, squares automatically fulfill the definition of a rectangle.
  • Conversely, statement A is false because rectangles do not require all 4 side boundaries to be equal in length.
Problem 8 (Decimal Place Value)
Which digit in the decimal number 4.392 holds the hundredths place value position?
Correct Answer: C) 9
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Identify values moving right from the decimal separator point:
    $$\text{4 [Ones]} \ . \ \mathbf{\text{3 [Tenths]}} \ \mathbf{\text{9 [Hundredths]}} \ \mathbf{\text{2 [Thousandths]}}$$
  • The digit occupying the hundredths spot is 9.
Problem 9 (Averages & Rate)
Sarah drove a total cumulative distance of 315 miles across a span of 5 hours. What was her average traveling speed in miles per hour?
Correct Answer: B) 63 mph
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • To calculate average tracking speeds, divide total distance output units by hours used:
    $$315 \div 5 = 63 \text{ miles per hour}$$
Problem 10 (Order of Operations)
Simplify the decimal mathematical expression: \(9.2 - (2.45 + 1.3)\)
Correct Answer: A) 5.45
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Step 1: Always resolve equations tucked inside parentheses boundaries first. Align decimal spaces correctly:
    $$2.45 + 1.30 = 3.75$$
  • Step 2: Carry out subtraction against the remaining front term:
    $$9.20 - 3.75 = 5.45$$
Problem 11 (Algebraic Equations)
Find the missing factor value for variable \(X\) inside the equation: \(4X = 728\)
Correct Answer: C) X = 182
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Isolate the value of variable \(X\) by dividing both components of the equation by 4:
    $$X = 728 \div 4$$
  • Solve using long division steps:
    $$728 \div 4 = 182$$
Problem 12 (Symmetry)
Which of these upper-case letters has exactly two lines of reflectional symmetry?
Correct Answer: B) H
Step-by-Step Explanation:
  • Letters M and T possess only 1 single vertical line of symmetry down their centers.
  • Letter Z has rotational symmetry but no reflectional mirror lines.
  • Letter H can be folded perfectly in half along a vertical line and a horizontal line, giving it exactly 2 lines of symmetry.
