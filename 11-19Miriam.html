4. What is the sum of $\sqrt{36}$ and $\sqrt{64}$?
Answer: $14$
Since $6 \times 6 = 36$, $\sqrt{36} = 6$. Since $8 \times 8 = 64$, $\sqrt{64} = 8$. $$\sqrt{36} + \sqrt{64} = 6 + 8 = 14$$

*8.Analyze Find the fourth multiple of $6$. Then find the third multiple of $8$. Compare these two multiples.
Answer: Both equal $24$; they are equal ($24 = 24$).
Fourth multiple of $6$: $6 \times 4 = 24$ (multiples: $6, 12, 18, 24$).
Third multiple of $8$: $8 \times 3 = 24$ (multiples: $8, 16, 24$).

*30. What is the sum of these lengths? Write three answers using different units. $$1\text{ yard} + 2\text{ feet} + 12\text{ inches}$$
Answer: $2\text{ yards}$, $6\text{ feet}$, $72\text{ inches}$
Since $12\text{ inches} = 1\text{ foot}$ and $3\text{ feet} = 1\text{ yard}$:
  • In feet: $3\text{ feet} + 2\text{ feet} + 1\text{ foot} = 6\text{ feet}$
  • In yards: $6\text{ feet} \div 3 = 2\text{ yards}$
  • In inches: $6\text{ feet} \times 12 = 72\text{ inches}$
Real-World Connection Each day Jamaal delivers $30$ newspapers in $1\text{ hr } 30\text{ min}$. At this rate, how many newspapers would he deliver each hour? Explain your answer.
Answer: $20\text{ newspapers per hour}$
$1\text{ hr } 30\text{ min}$ equals $90\text{ minutes}$, which is three $30$-minute half-hours. Dividing $30$ newspapers into $3$ equal half-hours gives $30 \div 3 = 10$ newspapers every $30$ minutes. In $1\text{ hour}$ ($60\text{ minutes}$, or two half-hours), Jamaal delivers $10 \times 2 = 20$ newspapers.
