Topic 1: Calculating the Mean (Based on Q1)

1. What is the mean of 1.5, 2.5, and 3.5?
Explanation: To find the mean, add the numbers together and divide by how many numbers there are.
$1.5 + 2.5 + 3.5 = 7.5$
$7.5 \div 3 = 2.5$.
2. What is the mean of 10.2, 11.4, and 12.0?
Explanation: Add the three numbers: $10.2 + 11.4 + 12.0 = 33.6$.
Divide the sum by 3: $33.6 \div 3 = 11.2$.
3. What is the mean of 5.5, 6.1, and 7.3?
Explanation: Add the three numbers: $5.5 + 6.1 + 7.3 = 18.9$.
Divide the sum by 3: $18.9 \div 3 = 6.3$.

Topic 2: Ordering Numbers (Based on Q7)

4. Arrange these numbers from least to greatest: $-3, 2, -1, 0, \frac{1}{4}$
Explanation: Negative numbers are smaller than zero, and the further left on the number line, the smaller they are (so $-3$ is less than $-1$). Then comes $0$, followed by positive fractions ($\frac{1}{4}$), and finally whole positive numbers ($2$).
5. Arrange these numbers from least to greatest: $5, -5, -2, \frac{1}{3}, 1$
Explanation: $-5$ is the smallest number. Next is $-2$. Then come the positive numbers in increasing order: the fraction $\frac{1}{3}$, followed by the integers $1$ and $5$.
6. Arrange these numbers from least to greatest: $0, -10, 10, -5, \frac{1}{2}$
Explanation: The negatives go first, with $-10$ being smaller than $-5$. Zero comes exactly in the middle of negatives and positives. Finally, $\frac{1}{2}$ comes before $10$.

Topic 3: Area of a Triangle (Based on Q9)

7. A right triangle has a base of 10 mm and a height of 8 mm. What is its area?
Explanation: The formula for the area of a triangle is $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$.
$A = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 8 = 5 \times 8 = 40$ sq mm.
8. A right triangle has a base of 12 inches and a height of 5 inches. What is its area?
Explanation: Use the area formula: $A = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$.
$A = \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 5 = 6 \times 5 = 30$ sq inches.
9. A right triangle has a base of 6 cm and a height of 6 cm. What is its area?
Explanation: Use the area formula: $A = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$.
$A = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 6 = 3 \times 6 = 18$ sq cm.

Topic 4: Directional Turns (Based on Q13)

10. Sarah was facing East. She turned to her left $90^\circ$. What direction is she facing now?
Explanation: If you are looking East (right on a map), a $90^\circ$ left turn rotates you counter-clockwise, meaning you will now be facing North (up on a map).
11. Marcus was facing South. He turned to his right $90^\circ$. What direction is he facing now?
Explanation: Facing South means looking down on a map. Turning $90^\circ$ to your right rotates you clockwise. You will be facing West (left on a map).
12. Emma was facing West. She turned to her left $90^\circ$. What direction is she facing now?
Explanation: Facing West means looking left on a map. A $90^\circ$ left turn rotates you counter-clockwise, putting you facing South.

Topic 6: Subtracting Mixed Numbers (Based on Q19)

16. Subtract: $4\frac{1}{4} - 1\frac{3}{4}$
Explanation: You cannot subtract 3 from 1 in the fraction part, so you must borrow 1 from the whole number 4.
$4\frac{1}{4} = 3\frac{5}{4}$.
Now subtract: $3\frac{5}{4} - 1\frac{3}{4} = 2\frac{2}{4}$.
Simplify the fraction: $2\frac{2}{4} = 2\frac{1}{2}$.
17. Subtract: $6\frac{2}{5} - 2\frac{4}{5}$
Explanation: Borrow 1 from the 6.
$6\frac{2}{5} = 5\frac{7}{5}$.
Now subtract: $5\frac{7}{5} - 2\frac{4}{5} = 3\frac{3}{5}$.
18. Subtract: $3\frac{1}{3} - 1\frac{2}{3}$
Explanation: Borrow 1 from the 3.
$3\frac{1}{3} = 2\frac{4}{3}$.
Now subtract: $2\frac{4}{3} - 1\frac{2}{3} = 1\frac{2}{3}$.

Topic 7: Simplifying Units and Rates (Based on Q29)

19. Simplify: 5 cm + 50 mm (Write the answer in millimeters.)
Explanation: First convert centimeters to millimeters. Since 1 cm = 10 mm, 5 cm = 50 mm.
Add them together: 50 mm + 50 mm = 100 mm.
20. Simplify: 200 apples ÷ 25 baskets
Explanation: Divide the numbers and keep the units as a rate.
$200 \div 25 = 8$. The unit becomes "apples per basket".
21. Simplify: 3 cm + 20 mm (Write the answer in millimeters.)
Explanation: Convert centimeters to millimeters (1 cm = 10 mm). So, 3 cm = 30 mm.
Add them together: 30 mm + 20 mm = 50 mm.
