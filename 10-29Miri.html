3.(31)
A llama weighs about 375 pounds. A coyote weighs about 75 pounds. A llama weighs about how many pounds more than a coyote?

Solution

Answer
\(5 \times 6 = 30, \quad 6 \times 5 = 30, \quad 30 \div 5 = 6, \quad 30 \div 6 = 5\)

7.(27)

Answer

Solution

26.(Inv. 2, 21)
MULTIPLE CHOICE The radius of a circle is 3 ft. Which of the following is not the diameter of the circle?
A 36 in.   B 6 ft   C 2 yd   D 72 in.
Answer
\(\text{A}\)
Solution
The diameter is twice the radius: \(2 \times 3 = 6\) ft. Check each choice: B is 6 ft ✓; 6 ft = 72 in., so D is the diameter ✓; 6 ft = 2 yd, so C is the diameter ✓; but 36 in. = 3 ft, which is the radius, not the diameter ✗. So A is not the diameter.
27.(23)
MULTIPLE CHOICE Which of these angles is acute?
Answer
\(\text{B}\)
Solution
An acute angle measures less than 90°. A is obtuse (more than 90°), B is acute (less than 90°), C is a right angle (exactly 90°, marked with the square corner), and D is a straight angle (180°). So B is the acute angle.

29.(49)
ESTIMATE One human hand has 27 bones. One human foot has 26 bones. About how many bones are in two hands plus two feet? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.
Answer
\(\text{About }120\text{ bones}\)
Solution
Round each number to the nearest ten: 27 rounds to 30 and 26 rounds to 30. Two hands: \(2 \times 30 = 60\). Two feet: \(2 \times 30 = 60\). Total: \(60 + 60 = 120\) bones. The estimate is reasonable because each count was rounded to a nearby ten that is easy to multiply and add, and 120 is close to the exact total of \(27 + 27 + 26 + 26 = 106\).
30.(11, 17)
ESTIMATE The land area of Booker T. Washington National Monument in Virginia is 239 acres. The land area of Cabrillo National Monument in California is 160 acres. What is a reasonable estimate of the total acreage of these two national monuments? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.
Answer
\(\text{About }400\text{ acres}\)
Solution
Round to the nearest hundred: 239 rounds to 200 and 160 rounds to 200. Then \(200 + 200 = 400\) acres. The estimate is reasonable because the exact total is \(239 + 160 = 399\) acres, so the estimate of 400 is almost exact.
