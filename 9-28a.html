Problem 1
Four hundred sixty-five is how much greater than twenty-four? Write and solve an equation.
Answer:
Equation: $465 - 24 = d$
Difference: $441$
Explanation: To find how much greater $465$ is than $24$, subtract $24$ from $465$: \[ 465 - 24 = 441 \]
Problem 2
Marcie had four hundred twenty marbles. Kareem had one hundred twenty-three marbles. How many fewer marbles did Kareem have? Write and solve an equation.
Answer:
Equation: $420 - 123 = m$
Solution: $297\text{ marbles}$
Explanation: Subtract Kareem's count from Marcie's count: \[ 420 - 123 = 297 \]
Problem 3
On 1-cm grid paper, draw a square that is $4\text{ cm}$ on each side.
a. What is the perimeter of the square?
b. What is the area of the square?
Answer:
a. Perimeter = $16\text{ cm}$
b. Area = $16\text{ cm}^2$
Explanation:
a. Perimeter is the distance around: $P = 4 \times 4\text{ cm} = 16\text{ cm}$.
b. Area is side length squared: $A = 4\text{ cm} \times 4\text{ cm} = 16\text{ cm}^2$.
Problem 4

Problem 5
Draw a circle that has a diameter of $4\text{ centimeters}$. What is the radius of the circle?
Answer:
Radius = $2\text{ cm}$
Explanation: The radius of a circle is half of its diameter: \[ r = \frac{\text{Diameter}}{2} = \frac{4\text{ cm}}{2} = 2\text{ cm} \]
Problem 6
Jharma arrived home from school at the time shown on the clock ($3:40$) and finished her homework $1\text{ hour } 35\text{ minutes}$ later. What time did Jharma finish her homework?
Answer:
$5:15\text{ PM}$
Explanation:
1. Start time = $3:40\text{ PM}$.
2. Add $1\text{ hour}$: $3:40 + 1\text{ hour} = 4:40\text{ PM}$.
3. Add $35\text{ minutes}$: $4:40 + 20\text{ min} = 5:00\text{ PM}$, plus remaining $15\text{ min} = 5:15\text{ PM}$.

Problem 21
If the radius of a circle is $12\text{ inches}$, then the diameter of the circle is how many feet?
Answer:
$2\text{ feet}$
Explanation:
1. Diameter $= 2 \times \text{Radius} = 2 \times 12\text{ in} = 24\text{ inches}$.
2. Convert inches to feet: $24\text{ inches} \div 12 = 2\text{ feet}$.
Problem 22
Five squared is how much more than $5 + 5$?
Answer:
$15$
Explanation:
1. Five squared $= 5^2 = 25$.
2. $5 + 5 = 10$.
3. Difference: $25 - 10 = 15$.

Problem 30
Estimate: M'Lisa would like to purchase about 100 balloons for her birthday party. One bag of 25 balloons costs $\$2.49$. What is a reasonable estimate of M'Lisa's cost to purchase about 100 balloons? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.
Answer:
Estimated cost: $\$10.00$
Explanation:
1. Calculate the number of bags needed: $100 \div 25 = 4\text{ bags}$.
2. Round the cost per bag to a friendly number: $\$2.49 \approx \$2.50$.
3. Multiply: $4 \times \$2.50 = \$10.00$. This estimate is reasonable because $\$2.49$ is very close to $\$2.50$, making mental multiplication fast and accurate.
