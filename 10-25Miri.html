1.
Conclude Use the numbers 0.5, 0.9, and 1.4 to write two addition facts and two subtraction facts.
Answer
\(0.5 + 0.9 = 1.4; \quad 0.9 + 0.5 = 1.4; \quad 1.4 - 0.5 = 0.9; \quad 1.4 - 0.9 = 0.5\)
Solution
The three numbers form a fact family: the two smaller numbers add to the largest, and subtracting either smaller number from the largest leaves the other.
2.
A whole hour is 60 minutes. How many minutes is three fourths \(\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)\) of an hour?
Answer
\(45\text{ minutes}\)
Solution
One fourth of 60 minutes is \(60 \div 4 = 15\) minutes, so three fourths is \(3 \times 15 = 45\) minutes.
3.
Explain An airplane flew at an altitude of 212 miles above the Earth. A drone flew at an altitude of 38 miles above the Earth. The airplane was how much higher than the drone? Explain why your answer is reasonable.
Answer
\(174\text{ miles higher}\)
Solution
\(212 - 38 = 174\) miles. The answer is reasonable because rounding gives about \(210 - 40 = 170\), which is close to 174.

Solution

9.
How many circles are shaded?
Answer
\(2\frac{1}{4}\)
Solution
Two circles are completely shaded and the third circle has one of its four equal parts shaded, so \(2 + \frac{1}{4} = 2\frac{1}{4}\) circles are shaded.

13.
Compare: \(20 - (9 - 5) \bigcirc (20 - 9) - 5\)
Answer
\(20 - (9 - 5) > (20 - 9) - 5\)
Solution
Left side: \(20 - (9 - 5) = 20 - 4 = 16\). Right side: \((20 - 9) - 5 = 11 - 5 = 6\). Since \(16 > 6\), the symbol is \(>\).

Model Draw a rectangle that is 4 in. by 3 in.
a. The perimeter of the rectangle is how many inches?
b. The area of the rectangle is how many square inches?
Answer
\(\text{a. }14\text{ in.} \qquad \text{b. }12\text{ sq. in.}\)

26.
Multiple Choice Which of the following segments is a diameter of the circle?
A. \(\overline{MN}\)   B. \(\overline{OP}\)   C. \(\overline{OM}\)   D. \(\overline{OQ}\)
Answer
\(\text{A} — \overline{MN}\)
Solution
A diameter is a segment through the center with both endpoints on the circle. Only \(\overline{MN}\) passes through the center O with both endpoints on the circle; the others are radii.
27.
Formulate Ana solved the first puzzle in 58 seconds. She solved the second puzzle in 36 seconds. The first puzzle took how much longer to solve than the second puzzle? Write an equation to solve the problem.
Answer
\(22\text{ seconds}\)
Solution
Equation: \(58 - 36 = 22\). The first puzzle took 22 seconds longer.
28.
Describe the order of operations in each expression. Then find the number each expression equals.
a. \(24 - (9 - 4)\)   b. \((24 - 9) - 4\)
Answer
\(\text{a. }19 \qquad \text{b. }11\)
Solution
a. Parentheses first: \(9 - 4 = 5\), then \(24 - 5 = 19\). b. Parentheses first: \(24 - 9 = 15\), then \(15 - 4 = 11\). Grouping changes the answer, so subtraction is not associative.

Answer

Solution
\(104 - 83 = 21\) degrees.

33.
Percent A school library has 200 books on a display shelf. If 30% of the books are mystery books:
a. Express 30% as both a fraction in simple form and as a decimal.
b. How many mystery books are on the display shelf?
Answer
\(\text{a. Fraction: }\frac{3}{10}, \text{ Decimal: }0.30 \qquad \text{b. }60\text{ mystery books}\)
Solution
a. \(30\% = \frac{30}{100} = \frac{3}{10} = 0.30\). b. \(\frac{3}{10}\) of 200 is \(200 \div 10 \times 3 = 60\) mystery books.
34.
Order of Operations Evaluate the expression step by step using PEMDAS (Parentheses, Multiplication/Division, Addition/Subtraction): \(\left(\frac{2}{5} + 0.35\right) \times 10 + (-3)\)
Answer
\(4.5\)
Solution
Parentheses first: \(\frac{2}{5} = 0.40\), so \(0.40 + 0.35 = 0.75\). Then multiplication: \(0.75 \times 10 = 7.5\). Finally addition: \(7.5 + (-3) = 4.5\).

40.
Order of Operations Simplify: \(3^2 - (-2) \times 1.5 + \frac{1}{4} - 0.25\)
Answer
\(12\)
Solution
Exponents first: \(3^2 = 9\). Then multiplication: \((-2) \times 1.5 = -3\), so \(9 - (-3) = 12\). Finally \(12 + \frac{1}{4} - 0.25 = 12 + 0.25 - 0.25 = 12\).
