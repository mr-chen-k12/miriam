Cumulative Practice Set - 30 Questions

1. Fifty-six days is how many weeks?

There are 7 days in 1 week.

$$56 \div 7 = 8$$

Answer: 8 weeks

2. List the factors of 12.

Factors are numbers that divide into 12 evenly:

  • \(1 \times 12 = 12\)
  • \(2 \times 6 = 12\)
  • \(3 \times 4 = 12\)

Answer: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

3. Forty apples were placed equally into 4 baskets. How many apples were in each basket?

Divide the total number of apples by the number of baskets:

$$40 \div 4 = 10$$

Answer: 10 apples

4. Draw a circle and shade 50% of it.

50% is equal to one half (\(\frac{1}{2}\)) of the circle.

Answer: Shade half of the circle.

5. Fifty years equals how many decades?

1 decade = 10 years.

$$50 \div 10 = 5$$

Answer: 5 decades

6. Find the sixth multiple of 5. Then subtract 10. What is the answer?

Step 1 (6th multiple of 5): \(6 \times 5 = 30\)

Step 2 (Subtract 10): \(30 - 10 = 20\)

Answer: 20

7. Compare: \(50\% \bigcirc \frac{3}{4}\)

\(\frac{3}{4}\) is equal to 75%.

50% is less than 75%.

Answer: <

8. Segment AB is 5 cm long. Segment AC is 12 cm long. How long is segment BC?

The total length AC is the sum of AB and BC.

\(AC - AB = BC\)

\(12\text{ cm} - 5\text{ cm} = 7\text{ cm}\)

Answer: 7 cm

9. What is the perimeter of a rectangle that is 30 mm by 15 mm?

Perimeter = Sum of all sides.

\(P = 30 + 15 + 30 + 15\)

\(P = 90\)

Answer: 90 mm

10. Round 7,642 to the nearest thousand.

The thousands digit is 7. Look at the hundreds digit (6). Since 6 is 5 or greater, we round up.

Answer: 8,000

To find the missing addend, subtract the known addend from the total:

1 hour = 60 minutes.

$$3 \times 60 = 180$$

Answer: 180 minutes

23. There are 5 rows of chairs with 12 chairs in each row. How many chairs are there in total?

Multiply the number of rows by the number of chairs per row:

$$5 \times 12 = 60$$

Answer: 60 chairs

\(A = 7 \times 4\)
