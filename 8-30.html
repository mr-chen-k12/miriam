Question 1

Nine feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply 9 by 12:

9 feet × 12 inches = 108 inches

Question 2

Sofia handed the cashier a $50 bill to buy a board game that cost $27.85. How much change did she receive?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the cost from $50.00:

$50.00 − $27.85 = $22.15

Question 3

Liam is 9 years younger than Grace. Grace is seven times as old as Zoe. If Grace is 35 years old, how old are Liam and Zoe?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Liam: 35 − 9 = 26 years old.

2. Zoe: 35 ÷ 7 = 5 years old.

Liam is 26 and Zoe is 5.

Question 5

One-sixth of the 42 students in Mr. Patel's class are on the school chorus. How many students are in the chorus?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Divide the total by 6:

Question 6

Looking back at Question 5, how many students are not in the chorus?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the 7 chorus students from the total:

42 − 7 = 35 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 87 and 34 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then find the exact difference.

Step-by-Step Solution:
