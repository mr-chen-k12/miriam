Math Worksheet Problems

1. Pears cost 59¢ per pound. How much would 4 pounds of pears cost?
Answer:

$2.36 (or 236¢)

Explanation:

To find the total cost, multiply the cost per pound by the number of pounds:
$59 \times 4 = 236$
Since the cost is in cents, 236¢ is equal to $2.36.

2. Find the perimeter and area of this rectangle:
Answer:

Perimeter = 20 units; Area = 24 square units

Explanation:

Perimeter is the distance around the outside of the rectangle. Add all four sides: $6 + 4 + 6 + 4 = 20$ units.
Area is the number of square units inside. Multiply the length by the width: $6 \times 4 = 24$ square units.

3. There were three hundred sixty books on the floor. Frankie put one fourth of the books on a table.
(a) How many books did Frankie put on the table?
(b) How many books were still on the floor?
Answer:

(a) 90 books
(b) 270 books

Explanation:

(a) Find one fourth of 360 by dividing by 4: $\frac{360}{4} = 90$.
(b) Subtract the books moved to the table from the total to find how many are left: $360 - 90 = 270$.

4. What percent of the books in problem 3 were left on the floor?
Answer:

75%

Explanation:

Since $\frac{1}{4}$ of the books were moved to the table, $\frac{3}{4}$ of the books were left on the floor. $\frac{3}{4}$ as a percentage is $75\%$.

5. To what mixed number is the arrow pointing?
Answer:

$201 \frac{4}{9}$

Explanation:

The space between 201 and 202 is divided into 9 equal intervals, meaning each mark represents $\frac{1}{9}$. The arrow points to the 4th mark past 201, which is $201 \frac{4}{9}$.

6. Estimate the sum of 272 and 483. Begin by rounding each number to the nearest hundred.
Answer:

800

Explanation:

First, round 272 to the nearest hundred: 300.
Next, round 483 to the nearest hundred: 500.
Then, add the rounded numbers: $300 + 500 = 800$.

8. One quart of milk is how many ounces?
Answer:

32 ounces

Explanation:

There are 8 fluid ounces in 1 cup, 2 cups in 1 pint (16 oz), and 2 pints in 1 quart. Therefore, $16 \times 2 = 32$ fluid ounces in one quart.

26. $3A = 30 + 30$
Answer:

$A = 20$

Explanation:

First, simplify the right side: $30 + 30 = 60$. The equation is now $3A = 60$. Divide both sides by 3 to isolate A: $60 \div 3 = 20$.

27. $3^2 - 2^3$
Answer:

1

Explanation:

Calculate the exponents first: $3^2 = 3 \times 3 = 9$. Next, $2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8$. Subtract the results: $9 - 8 = 1$.

28. $4 + 7 + 3 + 5 + 6 + 5 + 7 + 2 + N = 43$
Answer:

$N = 4$

Explanation:

Add all the known numbers together: $4 + 7 + 3 + 5 + 6 + 5 + 7 + 2 = 39$. The equation is now $39 + N = 43$. Subtract 39 from 43 to find N: $43 - 39 = 4$.

29. Which segment appears to be perpendicular to segment $BC$?
Answer:

Segment $AC$

Explanation:

Perpendicular lines intersect to form a 90-degree (right) angle. Looking at the right triangle drawn, vertical segment $AC$ intersects the horizontal segment $BC$ at a right angle.

30. Draw a triangle similar to but not congruent to $\Delta ABC$ in problem 29.
Answer:

A smaller or larger right triangle.

Explanation:

Similar triangles have the exact same shape (identical internal angles) but are different sizes. Congruent triangles are the same size and shape. To answer this, you draw a right triangle with the same proportions as $\Delta ABC$ but scale it up or down in size.
