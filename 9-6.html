Question 1

Eight feet equals how many inches? (Hint: 1 foot = 12 inches)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. Multiply the number of feet by 12:

8 feet × 12 inches = 96 inches

Question 2

Sophia handed the cashier a $50 bill to buy a winter jacket that cost $34.20. How much change did she receive back?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the cost of the jacket from $50.00:

$50.00 − $34.20 = $15.80

Question 3

Ethan is 6 years younger than Mason. Mason is four times as old as Lily. If Mason is 24 years old, how old are Ethan and Lily?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Find Ethan's age: Ethan is 6 years younger than Mason → 24 − 6 = 18 years old.

2. Find Lily's age: Mason is 4 times as old as Lily → 24 ÷ 4 = 6 years old.

Ethan is 18 and Lily is 6.

Question 5

One-fourth of the 32 students in Mrs. Davis's art class play guitar. How many students play guitar?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-fourth of a group, divide the total number of students by 4:

32 ÷ 4 = 8 students

Question 6

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract guitar players from the total class:

32 − 8 = 24 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 84 and 39 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then, find the exact difference.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Estimate: Round 84 down to 80. Round 39 up to 40. Subtract: 80 − 40 = 40.

2. Exact Difference: Subtract the original numbers: 84 − 39 = 45.

Question 14

A movie started at 2:15 PM and finished at 4:45 PM. How long did the movie last?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. From 2:15 PM to 4:15 PM is 2 hours.

2. From 4:15 PM to 4:45 PM is 30 minutes.

Total time: 2 hours and 30 minutes (or 150 minutes).

Question 17

Is the sum of 324 + 157 an even number or an odd number? Explain how you know by looking at the ones digits.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

2. An even number plus an odd number always equals an odd number (324 + 157 = 481).

Question 20

Mia has 4 one-dollar bills, 3 quarters, 2 dimes, 3 nickels, and 4 pennies. What is her total amount of money?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

• 4 Dollar Bills = $4.00

• 3 Quarters = 3 × 25¢ = 75¢ ($0.75)

• 2 Dimes = 2 × 10¢ = 20¢ ($0.20)

• 3 Nickels = 3 × 5¢ = 15¢ ($0.15)

• 4 Pennies = 4¢ ($0.04)

Total: $4.00 + $0.75 + $0.20 + $0.15 + $0.04 = $5.14

Question 26

Question 28

A watermelon weighs 4 pounds and 10 ounces. What is its total weight in ounces? (Hint: 1 pound = 16 ounces)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Convert 4 pounds to ounces: 4 × 16 = 64 ounces.

2. Add the 10 ounces: 64 + 10 = 74 ounces.

Question 29

A library had 420 visitors in the morning. By noon, 110 visitors left, and in the afternoon 75 new visitors entered. How many visitors were in the library by the end of the day?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Subtract visitors who left: 420 − 110 = 310 visitors.

2. Add new visitors: 310 + 75 = 385 visitors.
