Lesson 94: Two-Step Word Problems

Problem 1
Gabriel gave the clerk a $5 bill to pay for a half gallon of milk that cost $1.06 and a box of cereal that cost $2.39. How much money should he get back?
Step 1 (Find total cost): $\text{\$1.06} + \text{\$2.39} = \text{\$3.45}$
Step 2 (Calculate change): $\text{\$5.00} - \text{\$3.45} = \text{\$1.55}$
Answer: $1.55
Problem 2
Eighty-one animals crossed the bridge. One third of them were billy goats. The rest were bears. How many bears crossed the bridge? (Hint: First find how many billy goats crossed the bridge.)
Step 1 (Find billy goats): $\frac{1}{3} \text{ of } 81 = 81 \div 3 = 27 \text{ billy goats}$
Step 2 (Find bears): $81 - 27 = 54$
Answer: 54 bears
Problem 3
Johnny planted 8 rows of apple trees. There were 15 trees in each row. How many trees did he plant?
Calculation: $8 \times 15 = 120$
Answer: 120 trees
Problem 4
Four pounds of bananas cost Titania one hundred fifty-six rubles. Each pound of bananas cost how many rubles?
Calculation: $156 \div 4 = 39$
Answer: 39 rubles
Problem 5
This scale shows a mass of how many grams?
Explanation: The dial marks go up by increments of 200. The needle is pointing perfectly halfway between 400 and 600.
Calculation: $\frac{400 + 600}{2} = 500$
Answer: 500 grams
Problem 6
Write the tally for 16.
Explanation: Tallies are collected in groups of 5. For 16, we write three sets of 5-tally markers, plus one single unit marker remaining: $5 + 5 + 5 + 1 = 16$.
Answer: Shown in the diagram above.
Problem 7
Name the shaded part of the large square (a) as a fraction, (b) as a decimal number.
Explanation: The large grid consists of 100 small squares total. Exactly 7 individual squares are shaded in the bottom row.
(a) Fraction: $\frac{7}{100}$
(b) Decimal: $0.07$
Answer: (a) $\frac{7}{100}$, (b) 0.07
Problem 8
Estimate the product of 32 and 48. Then find the exact product.
Estimation: Round 32 to 30 and 48 to 50. $30 \times 50 = 1500$
Exact Calculation: $32 \times 48 = 1536$
Answer: Estimate = 1500; Exact = 1536
Problem 9
One third of the 24 camels were Bactrian. How many camels were Bactrian? Draw a picture to illustrate the problem.
Calculation: $\frac{1}{3} \times 24 = 24 \div 3 = 8$
Answer: 8 camels
Problem 10
A quart is a quarter of a gallon. A quart is what percent of a gallon?
Explanation: A "quarter" means one-fourth ($\frac{1}{4}$). Converting a quarter to a percentage:
$\frac{1}{4} \times 100\% = 25\%$
Answer: 25%
Problem 11
For each statement, write either "true" or "false."
(a) Every square is also a rectangle.
(b) Every rectangle is also a square.
(a) True: A rectangle requires four 90° angles, which a square satisfies.
(b) False: A square requires all four sides to be equal lengths, which standard rectangles do not satisfy.
Answer: (a) true, (b) false
Problem 12
Four hundred seventy-one of the one thousand students in the school were girls. Girls made up what fraction of the students in the school?
Explanation: Put the target quantity over the total quantity.
Answer: $\frac{471}{1000}$
Problem 13
Write the answer to problem 12 as a decimal number. Then use words to name the number.
Decimal Form: $0.471$
Word Form: four hundred seventy-one thousandths
Answer: 0.471; four hundred seventy-one thousandths
Problem 14
Which digit in 1.875 is in the tenths place?
Explanation: Place values right of the decimal run: Tenths, Hundredths, Thousandths. The first digit to the right of the decimal point is 8.
Answer: 8
Problem 15
Matthew traveled 496 miles in 8 hours. He traveled an average of how many miles per hour?
Calculation: $496 \div 8 = 62$
Answer: 62 miles per hour
Problem 16
Simplify: $8.3 - (1.74 + 0.9)$
Step 1 (Parentheses): $1.74 + 0.90 = 2.64$
Step 2 (Subtraction): $8.30 - 2.64 = 5.66$
Answer: 5.66
Problem 17
Multiply: $63 \times 1000$
Explanation: Shift place value positions 3 steps up by appending three zeros.
Answer: 63,000
Problem 18
Multiply: $80 \times 50\text{¢}$
Calculation in Cents: $80 \times 50 = 4000\text{¢}$
Convert to Dollars: $4000 \div 100 = \text{\$40.00}$
Answer: $40.00 (or 4000¢)
Problem 19
Add the column: $37 + 81 + 45 + 139 + 7 + 15 + 60$
Step-by-step Addition:
$37 + 81 = 118$
$118 + 45 = 163$
$163 + 139 = 302$
$302 + 7 = 309$
$309 + 15 = 324$
$324 + 60 = 384$
Answer: 384

Problem 24
Find $N$: $3N = 624$
Calculation: Divide both sides by 3:
$N = 624 \div 3 = 208$
Answer: 208
Problem 25
Find $W$: $5 + W = 5^2$
Step 1: Evaluate exponent: $5^2 = 25$
Step 2: Solve expression: $5 + W = 25 \rightarrow W = 25 - 5$
Answer: 20
Problem 26
Draw and shade rectangles to show that $1\frac{2}{5}$ equals $\frac{7}{5}$.
Explanation: Split rectangles into 5 segments each. One full rectangle ($5/5$) plus $2/5$ of the next rectangle matches a total of $7/5$ shaded blocks.
Answer: Visual confirmation shown above.
Problem 27
A room is 5 yards long and 4 yards wide. How many square yards of carpeting are needed to cover the floor?
Area Formula: $\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{width}$
Calculation: $5 \text{ yards} \times 4 \text{ yards} = 20 \text{ sq yards}$
Answer: 20 square yards
Problem 28
The radius of this circle is 15 millimeters. The diameter of the circle is how many centimeters?
Step 1 (Find diameter in mm): $\text{Diameter} = 2 \times \text{radius} = 2 \times 15 \text{ mm} = 30 \text{ mm}$
Step 2 (Convert mm to cm): Since $10 \text{ mm} = 1 \text{ cm}$, divide by 10:
$30 \div 10 = 3 \text{ cm}$
Answer: 3 centimeters
Problem 29
Which of these letters has two lines of symmetry?
        V    W    X    Y    Z
Explanation:
- V, W, Y: have only 1 vertical line of symmetry.
- Z: has rotational symmetry but no reflection lines.
- X: has 2 lines of symmetry (both vertical and horizontal axes match).
Answer: X
Problem 30
The angle formed by the letter V in problem 29 measures about how many degrees?
A. 45°      B. 90°      C. 135°      D. 180°
Explanation: A right angle is 90°. The vertex inside a standard typographic letter 'V' is sharp and acute, which means it measures distinctly less than 90°. 45° is the only reasonable option.
Answer: A. 45°
