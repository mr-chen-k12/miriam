1.(11, 30)
FORMULATE Write and solve equations for problems 1 and 2.
Four hundred ninety-five oil drums were on the first train. Seven hundred sixty-two oil drums were on the first two trains combined. How many oil drums were on the second train?
Answer
\(267\text{ oil drums}\)
Solution
Equation: \(495 + n = 762\). Solve: \(n = 762 - 495 = 267\) oil drums on the second train.

8.(23)
CONCLUDE What type of angle is formed by the hands of this clock?
Answer
\(\text{acute angle}\)
Solution
The hands point at 10 and 12, which is 2 hour-marks apart. Each hour-mark is \(30^\circ\), so the angle is \(2 \times 30^\circ = 60^\circ\). Since \(60^\circ < 90^\circ\), it is an acute angle.

MODEL Draw a rectangle 3 in. long and 1 in. wide.
a. What is its perimeter?
b. What is its area?
Answer
\(\text{a. }8\text{ in.} \qquad \text{b. }3\text{ sq. in.}\)

27.(21)
MULTIPLE CHOICE Rosario noticed that the distance from the pole in the center of the tetherball circle to the painted circle was about six feet. What was the approximate radius of the tetherball circle?
A 12 ft   B 4 yd   C 3 ft   D 2 yd
Answer
\(\text{D } — 2\text{ yd}\)
Solution
The radius is the distance from the center to the circle, which is about 6 feet. Since 3 feet = 1 yard, 6 feet = 2 yards. So the answer is D.
28.(4, 22)
Tyrique, Dominic, and Tamasha checked their pockets for change. Tyrique had two dimes and a penny. Dominic had three nickels and two pennies. Tamasha had a nickel, a dime, and a penny. Using dollar signs and decimal points list the three amounts in order from least to greatest.
Answer
\($0.16, \quad $0.17, \quad $0.21\)
Solution
Tyrique: \(10 + 10 + 1 = 21\)¢ = \($0.21\). Dominic: \(5 + 5 + 5 + 1 + 1 = 17\)¢ = \($0.17\). Tamasha: \(5 + 10 + 1 = 16\)¢ = \($0.16\). Least to greatest: \($0.16\) (Tamasha), \($0.17\) (Dominic), \($0.21\) (Tyrique).
29.(3, 32)
PREDICT What is the twelfth term of the sequence below?
\(12, 24, 36, 48, 60, \ldots\)
Answer
\(144\)
Solution
Each term increases by 12, so the terms are multiples of 12: the \(n\)th term is \(12n\). The twelfth term is \(12 \times 12 = 144\).

Real-World Connection.(RWC)
Cecilia's book had 58 pages. She read for 6 hours and had 4 pages left.
a. About how many pages did Cecilia read each hour? Write a division problem to solve the problem.
b. What number will go in the division box? Explain why.
Answer
\(\text{a. }9\text{ pages per hour} \qquad \text{b. }54\)
Solution
a. Pages read: \(58 - 4 = 54\). Division problem: \(54 \div 6 = 9\), so she read about 9 pages each hour.
b. 54 goes in the division box. The box holds the dividend — the total number of pages she actually read (58 pages in the book minus the 4 pages left) — which is divided by the 6 hours.
