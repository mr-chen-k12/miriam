Addition Word Problems

1.

Twenty-eight girls sat in the first row. Thirty-four girls sat in the second row. Fifty girls sat in the third row. Altogether how many girls sat in the first three rows?

Answer: \(112\) girls.

Explanation: Add the three rows: \(28 + 34 + 50 = 62 + 50 = 112\).

2.

A baker sold 15 loaves of bread on Monday, 26 loaves on Tuesday, and 40 loaves on Wednesday. How many loaves did the baker sell in all three days?

Answer: \(81\) loaves.

Explanation: \(15 + 26 + 40 = 41 + 40 = 81\).

3.

Luis read 33 pages in the morning, 25 pages in the afternoon, and 60 pages in the evening. How many pages did Luis read altogether?

Answer: \(118\) pages.

Explanation: \(33 + 25 + 60 = 58 + 60 = 118\).

Rounding Money

Counting Sequences

22.

Find the missing numbers: \(35, \text{___}, 29, 26, \text{___}, \text{___}, \ldots\)

Answer: \(35, 32, 29, 26, 23, 20, \ldots\)

Explanation: The pattern counts down by 3: \(35 - 3 = 32\), \(32 - 3 = 29\), \(29 - 3 = 26\), \(26 - 3 = 23\), \(23 - 3 = 20\).

23.

Find the missing numbers: \(50, \text{___}, \text{___}, 38, 34, \text{___}, \ldots\)

Answer: \(50, 46, 42, 38, 34, 30, \ldots\)

Explanation: The pattern counts down by 4: \(50 - 4 = 46\), \(46 - 4 = 42\), \(42 - 4 = 38\), \(38 - 4 = 34\), \(34 - 4 = 30\).

24.

Find the missing numbers: \(22, \text{___}, 30, 34, \text{___}, \text{___}, \ldots\)

Answer: \(22, 26, 30, 34, 38, 42, \ldots\)

Explanation: The pattern counts up by 4: \(22 + 4 = 26\), \(26 + 4 = 30\), \(30 + 4 = 34\), \(34 + 4 = 38\), \(38 + 4 = 42\).
