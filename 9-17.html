1. (FORMULATE)
The room was full of students when the bell rang. Then forty-seven students left the room. Twenty-two students remained. How many students were there when the bell rang? Use the subtraction formula to write an equation and solve the problem.

Equation: $s - 47 = 22$

Explanation: Using the separation formula $\text{Some} - \text{Went Away} = \text{Remaining}$ ($S - A = R$). To find the starting number of students ($s$), add $47$ to $22$: $s = 22 + 47 = 69$.

Answer: $69\text{ students}$

2. (FORMULATE)
On Friday, 56 fourth grade students wore black shoes to school. There are 73 fourth grade students in all. How many fourth grade students did not wear black shoes to school on Friday?

Equation: $73 - 56 = n$ (or $56 + n = 73$)

Explanation: Subtract the number of students wearing black shoes ($56$) from the total number of students ($73$): $73 - 56 = 17$.

Answer: $17\text{ students}$

3. (MULTIPLE CHOICE)
A nickel is worth $5\text{¢}$. Gilbert has an even number of nickels in his pocket. Which of the following could not be the value of his nickels?
  • A $45\text{¢}$
  • B $70\text{¢}$
  • C $20\text{¢}$
  • D $40\text{¢}$

Explanation: An even number multiplied by $5\text{¢}$ always ends in $0$ (a multiple of $10\text{¢}$). $45\text{¢} \div 5\text{¢} = 9$ nickels, which is an odd number. Therefore, $45\text{¢}$ cannot be the value of an even number of nickels.

Answer: A ($45\text{¢}$)

4.
Jillian's social studies class ends 15 minutes later than the time shown on the clock (clock shows 1:20). What time does Jillian's class end?

Explanation: The clock shows $1:20$. Adding $15\text{ minutes}$ to $1:20$ gives $1:20 + 15\text{ min} = 1:35$.

Answer: 1:35

5. (PREDICT)
What is the sixth number in this counting sequence? $$6, 12, 18, \dots$$

Explanation: The sequence counts by $6$s. The $6\text{th}$ number is found by multiplying $6 \times 6 = 36$.

Answer: $36$

6. (REPRESENT)
To what number is the arrow pointing on the number line between 400 and 500?

Explanation: The distance between $400$ and $500$ is divided into $10$ equal intervals of $10$ units each. The arrow points to the $7\text{th}$ tick mark past $400$, which is $400 + 70 = 470$.

Answer: $470$

29. (JUSTIFY)
Is $\$500$ a reasonable estimate for the difference $\$749 - \$259$? Explain why or why not.

Explanation: Rounding $\$749$ to $\$750$ and $\$259$ to $\$250$ gives an estimated difference of $\$750 - \$250 = \$500$. The exact difference is $\$749 - \$259 = \$490$, which is very close to $\$500$. Therefore, $\$500$ is a reasonable estimate.

Answer: Yes, because $\$749 \approx \$750$ and $\$259 \approx \$250$, so $\$750 - \$250 = \$500$ (actual difference is $\$490$).

  • Addition equation: $b + a = c$
  • Subtraction equation 1: $c - a = b$
  • Subtraction equation 2: $c - b = a$

Answer: $b + a = c$, $c - a = b$, $c - b = a$

Real-World Connection
Real-World Connection
Paolo had $\$12.70$. Then his mother paid him $\$3.25$ for mopping the floor. He bought a paperback book that costs $\$4.99$.
  • a. Use compatible numbers to estimate how much money Paolo has now.
  • b. Then find the actual amount of money Paolo has now.
  • c. Was your estimate reasonable? Explain why or why not.

Explanation:

  • a. Estimate: Use compatible numbers such as $\$12.75$ for $\$12.70$, $\$3.25$, and $\$5.00$ for $\$4.99$: $$\$12.75 + \$3.25 = \$16.00$$ $$\$16.00 - \$5.00 = \$11.00$$
  • b. Actual Amount: $$\$12.70 + \$3.25 = \$15.95$$ $$\$15.95 - \$4.99 = \$10.96$$
  • c. Reasonableness: Yes, the estimate of $\$11.00$ is extremely close to the actual amount of $\$10.96$ (differing by only $4\text{¢}$).

Answer: a. $\$11.00$ | b. $\$10.96$ | c. Yes, because $\$11.00$ is within $4\text{¢}$ of $\$10.96$.
