Cumulative Test 11B

1. Forty-two days is how many weeks?
Answer: 6 weeks

Explanation: There are 7 days in a single week. To find out how many weeks are in 42 days, you divide the total number of days by 7.
$42 \div 7 = 6$

2. List the factors of 15.
Answer: 1, 3, 5, 15

Explanation: Factors are the whole numbers you multiply together to get another number.
$1 \times 15 = 15$
$3 \times 5 = 15$
Therefore, the factors of 15 in order are 1, 3, 5, and 15.

3. Thirty books were put into three equal stacks. How many books were in each stack?
Answer: 10 books

Explanation: You are taking a total of 30 books and dividing them equally into 3 groups.
$30 \div 3 = 10$

4. Draw a circle and shade 25% of it.
Answer:

Explanation: 25% is equal to the fraction $\frac{1}{4}$. To show this, draw a circle, divide it into 4 equal quarters, and shade in exactly 1 of those quarters.

5. Thirty years equals how many decades?
Answer: 3 decades

Explanation: One decade is equal to 10 years. To find out how many decades are in 30 years, divide by 10.
$30 \div 10 = 3$

6. Find the eighth multiple of 4. Then subtract 12. What is the answer?
Answer: 20

Explanation:
Step 1: Find the 8th multiple of 4 by multiplying: $8 \times 4 = 32$.
Step 2: Subtract 12 from that result: $32 - 12 = 20$.

7. Compare: $\frac{1}{4}$ ◯ $50\%$
Answer: < (Less than)

Explanation: To compare them easily, convert the fraction to a percentage.
$\frac{1}{4} = 25\%$
Since $25\%$ is less than $50\%$, the correct symbol is $<$.
$\frac{1}{4} < 50\%$

8. Segment DE is 4 cm long. Segment DF is 8 cm long. How long is segment EF?
Answer: 4 cm

Explanation: Looking at the line segment, point E sits between D and F. Therefore, the length of $DE$ plus the length of $EF$ equals the total length of $DF$.
$DE + EF = DF$
$4\text{ cm} + EF = 8\text{ cm}$
$EF = 8 - 4 = 4\text{ cm}$

9. What is the perimeter of this rectangle?
Answer: 78 mm

Explanation: The perimeter is the total distance around the outside of the rectangle. A rectangle has two equal lengths and two equal widths.
Formula: $P = 2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width})$
$P = 2 \times (25 + 14)$
$P = 2 \times 39$
$P = 78\text{ mm}$

10. Round 5280 to the nearest thousand.
Answer: 5000

Explanation: Look at the number in the thousands place (5) and then look at the number directly to its right in the hundreds place (2). Because 2 is less than 5, we round down, keeping the 5 and turning the rest of the digits into zeros.

12. $$ \begin{array}{r} 73,129 \\ + \quad 8,293 \\ \hline \end{array} $$
Answer: 81,422

Explanation: Add column by column from right to left, carrying over when the sum is 10 or greater.
$$ \begin{array}{r} \small{1}\small{1}\small{1}\quad\quad \\ 73,129 \\ + \quad 8,293 \\ \hline 81,422 \end{array} $$

14. $$ \begin{array}{r} 79 \\ \times \quad 7 \\ \hline \end{array} $$
Answer: 553

Explanation: Multiply the ones column first: $9 \times 7 = 63$. Write down the 3 and carry the 6. Then multiply the tens column: $7 \times 7 = 49$. Add the carried 6: $49 + 6 = 55$.
$$ \begin{array}{r} \small{6} \\ 79 \\ \times \quad 7 \\ \hline 553 \end{array} $$

15. $3.47 + 2.6 + 12.4$
Answer: 18.47

Explanation: When adding decimals, always line up the decimal points. You can add zeros as placeholders to the right to make it easier to read.
$$ \begin{array}{r} \small{1}\quad\quad \\ 3.47 \\ 2.60 \\ + 12.40 \\ \hline 18.47 \end{array} $$

16. $\sqrt{25} + (20 \div 4)$
Answer: 10

Explanation: Follow the order of operations. Solve the square root and the parentheses first.
The square root of 25 is 5 (because $5 \times 5 = 25$).
The expression inside the parentheses is $20 \div 4 = 5$.
Add the two results together: $5 + 5 = 10$.

17. $49 \div 6$
Answer: 8 R 1 (or 8 $\frac{1}{6}$)

Explanation: Divide 49 by 6.
$6 \times 8 = 48$.
$49 - 48 = 1$, so there is a remainder of 1.
Written as a mixed number, it is $8 \frac{1}{6}$.

Explanation: Subtract column by column, regrouping where necessary.
$$ \begin{array}{r} \small{1}\small{9}\small{18} \\ \cancel{2}\cancel{0}\cancel{8} \\ - \quad 69 \\ \hline 139 \end{array} $$

Find the missing number:
19. $$ \begin{array}{r} n \\ - \quad 351 \\ \hline 600 \end{array} $$
Answer: $n = 951$

Explanation: To find the starting number ($n$) before 351 was subtracted from it, you do the opposite operation: addition. Add the difference (600) and the number subtracted (351) together.
$600 + 351 = 951$
So, $n = 951$.

Explanation: To find the missing addend ($z$), subtract the known addend (853) from the total sum (1172).
$$ \begin{array}{r} 1172 \\ - \quad 853 \\ \hline 319 \end{array} $$ So, $z = 319$.
