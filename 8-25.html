Question 1

Eight yards equals how many feet? (Hint: 1 yard = 3 feet)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Rule: 1 yard = 3 feet.

2. Multiply the number of yards by 3:

8 yards × 3 feet = 24 feet

Question 2

Ethan handed the cashier a $20 bill to buy a board game that cost $14.35. How much change did he receive back?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the cost of the board game from $20.00:

$20.00 − $14.35 = $5.65

Question 3

Olivia is 5 years older than Lucas. Lucas is three times as old as Noah. If Lucas is 18 years old, how old are Olivia and Noah?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Find Olivia's age: Olivia is 5 years older than Lucas → 18 + 5 = 23 years old.

2. Find Noah's age: Lucas is 3 times as old as Noah → 18 ÷ 3 = 6 years old.

Olivia is 23 and Noah is 6.

Question 5

One-fourth of the 28 students in Mrs. Davis's art class play the flute. How many students play the flute?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-fourth of a group, divide the total number of students by 4:

28 ÷ 4 = 7 students

Question 9

Estimate the difference between 93 and 38 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then, find the exact difference.

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Question 14

A movie started at 2:15 PM and finished at 4:45 PM. How long did the movie last?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Question 20

Ava has 3 one-dollar bills, 3 quarters, 2 dimes, 4 nickels, and 8 pennies. What is her total amount of money?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

• 3 Dollar Bills = $3.00

• 3 Quarters = 3 × 25¢ = 75¢ ($0.75)

• 2 Dimes = 2 × 10¢ = 20¢ ($0.20)

• 4 Nickels = 4 × 5¢ = 20¢ ($0.20)

• 8 Pennies = 8¢ ($0.08)

Total: $3.00 + $0.75 + $0.20 + $0.20 + $0.08 = $4.23

Question 26

(b) XXIV

(c) LXXV

Question 28

A bag of potatoes weighs 3 pounds and 8 ounces. What is its total weight in ounces? (Hint: 1 pound = 16 ounces)

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Convert 3 pounds to ounces: 3 × 16 = 48 ounces.

2. Add the 8 ounces: 48 + 8 = 56 ounces.

Question 29

A library had 420 books on display. In the morning, 85 books were checked out, and in the afternoon 50 new books were returned. How many books were on display by the evening?

Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Subtract checked out books: 420 − 85 = 335 books.

2. Add returned books: 335 + 50 = 385 books.
