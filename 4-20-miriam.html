Practice Math Test

1. Jason has six days to complete a 150-page reading assignment. If he wants to read the same number of pages each day, how many pages should Jason read daily?
Answer: 25 pages
Explanation: To find the daily page count, divide the total number of pages by the total number of days.
$$150 \div 6 = 25$$
2. When Jason has read three eighths of his assignment, what fraction will he still have left to read?
Answer: $\frac{5}{8}$
Explanation: The entire assignment represents 1 whole, which can be written as $\frac{8}{8}$. Subtract the part he has read from the whole.
$$\frac{8}{8} - \frac{3}{8} = \frac{5}{8}$$
Use the information given below to answer problems 3-5.
One hundred fifty students voted for their favorite fruit. Seventy voted for apples. Bananas received twenty fewer votes than apples. The remaining students voted for oranges.
3. How many students chose bananas as their favorite fruit?
Answer: 50 students
Explanation: The problem states bananas received 20 fewer votes than apples. Apples received 70 votes.
$$70 - 20 = 50$$
4. How many students chose oranges as their favorite fruit?
Answer: 30 students
Explanation: First, find the total votes for apples and bananas: $70 + 50 = 120$.
Subtract this from the total number of students (150) to find the orange votes:
$$150 - 120 = 30$$
5. Which fruit received the fewest votes?
Answer: Oranges
Explanation: Compare the votes for each fruit:
Apples: 70
Bananas: 50
Oranges: 30
Oranges have the lowest number of votes.
6. How many hours equal 4 days?
Answer: 96 hours
Explanation: There are 24 hours in a single day. Multiply the number of days by 24.
$$4 \times 24 = 96$$
7. In the word CONNECTICUT, what fraction of the letters are Cs?
Answer: $\frac{3}{11}$
Explanation: First, count the total number of letters in CONNECTICUT, which is 11.
Next, count the number of 'C's in the word, which is 3.
The fraction is the number of 'C's over the total letters: $\frac{3}{11}$.
8. A recipe calls for 5 kilograms of flour. Five kilograms is how many grams?
Answer: 5000 grams
Explanation: There are 1,000 grams in 1 kilogram. Multiply the number of kilograms by 1,000.
$$5 \times 1000 = 5000$$
9. Mia entered a 24-mile hiking trail but stopped to rest after completing one quarter of the distance. How many miles did Mia hike before resting?
Answer: 6 miles
Explanation: To find one quarter of 24, multiply 24 by $\frac{1}{4}$ (or divide 24 by 4).
$$24 \div 4 = 6$$
10. What is the perimeter of this triangle?
Answer: 60 mm
Explanation: The perimeter is the total distance around the outside of the shape. Add the lengths of all three sides together.
$$10 + 24 + 26 = 60$$
11. The length of $\overline{AB}$ is 40 mm. The length of $\overline{AC}$ is 90 mm. What is the length of $\overline{BC}$?
Answer: 50 mm
Explanation: Point B lies between A and C, meaning the length of AC is the sum of AB and BC ($AC = AB + BC$). To find BC, subtract the length of AB from AC.
$$90 - 40 = 50$$
12. $\$60 - (\$15.25 + 50\text{¢})$
Answer: $44.25
Explanation: First, convert $50\text{¢}$ to dollars ($0.50). Then, solve the operations inside the parentheses by adding.
$$\$15.25 + \$0.50 = \$15.75$$
Next, subtract this sum from $\$60.00$.
$$\$60.00 - \$15.75 = \$44.25$$

16. $415 \div 4$   (Shown as $4 \overline{) 415}$)
Answer: 103 R 3
Explanation: Perform long division. 4 goes into 4 one time ($4 \times 1 = 4$). Subtract 4 from 4 to get 0. Bring down the 1. 4 goes into 1 zero times ($4 \times 0 = 0$). Bring down the 5 to make 15. 4 goes into 15 three times ($4 \times 3 = 12$). Subtract 12 from 15 to leave a remainder of 3.
17. $923 \div 8$   (Shown as $8 \overline{) 923}$)
Answer: 115 R 3
Explanation: Perform long division. 8 goes into 9 one time ($8 \times 1 = 8$). Subtract 8 from 9 to get 1. Bring down the 2 to make 12. 8 goes into 12 one time ($8 \times 1 = 8$). Subtract 8 from 12 to get 4. Bring down the 3 to make 43. 8 goes into 43 five times ($8 \times 5 = 40$). Subtract 40 from 43. The remainder is 3.
18. $4p = 240$
Answer: $p = 60$
Explanation: Isolate the variable $p$ by performing the inverse operation. Divide both sides by 4.
$$p = 240 \div 4$$ $$p = 60$$
19. Find the missing number $n$:
  215
  180
+   $n$
  600
Answer: $n = 205$
Explanation: First, add the two known numbers together.
$$215 + 180 = 395$$
Next, subtract that sum from the total to find the missing addend.
$$600 - 395 = 205$$
20. Compare: $4 \times 3^3 \bigcirc \sqrt{144} \times \sqrt{49}$
Answer: > (Greater than)
Explanation: Evaluate both sides of the comparison.
Left side: Solve the exponent first ($3^3 = 3 \times 3 \times 3 = 27$), then multiply.
$$4 \times 27 = 108$$
Right side: Find the square roots ($\sqrt{144} = 12$ and $\sqrt{49} = 7$), then multiply.
$$12 \times 7 = 84$$
Since $108 \gt 84$, the correct symbol is $\gt$.
