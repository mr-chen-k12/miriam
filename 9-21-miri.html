Problem a
Only $28\%$ of the lights were on. What percent of the lights were off? Include the equation you used to solve the problem.
Answer: $72\%$ (Equation: $28\% + p = 100\%$ or $100\% - 28\% = p$)
The total set of lights represents $100\%$.
$$ ext{Equation: } 28\% + p = 100\%$$ $$p = 100\% - 28\% = 72\%$$

Answer: $ rac{4}{7}$ (Equation: $ rac{3}{7} + f = 1$ or $1 - rac{3}{7} = f$)

$$ ext{Equation: } rac{3}{7} + f = 1$$ $$f = rac{7}{7} - rac{3}{7} = rac{4}{7}$$

Problem d
Rolling a standard number cube once, what is the probability of rolling a number less than $3$?
Answer: $ rac{1}{3}$
A standard number cube has $6$ faces: $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$.
Outcomes less than $3$ are $\{1, 2\}$ ($2$ favorable outcomes). $$P( ext{less than } 3) = rac{2}{6} = rac{1}{3}$$

Problem i
A spinner is divided into one half (section C) and two fourths (sections A and B).
What is the probability of this spinner not stopping on section $A$?
Answer: $ rac{3}{4}$
The probability of stopping on $A$ is $ rac{1}{4}$. The probability of NOT stopping on $A$ is: $$P( ext{not } A) = 1 - P(A) = 1 - rac{1}{4} = rac{3}{4}$$

Problem 31 (Terminating Decimal Division)
Find the quotient as a decimal: $$14.7 \div 6$$
Answer: $2.45$
Divide $14.7$ by $6$ using long division:
• $14 \div 6 = 2$ with a remainder of $2$. Place decimal point.
• Bring down $7 \implies 27 \div 6 = 4$ with a remainder of $3$.
• Add a trailing zero $\implies 30 \div 6 = 5$ with no remainder.
$$14.7 \div 6 = 2.45$$ Since the remainder becomes zero, $2.45$ is a terminating decimal.
