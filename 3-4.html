Lesson 61: Problem Set Solutions

Problem 1
The diameter of Filomena's bicycle tire is 24 inches. What is the radius of the tire?

Answer: 12 inches

Explanation: The radius of a circle is exactly half of its diameter. Therefore, we divide the diameter by 2:

$$ \text{Radius} = \frac{\text{Diameter}}{2} = \frac{24}{2} = 12 \text{ inches} $$
Problem 2
Five sparrows could crowd into each birdhouse. If there were thirty-five birdhouses, how many sparrows could crowd in?

Answer: 175 sparrows

Explanation: Multiply the number of sparrows per birdhouse by the total number of birdhouses.

$$ 5 \times 35 = 175 $$
Problem 3
(a) Two nickels are what fraction of a dollar?
(b) Two nickels are what decimal part of a dollar?

Answer: (a) $\frac{1}{10}$ (b) $0.10$

Explanation: A dollar is worth 100 cents. Two nickels are worth 10 cents ($2 \times 5 = 10$).

  • (a) Fraction: $\frac{10}{100}$, which simplifies to $\frac{1}{10}$.
  • (b) Decimal: $10 \div 100 = 0.10$.
Problem 4
The Gilbreth family drank 39 quarts of milk in 3 days. That averages to how many quarts of milk each day?

Answer: 13 quarts each day

Explanation: Divide the total quarts of milk by the number of days.

$$ \frac{39}{3} = 13 $$
Problem 5
Carl weighed 88 pounds. He put on his clothes, which weighed 2 pounds, and his shoes, which weighed 1 pound each. Finally he put on a jacket that weighed 3 pounds and stepped on the scale. How much did the scale show that he weighed?

Answer: 95 pounds

Explanation: Add all the weights together. Remember he has two shoes, so $1 \times 2 = 2$ pounds for shoes.

$$ 88 \text{ (Carl)} + 2 \text{ (clothes)} + 2 \text{ (shoes)} + 3 \text{ (jacket)} = 95 $$
Problem 6
What fraction of this rectangle is not shaded? (A 2x5 grid is shown)

Answer: $\frac{10}{10}$ or $1$

Note: In the provided image, none of the squares appear to be shaded. There are 10 squares in total.

Explanation: If 0 out of 10 squares are shaded, then all 10 squares are unshaded.

$$ \frac{10 \text{ unshaded}}{10 \text{ total}} = 1 $$
Problem 7
Which of these numbers is a multiple of 10?
A. 2    B. 5    C. 25    D. 50

Answer: D. 50

Explanation: A multiple of 10 must end in a zero. $10 \times 5 = 50$.

Problem 8
The pumpkin pie was sliced into 6 equal pieces. After 1 piece was taken, what fraction of the pie was left?

Answer: $\frac{5}{6}$

Explanation: The whole pie is $\frac{6}{6}$. Subtract the $\frac{1}{6}$ that was taken.

$$ \frac{6}{6} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{6} $$
Problem 9
Compare these fractions. Draw and shade two congruent circles to show the comparison. $\frac{2}{3} \bigcirc \frac{3}{4}$

Answer: $\frac{2}{3} < \frac{3}{4}$

Explanation: To compare exactly, find a common denominator (12). $\frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{12}$ and $\frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{12}$. Since 8 < 9, $\frac{2}{3} < \frac{3}{4}$.

Problem 10
Estimate the sum of 5070 and 3840 by rounding each number to the nearest thousand before adding.

Answer: 9000

Explanation:

  • 5070 rounds to 5000 (since 0 in the hundreds place is less than 5).
  • 3840 rounds to 4000 (since 8 in the hundreds place is 5 or greater).
$$ 5000 + 4000 = 9000 $$
Problem 11
If 60% of the answers were true, then were there more true answers or more false answers?

Answer: More true answers

Explanation: The total must be 100%. If 60% are true, then $100\% - 60\% = 40\%$ are false. Since 60% > 40%, there were more true answers.

Problem 12
A rectangle is shown with sides 8 cm and 4 cm.
(a) What is the perimeter of this rectangle?
(b) What is the area of this rectangle?

Answer: (a) 24 cm (b) 32 cm²

Explanation:

  • (a) Perimeter = $2 \times (\text{length} + \text{width}) = 2 \times (8 + 4) = 2 \times 12 = 24 \text{ cm}$.
  • (b) Area = $\text{length} \times \text{width} = 8 \times 4 = 32 \text{ cm}^2$.

Problem 15
$1000 - (110 \times 9)$

Answer: 10

Explanation: Solve inside the parentheses first (Order of Operations).

$$ 110 \times 9 = 990 $$
$$ 1000 - 990 = 10 $$

Problem 20
$39 \div 5$

Answer: 7 R 4 (or 7.8, or $7\frac{4}{5}$)

Explanation: 5 goes into 39 seven times ($5 \times 7 = 35$). The remainder is $39 - 35 = 4$.

Problem 21
$39 \div 7$

Answer: 5 R 4 (or $5\frac{4}{7}$)

Explanation: 7 goes into 39 five times ($7 \times 5 = 35$). The remainder is $39 - 35 = 4$.

Problem 22
$39 \div 4$

Answer: 9 R 3 (or 9.75, or $9\frac{3}{4}$)

Explanation: 4 goes into 39 nine times ($4 \times 9 = 36$). The remainder is $39 - 36 = 3$.

Problem 23
$16 \div 3$

Answer: 5 R 1 (or $5\frac{1}{3}$)

Explanation: 3 goes into 16 five times ($3 \times 5 = 15$). The remainder is $16 - 15 = 1$.

Problem 24
$26 \div 6$

Answer: 4 R 2 (or $4\frac{1}{3}$)

Explanation: 6 goes into 26 four times ($6 \times 4 = 24$). The remainder is $26 - 24 = 2$.

Problem 25
$36 \div \sqrt{36}$

Answer: 6

Explanation: First, evaluate the square root: $\sqrt{36} = 6$. Then divide:

$$ 36 \div 6 = 6 $$
Problem 26
Point A represents what number on this number line? (Point A is 3 tick marks to the left of 0, with -5 being 5 tick marks to the left).

Answer: -3

Explanation: There are 5 intervals between 0 and -5, so each tick mark represents 1 unit. Moving 3 units to the left of 0 gives us -3.

Problem 27
Compare: $-5 \bigcirc -3$

Answer: $-5 < -3$

Explanation: On a number line, numbers further to the left are smaller. Because -5 is to the left of -3, -5 is less than -3.

Problem 28
A 10x10 square grid is shown.
(a) What fraction of the large square is not shaded?
(b) What percent of the large square is not shaded?
(c) The unshaded part of the large square represents what decimal number?

Answer: (a) $\frac{100}{100}$ (or 1) (b) 100% (c) 1.0

Note: In the provided image, none of the squares in the 100-square grid appear to be shaded. The answers below assume an entirely unshaded grid.

Explanation: Since all 100 small squares out of 100 are unshaded:

  • (a) Fraction: $\frac{100}{100} = 1$
  • (b) Percent: 100 out of 100 is 100%
  • (c) Decimal: $100 \div 100 = 1.0$
