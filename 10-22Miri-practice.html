1.(35)
a. Sofia had two quarters, a dime, and four pennies. Write this amount with a dollar sign and decimal point.
b. Ben had a quarter, three dimes, and two pennies. Write this amount with a dollar sign and decimal point.
c. Ava had three dimes, a nickel, and six pennies. Write this amount with a dollar sign and decimal point.
Answers
a. \(\$0.64\)
b. \(\$0.57\)
c. \(\$0.41\)
Solutions
a. Two quarters = 50¢, a dime = 10¢, four pennies = 4¢: \(50 + 10 + 4 = 64\) cents.
b. A quarter = 25¢, three dimes = 30¢, two pennies = 2¢: \(25 + 30 + 2 = 57\) cents.
c. Three dimes = 30¢, a nickel = 5¢, six pennies = 6¢: \(30 + 5 + 6 = 41\) cents.
2.(11)
a. Marion is 145 miles west of Cleveland. Lima is 60 miles west of Cleveland. How far is it from Lima to Marion?
b. Reno is 218 miles west of Salt Lake City. Elko is 130 miles west of Salt Lake City. How far is it from Elko to Reno?
c. Yuma is 184 miles west of Phoenix. Gila Bend is 69 miles west of Phoenix. How far is it from Gila Bend to Yuma?
Answers
a. \(85\text{ miles}\)
b. \(88\text{ miles}\)
c. \(115\text{ miles}\)
Solutions
a. Both cities are west of Cleveland, Marion farther: \(145 - 60 = 85\) miles.
b. Both cities are west of Salt Lake City, Reno farther: \(218 - 130 = 88\) miles.
c. Both cities are west of Phoenix, Yuma farther: \(184 - 69 = 115\) miles.

9.(23)
a. Which letter below has a right angle?
P R T U
b. Which letter below has a right angle?
C E G S
c. Which letter below has a right angle?
F J O V
Answers
a. \(\text{T}\)
b. \(\text{E}\)
c. \(\text{F}\)
Solutions
a. The top bar of the T meets its stem at a square corner — a right angle.
b. The E is built from straight lines meeting at right angles; C, G, and S are curves.
c. The F's strokes meet at right angles; J and O are curved, and V has only an acute angle.

a. \(48\text{ inches}\)

c. \(18\text{ feet}\)
Solutions
a. Diameter \(= 2 \times 2 = 4\) feet; \(4 \times 12 = 48\) inches.
