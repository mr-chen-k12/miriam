Problem 1
Liquid Measurement Equivalences

Complete the following liquid measurement conversion chain using standard rules:
$$\text{2 gallons} = \text{\_\_\_\_\_ quarts} = \text{\_\_\_\_\_ pints} = \text{\_\_\_\_\_ cups}$$

Solution:
• \(2 \text{ gallons} \times 4 = \mathbf{8} \text{ quarts}\)
• \(8 \text{ quarts} \times 2 = \mathbf{16} \text{ pints}\)
• \(16 \text{ pints} \times 2 = \mathbf{32} \text{ cups}\)
Final answer chain: \(2 \text{ gal} = 8 \text{ qt} = 16 \text{ pt} = 32 \text{ c}\).
Problem 2
Algebraic Word Problem: Scaffolded Building Blocks

Scenario: Liam is $y$ years old. His older sister, Maya, is $4$ years older than twice Liam's age. The sum of their ages is $31$ years. Let's break down how to represent this scenario mathematically.

Part A: Which algebraic expression properly represents Maya's age in terms of Liam's age $y$?
  • A) \(4y + 2\)
  • B) \(2y + 4\)
  • C) \(2(y + 4)\)
  • D) \(y + 4\)
Part B: Which expression correctly represents the sum of Liam's age and Maya's age combined before simplifying?
  • A) \(y + (2y + 4)\)
  • B) \(y + (4y + 2)\)
  • C) \(2y + 4\)
  • D) \(y + 31\)
Part C: Write and solve the complete equation to find Liam's current age (\(y\)), and then determine Maya's age.
Solution & Grading Key:
• Part A: The correct choice is B) \(2y + 4\) ("4 years older than [\(+4\)] twice Liam's age [\(2y\)]").
• Part B: The correct choice is A) \(y + (2y + 4)\) (Liam's age \(y\) plus Maya's age \(2y + 4\)).
• Part C Calculation:
$$\text{Sum Equation: } y + 2y + 4 = 31$$
$$3y + 4 = 31$$
$$3y = 27 \implies y = 9$$
• Final Ages: Liam is 9 years old, and Maya is \(2(9) + 4 = \mathbf{22}\) years old.
Problem 3
Percent Discounts and Final Cost

A video game console normally costs $250. This week, the store is running a promotion offering a $15\%$ discount off its original price. What is the sales price of the console before tax?

Solution:
• Calculate the discount amount: \(250 \times 0.15 = \$37.50\).
• Subtract the discount from the baseline total: \(250 - 37.50 = \$212.50\).
Final Sale Price: $212.50.
