Question 1

Three gallons equals how many quarts? (Hint: 1 gallon = 4 quarts)

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Know the rule: 1 gallon = 4 quarts.

2. Multiply the number of gallons by 4:

3 gallons × 4 quarts = 12 quarts

Question 3

Mia is 3 years younger than Jordan. Jordan is 4 times as old as Leo. If Mia is 13 years old, how old is Leo?

(a) Choose the expression that can help you find Jordan's age.

(b) After finding Jordan's age, choose the expression that can help you find Leo's age.

(c) How old is Leo? Show or explain your thinking.

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

(a) Mia is 3 years younger than Jordan, so Jordan is 3 years older than Mia. The helpful expression is 13 + 3.

13 + 3 = 16, so Jordan is 16 years old.

(b) Jordan is 4 times as old as Leo, so divide Jordan's age by 4. The helpful expression is 16 ÷ 4.

(c) 16 ÷ 4 = 4, so Leo is 4 years old.

Question 5

One-fourth of the 32 students in Ms. Rivera's class voted to play dodgeball at recess. How many students voted to play dodgeball?

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

To find one-fourth of a group, divide the total number of students by 4:

32 ÷ 4 = 8 students

Question 6

Looking back at Question 5, how many students did not vote to play dodgeball?

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

Subtract the students who voted for dodgeball from the total class size:

32 − 8 = 24 students

Question 9

Estimate the sum of 68 and 47 by rounding each number to the nearest ten. Then, find the exact sum.

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Estimate: Round 68 up to 70. Round 47 up to 50. Add them up: 70 + 50 = 120.

2. Exact Sum: Add the original numbers together: 68 + 47 = 115.

Question 10

Noah tracked the number of laps he ran over the last few gym classes: 5, 7, 5, 8, 6.

(a) Which number of laps shows up the most often (the mode)?

(b) How many laps did he run in all?

✏️ Show your work or explain your thinking here.
Teacher Answer Key

Answers:

(a) Mode: 5 (It appears twice, which is more than any other number).

(b) Total: 5 + 7 + 5 + 8 + 6 = 31 laps.
