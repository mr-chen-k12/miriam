Problem 1
Write and solve equations for problems 1 and 2.
There were two hundred fourteen parrots, seven hundred fifty-two crows, and two thousand forty-two blue jays. How many birds were there in all?
Answer:
3,008 birds
Equation: $214 + 752 + 2042 = 3008$
Problem 2
K'Shella used one bag of soil to pot 8 plants. How many bags of soil would she need to pot 2 dozen plants?
Answer:
3 bags
1 dozen = 12, so 2 dozen = 24 plants. Equation: $24 \div 8 = 3$ bags.

Problem 4
Fifty percent of an hour is how many minutes?
Answer:
30 minutes
An hour has 60 minutes. 50% is half. $60 \times 0.50 = 30$.
Problem 5
Mount St. Helens is a volcano in Washington State. After erupting in May 1980, the peak of the volcano was 8363 feet about sea level. During the eruption, the volcano lost 1314 feet of its height. What is a reasonable estimate of the height of the volcano before its eruption? Explain your thinking.
Answer:
Approx. 9,700 feet
Before the eruption, the height was the current peak plus what it lost. Estimate by rounding: $8400 + 1300 = 9700$ (or $8000 + 1000 = 9000$ depending on the rounding rules taught, though 9700 is closer to the true sum of 9677).
Problem 6
Which of these numbers is not a multiple of 2?
A 23   B 24   C 32   D 46
Answer:
A. 23
Multiples of 2 are even numbers. 23 is an odd number.
Problem 7
Write the time "a quarter to seven in the morning" in digital form.
Answer:
6:45 a.m.
"A quarter to" means 15 minutes before the hour. 15 minutes before 7:00 is 6:45.
Problem 8
Solve for n: $3n = 3 \times 5$
Answer:
$n = 5$
Simplify the right side: $3 \times 5 = 15$. So, $3n = 15$. Divide by 3 to find $n = 5$. Notice also that the 3s balance out on both sides.
Problem 9
The product of 6 and 7 is how much greater than the sum of 6 and 7?
Answer:
29
Product: $6 \times 7 = 42$. Sum: $6 + 7 = 13$. Difference: $42 - 13 = 29$.
Problem 10
What is the length of segment BC?
Answer:
3 cm
Point B is at 5 cm and Point C is at 8 cm. $8 - 5 = 3$.
Problem 11
Compare: $(32 \div 8) \div 2 \bigcirc 32 \div (8 \div 2)$
Answer:
$<$ (Less than)
Left side: $(32 \div 8) \div 2 = 4 \div 2 = 2$. Right side: $32 \div (8 \div 2) = 32 \div 4 = 8$. Since $2 < 8$, the correct symbol is $<$.

Problem 21
a. A nickel is what fraction of a dollar?
b. A nickel is what percent of a dollar?
Answer:
a. $\frac{1}{20}$ (or $\frac{5}{100}$)
b. $5\%$
A nickel is 5 cents out of 100 cents. As a fraction, this simplifies to $\frac{1}{20}$. As a percentage, it is $5\%$.
Problem 22
Perfect squares have an odd number of factors. The numbers 9 and 25 are perfect squares. The three factors of 9 are 1, 3, and 9. What are the three factors of 25?
Answer:
1, 5, and 25
The numbers that multiply to give 25 are $1 \times 25$ and $5 \times 5$.
Problem 23
Compare: $5\% \bigcirc \frac{1}{2}$
Answer:
$<$ (Less than)
$5\%$ is equivalent to $0.05$. $\frac{1}{2}$ is equivalent to $0.50$ (or $50\%$). Therefore, $5\% < \frac{1}{2}$.
Problem 24
Refer to figure ABCD to answer parts a and b.
a. What type of angle are angles A and C?
b. What type of angle are angles B and D?
Answer:
a. Obtuse
b. Acute
Angles A and C are wider than 90 degrees (obtuse). Angles B and D are narrower than 90 degrees (acute).
Problem 25
The rectangular room is 5 yards long and 4 yards wide.

a. How many yards of molding are needed to go around the room?
b. How many square yards of carpet are needed to cover the floor?
Answer:
a. 18 yards
b. 20 square yards
a. Molding goes around the perimeter: $P = 2(5 + 4) = 18$ yards.
b. Carpet covers the area: $A = 5 \times 4 = 20$ square yards.
Problem 26
If $n + 10 = 25$, then which of these equations is not true?
A. $n + 11 = 26$
B. $n + 12 = 27$
C. $n - 5 = 20$
D. $n + 9 = 24$
Answer:
C. $n - 5 = 20$
First, find $n$: $n = 25 - 10 = 15$. If $n = 15$, then $15 - 5 = 10$, not 20. Therefore, equation C is false.
Problem 27
(a) Compare: $8 \div (4 \div 2) \bigcirc (8 \div 4) \div 2$
(b) Look at your answer to part (a). Does the associative property apply to division?
Answer:
a. $>$ (Greater than)
b. No
a. Left side: $8 \div (4 \div 2) = 8 \div 2 = 4$. Right side: $(8 \div 4) \div 2 = 2 \div 2 = 1$. Since $4 > 1$, use $>$.
b. Because the parenthesis grouping changed the result, the associative property does NOT apply to division.
Problem 28
a. Nineteen pennies are what fraction of a dollar?
b. Nineteen pennies are what percent of a dollar?
c. Write the value of nineteen pennies as a decimal part of a dollar.
Answer:
a. $\frac{19}{100}$
b. $19\%$
c. $\$0.19$
A dollar equals 100 pennies, so 19 pennies is $\frac{19}{100}$, which translates directly to $19\%$ and $0.19$.
Problem 29
At the restaurant Jackson ordered a meal for $\$7.95$, a glass of milk for $\$1.75$, and a dessert for $\$3.95$. Estimate Jackson's restaurant bill.
Answer:
$\$14.00$
Round each item to the nearest dollar: $\$8.00 + \$2.00 + \$4.00 = \$14.00$.
Problem 30
a. Name a segment that is parallel to EF.
b. Name a segment that is perpendicular to BF.
Answer:
a. AB, DC, or HG
b. EF, FG, AB, or BC
a. Parallel segments run in the same direction and never intersect. AB, DC, and HG are all parallel to EF.
b. Perpendicular segments meet at a 90-degree angle. Segments forming a corner with BF (like EF, FG, AB, or BC) are perpendicular to it.
