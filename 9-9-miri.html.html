*1. Subtraction Word Problem

Ms. Rivera had $165$ craft sticks for an art project. She handed out $97$ craft sticks to her 4th grade students. How many craft sticks does she have left?

Answer: 68 craft sticks
Explanation: Subtract the number of craft sticks handed out from the initial total:
$$165 - 97 = 68\text{ craft sticks}$$
2. Time & Division

One full hour has $60$ minutes. How many minutes are in one-quarter ($\frac{1}{4}$) of an hour?

Answer: 15 minutes
Explanation: Divide the total number of minutes in an hour by $4$:
$$60 \div 4 = 15\text{ minutes}$$

*6. Geometry - Circles

The radius of a circular backyard trampoline is $7$ feet. What is the diameter of the trampoline?

Answer: 14 feet
Explanation: The diameter ($d$) is twice the length of the radius ($r$):
$$d = 2 \times r = 2 \times 7\text{ ft} = 14\text{ feet}$$
*7. Time Elapsed & Visual Clock

An afternoon music practice session begins at the time shown on the clock below. The practice session ends at 4:05 p.m. How long is the music practice session?

Answer: 50 minutes
Explanation: The clock displays 3:15 p.m.
From 3:15 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. is $45$ minutes.
From 4:00 p.m. to 4:05 p.m. is $5$ minutes.
$$45\text{ min} + 5\text{ min} = 50\text{ minutes}$$

*10. Perimeter

Calculate the perimeter of a rectangle that is $8\text{ cm}$ long and $5\text{ cm}$ wide. What is its perimeter?

Answer: 26 cm
Explanation: A rectangle has two lengths of $8\text{ cm}$ and two widths of $5\text{ cm}$.
$$\text{Perimeter} = 8 + 5 + 8 + 5 = 2 \times (8 + 5) = 26\text{ cm}$$

*24. Multiple Choice - Metric Conversion

Which of these measurements is NOT equivalent to $1\text{ meter}$?

A $100\text{ cm}$
B $1,000\text{ mm}$
C $0.001\text{ km}$
D $10\text{ cm}$
Answer: D ($10\text{ cm}$)
Explanation: $1\text{ meter} = 100\text{ centimeters} = 1,000\text{ millimeters} = 0.001\text{ kilometers}$. $10\text{ cm}$ is equal to only $0.1\text{ m}$ (one-tenth of a meter).
*25. Geometry Concepts

How is an acute angle different from an obtuse angle?

Answer: Geometric Difference
Explanation: An acute angle measures greater than $0^\circ$ but less than $90^\circ$ (smaller than a right angle). An obtuse angle measures greater than $90^\circ$ but less than $180^\circ$ (larger than a right angle).
26. Multi-Step Trail Miles Equation

The Green Valley Trail and Silver Creek Trail have a combined length of $820$ miles. The Green Valley Trail is $470$ miles long. Write and solve an equation to find the length of the Silver Creek Trail.

Answer: Equation: $470 + s = 820$; $s = 350$ miles
Explanation: Let $s$ represent the length of the Silver Creek Trail.
Equation: $$470 + s = 820$$ Solve for $s$: $$s = 820 - 470 = 350\text{ miles}$$
27. Estimation Word Problem

At a book fair, a novel costs $\$4.85$ and a bookmark costs $\$1.95$. What is a reasonable estimate of the total cost? Explain why your estimate is reasonable.

Answer: About $6.80 to $7.00
Explanation: Rounding $\$4.85$ to the nearest dollar gives $\$5.00$ (or $\$4.90$ to nearest 10 cents), and $\$1.95$ rounds to $\$2.00$.
$$\$5.00 + \$2.00 = \$7.00 \quad \text{or} \quad \$4.90 + \$1.95 = \$6.85 \approx \$6.80\text{--}\$7.00$$ This estimate is reasonable because each price was rounded to a convenient nearby amount for quick mental addition.
28. Integers & Temperature Differences

In Chicago, the high temperature on a cold morning was $18^\circ\text{F}$ and the low temperature was $-4^\circ\text{F}$. How many degrees warmer was the high temperature than the low temperature?

Answer: $22^\circ\text{F}$ warmer
Explanation: Subtract the low temperature from the high temperature:
$$18 - (-4) = 18 + 4 = 22^\circ\text{F}$$ Alternatively, from $-4^\circ\text{F}$ up to $0^\circ\text{F}$ is $4$ degrees, and from $0^\circ\text{F}$ up to $18^\circ\text{F}$ is $18$ degrees. $4 + 18 = 22^\circ\text{F}$.

Real-World Connection

Three elementary classrooms held a book drive. Class 1 collected $155$ books, Class 2 collected $135$ books, and Class 3 collected $160$ books. The school donated $280$ books to a local community library. Are there more books left over or were more books donated? How do you know?

Answer: More books were donated ($280$ donated vs $170$ remaining).

Step 1: Calculate the total number of books collected:
$$155 + 135 + 160 = 450\text{ total books}$$

Step 2: Subtract the donated books ($280$) to find how many books remain:
$$450 - 280 = 170\text{ books remaining}$$

Step 3: Compare the donated books ($280$) with the remaining books ($170$):
$$280\text{ (donated)} > 170\text{ (remaining)}$$

Since $280 > 170$, more books were donated than left over.
