* Represent The 1-gallon container of milk held 3.78 L of milk. Use words to write 3.78 L.
Answer
\(\text{three and seventy-eight hundredths liters}\)
Solution
3.78 = 3 ones + 78 hundredths (the 8 is in the hundredths place), so: “three and seventy-eight hundredths” liters.
2.(33)
Represent Silviano compared two numbers. The first number was forty-two thousand, three hundred seventy-six. The second number was forty-two thousand, eleven. Use digits and a comparison symbol to show the comparison.
Answer
\(42{,}376 > 42{,}011\)
Solution
First number: 42,376. Second number: 42,011. The thousands and hundreds digits match, but 7 tens > 1 ten... compare place by place: both are 42 thousand; 376 > 011, so \(42{,}376 > 42{,}011\).
3.(41, 43)
* Represent The ticket cost \$3.25. Mr. Chen paid for the ticket with a \$5 bill. How much change did he receive? Is your answer reasonable? Why or why not?
Answer
\(\$1.75;\ \text{yes, reasonable}\)
Solution
\(\$5.00 - \$3.25 = \$1.75\). It is reasonable: the change must be less than \$5, and since \$3.25 is a little more than \$3, the change should be a little less than \$2 — \$1.75 fits.
4.(Inv. 3, 31)
Nine squared is how much more than the square root of nine?
Answer
\(78\)
Solution
\(9^2 = 81\) and \(\sqrt{9} = 3\). Difference: \(81 - 3 = 78\).

6.(40)
* Connect Eight fluid ounces of water is one cup of water. How many fluid ounces of water is a pint of water?
Answer
\(16\text{ fluid ounces}\)
Solution
A pint is 2 cups, and each cup is 8 fluid ounces: \(2 \times 8 = 16\) fluid ounces.
7.(35)
How many circles are shaded?
Answer
\(3\dfrac{1}{3}\)
Solution
Three circles are shaded completely, and the fourth circle has 1 of its 3 equal parts shaded \(= \frac{1}{3}\). Total: \(3 + \frac{1}{3} = 3\frac{1}{3}\) circles.

26.(Inv. 3, 41)
* Multiple Choice If the area of this rectangle is 6 sq. cm, then the length of the rectangle is which of the following?
A 3 cm B 4 cm
C 10 cm D 12 cm
Answer
\(\text{A — }3\text{ cm}\)
Solution
Area = length × width: \(6 = L \times 2\), so \(L = 6 \div 2 = 3\) cm.

28.(38)
Explain How can you pay \$1.23 using the fewest number of bills and coins?
Answer
\(\text{1 one-dollar bill, 2 dimes, 3 pennies (6 bills/coins)}\)
Solution
Use the largest denominations first: \$1.23 = \$1 bill + 23¢. For 23¢, the fewest coins are 2 dimes (20¢) + 3 pennies (3¢) — a quarter is too much. Total: 1 bill + 5 coins.

30.(25, 41)
* The Sutlej River in Asia is 900 miles long. The Po River in Europe is 405 miles long. How many miles longer is the Sutlej River?
Answer
\(495\text{ miles}\)
Solution
Equation: \(405 + d = 900\), so \(d = 900 - 405 = 495\) miles.

Real-World Connection The school choir is ordering new choir shirts and blouses. There are 15 girls and 11 boys in the choir. The girls’ blouses cost \$9 each. The boys’ shirts cost \$8 each. What will be the total cost for the choir shirts and blouses?
Answer
\(\$223\)
Solution
Girls’ blouses: \(15 \times \$9 = \$135\). Boys’ shirts: \(11 \times \$8 = \$88\). Total: \(\$135 + \$88 = \$223\).
