Lesson 91: Decimal Place Value - Problem Solutions

1. Three quarters, four dimes, two nickels, and seven pennies is how much money?

Answer: $1.32

Explanation: Calculate the value of each coin type and sum them up:

  • 3 quarters = \( 3 \times \$0.25 = \$0.75 \)
  • 4 dimes = \( 4 \times \$0.10 = \$0.40 \)
  • 2 nickels = \( 2 \times \$0.05 = \$0.10 \)
  • 7 pennies = \( 7 \times \$0.01 = \$0.07 \)

Total = \( \$0.75 + \$0.40 + \$0.10 + \$0.07 = \$1.32 \)

(Note: The student wrote $1.36 on the worksheet, which is incorrect due to an addition error.)

2. Carmen separated the 37 math books as equally as possible into 4 stacks.
(a) How many stacks had exactly 9 books?
(b) How many stacks had 10 books?

Answer: (a) 3 stacks, (b) 1 stack

Explanation: Divide the total number of books by the number of stacks: \( 37 \div 4 = 9 \) with a remainder of \( 1 \).

This means if we put 9 books in each of the 4 stacks, we have used 36 books. There is 1 leftover book. To distribute as equally as possible, we place that 1 extra book on one of the stacks. Therefore, 1 stack gets 10 books, and the remaining 3 stacks keep 9 books.

3. Lily paid $1 for a folder and received 52¢ back in change. If the tax was 3¢, how much did the folder cost without tax?

Answer: $0.45

Explanation: First, find the total cost of the folder including tax by subtracting the change from the amount paid:

\( \$1.00 - \$0.52 = \$0.48 \) (total cost with tax)

Next, subtract the tax amount to find the base cost:

\( \$0.48 - \$0.03 = \$0.45 \)

4. Frank wrote each of his 12 spelling words five times. In all, how many words did he write?

Answer: 60 words

Explanation: Multiply the number of words by the number of times each was written:

\( 12 \text{ words} \times 5 \text{ times} = 60 \text{ words in total} \).

5. Round 5456 to the nearest thousand. Round 2872 to the nearest thousand. Find the sum of the two rounded numbers.

Answer: 8000

Explanation:

  • \( 5456 \) rounded to the nearest thousand is \( 5000 \) (because the hundreds digit '4' is less than 5, we round down).
  • \( 2872 \) rounded to the nearest thousand is \( 3000 \) (because the hundreds digit '8' is 5 or greater, we round up).

Sum: \( 5000 + 3000 = 8000 \).

6. What is the tally for 10?

Answer: Two groups of five tally marks.

Explanation: In tally marks, each group of five is represented by four vertical lines and one diagonal line crossing them out. For 10, you draw two such groups.

7. Name the shaded part of this square (a) as a fraction. (b) as a decimal number.

Answer: (a) \( \frac{6}{10} \) or \( \frac{3}{5} \)    (b) 0.6

Explanation: The square is divided into 10 equal horizontal strips. Out of the 10 strips, 6 are shaded.

As a fraction, this is 6 out of 10, or \( \frac{6}{10} \). As a decimal, six tenths is written as 0.6.

8. One sixth of the 48 crayons are broken. How many crayons are broken? Draw a picture to illustrate the problem.

Answer: 8 crayons

Explanation: To find one sixth of a number, divide it by 6. \( 48 \div 6 = 8 \).

We can draw 6 boxes to represent the sixths, each containing 8 crayons, and shade one box to show the broken ones.

9. Segment AB is 32 mm long. Segment BC is 26 mm long. Segment AD is 91 mm long. How many millimeters long is segment CD?

Answer: 33 mm

Explanation: The total length of segment AD is the sum of its parts: \( AD = AB + BC + CD \).

We know: \( 91 = 32 + 26 + CD \)

\( 91 = 58 + CD \)

\( CD = 91 - 58 = 33 \text{ mm} \).

10. Which digit in 6.125 is in the hundredths place?

Answer: 2

Explanation: In the decimal 6.125:

  • The 1 is in the tenths place.
  • The 2 is in the hundredths place.
  • The 5 is in the thousandths place.
11. If a pint of water weighs about one pound, then about how many pounds does a quart of water weigh?

Answer: 2 pounds

Explanation: Since 1 quart equals 2 pints, and each pint weighs about 1 pound, a quart weighs about \( 2 \times 1 = 2 \text{ pounds} \).

13. \( 5^2 + \sqrt{25} + N = 30 \)

Answer: N = 0

Explanation: Evaluate the exponents and roots first:

\( 5^2 = 25 \)

\( \sqrt{25} = 5 \)

Substitute these values back into the equation:

\( 25 + 5 + N = 30 \)

\( 30 + N = 30 \)

\( N = 0 \)

(Note: The student incorrectly wrote N = 1.)

22. \( \$50.40 \div 7 \)

Answer: $7.20

Explanation: Divide keeping the decimal in place:

  • \( 50 \div 7 = 7 \) (remainder 1)
  • Bring down 4 past the decimal, making 14. \( 14 \div 7 = 2 \) (remainder 0)
  • Bring down 0, \( 0 \div 7 = 0 \)

Result: \$7.20

23. Draw and shade circles to show that \( 1 \frac{1}{4} \) equals \( \frac{5}{4} \).

Answer & Explanation:

Draw two whole circles and divide each into 4 equal quadrants (representing fourths). Shade all 4 quadrants in the first circle (representing 1 whole, or \( \frac{4}{4} \)). Shade 1 quadrant in the second circle (representing \( \frac{1}{4} \)).

Counting the shaded parts, you have exactly 5 shaded quadrants, which demonstrates that \( 1 \frac{1}{4} = \frac{5}{4} \).

25. Shade 50% of the square you drew in problem 24. How many square centimeters did you shade?

Answer: 8 square centimeters

Explanation: First, find the total area of the square: \( 4 \text{ cm} \times 4 \text{ cm} = 16 \text{ cm}^2 \).

To shade 50%, we shade exactly half the area. \( 50\% \text{ of } 16 \text{ cm}^2 = 8 \text{ cm}^2 \).

26. Write twenty-one thousandths as a fraction and as a decimal number.

Answer: Fraction: \( \frac{21}{1000} \), Decimal: 0.021

Explanation: The term "thousandths" means the denominator of the fraction is 1000. So we have \( \frac{21}{1000} \).

To write this as a decimal, the number 21 must end in the thousandths place (the third digit after the decimal point). Therefore, we place a zero in the tenths place: 0.021.

27. Which of these polygons has no lines of symmetry? (A, B, C, D)

Answer: D

Explanation: Let's check each shape:

  • A: Equilateral triangle. Has 3 lines of symmetry.
  • B: Isosceles trapezoid. Has 1 vertical line of symmetry down the middle.
  • C: Square. Has 4 lines of symmetry.
  • D: Right trapezoid. Because one side is straight up and the other is slanted, there is no way to fold it in half such that both sides match perfectly. It has 0 lines of symmetry.
28. Which polygon in problem 27 is not a quadrilateral?

Answer: A

Explanation: A quadrilateral is a polygon with exactly 4 sides. Shape A is a triangle, which has 3 sides. Shapes B, C, and D all have 4 sides.

29. In half an hour the minute hand of a clock turns how many degrees?

Answer: \( 180^\circ \)

Explanation: A full clock circle represents one hour and spans \( 360^\circ \). Half an hour covers exactly half of the circle, which is \( 360^\circ \div 2 = 180^\circ \).

30. Compare: \( 4.2 \bigcirc 4.200 \)

Answer: \( 4.2 = 4.200 \)

Explanation: Trailing zeros after a decimal point do not change the value of the number. Both numbers have a 4 in the ones place and a 2 in the tenths place, and no other non-zero digits. Thus, they are equal.
