Practice Math Test Solutions

1. Mark has eight days to read a 320-page book. If he wants to read the same number of pages each day, how many pages should Mark read each day?
Answer: 40 pages
Explanation: To find the daily page count, divide the total number of pages by the total number of days.
$$ 320 \div 8 = 40 $$
2. When Mark has read two sevenths of the book, what fraction will he still have left to read?
Answer: $ \frac{5}{7} $
Explanation: The entire book represents 1 whole, which can be written as $ \frac{7}{7} $. Subtract the part he has read from the whole.
$$ \frac{7}{7} - \frac{2}{7} = \frac{5}{7} $$
Use the information given below to answer problems 3-5.
Ninety students voted for their favorite sports. Forty-five voted for basketball. Soccer received twelve fewer votes than basketball. The remaining students voted for baseball.
3. How many students chose soccer as their favorite sport?
Answer: 33 students
Explanation: The problem states soccer received 12 fewer votes than basketball. Basketball received 45 votes.
$$ 45 - 12 = 33 $$
4. How many students chose baseball as their favorite sport?
Answer: 12 students
Explanation: First, find the total votes for basketball and soccer: $ 45 + 33 = 78 $.
Subtract this from the total number of students (90) to find the baseball votes:
$$ 90 - 78 = 12 $$
5. Which sport received the fewest votes?
Answer: Baseball
Explanation: Compare the votes for each sport:
Basketball: 45
Soccer: 33
Baseball: 12
Baseball has the lowest number of votes.
6. How many months equal 5 years?
Answer: 60 months
Explanation: There are 12 months in a single year. Multiply the number of years by 12.
$$ 5 \times 12 = 60 $$
7. In the word MISSISSIPPI, what fraction of the letters are Is?
Answer: $ \frac{4}{11} $
Explanation: First, count the total number of letters in MISSISSIPPI, which is 11.
Next, count the number of 'I's in the word, which is 4.
The fraction is the number of 'I's over the total letters: $ \frac{4}{11} $.
8. John ran a 3-kilometer race. Three kilometers is how many meters?
Answer: 3000 meters
Explanation: There are 1,000 meters in 1 kilometer. Multiply the number of kilometers by 1,000.
$$ 3 \times 1000 = 3000 $$
9. Sarah entered a 15-kilometer bike ride but walked her bike one third of the distance. How many kilometers did Sarah walk?
Answer: 5 kilometers
Explanation: To find one third of 15, multiply 15 by $ \frac{1}{3} $ (or divide 15 by 3).
$$ 15 \div 3 = 5 $$
10. What is the perimeter of this triangle?
Answer: 60 mm
Explanation: The perimeter is the total distance around the outside of the shape. Add the lengths of all three sides together.
$$ 15 + 20 + 25 = 60 $$
11. The length of $\overline{XY}$ is 35 mm. The length of $\overline{XZ}$ is 80 mm. What is the length of $\overline{YZ}$?
Answer: 45 mm
Explanation: Point Y lies between X and Z, meaning the length of XZ is the sum of XY and YZ ($XZ = XY + YZ$). To find YZ, subtract the length of XY from XZ.
$$ 80 - 35 = 45 $$
12. $\$45 - (\$22.50 + 75\text{¢})$
Answer: $21.75
Explanation: First, convert $75\text{¢}$ to dollars ($0.75). Then, solve the operations inside the parentheses by adding.
$$ \$22.50 + \$0.75 = \$23.25 $$
Next, subtract this sum from $\$45.00$.
$$ \$45.00 - \$23.25 = \$21.75 $$

16. $258 \div 5$   (Shown as $5 \overline{) 258}$)
Answer: 51 R 3
Explanation: Perform long division. 5 goes into 25 five times ($5 \times 5 = 25$). Subtract 25 from 25 to get 0. Bring down the 8. 5 goes into 8 one time ($5 \times 1 = 5$). Subtract 5 from 8 to leave a remainder of 3.
17. $638 \div 7$   (Shown as $7 \overline{) 638}$)
Answer: 91 R 1
Explanation: Perform long division. 7 goes into 63 exactly 9 times ($7 \times 9 = 63$). Subtracting gives 0. Bring down the 8. 7 goes into 8 one time ($7 \times 1 = 7$). Subtract 7 from 8. The remainder is 1.
18. $6y = 420$
Answer: $y = 70$
Explanation: Isolate the variable $y$ by performing the inverse operation. Divide both sides by 6.
$$ y = 420 \div 6 $$ $$ y = 70 $$
19. Find the missing number $n$:
  412
  185

  800
Answer: $n = 203$
Explanation: First, add the two known numbers together.
$$ 412 + 185 = 597 $$

20. Compare: $2 \times 5^2 \bigcirc \sqrt{81} \times \sqrt{25}$
Answer: > (Greater than)
Explanation: Evaluate both sides of the comparison.
Left side: Solve the exponent first ($5^2 = 25$), then multiply.
$$ 2 \times 25 = 50 $$
Right side: Find the square roots ($\sqrt{81} = 9$ and $\sqrt{25} = 5$), then multiply.
$$ 9 \times 5 = 45 $$
Since $50 > 45$, the correct symbol is $>$.
